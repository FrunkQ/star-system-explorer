Measured 24 Nov 2026 (RS3, `crates/sse-nav/tests/sol_today_logs.rs`): Cassini's log against Horizons at seven cruise dates is off by 2 thousand to 8 million km, at most 1.4% of its distance from the Sun (worst mid-way from Earth to Jupiter), the arcs being solved to the planets' centres on the NAV fixture's own planet orbits. In low orbit (the ISS log, one Horizons orbit a year): 41 km off an hour after a sample, 200 km after 6 hours, 720 km after a day, 5,000 km after a week and 12,900 km (the far side of Earth) after a month, as drag and the Earth's oblateness move the phase; so low-orbit logs need a sample every few hours to be right in place, and are right in size and plane between. Ion-driven Dawn and JWST near L2 are sampled every few months for the same reason.
