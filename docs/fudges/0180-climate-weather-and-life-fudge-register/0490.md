| F47. `crates/sse-cosmos/src/feed.rs` (GH3) | A galaxy that is looked at (the galaxy chart, the home galaxy) is fed by its assembly tree at the Chart level (ψ ≥ 10⁻³): each member's own G3 galaxy and GH2 orbit decide what arrives and when (stars lost on the way to the stellar halo, gas pushed out to the hot reservoir, the rest with its black hole at the centre). The members' own galaxies are fed by nothing (recursion one level deep), their orbits run in the unfed host's potential (one pass), and their stars and gas are read at their own G3 runs as if isolated until their star formation stops. Responses by ratios: bulge growth = thin disc × μ × (1 − gas share) (Hopkins et al. 2009); disc heating = 0.1 × satellite mass over disc mass at a merger and 0.02 × the same × v_c/v_z at a disc pass (after Toth and Ostriker 1992; the two numbers set so a Milky-Way-mass host keeps a thick disc of a tenth to a third of its disc); G3's burst at mergers, none at passes; a merger's stars all go to the bulge. Marks: strength x/(x + half) of a driving ratio per kind, fading by e over a fixed time per kind (design 9.4's "Lasts" column); shapes are a few numbers. A leading arm (design 9.4) is driven by the backwards share of a closest approach or meal × a mass term (full at μ = 0.01, falling as 1/(1 + (μ/0.1)²)) × exp(−pericentre / 0.4 disc scale lengths) × how far the halo outweighs the disc inside the pass (none at 2, full at 8), all from one set of 2D, in-plane runs of NGC 4622 (Byrd, Freeman and Howard 1993); it fades by e in 0.9 Gyr (a third left after a Gyr, about three rotations of the disc's edge) rather than by the disc's own rotation; checked against the 1-in-100 yardstick (Iye et al. 2019). Below ψ = 10⁻³ nothing arrives (the halo's smooth growth counts it).
