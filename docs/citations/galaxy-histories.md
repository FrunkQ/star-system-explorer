# Galaxy histories (track GH): the design's references

GH0, citation half. Every DOI in `handover/design/SSE4-galaxy-histories-design.md` was checked on Crossref
on 22 Nov 2026, apart from the ones DM1 put on the data sheet, which are in
[matter-reference.md section A5](matter-reference.md#a5-the-cosmos-group-dm1). The design was written from
memory, so this checks that each DOI is the paper named, by the authors and in the year given.

**"verified (metadata)"** means the DOI, the first author, the year and the journal match. The numbers and
relations the design takes from a paper are checked against the paper itself when the GH package that relies
on them is built. That package's row then goes into the area file it belongs to, with the full status words of
[the register](../citations.md).

## Summary

- 113 references checked: 111 match, 1 was wrong and is corrected, and 1 could not be fetched.
- **Corrected (metadata):** Bekki and Chiba 2006 had the DOI of a medical paper (Shulman et al. 2006, Clin.
  Infect. Dis.). The paper the design means is ApJ 637, L97, doi:10.1086/500304, on dark subhaloes passing
  through extended HI discs and leaving holes and new stars. That is the claim the design makes for it, and the
  design now has the right DOI.
- **Unverified:** Nagamine and Loeb 2003 (New Astronomy). Crossref refused the fetch twice, perhaps because of
  the brackets in the DOI. It is left as written.
- **Year differences:** nine papers came out online the year before their print issue: Horta 2021, Mancera Piña
  2022, Di Cintio 2014, Benítez-Llambay 2017, Harmsen 2017, van den Bosch 2018, Cautun 2019, D'Souza and Bell
  2018 and Parkinson, Cole and Helly 2008. The design gives the print year,
  which is the usual form, so nothing changes.
- Williams, Turyshev and Boggs 2004 (the lunar laser ranging limit on G's drift) is cited by DM6, not by the
  design. It is listed here because the time-law rule rests on it.
