# Naming (track NM): sources and licences

NM0. Every source in `handover/design/SSE4-naming-design.md` section 8 was checked on 22 Nov 2026, along with
the licence of each name list that might ship. Status words are as in [the register](../citations.md). For a
naming rule or scheme, "verified" means the page says what the design relies on.

| Where | Source | What is relied on | Status | Checked, and the licence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Catalogue scheme, exclusions | IAU, Naming of astronomical objects, https://www.iau.org/public/themes/naming/ ; Naming of exoplanets, https://www.iau.org/public/themes/naming_exoplanets/ | Who names what. Planets are lettered from b **in discovery order**. Public names may not be pets, purely commercial, people or places known mainly for political, military or religious activity, or living people. Minor planets wait 100 years for political or military names. | verified | Quoted from both pages. IAU products are CC BY 4.0, so credit the IAU. |
| Real star names (night sky packs) | IAU WGSN list, https://www.pas.rochester.edu/~emamajek/WGSN/IAU-CSN.txt (IAU page https://iau.org/public/themes/naming_stars) | Approved proper names of real stars | corrected (metadata) | The file has 451 names (1 Jan 2021), and the list is still growing. | Many nations, how the world names things | IAU NameExoWorlds 2015, 2019, 2022, https://nameexoworlds.iau.org/final-results | Names proposed by country, each pair on a theme, with meanings | verified | Country, proposer and meaning are in the downloadable sheets, not in the page text.
