name:    Vesh
about:   A hive-building people's tongue. Guttural, with one click and doubled stops. Short hard words; the describing word comes first, the thing last.
script:  latin
kind:    invented

  E = k z kh rr q

  V = a 6, i 3, u 3, aa 2
  E = k 4, z 3, kh 3, q 2, rr 2
shape:   CV 4, CVE 5, VE 1
length:  2-3
never:   aa+aa, aa+a, a+aa, a+a, i+i, u+u, x+x, k+k, k+kh, kh+kh, q+q, z+z, rr+r, rr+rr
stress:  first
