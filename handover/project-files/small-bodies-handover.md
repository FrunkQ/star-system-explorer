# Small bodies handover (from Build plumes, frosts and small bodies, 24 Nov 2026)

## Approach for small bodies (TRUE5, from Alex 24 Nov 18:02 and 18:05)
- **One resolving method.** A belt or ring is a probability field until you look at one member. Looking resolves it, deterministically from its seed and the field, into a definite body. It is the same method that pinpoints and mines asteroids (Traveller PR 400, extraction and stock), extended rather than duplicated. A resolved member carries its type (C, S, M, P and so on), its structure (monolith, rubble pile, contact binary, separated pair), its shape, and a short history that explains it.
- **Light, cited distributions, no per-asteroid simulation.** Type fractions by distance from the star (DeMeo and Carry 2014), rubble-pile share by size (the spin barrier near 2.2 h for bodies over about 200 m, Pravec and Harris 2000), and contact and separated binary shares (radar about 1 in 7 contact for near-Earth asteroids over 200 m, Benner et al. 2008; about 1 in 6 with a moon, Margot et al. 2002, Pravec et al. 2006). Each is an editable cited number, to be checked against its source before it goes in.
- **Shapes from physics.** Spin against self-gravity: "Build giant bands, clouds and hazes"'s `sse.shape.spin_flattening` gives q. A new block of this track, `sse.shape.spin_figure`, in its own formulas file, takes q and gives the Maclaurin, Jacobi or two-lobe figure for rubble piles near breakup. Strength-held monoliths keep impact-shaped lumps. True draws the kernel's own ground heights on small bodies (displaced globe), not a sphere. The reference shapes to check are Arrokoth, 67P, Itokawa, Bennu and Vesta.
- **Notable bodies pre-resolved.** Bodies that formation made, such as a Psyche-like stripped core from the violent phase, exist as definite tracked individuals, seeded from what the formation stages record (asked of "Build kernel resolve and rules").
- **Add an object.** "Build planet and star editing" adds "Small body" and "Comet" in ED4 and ED6 from plain recipe objects (id, label, kernel kind, inputs with defaults, citation) and a kernel read that makes a small body from a seed and a few inputs. This track supplies both.
- **Formation seeds (from the kernel thread, 24 Nov).** `sse_formation::violent::Upheaval` (violent.rs, about line 520) lists `UpheavalEvent`s, among them Impact {t_myr, target, impactor, outcome merge | merge-moon | hit-and-run | erosion | shattered, v_over_vesc, angle_deg, debris_earth}. Each `World` has a `WorldLedger` whose `solid()` gives the metal share (erosion strips the mantle first, REF_MARCUS10). `leftovers.rs` has `SmallBody` {address, kind, rank, diameter_km, mass_kg, a_au, e, i_deg}, resolved per address. A hit-and-run runner is not kept as its own body yet: a Psyche-like core would be a new SmallBody made from a shattered or hit-and-run outcome, its mass and metal share taken from the ledger. The op is `run_formation`, built in sse-kernel `systems.rs`.
