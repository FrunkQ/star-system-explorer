Source: Don P. Mitchell's Venera catalogue, http://mentallandscape.com/C_CatalogVenus.htm (reprocessed panoramas); original data from the Soviet Venera programme (IKI RAN)

Picked for: the only pictures from Venus's surface: an orange-lit, flat basalt plain under thick cloud. The colour test for light through Venus's deep air (sunlight reddened by the CO2 and clouds) on the ground.
