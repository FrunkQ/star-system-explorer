## Alex's decisions for this track (24 Nov), so nobody has to ask again
- All in `handover/design/SSE4-playbook-and-switchboard-design.md` (accepted in full; section 19 says how to pick it up without the chat). In short: no Next and no script; a Playbook of chapters, pages and sections whose presets fire at roles, never devices; the Switchboard is the one "nerve centre", available on every desk, replacing the Seats dialog and seat rows; bookmarks say what, layouts how it looks, playbooks store them, the Switchboard says where; nothing about players is added anywhere but the Switchboard; interference is a switch on every look, on by default, last kind used; OBS out early, OBS in only on GM-machine screens, NDI parked.
- Atlas design, question 15 (Alex, 24 Nov 20:14, "Yes to all"): the Playbook's look card is cut down to what is used at the table: pick a look, pick a palette, the interference switch and its live dial. No new work on the Workshop's Screens composer or Styles (they stay at Engine depth); making or changing an interface goes through Orac and the layout editor (7.4). This replaces the four-row look card of design 7.4 for PB10.
- Atlas note 6 (Alex, 20:17): a player's screen has no Edition switch; an Atlas in an edition is a bookmark, fired at Everyone, a role or a screen like any other source, and whoever is there reads and steps through it. The edition travels with what was sent, not with the role.
- Chart room (24 Nov): the on-air chip sits right of the desk switch on Run only; Bookmarks go through Stage's `extraTools` snippet so Stage imports nothing from run/.
