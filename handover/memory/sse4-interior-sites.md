Alex, 23 Nov 2026 19:28 and 19:33: games in SSE4 will be space trucker, frontier explorer or military team stories with horror (he loves Mothership), run inside SSE4 "even down to crashed spaceship wreckage and entering a lava tube"; SSE4 supplies exploring, mining and hauling, abstracted from rules.

Design note (PROPOSED, not accepted): `/mnt/project-files/sse4/SSE4-interior-sites-design.md`, repo copy `handover/design/` (PR 309). Mockup `/mnt/project-files/sse4/IS-screens/site-survey-mockup.html` plus screenshots, artifact "SSE4 site survey" https://claude.ai/artifact/TPrbGRF7LBmyNHMpqgbR6V. Thread "Design sites you can go inside".

The proposal: one model, an interior is spaces joined by ways. Ground spaces come from the world's history (the S14 passage graph); construct spaces are the generator's own parts and nothing finer (keeps the no-deck-plans ruling; only wrecks, derelicts and ground sites in 4.0, not working ships). A wreck is any construct given one dated `construct.wrecked` event; damage derived by a small rule logged as a fudge, every part overridable. Mines sized from the amount taken out. Conditions inside per space (air, temperature, shelter, gravity, light, water, soundness, contact) in the existing hazard shape and difficulty words; nothing refused or rolled. Found by real survey methods as sensor rules shown in bands. Main view is a survey map (plan and profile) on every device; walk view later. Exploring inks the party's map through Known to ([[sse4-secrets-and-visibility]]). No wreck appears by itself in 4.0 (lighter touch). Packages IS1 to IS10 with owner threads in the note's section 12; proposed example pack "Deep places".
