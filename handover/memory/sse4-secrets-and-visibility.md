Alex, 23 Nov 2026 19:25: V3 kept secrets with a small visibility icon by each fact; SSE4 should be more intuitive, in far more places, "subtle yet not overwhelming".

Design note (direction ACCEPTED 23 Nov 19:37: "this is a work of GENIUS" apart from the dot by the name; 19:38: "WORDS too much... maybe just a small eye"): `/mnt/project-files/sse4/SSE4-secrets-and-visibility-design.md`, repo copy `handover/design/` (PR 306). Mockup `/mnt/project-files/sse4/SV-screens/known-to-mockup.html` plus screenshots, artifact https://claude.ai/artifact/DiBSjywKnQGqcSRzWAHCeC.

The proposal: one control "Known to" on any thing or fact. On the GM chart, ink is what the table knows and pencil (dotted, quieter) is GM only. One small EYE is mark and button (Alex chose the eye over a dot, and over words): closed GM only, half open Heard of, open Known; one press reveals to the table (replaces the Scanned button), a popup from the dot holds audiences, one-fact exceptions, cover stories ("Told instead"), dates and bulk scope. Also Hand over (give one audience what another knows), peek, a table ledger on Run. No marks on Explore or on player screens. Built on existing data views, audiences, `observe.grant`, record 8A; nothing spreads knowledge by itself in 4.0.

Packages SV1 (kernel) to SV7; SV1 and SV2 (chart room) first. Links ([[sse4-highways]]) and territory layers use this control and add none of their own.

The ten questions (section 12) are taken as yes with the eye change unless Alex says otherwise; he has not answered them one by one.

**How to apply:** secrecy marks are small symbols, never worded labels; do not bring back the dot.
