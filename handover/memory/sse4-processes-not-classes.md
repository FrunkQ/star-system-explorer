Alex, 21 Nov 2026 ("do the new rules - LEAN INTO THE PHYSICS!"): no physics stage gates on a class or a typed cut-off (e.g. ice-shell when water_fraction >= 0.05). Each process computes a 0-to-1 strength from its governing ratio (Rayleigh number, stress over yield, vapour pressure over ambient, supply over removal), all run weighted, near-zero skipped; class atlas reads the label off the result; hard edges only at real phase or force boundaries, computed from composition, with natural width.

**Why:** cut-offs misclassified Pluto, Callisto, Enceladus, Miranda; Alex wants everything as understandable physics blocks.
**How to apply:** doc /mnt/project-files/sse4/SSE4-processes-not-classes.md (surface gate audit, S0b reframed); standing rule in build plan section 1. Weather got its own review thread "Review weather against new geology"; stellar granulation and Rossby spots and flares went to "Build light and nebulae". See [[sse4-exogeology-review]].
