Alex, 23 Nov 2026: "we wont DO the skill checks... we just want enough good information for the GM to make those judgement calls." Physics never forbids a manoeuvre (high-g aerobrake, hard landing); the kernel reports hazards (ratio to limit, chance 0..1 with basis, subject, role, kept-value effects) as information on the GM screen.

**Why:** the GM runs the table; SSE4 informs, it does not adjudicate.
**How to apply:** SSE4 never rolls or adjudicates. Updated by Alex 23 Nov 19:33 and 22:12: a game pack MAY declare its difficulty ladder (dice, names, targets) and skill names, and the GM screen shows the system's target and skill beside SSE4's own words ("Pilot, Difficult 10+"), computed from the hazard's odds through the system's dice; information only, GM-only, nothing rolled. Effects may name kept values (ship.readiness, ship.discipline from TG9) as information only. Related: [[traveller-pack-track]].
