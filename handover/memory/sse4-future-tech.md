Design note: `/mnt/project-files/sse4/SSE4-future-tech-design.md` (repo copy in handover/design). ACCEPTED by Alex 23 Nov 2026 in four steps; all 25 questions yes.

Core (16:15): technology is how far each of ten limits is pushed (pack `base-tech`); no switches, an advance moves a cited figure and the same sizing block runs; "fictional" read off arithmetic; one ladder per setting for real and imagined tech; capability on a civilisation, inherited and dated; needs computed from parts; availability never says no; "Develop it" is a hand-pressed dated event; "found, not built"; honesty inks built / not yet built / fictional; "show it without"; same rules for all constructs. Set up on Reference under "This setting" (Technology page) and on each civilisation's page. FTL rule held per civilisation.

4.1 ruling (17:49): culture and tech grow from life in 4.1, so 4.0 is hand-set data only; FT2 has no growth hooks.

Size rule, section 5.2 (19:20): ten times was only a rule of thumb. Immaturity acts on the advance's figure by part family (engines a seventh, bulk materials a fifth, energy stores a third, near-ceiling figures two-thirds of the way, plain mass unchanged); ten times kept only for fictional or unknown devices; a prototype never worse than what they hold; past home level improve until the cited ceiling.

Section 15 "Keeping the books" (20:14, wins over earlier sections): balance checks on every design (energy, jet power, momentum, heat flows downhill), so fictional also means a balance that does not close; waste heat carries its temperature, radiator no hotter than its source unless a heat pump is paid for; every fictional advance states light, heat, momentum, energy; figures stored as real quantities, advances on one figure never multiply; Technology page gets a scale with marks and a live reference construct; real-hardware test of about ten craft at "Sol today" (data from the real spaceflight pack thread); family defaults sit in the fudge register as rules of thumb.
