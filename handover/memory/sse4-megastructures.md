Alex, 23 Nov 2026 (V3 gap list, point 2): "constructs are just constructs". A megastructure is big enough to affect the system at stellar level, so it is a construct with physics blocks attached (like an autopilot is attached), e.g. thrust blocks for a stellar engine; its effects go through the same blocks as everything else, no special case.

**Why:** one general mechanism over specials; physics, never faked.
**How to apply:** nothing to build yet. Lands late in stage 5 after a short design note written by the Traveller thread (owns constructs/templates, J7) with the kernel thread. Related: [[traveller-pack-track]], [[sse4-coming-together]].
