Alex's rulings, 19 Nov 2026:
- V4 scope is "they exist": evolutionary biology is there to do planetary work (gas mixes, soil, ground cover). "What they look like" (appearance, body plans) is a later version.
- Soil is "SO important": soil plus rock-breaking, fungus-like life is a terraforming pair most living planets would evolve. Treat soil as first-class state; weatherers and decomposers are roles by function, not Earth's fungi.
- Biomes must form naturally, "like Earth, not like sci-fi". Life should arise from energy gradients so non-carbon life can evolve; high oxygen is both hazard and boost with competing sinks.

Accepted by Alex 21 Nov 2026 ("Evo - go for it"): exotic chemistry defaults to the "Plausible chemistry" preset; big leaps gated by environment with hard-step dials kept; Evolution Lab adopts the same gradient catalogue format. His steer, same message: do more real physics, chemistry and biology in the browser wherever practical; back off only when impractical on a modern browser or phone; devices 5+ years old may struggle; baseline is "good enough". **How to apply:** prefer the real calculation, fall back only on a measured need, and log the measurement.
