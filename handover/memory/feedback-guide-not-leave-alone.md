Alex, 24 Nov 2026 14:44 to 14:46, in the thread "Design planet and star editing": "we guide better now rather than leave them alone." "We *should suggest*: this world *could* be human habitable with an atmosphere... and that in turn may need a mag field to protect it... and a user can do one and not the other." "We let them and point out things that break physics (kept to GM)." Alien compatibility: "hooks in now, implemented in the aliens update 4.1".

**Why:** V3 left the builder alone with sliders; SSE4's physics knows what a world is close to being, so it should say so.

**How to apply:** any editing or building surface offers forward-looking suggestions (the "Could be…" recipes in [[sse4-editing-design]]), with each step separate and optional, dependencies shown, and the cost of skipping stated. Never refuse; never nag (one line, can be silenced). Anything that breaks physics is listed for the GM only, never for players. "Habitable for whom" is a requirement profile as pack data (Humans in 4.0) so 4.1 species plug in. My reading: this extends "danger is drama, never forbidden" from play to building.
