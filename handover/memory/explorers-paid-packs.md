Design file: /mnt/project-files/sse4/SSE4-explorers-paid-packs.md (proposed 21 Nov 2026, thread "Design paid packs on Explorers"). ACCEPTED by Alex 21 Nov 16:46 ("Stripe Connect - yup", "Yes to recs"): all section 13 recommendations are rulings.

Alex's own words, 21 Nov 2026:
- Creators of detailed world packs may charge a few pounds on Explorers; he takes 20% off the top for hosting, creator gets the rest. Build can start in parallel but must not be surfaced in the UI.
- SSE4 itself never takes money. He does not plan to sell packs himself, only take the cut.
- Fan-made work based on other people's settings cannot be monetised; a price is blocked on anything using content that is not the seller's. (Decided, not open.)
- He already runs a VAT-registered business, details on sublime.co.uk. That business can be the seller of record.

Alex 21 Nov 16:43: build all the charging now to test, behind one admin master switch (off by default); the cut shown openly to buyer and seller; free is always free; creators can always host elsewhere; copyright can move to his company if needed (design says not needed). Priced items are maps and systems as well as packs.

Accepted rulings: Stripe Connect; the site is the deemed seller for VAT (HMRC platform rule), so UK VAT on his normal return and non-Union One Stop Shop for EU; tax and card fees off first then 80/20; £2 minimum; monthly payouts over £25 after a 30-day hold; no DRM, signed receipt; buying only on the website, never inside SSE4; Gaia data by reference only in paid packs. Build packages PP1 to PP8 for the thread "Integrate Explorers site with SSE4"; PP1 is pure code with no migration, PP2 onward each need his yes.

Checked 21 Nov 2026: Paddle bans marketplaces; Stripe Managed Payments (merchant of record, 3.5% extra) does not support Connect or marketplaces; Lemon Squeezy reported closing 31 Mar 2027.
