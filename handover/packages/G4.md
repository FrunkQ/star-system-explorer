# G4: patch envelopes from a galaxy, green regions, outcome-first queries

Status: done (19 Nov 2026). Kernel track (cosmos suite), PR into `main`. Design: galaxy design sections 7 and 12.

## What was built
`sse-cosmos::envelope`, over a G3 galaxy and its rings (`GalaxyEnvelope { galaxy, disc, params, seed, address }`):
- `surface(r, t)`: ring state per unit area, linear in radius between ring centres and in time between 50 Myr
  steps, so neighbouring patches agree at a shared face. G3's `Disc` now stores every ring's state at every step
  (`Disc::states`, `RingState`: gas, stars, [Fe/H], [alpha/H], [CN/H], [s/H], [r/H], [K-40/H], star formation,
  supernova rates, neutron-star mergers so far).
- `local(place, green)`: the envelope at radius, azimuth, height and time: gas, stars and systems per pc^3,
  abundances ([Fe/H], [alpha/Fe], C/O by number with solar 0.55, [r/Fe], [K-40/Fe]), star formation and supernova
  rates per pc^3, times a two-arm log-spiral density wave (pitch 12 degrees, pattern speed 25 km/s/kpc, contrast
  0.6), times vertical profiles (gas and young stars 100 pc, thin 300 pc, thick 900 pc with 12% of stars), times a
  lognormal texture (0.2 dex) from keyed value noise on a 50 pc lattice, trilinear so it is smooth.
- Hazards: supernovae within 10 pc per Gyr; passages within 0.1 pc per Myr (n pi b^2 sqrt 2 sigma, sigma 35 km/s
  at the Sun's radius rising inward); lethal long gamma-ray bursts within 2 kpc per Gyr, suppressed above a third
  solar metallicity; active-nucleus dose from the galaxy's episodes in the last Gyr over R^2; Gyr since the gas
  passed the rocky-planet floor.
- `green(local, criteria)`: each criterion (metals enough, not too many, few supernovae, few passages, few bursts,
  quiet nucleus, quiet long enough) and two verdicts: habitable (simple life could start) and complex (it has also
  had 4 Gyr). All criteria are pack settings (`provides.other.cosmology.green`).
- `green_band(t, criteria, complex, step)`: the widest run of green radii at a time (midplane, arms and texture
  off): what the galaxy chart's hatched band shows. `find_green(t, criteria, n)`: outcome-first, keyed places in
  the band that pass, safest first.
- `patch_model(base)`: G0's patch model with enrichment (Sun's ring now, gradient, a fitted clock tau), [alpha/Fe]
  now, star formation rate and disc length from the galaxy, so `form_patch` makes systems from a G3 galaxy.
- Pack: `provides.other.cosmology.envelope` and `.green` in `packs/base-cosmology`.
- Ops list: `cosmos.galaxy`, `cosmos.rings`, `cosmos.envelope`, `cosmos.green_band` with cost classes.

## Checks (`cargo test -p sse-cosmos g4`, 6 tests)
Milky-Way-like galaxy from G3 (radii scaled to the Milky Way's R_d = 2.6 kpc):
- Continuity: either side of every ring edge and centre (0.1 mm apart), at two times, [Fe/H] within 0.01 dex and
  gas, stars, star formation and supernova rates within 1%; texture continuous across lattice faces; gas
  continuous in time between steps.
- The Sun's neighbourhood now (azimuthal mean): 0.099 systems per pc^3 (band 0.03 to 0.3; Reid et al. 2002),
  midplane gas 0.029 solar masses per pc^3 (McKee et al. 2015 give 0.04 to 0.05: ours is a little low),
  0.93 supernovae within 10 pc per Gyr (Gehrels et al. 2003), 0.41 lethal bursts per Gyr (Piran and Jimenez 2014
  give 0.5 to 1), 0.16 passages within 0.1 pc per Myr, C/O 0.46; arms raise and lower gas by the contrast.
- The green band: complex life now from 5.4 to 20.7 kpc (the outer value is the disc's edge); 5 Gyr ago 5.8 to
  11.4, so it grows outward with the metals front; no complex band before the universe was 6 Gyr old (it appears
  at a universe age between 6 and 8 Gyr, 6 to 8 Gyr ago; Lineweaver et al. 2004 say about 8); habitable first at 4 Gyr,
  8.5 to 9.1 kpc. Removing the metallicity ceiling moves the inner edge in.
- Outcome-first: 5 places found, all complex, safest first, deterministic.
- Bridge: the derived patch model validates, reproduces the Sun's ring at the Sun's birth within 0.02 dex,
  and `form_patch` resolves systems from it (gradient -0.035, tau 27.6 Gyr, [Fe/H] now +0.13).
- Cross-device: golden `tests/golden/envelope.json` (new), harness engine `envelope`.
- `tests/golden/galaxy.json` changed on purpose: the rings digests now include the per-step states G4 needs
  (no other value changed).

## Known limits
- No bar, no bulge or nuclear terms, no flaring of the gas layer, no events still in effect (supernova remnants,
  star-forming complexes, streams: G6). The arms are a fixed density wave, not transient.
- The burst yield and the encounter and supernova thresholds are pack values chosen against the cited ranges;
  the inner edge of the band comes mostly from metallicity above +0.3 and passages, not from supernovae.
- The band is radial (azimuthally averaged); arm and texture variation show only in `local` and `find_green`.
- Octree refinement and the leaf grid live with G5 (cohorts, frames, promotion); G4 answers at any point.

## Integration points
- G5 reads `local` for cohort densities and abundances, and replaces G0's fixed cohorts.
- The galaxy chart (UI track) draws `green_band` over time and `local` on hover.
- Shared files touched: `crates/sse-wasm` (engine `envelope`), the harness lists, `packs/base-cosmology`.
