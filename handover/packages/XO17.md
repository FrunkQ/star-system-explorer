# XO17: Composite in the band switch, on every view

Track XO, design `handover/design/SSE4-exotic-objects-design.md` section 6A (Alex, 24 Nov 2026 17:03; and 17:15: "the composite view should be everywhere!"). Built 24 Nov 2026 by "Build exotic stars and black holes". The renderer thread handed over all of Composite (contract, control and layering).

## What was done

- **Contract** (`packages/ui-contract/src/view-controls.ts`, new `composite.ts`, `composite-presets.json`):
  - Band is now `BandPick`: the six bands or `"composite"`, the last entry of the band switch on every view. The mix is `ViewChoice.composite` (`{ layers: [{ band, colour }], preset? }`), kept on the device like the rest of the view choice and kept while another band is shown, so Composite comes back as it was.
  - `viewOptions(spec, choice, limits?)` also returns `layers` (one for a single band, so every view reads layers the same way), `composite`, `drawable` and `maxLayers`. `shown.band` stays one band: for Composite, its Visible layer, else its first (`baseBand(choice)` gives the same where only one band can be used, such as the Light panel's recipe).
  - A view says in which styles it lays layers with `ViewControlsSpec.composite`; elsewhere Composite is listed quiet with the reason (rule 12: never hidden). It needs two drawable bands.
  - Limits: a seat ticks only the bands its sensors carry (`limits.seat`; Visible is never taken away); a phone lays at most two (`PHONE_LAYERS`, open point 14) and says why on the third (`deviceMaxLayers()` reads the media query, none outside a browser).
  - Presets are data (`composite-presets.json`: Observatory, Hot and cold, Radio over visible); `withCompositePresets()` lets a pack or Orac add more. Starting colours follow observatory habit.
  - Legend: `compositeLegend()` lists every layer and its colour ("Composite: X-ray violet · Visible actual · Infrared red · Radio orange · each band scaled to its own brightest · mapped").
- **Control** (`packages/chart-grammar/src/ViewControls.svelte`): Composite in the Band row; pressing it keeps the fold open on a tick list with presets, each band's tick and colour swatch, the refusal reason, and the legend under the bar. On a phone the fold now opens edge to edge under the bar (before, it ran off the right side).
- **Layering, one mechanism for every view** (`packages/chart-grammar/src/CompositeStack.svelte`): a view wraps its picture in a snippet that draws itself in a band; the stack draws it once per layer, tints every layer but Visible (an SVG colour matrix on its canvas: light in its colour on a dark ground, ink in its colour on the chart room's paper), and adds them (`plus-lighter` on dark, `multiply` on paper). Only the bottom layer draws the HUD and takes the hands; presses, drags and the wheel on it are passed to the same point of every other layer, so Free view turns them together. Upper layers show only their picture. The pixel maths for views holding their own frames is `packages/renderer/src/composite.ts` (`composite()`, each band scaled to its own brightest; `tintMatrix`, `inkMatrix`).
- **Views using it now**: the system chart in 3D and True; the galaxy chart in 3D and True; the globe (Visible with Mag shows the globe with the field's cut beside it). The flat system chart and the face-on galaxy chart list Composite quiet with "turn it to 3D or True"; views drawing one band (Window: Visible and Infrared only; surface; cutaway) list it quiet with the reason. `docs/view-controls.md` regenerated with a Composite column.
