# U7: Biosphere view and life timeline (UI track)

Status: part 1 done, 19 Nov 2026. The view draws the kernel's `life_view` (L1d) for the five example worlds. It asks for the selected world's own biosphere first and says plainly when the kernel has none. Worlds on the chart get theirs when adopted systems carry habitat graphs (kernel). The LifeFlow board (the life pipeline as a node flow) is part 2.

## What it does

**Life screen** (`documents/life.gm.json`, tab "Life", also in the phone screen menu). `sse.view.biosphere` sits in the chart area, with the body card beside it and the ruler below.

- **Source.** With a world selected on the chart, the view asks `life_view {body}`. If the kernel refuses (no habitat graph yet), it says so in a note and shows an example world. The example picker offers the L1d fixtures: Earth today, Archean Earth, Europa, Red-dwarf desert and Dead Earth. Each example is stamped "example world", and a line says what it shows. The kernel's evidence label ("plausible reconstruction") is always stamped beside the title.
- **Three lenses.** Ecology, Physics and History buttons choose how every reason is told: the epochs' and hooks' `why`. The default is the module's `lens` param.
- **Timeline.**
  - Epochs are bands on a time axis from formation to now. Major epochs are tinted, and names are shown where they fit.
  - Production is drawn on a log axis, with its decade range stated.
  - Origins and catastrophes are ticks (losses are dashed and accented). Capability gains are dots.
  - Picking a band, or the Epoch list (keyboard), tells that epoch's story in the chosen lens. Without a pick, the current epoch is told.
  - Below the timeline, the dated events are listed with the kernel's reasons and the dials they lean on.
- **Who feeds whom.** The food web is laid out by feeding level.
  - Producers sit on the bottom row (filled discs), and consumers and parasites sit above (open).
  - Recyclers (dashed) and symbionts (ringed) sit in a side column.
  - Disc radius follows the order of magnitude of the carbon a guild makes or takes in.
  - Links: consumers eat the level below in their own habitat kind, and recyclers take from everything in theirs.
  - The guild table (largest first) gives role, level, habitat, carbon and share of food. Picking a guild gives its physics name, grade, size limit and flight.
- **What a scan would show.** The view shows each L1d tier in words.
  - From afar: colour and seasons.
  - Scanned: gases, red edge, each signal's reason, the verdict offered with confidence (rounded down, so 0.995 reads 99%), and what the scan is blind to.
  - Surveyed: the summary, guild cards and hazard.
  - In the rocks: traces and how much survives.

  This is the GM's whole picture. Players get these fields through their data views by tier (L1d's disclosure map).
- **Story hooks** (`false_positive`, `hidden_life`, `dead_world_record`, ...), with their reason in the chosen lens.
- **Traits** (producer colour, starlight peak, solvent, chemistry, tallest producers, largest animals and flyers, senses, days, to visitors, origin) and **the dials** the story leans on, marked as unknowns.

Honesty: nothing is computed in the view. `life_view` is GM-only in the session, so a table seat is refused. An example is never passed off as the selected world.

## Checks

- `ui-contract/test/life.test.ts` (Node, real kernel):
  - All five examples load.
  - Earth's ten epochs are in order, and "The age of green mats" is major.
  - Production rises over decades.
  - Every guild is drawn inside the box, and every consumer above level 1 has food.
  - Earth reads green, with a candidate at 99% and abundant fossils.
  - Europa's scan is blind to the ice (`hidden_life`).
  - The red-dwarf desert is a candidate with no biosphere (`false_positive`) and an empty web.
  - Dead Earth has `dead_world_record`.
  - A table seat is refused.
