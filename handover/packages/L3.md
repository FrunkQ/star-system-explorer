# L3: life on the chart's own worlds

Status: done, 26 Nov 2026 (thread "Bring Life to your own worlds"). Stage 2 of `design/SSE4-coming-together.md`
("Habitats on adopted worlds so Life reads them").

## What was there

The kernel's `life_view` refused every `body` (`no_habitats`, "adopted systems do not carry habitat graphs yet") and
answered only for the five L1d fixtures. That covered every system on the chart, not only imported ones: grown,
catalogued and typed-in systems got no life either. The Life screen asked for the picked world, got the refusal and
showed an example world.

## What it does now

`life_view {body}` works for a body of any system on the chart, at the system's now:

1. **Reading** (`sse-kernel/src/life.rs`, `Kernel::body_reading`): the same physical description the weather and the
   survey read (`body_conditions`: the surface envelope's body, its climate conditions), plus the banded climate
   (`sse_climate::orbit`), the heat flow now off the regime timeline (radiogenic plus tidal), the regime, the ice
   ground (`sse_surface::ice_ground`), the lighting star's temperature from the body's own life line, and the
   system's birth year.
2. **World stack** (`sse-life/src/world_life.rs`, `stack`): from that reading, by `provides.other.life_from_world`
   in base-life (every number there has a source line):
   - land in five latitude bands (cover = the climate's land share), each with its seasonal temperature range,
     sunlight at the ground (`ebm::surface_sun_fraction`, the climate pack's own), the star's peak wavelength,
     the dose under the air's column, and phosphorus weathered in proportion to rain. Land with no rain gets
     water activity 0 (dry ground closes it, with the reason).
   - an ocean (in the climate's ocean basins) whose depth is the world's oceans spread over its sea, with the
     rivers' share of the land's phosphorus and vent hydrogen (and ferrous iron under an anoxic air) in
     proportion to inner heat;
   - on an ice world, an ice shell as thick as the conductive lid `651 ln(T_melt/T_s)/q` (ice's conductivity from
     the sheet) and an ocean under it when the water layer is thicker than that lid;
   - crust (pore water if the world has any water, warmer with depth by its heat flow), mantle (a heat source
     only above the vents threshold), air.
   - a giant gets air only: its cloud decks are not resolved into habitats yet, and `held` says so.
3. **Pipeline**: habitat graph, then a history in two epochs (nothing until the magma ocean froze, then today's
   graph held), coupled to the L2 air box sized to the world (its moles of air, area, and volcanism by heat), so
   oxygen is a balance the world's own life strikes; the biosphere is resolved when life holds on at the end;
   signatures, epochs and hooks as for the fixtures. Seeded by universe seed and body address.
4. **Response**: `life_viewed` gains `held` (absent for fixtures, so the contract golden is unchanged): what was held
   at today's value or left out, in words.

Signatures gain `reducing_air` (Observables, default false): methane in an air that is a tenth or more hydrogen is
what the gas makes by itself, so it is no longer read as a sign of life (Jupiter was a "false-positive paradise").

The Life screen shows the picked world's own life, with "What this reading holds fixed" listing `held`. A world
the surface suites cannot resolve (too few facts) gets "no biosphere for <world> yet" with the reason, and an
example. Picking an
example over a chart world shows it, with "Back to <world>"; picking another world reads that one.

## What the catalogued Solar System gives (checked in `life_tests.rs`)

- Earth: alive, land life, complex cells, oxygen built by its own life to about 0.2 of the air around 4.1 Gyr
  (later than Earth's 2.4 Gyr: today's air and heat are held, and the history has no catastrophes); production about
  46 Gt C a year (Earth's measured about 105, Field et al. 1998).
- Europa: microbes in the ocean under a lid about 12 km thick, hidden from every scan.
- Venus (737 K), the Moon (no air), Mars (cold and dry ground, 214 to 247 K in the top 5 km of crust): never alive.
  Mars keeps the "false positive" hook: its abiotic oxygen reads as life, as Viking's did.
- Jupiter: no ground, no false positive.
- Titan, Callisto, Enceladus: no life, because of what the surface suites give them today: Titan resolves with no
  air at 82 K (its measured air is the parked follow-up package), and Enceladus's heat flow of 0.0055 W/m² leaves
  its water frozen through.

## Not done (next)

- History over time: the climate, the young star's faintness and the world's catastrophes (impacts, glaciations)
  as epochs and blows, instead of today's state held.
- Habitats the stack does not make yet: caves and lava tubes from the surface chain, cloud decks on giants and
  Venus, hydrocarbon seas (no chemistry for them yet anyway), regional heat (Enceladus's south pole).
- `life_view` dials (kernel queue item 6) still unbuilt: the board's knobs stay read only.
- A cache: the coupled history runs on every ask (the nine Solar System worlds in the test take a few seconds
  together in release; not timed per world, and not in the browser).
