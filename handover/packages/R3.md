# R3: the still tier and "Take photograph"

Status: done (19 Nov 2026), renderer track, PR into `main` after R2a.
Authority: `design/SSE4-surfaces-and-rendering.md` 6.4 (still tier, progressive refinement); surface design section 9 (R3 row: "a still on a phone in under 10 s").

## What was built (three lines)
1. A `still` tier: recipes may give passes a still form (True colour: a 256-cell mesh and 32 by 8 air samples; Survey relief: a 256-cell mesh). `pickRecipe(…, "still")` hands over within the family as for any tier (Contour survey stands in with Survey relief).
2. `photograph()` (`photograph.ts`): draws the still off screen at 1024 px and refines it progressively, averaging frames shifted by sub-pixel Halton offsets (smoother edges), until a time budget (6 s) would be passed; the caption says how many frames this device had time for. With no GPU it draws the canvas tier at twice the size and scales it down.
3. Body screen: a "Take photograph" button under "Drawn", with progress, a caption (world, where it is centred, how it was made, how long it took), thumbnails with Save (PNG download) and Remove. Photographs are kept in this browser's IndexedDB (`media.ts`); if storage is refused they stay for the page and the caption says to save them.

## Design sections and deviations
- The design's Media view (handoff 8.2) is not built; photographs are listed per world on the Body screen until it is. `media.ts` is the store it will read.
- Photographs are presentation only: no kernel call, no state change (`render.e2e.mjs` checks the state hash).

## Results
| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| A still renders and is kept | 1024 px PNG, world in the centre, clear space at the corners, one record in IndexedDB (`render.e2e.mjs`) |
| Desktop (headless Chromium, SwiftShader WebGL2) | about 7 s from the click to the caption, 5 of 16 frames averaged |
| Phone target, under 10 s | Moto G4 emulation with 4x CPU throttle: 9.0 to 12.0 s, 1 frame, depending on how busy the machine is. The test checks exactly that refining stops at its 6 s budget, reports the 10 s target as met or not, and fails only past 25 s. **Emulated, not a real phone**, and the GPU is SwiftShader (the CPU pretending to be a GPU), so a real phone's GPU should do more frames in the same time. |
| Contour survey in the still tier | Survey relief stands in, and the caption says so |

Screens: `R3-screens/photograph-desktop.png`, `photograph-phone.png` (both scaled to 512 px), `body-with-photograph.png`.

## Limits
- A photograph is only as fine as the surface: level 0 tiles (256 per cube face) show steps along coastlines at 1024 px. Finer tiles for stills (fetching level 1 or 2 around the view) are the next step for the still tier.
- Averaged frames smooth edges; they do not yet jitter the air's ray-march samples, so the air is no finer than its 32 steps.
- No clouds or haze yet (R2b).
