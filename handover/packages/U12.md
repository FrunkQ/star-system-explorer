# U12: Tactility pass (UI track)

Status: part 1 done 19 Nov 2026. Alex, 19 Nov: he likes the interface and wants it kept ("dynamic minimalism": vast data under the hood, approachable on top). He wants more "user tactility": dynamic popups and animation flourishes. The standing rules are in `handover/interface-style.md`.

## What it does

- **Motion tokens.** `motion-quick` (120 ms, feedback on touch), `motion` (200 ms, a change of state) and `ease` (eases out). They live in the theme schema (`packages/skins/chart-room`), so a theme can slow, speed or stop motion. Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, the shell forces both durations to 0, whatever the theme says.
- **Every control answers a touch.** Buttons, selects and role=button elements ease their colour and shadow changes and sink 1 px while pressed. Tabs and other segmented controls shade on hover.
- **Hover peek on the chart.** With a mouse, resting on a body shows its name and kind beside it, and the body's hit area shades. It doesn't appear on touch, for the selected body (the card already describes it), or while dragging or measuring.
- **The selection ring closes in** on the body you choose.
- **Cards open from what they describe.** A newly chosen body's card comes in from the direction of its body on the chart: a short slide (30 % of the way), a slight scale, a fade. Edits to the same body don't animate.
- **Dialogs open from the button that opened them** (`tactileDialogs`, one document listener for every `<dialog>`, on the GM and player pages). Closing is instant: holding a dialog's close back would delay the person.
- **The time ruler glides** when zoomed by buttons, keys or Show. The span changes on a log scale (`between` in `calendar.ts`), so going from 14 Gyr down to a year reads as a steady zoom. The readout, register, stepping and `data-span` use the target at once, so nothing waits on the motion. The wheel stays direct.

Helpers in `packages/chart-grammar/src/motion.ts`: `motionMs`, `easing`, `reducedMotion`, `opensFrom` (a Svelte action), `fromKeyframes` and `tactileDialogs`. Only transform and opacity are animated. Other interface threads should use these rather than write their own.
