# F3: growth and giants, modern block

Status: done (18 Nov 2026). Physics track. Design: `SSE4-system-formation-design.md` section 3 (stages 4 and 5) and 8.
Standard level: published rate laws stepped in time on the F1 disc (80 log steps from 0.05 Myr to the disc's end),
keyed draws (stream `formation.growth.v1`) where outcomes are chaotic.

## What was built (`crates/sse-formation/src/growth.rs`)
- Planetesimal rings: a geometric ladder of embryos from the inner edge out to 1.5 rc0 (or a companion's
  truncation), spacing drawn 1.35 to 1.7; the first and heaviest ring at the water line (Drazkowska and Alibert 2017).
  Each claims its seed mass (0.01 sqrt(r/AU) Earth masses) from the local F1 band, only groups condensed there; band
  vapour condenses as the disc cools.
- Pebble growth: a pebble stream of 1.5 Z times the gas accretion flow, drawn from the outermost bands first, drifts
  inward past every embryo; each takes the 2D Hill share (Lambrechts and Johansen 2014, St 0.1); ices sublimate
  inside their lines. Pebble isolation (Bitsch et al. 2018 eq. 26, with its viscosity factor) stops the flow and
  piles the rest into a ring outside the planet (F5's belts).
- Gas: cores in the pebble flow hold a thin envelope (Lee and Chiang 2015 scaling); isolated cores contract on a
  Kelvin-Helmholtz time 1e11 yr (M/Earth)^-3 and run away, limited by 80 percent of the disc flow (Lubow and
  D'Angelo 2006) and the gap flow (Tanigawa and Tanaka 2016). The gas disc's end is the deadline.
- Migration: one torque for both regimes (Tanaka 2002 type I divided by the gap depth 1 + 0.04 K, Kanagawa et al.
  2018), gap open past K = 25, gapped giants slowed a further 0.02 (calibrated, below); a water-line planet trap
  holds 3 to 15 Earth-mass cores (Hasegawa and Pudritz 2011); the inner edge is the magnetospheric cavity at about
  8-day orbits (Lee and Chiang 2017).
- Resonances and merges: convergent pairs are caught at a drawn first-order resonance (2:1, 3:2 or 4:3); pairs
  closer than 2 sqrt(3) mutual Hill radii merge (Gladman 1993), ledgers kept whole.
- Budgets in integer units: solids initial + condensed = bands + bodies + rings + sublimated + into the star, per
  element group; gas initial = bands + bodies. Each body's ledger lists every claim (time, F1 band, units by group,
  gas) and merged bodies whole; it equals the body exactly.
- `protoplanets()` hands every body to F6. Events: embryo forms, pebble isolation, runaway gas, gap opens, captured
  in resonance, embryos merge, parked at the inner edge, gas gone.

## Checks (`cargo test -p sse-formation`, 33 tests, 4 new in `growth_tests.rs`)
- Population of 10,000 systems (2,000 each: Sun-like at [Fe/H] -0.3, 0, +0.3; 0.4 and 1.6 solar masses at solar):

| Population | giants within 10 AU | hot giants (< 0.1 AU) | systems with 3+ close planets | of those in a chain |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1.0, [Fe/H] 0 | 21.2% | 7.1% | 77 | 72 |
| 1.0, +0.3 | 39.3% | 11.6% | 149 | 139 |
| 1.0, -0.3 | 7.8% | 3.7% | 54 | 54 |
| 0.4, 0 | 2.1% | 0.4% | 42 | 42 |
| 1.6, 0 | 26.5% | 5.2% | 118 | 111 |

  Asserted: solar giants within a factor of two of 15 percent (Cumming et al. 2008; Fulton et al. 2021); +0.3 and
  -0.3 dex ratios 1.85 and 2.7, inside 1.5 to 3.5 (Johnson et al. 2010: 10^(1.2 [Fe/H]) gives 2.3); M dwarfs under
  half the solar rate, F/A stars above it; at least 70 percent of 3+ planet systems chained at the end of the gas
  (Izidoro et al. 2017, 2021: nearly all, most break later in F4); measured 418 of 440.
- Radius gap: every close-in (P < 100 d), non-giant planet above 1 Earth mass of the 6,000 Sun-like systems, through
  F6: counts per 0.2 Earth-radius bin from 0.8 are [2, 85, 110, 91, 15, 12, 14, 13, 13, 20, 17, 13, 20, 19, 22, 13]:
  a super-Earth peak at 1.2 to 1.6, a valley at 1.8 to 2.4, a sub-Neptune hump beyond (Fulton et al. 2017).
  Asserted: peak over three times the valley, hump over 1.1 times it.
- Determinism (40 systems twice, identical JSON); budgets close exactly; each ledger equals its body; ring sums.
- Water ice sublimates on the way in; inner rocky worlds carry no CO ice.

## Calibrated constants (each named in the code)
PEBBLE_FLUX_PER_Z 1.5, KH_COEFF_YR 1e11 (Ida and Lin's 10^k1 range is 1e8 to 1e10; dusty envelopes run slower),
LC_COEFF 0.02, TYPE_II_REDUCTION 0.02, TRAP_MASS 3 to 15. Tuned on the solar population only, then the metal and
star-mass trends were checked, not tuned.

## Known limits
- Hot giants come out in 7 percent of Sun-like systems against about 1 percent observed (Wright et al. 2012): 15 to
  20 Earth-mass cores leave the trap, reach the inner edge and run away there. Inner-disc gas depletion (Lee and
  Chiang 2016) is the planned fix.
- The sub-Neptune hump is weaker than Kepler's (sub-Neptunes are about as common as super-Earths there); F4's
  spreading of chains to longer periods and core-powered loss in F6 should strengthen it.
- No planetesimal accretion or embryo-embryo stirring in the gas phase; eccentricities stay at their small draws until F4.
