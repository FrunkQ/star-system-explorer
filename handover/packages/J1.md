# J1: the campaign record: notes, kept values, actors and relations

Status: done (19 Nov 2026). Kernel track, PR into `main`. Design: `handover/design/SSE4-record-logic-programs-design.md` (track J) sections 2, 8A and 9, section 10 row J1.

## What was built
**Crate `sse-record`.** Every change is a chronicle event; nothing reads these fields into physics or RESOLVE.
- **Notes** (`record.note`): text at an address (the first subject) with linked addresses, tags, the writing seat, an audience and an optional tier.
  - The audience is `"everyone"`, `"gm"` (the default) or a list of audience ids. The GM sees every note.
  - With a tier, a note shows only where the audience's data view shows that tier.
  - `arrives` is the event's `activated_at`: news dated when it happened, but not shown or sent before it arrives.
  - Notes are never edited. A correction `supersedes` the old note (and cites it as a cause); an empty note withdraws one. `notes_at` gives the live notes at a time.
  - Agent-written notes carry evidence `narrative`.
- **Kept values** (`record.set`): declared by packs under `provides.other.record.kept` as `{id, shape, unit?, range?, choices?, default?, tier, writers, describes}`.
  - Shapes: number, flag, choice, ref, text, list.
  - One keeper per kind: a second pack declaring the same id is refused.
  - A change rides in `payload.set`, so the ordinary fold reads it. Its data carries the writer, the reason, the previous value and (for numbers) the delta.
  - A write from anyone not in `writers`, or a value that does not fit the shape, range or choices, is refused before anything is written.
  - `misc.<name>` is the GM's schema-free corner (GM only, `secret` unless the stack says otherwise).
  - `kept_at` returns each value with its history ("why is this 14?"). Values whose pack is not loaded stay readable and are marked "kept by a pack that is not loaded".
- **Actors** (`record.actor`): `u:<id>/actor:<id>`, nested under a parent actor, with `name` and `actor.kind`. **Relations** are kept values of shape `ref`.
- Pack key choice: the design says a kept kind is "a quantity kind with `storage: "kept"`". Kept kinds include flags, text and lists, which are not quantities, so they get their own block (`provides.other.record.kept`) instead of `provides.quantity_kinds`. The fields are the same as the design's `KeptKind`.

**Secrecy (design 8A, added mid-package after Alex's concern).**
- **The record is GM-only.** The kernel keeps it in its own chronicle beside the universe's, never broadcast and never recomputed on a player device.
- **Two hashes.** Record activity never changes the universe state hash that ordinary seats check. Every record op returns the unchanged `state_hash` and a separate `record_hash` (the record chronicle's head).
- **Scenes.** `sse-observe` scenes now carry `notes` per subject, filtered by audience and tier. A note's address joins the scene even with no facts there (an actor known only from the record). Player scenes carry no record event ids; the GM's do. A scene with no notes serialises without the field, so every existing scene hash and golden is unchanged.
- **Kept values in scenes.** They are folded in on the GM machine and filtered like any fact. Each kind is at its declared tier, and `misc.*` is at the GM-only rung, unless the stack's disclosure lists the field. View precision applies, so a banded value is a band.
- **Disclosures.** New op `disclosures {audience, at_s}` is what a seat is sent. Per subject it holds the notes the audience may see (already arrived) and the record facts its view shows, at the view's precision. It has no event ids and no causes. Note ids (`n1`, `n2`, ...) mean something only in that stream and are the same at every subject a note names. A `stream_hash` lets the seat check what it got.

**Kernel ops.**
- `add_note`, `set_kept`, `add_actor`: GM-side writes.
- `record {address, at_s?}`: GM only; never send it to a seat.
- `disclosures {audience, at_s}`: what a seat is sent.
- `export_chronicle` now also carries `record` and `record_head`, and `import_chronicle` replays and checks them. Older exports without a record still import.

## Checks
- `sse-record` (12 tests):
  - audiences;
  - notes never touch state;
  - tiered notes;
  - supersede and withdraw, and the refusals;
  - narrative evidence;
  - news that arrives later and never before it happened;
  - a kept value folds and explains itself (history, writers, reasons, deltas, clearing);
  - unlisted writers and bad values refused, with nothing written;
  - relations between actors, and actor refusals;
  - unloading the pack leaves values readable;
  - one keeper per kind and declaration checks;
  - an empty record changes nothing.
- `sse-observe::record_notes_follow_audience_and_tier`:
  - crew, Vesh and GM each see their own notes;
  - an actor known only from the record shows only to its audience;
  - tiered notes wait for a scan;
  - player scenes have no event ids;
  - a scene with no notes hashes as before.
- Kernel `the_record_through_the_api`:
  - the universe hash is unchanged by every record op;
  - notes by audience and arrival;
  - a correction supersedes;
  - kept history and refusals;
  - the crew's disclosure has the credits as a band (500 to 1500) at long range and exactly once scanned;
  - nothing secret, no other audience's note and no event id appears anywhere in the disclosure text;
  - the Vesh get news only after it arrives;
  - save and reopen keeps the record, orphaned when its pack is not loaded;
  - a tampered record head is refused.

## For the UI track
- Do not send record ops' results or events to seats; the P2P log stays the universe's.
- Send each seat `disclosures` for its audience on its own channel.
- Player notes (`add_note` from a seat) are intents: the GM applies them, as for any intent.

## Limits
- Disclosures are a snapshot at a time, not yet a batched delta stream per time boundary (J4).
- Per-audience arrival is one `arrives` per note. News reaching different audiences at different times is several notes for now.
- Note stream ids come from the note's content and subjects. Two identical notes at the same place and time share one id.
- The record does not yet branch with look-ahead. Look-aheads cover the universe only, as before.
- The relay encryption and per-seat channels in 8A are UI and Worker work.
- The table secret and dice are J2.
