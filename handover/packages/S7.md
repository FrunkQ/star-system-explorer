# S7: cover, named features, built layer, aerostats and GM reshape tools (19 Nov 2026)

Surface design section 4 (L3 cover, L4 features, L5 built layer, GM editing) and section 6 (the aerostat band).
Needs S4 (done) and L1a; the life input is a number for now (below). Physics track.

## What was built
- `crates/sse-surface/src/cover.rs` (`surface-cover-v1`): `layers(settings, envelope, seed, atlas, inputs)`
  returns S7's `Layers` for an atlas. `Atlas::with_layers(layers)` adds them to every tile. Settings sit under
  `provides.other.surface.cover` in `packs/base-surface`. `CoverSettings::check` refuses unknown deposit
  kinds, built-rule factors and conditions.
- `crates/sse-surface/src/named.rs` (`surface-named-v1`): `register(feature settings, envelope, seed, atlas,
  GM names, renames)` returns the world's feature `Register`. Settings sit under
  `provides.other.surface.features`.

### Climate (what cover reads)
- **Mean temperature by latitude**: the two-mode energy balance (North 1975), T = T̄ − (ΔT / 1.5) P2(sin φ).
- **Equator-to-pole contrast**: ΔT = 120 K / (1 + x / 0.54), with the transport number x = p (9.81 / g) (288 / T)³,
  which grows with the radiative time constant. Values:
  - Earth 42 K;
  - Venus 10 K;
  - Mars 111 K;
  - Titan under 1 K;
  - airless worlds 120 K.
- **Height**: temperature falls at g / c_p, times 0.66 on wet worlds (Earth 6.45 K/km). Below 0.01 bar there is
  no lapse.
- **Circulation cells** per hemisphere: round(sqrt(a Ω / 50 m/s)). Earth 3, Mars 2, Venus 1, Titan 1.
- **Wetness**: rising branches are wet and sinking branches dry. Each band's prevailing wind (easterly in odd
  cells counted from the equator) carries moisture inland on a 2° grid. Moisture falls as exp(−distance from the
  sea / 1500 km) and as exp(−barrier upwind / 1500 m), the rain shadow.

### Cover and colour
- **Ice** (new cover code `ice`, index 4; the UI's `COVERS` and colour are updated):
  - Sea ice forms below a mean of 271 K.
  - Land ice forms below 266 K, and up to 10 K colder where the ground is dry.
  - On a dry world with water, ice survives only below the 165 K cold trap.
  - On Earth this puts the equatorial snow line at about 5.5 km and land ice from about 68°.
- **Colour** (`colour`, 0xRRGGBB): taken from the pack's table by cover, or by material where the ground is bare.
  Bare ground darkens with wetness. Ice raises albedo to 0.6.

### New tile layers (only when the atlas has layers)
- `wetness` (0 to 255).
- `deposit` (codes into `DEPOSITS`).
- `structure` (1 + built-kind index).
- `colour`.
- When layers are present, `pending` keeps only flow (if not worn) and biome.

### Deposits
Each kind names its source in the pack.
- **On the plate grid** (from the plate history):
  - copper where continental arcs are 5 to 300 Myr old (Kesler and Wilkinson 2008);
  - orogenic gold in collision belts older than 50 Myr;
  - nickel in flood-basalt provinces;
  - buried carbon where life (input > 0) meets S3's thick sediment.
- **Points from S4 craters**:
  - nickel under melt sheets of craters 100 km and wider;
  - ice in permanently shadowed polar craters, on airless worlds under 0.001 bar. A crater qualifies when the
    highest Sun (90° − |latitude| + obliquity) stays below the wall angle seen from the floor centre. The
    shadowed radius is ½ D (1 − tan(Sun) / tan(wall)).

### Built layer (L5)
- **Pack rules** score each 2° land cell on these factors:
  - water (wetness), coast, flat (slope under 10°), temperate (Gaussian about 288 K, width 15 K), low ground,
    resource (a deposit) and equator.
- **Placing by rule**:
  - Each rule has conditions on t_k, height_m, lat_abs_deg, land, coast and deposit.
  - Places are picked best first with a minimum spacing. The count is per Mkm² of land, up to a maximum.
  - A keyed jitter, well below any weight, breaks ties.
- **Kinds**: settlement, port, mine and base.
- **Only when asked**: `inputs.settle` names the kinds to place. An unsettled world builds nothing.
- **Why**: each place lists its score terms and the rule's source.
- **GM placements** (`Reshape::PlaceBase`) add their own kinds.

### Aerostats
- `aerostat_band(cover settings, envelope, air, wind)` gives the band with 0.5 to 1.5 bar and 273 to 323 K.
- **Air profile**: the adiabat runs up to a 0.1 bar tropopause (Robinson and Catling 2014) and is isothermal
  above. For a giant, the profile runs both ways from the 1 bar level.
- **Drift**: 60 m/s by default (Venus cloud-level order) until a weather stage gives winds. The band reports
  one circuit of the world in days, and what lies below.
- **Air guess**: `Air::guess` takes the molar mass and c_p from the pack's gas guesses until an atmosphere stage
  exists: H2-He for giants, CO2 above 10 bar, N2-CH4 when cold, N2-O2, and thin CO2.

### GM reshape events (`Reshape`)
- **Kinds**: raise range, drop crater, flood basin, pin a height, place a base.
- **Dates**: each edit is dated (Myr since the world formed) and shows only at epochs at or after its date.
- **Applied list**: every edit is recorded with a note and, where the physics refuses it, an `impossible`
  reason. The edit is kept and tagged, as design 4 asks.
- **Impossibility checks**:
  - **Range**: refused above the rock limit, 9 km × (9.81 / g), the same limit as S4's shields.
  - **Crater**: refused if wider than the world.
  - **Flood**:
    - Hydrocarbon worlds flood with hydrocarbon.
    - Below 611 Pa, or above the boiling point at that pressure (Clausius-Clapeyron), the flood is refused.
    - Below freezing, the basin floods but freezes over.
  - **Pin**: refused when the change exceeds the rock limit.
- **How edits reach the tiles**:
  - Ranges and pins are landforms, band-limited and in the tolerance like S6's.
  - Craters use S4's shapes and tolerance terms.
  - Floods set cover below their level within the radius.
- **Pins**: a pin becomes a smooth bump sized after measuring the fact-floor height at the pin, with earlier
  pins already in place. The floor tile at the pin then gives the pinned height (to 0.1 m), so the terrain
  honours pins.

### Named features (L4)
- **Read from the atlas's own level-0 tiles** on the cube grid (cover agrees with what any view draws):
  - oceans (water bodies of 10% of the surface or more), seas and lakes (100,000 km² or less);
  - continents (1% of the surface or more) and islands (10,000 km² or more);
  - ranges: land 1.5 standard deviations above the mean land height, 50,000 km² or more, each with its peak;
  - ice caps (100,000 km² or more);
  - the deepest point, and a summit when the highest point is not a range's peak.
- **From earlier stages**:
  - S4 craters 20 km and wider, shields and plains;
  - S6 landforms 100 km and longer;
  - the GM's ranges and craters;
  - built places, with a GM-given name kept as the original.
- **Per feature**: id, address `<body>/feature:<id>`, kind, place, extent, area, height or depth, date where
  known, cause, and a generated name.
- **Names**: from the pack's syllables and per-kind patterns, keyed by the feature id. A feature's name does not
  depend on the order features are found in. Names are unique on a world.
- **Renames** are authored events with a date, an optional audience (a seat's own names) and `restore`:
  - `name_of(id, audience, at)` returns the audience's latest name, else the general latest, else the original;
  - `names(id)` returns the history, original first;
  - an unknown feature or an empty name is refused;
  - renames never touch tiles or addresses.
- **GM names** (`AuthoredName`) add places the generator left unnamed.
- **Sifters** mark the highest point, deepest point, largest crater, crater lakes, canyons visible from orbit
  (rifts and chasmata 1000 km or longer), the largest body of liquid and the largest continent.

## Checks (`cargo test -p sse-surface cover_tests`)
- **Climate**:
  - Earth has 3 cells, a 42 ± 4 K contrast, a 6.5 K/km lapse and an equatorial snow line at 4.5 to 6.5 km.
  - Venus has 1 cell and under 15 K of contrast; Mars over 80 K and no lapse; Titan under 5 K and hydrocarbon
    seas.
  - Bands are wet at 0° and 60° and dry at 30° and 90°.
- **Ice**:
  - At least 90% of Earth's samples above 80° are ice, and low tropical ground has none.
  - Every ice sample is below its threshold.
  - Mars with water keeps a north cap and no equatorial ice.
- **Cover agrees between globe and ground**: at least 95% of level-1 samples match one of their 4 children.
  Heights stay within tolerance and albedo agrees.
- **Wetness**: coastal land is wetter than inland land at the same band wetness, and land behind 2 km of upwind
  high ground is drier.
- **Deposits**:
  - Earth has arc copper only on continental arc cells.
  - Buried carbon appears only with life.
  - The Moon has shadowed-crater ice only poleward of 55° and nickel under big craters.
  - Deposits are tagged in their tiles and explained.
- **Aerostat band matches the air profile**:
  - Venus comes out at 51.3 to 53.9 km, 0.75 to 0.51 bar, 323 to 302 K. The VIRA reference (Seiff et al. 1985)
    has about 1 bar and 350 K at 50 km, and 0.5 bar and 300 K at 55 km.
  - The band's edges are exactly the profile's values and lie inside the rule; just above, they lie outside.
  - Earth's band runs from 0 to about 2.3 km.
  - Mars is refused as too thin, and Jupiter as only hotter deeper.
- **GM edits**:
  - Each edit is dated.
  - A 40 km range on Earth, a crater wider than Earth and a lake on Mars are tagged impossible.
  - The range raises its tiles by over 4 km, the crater digs, and the flood covers.
  - Tiles stay within tolerance with the GM's relief.
- **Pins**: the fact-floor tile at the pin gives the pinned height within 0.15 m. A −60 km pin is tagged
  impossible.
- **Built layer**:
  - Earth settlements stay within the rule's temperatures and spacing, and ports exist.
  - Footprints show in level-8 tiles.
  - An unsettled world builds nothing, and the Moon gets bases.
- **Features**:
  - Earth has an ocean, continents, ranges with peaks and one deep; names are unique and non-empty.
  - Renames behave by date, audience and restore.
  - Addresses and tile hashes are unchanged by renames, and the same world gives the same register.
- **Sifters**: Earth's highest point, deepest point and largest body of liquid; the Moon's largest crater.
- **Determinism**: same layers, same tiles.
- **Golden**: `tests/golden/surface-cover.json` (new) covers Earth with plates worn, settled with 72 places,
  life, a GM range and pin, plus the Moon and the Venus band. It is in the cross-device harness (Node: all
  engines identical, including surface-cover). **No existing golden changed**: without layers, tiles and hashes
  are as before.
- **Timing** at pack defaults (256 samples a tile): layers about 20 ms, the level-0 register about 1 s, and one
  tile about 0.2 s (release build).

## Known limits and next steps
- **Climate**:
  - Annual means only: no seasons or obliquity cycles, and no tidally locked substellar pattern (locked worlds
    use the same zonal model).
  - Mars caps reach about 62° here, where the real residual caps reach about 80°.
  - Weather (track W) replaces winds and cloud.
- **Missing inputs**:
  - Life is a number (`inputs.life`) until the life suite's L1a/L1c output is wired in.
  - Air comes from pack guesses until an atmosphere stage exists.
- **The register reads level 0**:
  - Small lakes and islands below about 2 cells are not listed.
  - Cube cells are taken as equal area, which is off by up to about 30% near face corners.
- **The wetness grid** reads base heights, before the GM's edits.
- **Built placement** reads the 2° grid; its slope and coast checks are coarse.
- **Not yet built**:
  - no kernel op serves layers or the register yet (the UI's Body globe can read the new tile layers once one
    does);
  - rivers are not yet named features;
  - strata, flourishes, landmarks and polities are S9 to S13.
- **References**: several were written from memory (North 1975, Showman et al. 2013, Kesler and Wilkinson 2008,
  Goldfarb et al. 2001, Naldrett 1999, Lightfoot 2017, Landis 2003, Sánchez-Lavega et al. 2017) and need their
  DOIs checked, like the earlier packages' references.
