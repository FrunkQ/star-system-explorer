# W1: weather contract, circulation regime, climate averages (19 Nov 2026)

Track W (weather design `design/SSE4-weather-design.md` sections 1, 2 step 1, 6 and 8). New crate `crates/sse-weather`,
new pack `packs/base-weather`. Nothing in other crates changed (root `Cargo.toml` gains the workspace dependency line).

## What was built
- `contract.rs`: the swappable contract of design section 6. `WeatherBlock` (id, climate, field, systems, provenance),
  `FieldTile` of `Cell`s on the surface atlas's cube-sphere tiles (`sse_surface::atlas::TileKey`, same addressing as terrain),
  three height layers per cell (cover, type, condensate, optical depth, wind, turbulence), precipitation kind and rate,
  temperature offset, lightning, aerosols; `WeatherSystem` (nameable storms, plumes, polar features, with track, born, ends, why,
  evidence), `Pin` (a seen and named system a save keeps), `ClimateAverages` and the swap `TOLERANCE`, `Report`.
- `conditions.rs`: `Conditions`, the facts weather reads, keyed on conditions (solid surface or not, spin, star-locked, tilt,
  year, flux, albedo, temperature, pressure, molar mass, gas mix, surface liquid, ocean fraction, internal heat), never on a
  "planet" kind. `from_surface` builds them from the surface track's `Body` and `SurfaceEnvelope`, listing what it assumed.
- `circulation.rs`: the regime (first pack rule that holds), Hadley edge (Held and Hou 1980), cells per hemisphere (Rhines
  1975 arrest wavenumber), sinking and rising edges, storm tracks; what condenses first going up (Clausius-Clapeyron from the
  triple point, NIST WebBook data); chemical decks and hazes; seasonal rain belt; annual climate bands.
- `rules.rs`: the rule block `sse.weather.rules` v1, filling field tiles with the zonal (belted) climate at a date. W2 adds
  formations and drawn systems; `systems` returns only pins so far.

## Candidates to move into flows
The regime rules already are pack data. Hadley edge, Rhines cells, condensation level and supply could each be atoms
(`sse.weather.hadley_edge`, `.rhines_cells`, `.condensation_level`, `.latent_flux`); kept in Rust for W1 because the field
evaluates them per cell. Big Block: the field itself (UI: the cloud globe and a belt diagram).

## Known limits
- Mars counts as thin air (design table), so no Martian baroclinic storms; Titan's few methane clouds round to 0 % cover.
- Giants are only classified here; W3 fills them. `systems` returns pins only until W2.
- `from_surface` assumes spin 10 h, tilt 20°, an Earth-like gas mix and a sun-like star where the physics track does not yet
  publish them; it says so in its second return value.

## Integration
- Other tracks: depend on `sse-weather` and read only `contract.rs` types. Renderer: `field(conditions, date, tile, n)`.
- Physics track: `ClimateAverages.rain_cells` and `bands[].precip_m_yr` are ready for S3's rain term when you want them.
