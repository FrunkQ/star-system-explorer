# W4: smoke, ash, haze, smog and spray; climate effect of large veils (19 Nov 2026)

Weather design section 4 and stage W4. Crate `sse-weather`: new `aerosols.rs` (+ `aerosols_tests.rs`); pack key
`provides.other.weather_aerosols` (settings with sources, every reference with a DOI). One small hook in `rules.rs`:
`haze_from(list, conditions)`, which `rules::haze` now calls, so W4's extra hazes reuse the photochemical filter instead of
copying it. Not wired into `RuleBlock` or the kernel op yet (see Integration); every existing golden is unchanged.

## What was built
- **Sources are inputs** (`aerosols::Sources`), until S7 (volcanism), S12 (chronicle) and L1c (built layer) publish them:
  steady `volcanoes` (lat, lon, vent height, outgassing kg/s, optional vent speed), dated `eruptions` (lat, lon, day,
  duration, mass eruption rate or erupted DRE volume, SO₂ mass, vent height, optional vent speed), `impacts` (energy, or
  diameter with pack density and speed), `fires` (lat, lon, area, day range), `industry` (lat, lon, tech 0..1, population,
  basin flag). Serde, all optional lists, so a caller can send `{}`.
- **Plume height**: Earth from Mastin et al. 2009, H = 2.00 V^0.241 km above the vent (mass form 0.304 MER^0.241, magma
  2500 kg/m³). Other worlds scaled by (g/g_E)^¼ (ρ_E/ρ)^¼ (N_E/N)^¾ from buoyant plume rise H ∝ F^¼ N^-¾ (Morton, Taylor
  and Turner 1956) with F ∝ g·MER/ρ and N² = g²/(c_p T), c_p = 3.5 R/M, T the skin temperature T_eff/2^¼. Below
  `thin_air_bar` the plume is ballistic, H = v²/2g, vent speeds 0.5 km/s (steady, Prometheus-type) and 1.0 km/s (dated,
  Pele-type) after McEwen and Soderblom 1983; the heights come out of the formula.
- **Every plume is a `WeatherSystem`** of kind `plume`, strength = height km, evidence `rule`, id `plume-<source id>`, size
  the umbrella diameter, a "why" sentence with the numbers (`aerosols::plumes`). Steady volcanoes are permanent
  (`born f64::MIN`, `ends f64::MAX`), as W2's permanent systems.
- **Ash streak**: carried along the local zonal wind (`circulation::zonal_wind` at the vent, strengthened with plume top
  height as `zonal_cell` does), optical depth e-folding over (wind × top / settling speed), widening sideways and thinning as
  it widens; upwind only the umbrella. Airless or ballistic: a symmetric umbrella. Ash clears from the air over `linger_days`
  after the eruption ends.
- **Ash fall** (`AerosolOut::ash_fall_mm`, `ground_darkening`): the erupted mass so far laid on the ground in the streak's
  own shape, normalised so the deposit holds the mass; it stays after the eruption.
- **Volcanic gas**: steady outgassing makes a low sulphate haze (`haze`) downwind and `AerosolOut::acid_rain` where it rains
  through enough of it.
- **Sulphate veil and dated cooling** (`veil_cooling_k`): eruptions whose column top passes the tropopause
  (`tropopause_scale_heights` × scale height) put SO₂ into the stratosphere. A first-order chain SO₂ → sulphate (conversion
  τ fudge) → removed (residence 1 year, Robock 2000) → felt by the climate with a lag (response τ fudge, chosen so the peak
  lands near a year). Cooling = per-Tg fudge × Tg × normalised curve; tropical eruptions cool the whole world, others their
  own hemisphere and a share of the other. The veil is also in the air as a pale `haze`.
- **Impacts**: fireball and ejecta plume (`plume`, dust) above 1 Mt; regional dust veil above 1 Gt; world-wide soot veil
  (`smoke`) and impact winter cooling above 10⁶ Mt, through the same chain with its own time constants. All thresholds fudge.
- **Fire**: a smoke plume and haze downwind only when it is the day range, there is ground and air, `o2` ≥ `fire.min_o2`
  (fudge, no number cited) and it is the dry part of the year at that latitude (rain-belt wetness now below
  `dry_ratio` × its yearly mean). `fire_burns` returns the reason when it does not burn.
- **Smog**: only around `industry` entries: τ ∝ √population × 4t(1−t) of tech level t, deeper under subsiding subtropical
  air (an inversion flag from `Circulation::sinking_deg`) and in basins. Nothing on airless or thin-air worlds.
- **Sea spray**: over open liquid in wind (cell's low-layer wind), weighted by ocean fraction when the ground is not known.
- **Photochemical haze extended, not duplicated**: W4's own list (a sulphur haze) runs through `rules::haze_from` with
  outgassed SO₂ mixed through the column; Titan's organic haze from `rules::haze` is not added twice (tested).
- Every aerosol carries an optical depth and linear-sRGB colour (contract `Aerosol`); aerosols of one kind from several
  sources are merged per cell, colour weighted by optical depth; anything thinner than `tau_min` is left out.

## Results
| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Earth, V = 10⁴ m³/s | 18.4 km (2.00·10⁴^0.241 = 18.36), scaling factor 0.998 |
| Same eruption on Mars, Titan, Venus | 72 km (× 3.9), 23 km (× 1.25), 5.9 km (× 0.32): tall in thin air and low gravity, squat in thick |
| Io (g 1.796) | Prometheus-type 69.6 km, Pele-type 278.4 km, symmetric umbrellas, no veil |
| Pinatubo-like (4 km³ DRE in 2 days, 20 Tg SO₂, 15° N) | 22.5 km column; world-wide cooling 0.33 K at 6 months, peak 0.40 K at about 1 year, 0.34, 0.25, 0.18 K at 1.5, 2, 2.5 years |
| Streak at 45° N | ash τ 2.6 and 12 mm of ash 4° east of the vent on day 1; nothing 5° upwind |
| Basin city of 10 M at 34° N, tech 0.5 | smog τ about 1.3; none 1000 km away; none with tech 0 |
Real Pinatubo reached 35 to 40 km; Mastin's fit is a median with a wide scatter.

Mastin formula exact and its mass form within 0.2 %; Earth plume system = formula within 2 %; Mars taller, Venus squatter;
Io ballistic heights; ash downwind not upwind, narrower sideways, ash fall downwind and none upwind, ground stays grey after
the air clears; cooling zero before, peak 0.8 to 1.2 years after, a year later 0.3 to 0.45 of the year before (e-folding
about a year), still felt at 2.5 years, tropical eruption world-wide, high-latitude one 0.3 in the other hemisphere, no veil
below the tropopause; impact winter for 4×10²³ J and none for Tunguska-size, diameter form; smog only over industry, more
under sinking air and in a basin, none on the Moon; no fire at 10 % oxygen or in the wet season, fire in the dry season;
vog downwind, spray over sea only, Titan haze not doubled; same inputs give the same output. New golden
`tests/golden/weather-aerosols.json` (plumes on Earth and Io, five cells with aerosols and ground facts, cooling curve).
No existing golden changed.

## Candidates to move into flows
The plume height (Mastin, ballistic, the scaling) and the veil curve are atoms; fire and smog gates are logic blocks
(oxygen, season, tech level). Kept in Rust because they run per cell.

## Known limits
- Sources are passed in; nothing reads S7, S12 or L1c yet. Fire does not read living cover (life suite); the caller says
  where fuel is.
- Winds are zonal only (no meridional transport): streaks run east or west. No terrain: basins come from the `basin` flag,
  not the atlas.
- One veil curve per eruption, linear in SO₂ to a cap; no saturation from bigger particles, no ocean memory.
- Smog is GM-side truth in the field. Player data views (K9 disclosure) filter it like any other fact; nothing here knows
  who is looking. It is visible from orbit in the field, so a player view showing the field must go through K9's grants.
- Steady volcanoes' sulphur haze uses a crude column mix; Venus picks up the sulphur haze too (it has upper hazes).
- `imprint` does not touch `temp_offset_k`; add `veil_cooling_k` yourself (below), so it is not counted twice.

## Integration (for the parent: exactly what to call)
- Settings: `aerosols::AerosolSettings::from_packs(stack)`; add a field `aerosols: AerosolSettings` to `RuleBlock` (load in
  `RuleBlock::from_packs`).
- Sources: add `sources: aerosols::Sources` to the call path, e.g. a `RuleBlock` method taking `&Sources`, or a field on
  `RuleBlock` set per world; the kernel `weather` op gains an optional `sources` object (serde `Sources`, `{}` default).
- Systems: in `systems_at`, extend `out` with `aerosols::plumes(&self.aerosols, &self.settings, c, date, sources)` before
  the pin filter and sort (plume ids are `plume-<source id>`, so pins can name them).
- Field: in `field_with`, once per tile `let ap = aerosols::prepare(&self.aerosols, &self.settings, c, date, sources);`
  then per cell after `formations::imprint`:
  `let out = aerosols::imprint(&ap, &self.aerosols, c, sea, &mut cell);` (`sea` from `terrain.ground(d).1` when terrain is
  given, else `None`) and
  `cell.temp_offset_k += aerosols::veil_cooling_k(&self.aerosols, &self.settings, c, date, sources, cell.lat_deg);`.
  `out.ash_fall_mm`, `ground_darkening` and `acid_rain` are for the surface renderer and report; the kernel op can return
  them beside the tile (e.g. `want: "aerosols"` giving plumes and per-cell `AerosolOut`).
- One-call form for a single place: `aerosols::imprint_aerosols(a, w, c, date, sources, sea, &mut cell)`.
- Reports: `formations::report` already lists the cell's aerosols; add "ash falling" / "acid rain" lines from `AerosolOut`
  if wanted.

## Wired (19 Nov, weather thread)
`RuleBlock` has `aerosols` and `sources` (`with_sources`); `systems_at` adds plumes; `cell_at` calls `imprint_aerosols` after the formations. The kernel `weather` op takes `sources`. Golden field digests of `weather.json` and `weather-storms.json` changed on purpose (sea spray over Earth's ocean, Venus's sulphur haze); every climate digest is unchanged.
