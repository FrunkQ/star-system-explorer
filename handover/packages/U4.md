# U4: Time ruler, part 1: what-if branches

Status: part 1 done (19 Nov 2026), branch `claude/project-thread-adgixl`, in the PR into `main`. Look-ahead in time (ageing) is part 2. It needs the T2 kernel ops (`age`, `look_ahead`, `commit` with `move_now`), which the T2 log says are not in the message API yet.

## What was built
- **Session** (`ui-contract/src/session.ts`): `openBranch(label)`, `branchSend(branch, intent)`, `branchStateHash(branch, at_yr?)` and `discardBranch(branch)` over the kernel's existing `branch`, `branch_set`, `branch_state_hash` and `discard` ops. They are for the GM seat only; a player seat is refused.
- **Workspace**: `branch: WhatIf | null` holds the kernel's branch id, its name, the time it was opened and its edits in order.
- **Edits** (`ui-contract/src/edit.ts`):
  - While a what-if is open, `commitEdit` sends to the branch, so the card, the chart's drag and Page Up/Down all edit the what-if and the main line does not move.
  - `openWhatIf`, `dropWhatIf`, and `keepWhatIf`, which sends the branch's edits on the main line in order as the GM's own intents. That way they replicate to every player screen (V4) and each can be undone.
  - `compareScenes(main, branch)` lists every shown value that differs, as "set" on the branch or "follows" from what was set.
- **Chart room**:
  - A quiet "What if…" on the status line. While a branch is open, the stage gets a dashed accent outline and a "What if · not kept" stamp. The status line shows the branch's name, its number of changes, its own state hash, and Compare, Keep it and Throw away.
  - Compare opens "Control and changed": body, value, main line, what if, set or follows.
  - The chart and cards show the branch. Undo and redo are for the main line and are disabled while a what-if is open.
- The ruler's history (authored edits from the kernel) and scrubbing were already in place from U2.

## Choices and gaps (for Alex)
- **Keep re-sends the edits instead of using the kernel's `commit`.** The peer-to-peer protocol (V4) replicates intents. A kernel `commit` (`commit.branch` event) is not an intent, so players' replicas would not follow it. Re-sending gives the same values and one event per edit. The `commit.branch` event (H6.3) is not used until V4 can carry a commit. That is a protocol addition for when look-ahead lands.
- Only value edits go on a branch. Physics adoption and pack stacking stay on the main line.
- A what-if lives for the page session. A reload drops it, since the kernel's branches are in memory.
- Look-ahead (T2 ageing), comparing two branches with each other, and a branch drawn on the ruler as a fork come with part 2.

# U4 part 2: look-ahead (19 Nov, night)

This part builds on the kernel's `look_ahead` and `commit_look_ahead` ops (T2 through the API, `K6-additions.md`, last section). It reached this branch through PR 5's head (1cfdeb4), which also carries PR 4. The shell's stack now includes `packs/base-time` (time settings).

## What landed
- **`packages/ui-contract/src/fate.ts`**:
  - `specFromScene(scene)` turns the chart's system into the ageing model's `SystemSpec`, and lists what had to be assumed:
    - Z from [Fe/H] with Z☉ = 0.02, the calibration of the Hurley 2000 fits;
    - coplanar orbits with aligned periapses, because the chart carries no inclinations;
    - birth year 0, because the ruler counts from the star's birth;
    - planets only, since moons ride with their planet; "oceans" from the ocean class.
  - `sceneAt(scene, result, snapshot)` draws one snapshot:
    - the star's new mass, luminosity, radius, temperature, subtitle and prose;
    - each planet's new orbit;
    - engulfed planets leave the chart, and their moons go with them;
    - every subject is stamped "look-ahead", plus "plausible-future" past the horizon;
    - nothing is editable;
    - the ruler grows to fit.
  - `fateEvents` gives the ruler marks. Only the first cooling step goes on the ruler, and planets engulfed together share one mark.
- **Session**: `lookAhead(spec, target_myr)` and `commitLookAhead(branch, move_now_myr?)`, GM only. Discarding a look-ahead uses `discardBranch`.
- **`packages/shell/src/LookAhead.svelte`**: "Look ahead…" on the status line, beside "What if…"; the two never open together.
  - **Running it**: a target age (in Gyr, with +1, +5 and +20 presets), then Look ahead. The panel lists the assumptions.
  - **Scrubbing**: a scrubber runs through the snapshots, and the chart, card, light and sound follow it. A dashed outline marks the stage.
  - **Events**: listed with their ages. Each age jumps the scrubber there, each has a "why?", and plausible futures are marked.
  - **Ending it**: Throw away, Keep as history, or Keep and move now to the age shown. The last one also moves the ruler's now.
- **Time ruler**: history events read as what they are. Kept transitions show by name, "Look-ahead kept" marks the request, and epoch steps and commit bookkeeping stay off the ruler. Before this, every chronicle event read "Authored edit".

## Evidence
- `packages/ui-contract/test/fate.test.ts`:
  - the spec from Kestrel;
  - Kestrel aged to 40 Gyr: main hash unmoved, then leaves the main sequence, red giant, Ember, Sable and Tarn engulfed (one ruler mark), planetary nebula;
  - the white dwarf at the end, stamped plausible future;
  - Tarn and its moon gone, Vault and its moons kept, nothing editable;
  - keep and move now changes the main hash, and a second keep is refused with `unknown_branch`.
- `tests/ui/lookahead.e2e.mjs`:
  - the main hash holds while looking;
  - scrubbing to the end removes Tarn, and the card reads white dwarf;
  - throwing away restores Tarn and the hash;
  - keeping to 10 Gyr with now moved changes the hash and the status line.
- Screens: `U4-screens/u4-lookahead-end.png`, `u4-lookahead-kept.png`.

## Gaps
- **Peer-to-peer.** `commit_look_ahead` is a kernel commit, not an intent, so player replicas do not follow a kept look-ahead until V4 carries commits. Their screens keep the old now until the next snapshot. This is the same protocol addition part 1 named.
- **Kept orbits.** Keeping moves now and inks the transitions. The chart's authored orbits are not rewritten to the aged ones: an engulfed planet still shows at the new now. Writing the aged state back as facts belongs to the kernel, either in the ageing op or as an "adopt the fate" op. Asked for in the kernel thread's queue via the coordinator.
- **Ruler crowding.** Labels at 26.3 Gyr (nebula, engulfings, first cooling) still overlap. The ruler needs collision handling, which is a polish item.
- **Kestrel's oceans are lost at once.** It is at 1.24 times Earth's flux against the pack's runaway threshold of 1.1. That is a physics result, not a UI bug, but it may surprise a GM.

## Polish (19 Nov, night): ruler labels never overlap
`labelRows` (`chart-grammar/src/ruler.ts`) places each event label on the first of two rows that is clear. A label with no room keeps its marker, and its text stays in the tooltip. Before this, labels simply alternated rows, and crowded transitions such as the planetary nebula, the engulfings and the first cooling step at 26.3 Gyr overlapped. Test: `chart-grammar/test/grammar.test.ts`.
