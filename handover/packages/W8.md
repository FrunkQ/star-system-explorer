# W8 part 1: one species table (21 Nov 2026)

Weather review (`handover/design/SSE4-weather-review.md`) package W8, section 4.3, folded into the matter pack as MP3
(`handover/design/SSE4-matter-pack-design.md` sections 6.2 and 10). W8 lands in two parts. **Part 1** (this log)
is the species table. **Part 2** is `sse.volatile.exchange`: ground ice and air, Mars's pressure cycle, and Pluto's
and Triton's ices.

## What was built
- **Ice curves on the master data sheet** (`scripts/build-base-reference.py`, then `packs/base-reference`). Each
  curve is Fray and Schmitt 2009's recommended fit, ln P[bar] = A0 + Σ A_i/T^i, with the coefficients read from
  Lisse et al. 2021's reproduction of the table (SOM Table 2). The curves:
  - N2: β above 35.61 K, α below;
  - CO: β above 61.55 K, α below;
  - CO2: two ranges, split at 194.7 K;
  - CH4, NH3 and Ar;
  - water, by Feistel and Wagner 2007 in Fray and Schmitt's form.

  I worked out which fit covers which range, and that the unit is bar, by passing each fit through its own triple
  point: all are within 3 %. New sheet values:
  - triple points for CO and Ar;
  - solid densities for N2, CO, Ar and CH4 (from memory, marked unverified);
  - condensed colours for CH4, NH3, N2, CO and Ar, authored and moved from the climate pack.
- **Reader** (`sse-reference`):
  - the `feistel-wagner-2007` fit;
  - `sublimation_pa`, which picks `vapour_pressure` or `vapour_pressure_low` by range and goes on below the lowest
    range as Clausius-Clapeyron;
  - `sublimation_latent_j_mol`, read off the curve's slope.

  Two new load checks: a low piece must meet the main one, and a typed sublimation heat must agree with the slope
  where it was measured (CO2 and NH3 pass).
- **`sse.volatile.species`** (`sse_climate::settings::volatile_species`): the climate's substances are now every
  sheet row with a triple point and a vapour curve. Molar masses come from the formula; the latent heat is the
  sheet's, or the slope's. The climate pack's own `substances` list is retired, and a pack that still carries one
  is refused.
- `ebm::p_sat` reads the solid's curve below the triple point. `ebm::frost_k` is its exact inverse (60 bisection
  steps), and `strengths::condense_k` uses it. Ground frost, cloud and the collapse test therefore read one curve.
  CO and Ar now condense where their partial pressure allows.

## Tests
- `sse-reference::the_shipped_ice_curves_give_known_frost_points`:
  - water ice gives 0.1626 Pa at 200 K;
  - Mars's CO2 freezes near 148 K at 610 Pa;
  - Pluto's nitrogen freezes near 37 K at 1 Pa;
  - the slopes give CO2's 25 kJ/mol and N2's 6 to 7.6 kJ/mol.
- `sse-climate::species_tests`:
  - the climate reads its seven volatiles off the sheet and refuses a pack's own table;
  - `p_sat(frost_k(p)) = p` for every species from 10⁻⁶ to 0.3 of its triple pressure;
  - Pluto's N2 frost point at 1 Pa is 36 to 37.5 K, where the old two-point curve gave 33.5 K;
  - Mars's CO2 frost point at 610 Pa is 146 to 150 K;
  - a fixture pack adding a made-up "xenoice" with only a curve, density and colour gets ground frost, cloud and a
    condensable listing, with no code change (MP3's check).

## Goldens changed (once, on purpose)
`weather.json`, `weather-swap.json`, `weather-storms.json`, `surface-wear.json`, `surface-cover.json` and the
survey and weather lines of `contract.jsonl`. Reason: below the triple point, saturation now follows the ice's
measured curve instead of Clausius-Clapeyron from the triple point with the liquid's latent heat. Water over ice in
Earth's cold bands moves a little; Earth's eroded volume changes by 0.03 %. Nitrogen, methane and ammonia frost
points move by a few kelvin, and CO and Ar join the condensables.

## Known limits (docs/fudges.md, "Volatile species")
- Below a fit's range the curve is extrapolated.
- Over a liquid, saturation is Clausius-Clapeyron from the triple point.
- Four solid densities are unverified.
- The cloud's latent flux uses one heat per substance.
- The lower ends of the valid ranges are from memory.
- The giants' cloud decks still use their five temperature thresholds. Moving them onto these curves is MP3's last
  step and goes with W11.
