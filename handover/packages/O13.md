# O13: planet finding from afar (remote survey)

Design: `SSE4-nebulae-and-light-design.md` section 5B; stage O13 in section 8. Crate `sse-survey`, kernel op `survey_planets`.

## What was built
- `sse_survey::run(Survey { star, planets, observer, instrument, watch_s, settings })` returns, for every planet
  and every method, a `Finding { verdict: found | candidate | missed, snr, measured, why }`.
- The observer is a direction (from the star) and a distance; the tilt of each orbit to that line comes from the
  planet's inclination and node, so moving the observer changes what is found.
- Methods, all closed-form (no light-curve simulation):
  - **Transit**: transits only if the impact parameter a·cos i / R* < 1 + Rp/R*; depth (Rp/R*)², duration, and the number
    of transits inside the watch (from the orbital phase). Found needs `transits_needed` (3) and S/N ≥ 7.1.
  - **Wobble** (radial velocity): K from Lovis and Fischer; noise from the instrument precision and the star's jitter,
    sqrt of the number of visits; reports the minimum mass m sin i, never the true mass. A full orbit must be watched.
  - **Position wobble** (astrometry): α = (m/M*)(a/d), works face-on; reports true mass and tilt.
  - **Direct imaging**: separation against an inner working angle of 3λ/D and reflected contrast A_g (Rp/a)² / π
    against the instrument's contrast floor ("lost in the star's glare" when too close).
  - **Timing**: light-travel wobble a_* sin i / c against the timing precision (pulsars).
- Photometric noise: the instrument floor and the star's activity, combined with photon noise from aperture,
  efficiency and the star's visible power at the observer's distance.
- Kernel op `survey_planets { survey }` → `planets_surveyed { result }` (error `bad_survey`). It runs on the GM side
  against the true planets; what a player is shown is the `measured` list.

## Evidence (`cargo test -p sse-survey`, 8 tests; golden `tests/golden/survey.json`)
- The Sun's wobble from Jupiter is 12.5 m/s and from Earth 0.089 m/s; Jupiter's position wobble at 10 pc is about 500 µas;
  Earth's transit is 84 ppm deep and about 13 hours long.
- From the ecliptic, a Kepler-like photometer finds Earth in 4 years; a 1 m/s spectrograph with 20 µas astrometry finds
  Jupiter both ways in 12 years and misses Earth by wobble.
- Earth transits from 0.2° above its plane but not from 1° (its window is R☉/a = 0.27°).
- Face-on: no transit, zero wobble, but the position wobble still finds Jupiter.
- Six years of watching makes Jupiter only a candidate; 1.2 years cannot give Earth three transits.
- Imaging: an 8 m telescope with 10⁻¹⁰ contrast sees Jupiter at 10 pc, with 10⁻⁶ it does not; a 0.3 m one cannot separate
  Earth from the Sun at 100 pc.
- A 4.3 Earth-mass planet round a pulsar (PSR B1257+12 c) is found by 3 µs timing.

## Stand-ins (to add to docs/fudges.md)
- Noise is white and scales as 1/√N; no red noise, no stellar spots or flares in the light curve (O10 brings activity).
- Defaults: activity 15 ppm/hr, jitter 1 m/s, efficiency 0.5, IWA 3λ/D, geometric albedo 0.4 when not given.
- Thresholds: found at S/N 7.1 (Kepler), candidate at 5, three transits.
- Eccentric orbits use circular transit timing and the √(1 − e²) term in K only.
- Microlensing is not modelled (it is a one-off event, needs a lens star behind).
