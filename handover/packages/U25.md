# U25: One set of view controls on every diagram (UI track)

Status: done 22 Nov 2026. Alex ruled on 22 Nov: "We need those same consistent controls across all diagram displays."

The controls in question:
- the view style switch, with Chart, 3D, True and Wireframe;
- "Seen from", with Above, Tilted, Edge-on and Free, as on the galaxy chart mockup.

This ruling is now rule 12 of `handover/interface-style.md`.

## What it does

- **One control, one place.** `chart-grammar/src/ViewControls.svelte` draws two switches:
  - **View style:** Chart, 3D, True, Wireframe.
  - **Seen from:** Above, Tilted, Edge-on, Free.

  The words and their order are always the same. The shell puts the control at the top of the stage, beside the tools, whenever the screen's chart view is a diagram. On a phone it sits over the chart with no labels. Screens that are not diagrams, such as Reference and the Workshop, have none.
- **Nothing rearranges.** An option a view cannot do stays where it is: quiet, disabled, with the reason as its title. The reasons are shared so the same gap reads the same everywhere:
  - `WHY_TRUE_HELD`: "True is coming: the photoreal views are held for now".
  - `WHY_WIREFRAME_LATER`.
  - "This chart is flat, seen from above".
- **A flat chart turns.** The system's log chart is Chart, seen from above. Choosing Tilted, Edge-on or Free on it switches to 3D (`pickSeen`). Choosing Chart again goes back to the flat chart.
- **Free, as rule 11 says.** In 3D, dragging with a mouse switches to Free by itself (`ws.turned()`), and the hint line explains how Free works.
  - Shift-drag, or two fingers in Free, slides the view (the camera's look point moves along the plane).
  - The wheel zooms.
  - On touch, one finger scrolls the page until Free is chosen (`touch-action: pan-y`).
  - A fixed view sets the camera's height (`SEEN_ELEVATION`) and puts the slide back.
- **The choice is the viewer's.** It is the same for every seat, kept on the device (pref `view`), and never in the campaign. When a style is not drawn on one screen, that screen shows Chart, and the choice comes back on screens that do draw it. Per-panel choices come with the view styles design (later).
- **The 2D/3D buttons inside the system chart are gone.** The shared control replaces them.

## Per view

| View | Draws | Seen from |
|---|---|---|
| System chart | Chart (flat log chart), 3D | Above (Chart); all four in 3D, turning by hand becomes Free |
| Body globe | Chart = survey relief recipe, True = true-colour recipe (a recipe chosen in Engine still wins); "3D" is disabled: the globe is already 3D | Above = from the pole, Tilted = 30°, Edge-on = the equator, Free; dragging or arrow keys become Free |
| Cutaway, galaxy chart, galaxies nearby, cosmic web, sky from here, formation replay | Chart, flat (`flatChart(why)`) | Above only; the rest disabled with the reason |

The formation replay draws the same control in its own header (it lives in Make a system's dialog). It reads and writes the same choice through `MakeSystem`'s `view` and `onview` props.

## Contract (for other threads)

- `ui-contract/src/view-controls.ts`:
  - constants `VIEW_STYLES` and `SEEN_FROM`, with their words;
  - types `ViewControlsSpec` `{styles, flat?, seen?}` and `ViewChoice`;
  - functions `viewOptions(spec, choice)` (returns options, shown and turnable), `pickSeen`, `pickStyle`, `readViewChoice` and `flatChart(why3d)`;
  - `SEEN_ELEVATION` and `FREE_HINT`.
- `ViewModule.controls?: ViewControlsSpec`: a view declares what it draws. A module without it is not a diagram and gets no control.
- `Workspace.view?: ViewChoice` and `Workspace.turned?()`. A view draws `viewOptions(spec, ws.view).shown`, and calls `turned()` when the viewer turns it by hand.
- `Segmented` options accept `unavailable: string`, which renders the option disabled with the reason as its title.
