# F9: pins and reconstruction (19 Nov 2026)

Formation design section 6: a pinned world is honoured and the stages are solved around it; an imported
system gains a history labelled reconstructed.

## What was built
- `crates/sse-formation/src/pins.rs`: `WorldPin { label, mass_earth, a_au, e? }`, `Pins { worlds, tries,
  reconstruct }`, `solve(manifest, &pins, block) -> PinnedRun`.
  - Deterministic search: the pipeline runs for seeds `seed`, `seed#pin-search-1` ... up to `tries`; each run
    is scored per pin (log a at 0.1 dex, log m at 0.5 dex, squared, greedy assignment heaviest pin first).
    The lowest score wins; ties keep the earliest seed.
  - A world within `MATCH_LIMIT` (9) is moved onto its pin (mass rescaled in its own composition); otherwise
    the pin is placed as a new world built from the nearest world's material. `PinMatch` records which.
  - Generated worlds within `CLEAR_HILL` (8) mutual Hill radii of a pinned world are removed (listed in
    `removed`). F6 early life and the heat flow are re-run on the final set (`pipeline::rerun_heat`).
  - Evidence: `pins.worlds.i` is Authored; `history` is Reconstructed when `reconstruct` is set. A sentence
    in `notes` says which candidate won and how it compared with the manifest's own seed.
- `crates/sse-import-v3/src/reconstruct.rs`: `known_systems(&Import)` pulls each imported system's star mass
  and planets (mass, a, e), `reconstruct(&KnownSystem, tries)` solves with `reconstruct: true`, `summary`
  gives a JSON-friendly record. Systems without a usable star or planets are skipped and counted.
- CLI: `sse reconstruct-v3 <save.json>` prints one record per system. On `tests/fixtures/v3/synthetic-sol.json`:
  1 system, Earth pinned, candidate 14 of 16 won (fit 0.36 against 18.0 for the own seed), 11 worlds, 0 removed.
