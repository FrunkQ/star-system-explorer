# K9: disclosure tiers, data views, scene building

Status: done (18 Nov 2026). Track "kernel resolve and rules", branch `claude/project-thread-dzkz4p`.

## What was built
New crate `sse-observe`: the disclosure ladder, pack-supplied disclosure maps for fields and tags, player data views (tiers, tag filter, per-field and per-tag overrides with exact, rounded, band or hidden precision, omit or placeholder for the unknown), grants per audience with inheritance to children, the Scanned button (`scan_toggle`), the "never send secret-only authored events" check, scene building for any audience including the GM's all-knowledge scene, and scene diffs for subscriptions.

## Design sections implemented
H6.7, the audience part of H8.7 (grants per audience, "everyone" always exists), the honesty-mark rule of H8.6, and the scene half of H8.1 (views get plain data built after the filter). Choices the design left open:
- **Ladder** rungs carry the placeholder text for their tier: obvious "unknown", scanned "not yet scanned", surveyed "survey required", secret (never shown). Packs may relabel or add rungs; the last rung is always the GM-only one.
- **Disclosure map** keys are exact field names or prefixes with `*` (longest match wins). Anything unlisted defaults to the GM-only rung, so a new fact never leaks by omission. Bookkeeping fields (`resolve.*`, `observe.*`, `query.*`, `budget.*`) are internal and never shown.
- **Placeholders** appear only for the rung directly above the highest shown one (before a scan: the scanned fields say "not yet scanned"; after: the surveyed ones say "survey required"); secret fields never get one.
- **Band precision**: value rounded to `step`, shown as plus or minus `half_width` (281 K with step 10 and half width 10 shows 270 to 290 K, as on the mockup). Rounded precision is significant figures in pinned maths.
- **Grants** are `observe.grant` events setting `observe.view` (audience everyone) or `observe.view.<audience>`; the effective view is the address's own grant, else the nearest ancestor's (moons follow their planet), else `observe.view.default` on the universe root, else the campaign default. The Scanned button flips between the campaign's before and after views and pressing it again undoes; each press is history.
- **Subjects with nothing shown are left out**, so players do not learn a secret object exists.
- **Honesty marks** (`authored`, `impossible`, from the event that set the fact; `fictional`, from the pack labels the caller passes) are always on the GM scene and off on player scenes unless a view sets `show_honesty`.
- **Secret authored facts stay home**: `withheld(event)` lists an authored event's secret-tier fields and says when the whole event must not be sent. Splitting a mixed event into a player copy is for V4 (the P2P session), which owns what travels.

## Results
- Acceptance: the Kestrel system from the PlayerData mockup board (`tests/fixtures/observe/tarn-world.json`) gives scenes equal to the hand-written fixtures `tarn-before.json` (Tarn under long-range sensors: name, kind, size, colour, and "not yet scanned" for orbit, mass, air, surface, ocean, hazards) and `tarn-after.json` (after one tap: orbit, mass, air, ocean, hazards, temperature as 270 to 290 K, tags habitable, hazard, port, and "survey required" for interior, resources, settlements). The fixtures were written by hand from the mockup, not generated.
- Scanning Tarn changes only Tarn and its moon (the moon followed); a second tap restores the before scene exactly; three grants are in the history.
- Across all four views no player scene contains the GM note, the secret tag or an internal field; the survey view adds interiors and every non-secret tag; Vault stays "rumour only" (name and story line, no numbers) throughout.
- GM scene: everything, including secrets, with the `authored` mark on Tarn's GM-set mass; player scenes carry no marks.
- Audiences: scanning for "vesh" leaves everyone's scene unchanged; a moon's own grant beats its planet's; an undefined view is a readable error.

## Golden hashes
Added `tests/golden/observe.json`: scene hashes for Tarn before, after, and the GM scene. Native only until the kernel exposes `subscribe`.

## Cost classes and edges
`sse_observe::ops()`: building a scene is light (linear in facts in scope; every device builds its own); a scan is one event, GM only.

## Known limits
- Not yet in the kernel API (`subscribe(topic_query, data_view)` returning scene deltas): see START-HERE.
- Lenses (U9) reuse `DataView` as their "what data" part; cards, layers and tools are U9's.
- Sealed seats (V10) would build these scenes on the GM machine and send them; the builder is already independent of where it runs.
