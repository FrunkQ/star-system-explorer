# S6: the other regimes (19 Nov 2026)

Surface design sections 2.1 (regime table) and 6 (worlds without a solid surface). Needs S2 (done); uses S4.
Physics track.

## What was built
- `crates/sse-surface/src/regimes.rs`, settings under `provides.other.surface.regime_features` in
  `packs/base-surface` (version `surface-regimes-v1`). Each landform rule names its source.
- `regime_surface(settings, envelope, seed, inputs, epoch_gyr) -> RegimeSurface`: the regime's landforms, an
  episodic lid's dated global overturns, a giant's cloud deck, and notes for regimes the S0 sketch writes alone.
- Landforms are round (corona, patera, massif, chaos, tessera, lake, dune field, tar sea) or great-circle segments
  (double ridge, band, chasma, rift, scarp), each a smooth profile across its width with tapered ends.
- Per regime (rules in the pack):
  - **ice shell**: double ridges (lineae about 45° from east), bands, and chaos covering 25% × (q / 0.06)³ of the
    surface (Europa about 20%); relief within 300 m.
  - **heat pipe**: paterae (about 10 per Mkm²: Io's 417) and block mountains (3.2 per Mkm², 1 to 17 km tall).
  - **episodic lid**: coronae (1.1 per Mkm², 60 to 1000 km), chasmata, tessera plateaus, and global overturns
    as a Poisson count in the integrated renewal (capped at 64, dated, sorted).
  - **plume lid**: coronae and chasmata.
  - **stagnant lid**: contraction scarps, as many as the world has cooled ((q0 − q) / q0), taller on bigger
    worlds; a rift system beside the tallest S4 shield over 10 km (Valles Marineris beside Tharsis).
  - **hydrocarbon**: dune fields covering 15% within 30° of the equator (organic sediment); lakes and seas
    covering about 1% above 55°, 90% of them in the north (hydrocarbon cover).
  - **carbon-rich**: tar seas, labelled speculative.
  - **cloud deck**: prograde jets sqrt(a Ω / U) (the Rhines scale) with U 150 m/s for gas giants and 400 m/s for
    ice giants (under 50 Earth masses); 2 × jets bands evenly spaced in sin(latitude); alternating winds;
    storms (1.5 per jet); zone and belt albedo by cloud chemistry at the temperature; cloud tops ±half a scale
    height (zones high).
  - small bodies, ocean worlds, lava worlds, plates: the S0 sketch or S2 (checked here, not redrawn).
- **Dates**: on a renewed surface (an S4 `resurface` entry) a landform's count is the density × (1 − e^(−Z)) and
  its date is weighted so later renewal thins older ones, the same rule as S4's craters. So Europa's ridges
  average a few tens of Myr, and Ganymede's are Gyr old under the same grammar. Chaos follows today's heat flow
  (`heat-now`); scarps are dated uniformly over the cooling.
- **Explanations**: each landform's cause gives its date, what made it, the source and the heat flow then;
  `RegimeSurface::explain(lat, lon, radius)` lists what covers a place; the cloud deck says why it has its bands.
- **Atlas**: `Atlas::with_regime(rs)` adds landform relief (a landform appears at a level once four samples
  cross its width), cover and material inside regions (at every level), cloud albedo and cloud-top heights for
  giants, and tags (landforms and nearby storms). `tolerance_m` gains the landforms' added relief and carried
  curvature (twice each) and the cloud bands' curvature.

## Checks (`cargo test -p sse-surface regimes`)
- `ice_shells_crack_young_and_old`: Europa has more than 100 ridges and 10 bands, mean ridge age under 100 Myr,
  chaos 20%, lineae near 45°. Ganymede's ridges average over 500 Myr, with a tenth of Europa's chaos.
- `io_vents_and_raises_blocks`: 377 paterae and 119 mountains (observed about 417 and 135), the tallest 8 to
  17 km, all under 5 Myr old.
- `venus_has_coronae_rifts_and_overturns`: 543 coronae (observed about 500), median 150 to 400 km, chasmata,
  dated overturns.
- `shrinking_lid_worlds_get_scarps_and_mars_a_rift`; `titan_dunes_lie_low_and_lakes_lie_north` (dunes 15.0%,
  lakes 0.15%, all at the right latitudes, mostly north); `giants_band_by_rotation` (Jupiter 7 to 14 jets with
  ammonia clouds, fewer if slower, a Neptune-like ice giant 2 to 4).
- `the_sketch_regimes_hold_their_kind`: an ocean world has no land, a lava world is molten on the day side only,
  a small body is not round.
- `every_landform_answers_why`, `landforms_refine_within_tolerance` (Europa, Io, Venus, Titan and Jupiter, level 0
  to the fact floor: worst 48 to 76% of the tolerance), `titan_tiles_show_lakes_and_dunes`, `golden_regimes`
  (`tests/golden/surface-regimes.json`, new; no existing golden changed). Harness: `surface-regimes` added; Node
  passes all engines and the worker contract.

## Known limits
- The S0 sketch draws a small body's lumps too large: Vesta spans about 0.64 of its radius against the real 0.24.
  Fixing it changes the S0 and S1 goldens, so it waits for a package that re-blesses them on purpose.
- The scanned sketch's giant bands still come from the pack's default spin, while S6's surveyed cloud deck uses
  the world's rotation; the two can show different band counts until the sketch reads rotation too.
- S4's crater survival on an episodic lid is continuous renewal, not S6's discrete overturns; a later step should
  make craters post-date the last overturn.
- Ice-shell lineae follow a fixed 45° trend, not a cycloid from a stress model; plumes (Enceladus) and
  cryovolcanoes are not drawn yet.
- Wind speeds for the Rhines scale are empirical by class, not derived from the heat flux.
- Landforms do not yet feed S3 drainage.
