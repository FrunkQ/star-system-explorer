# O2: stars, planets, moons and built things in all bands

Design: `SSE4-nebulae-and-light-design.md` sections 1 and 5A; stage O2 in section 8. Builds on O1 (`O1.md`).

## What was built
- Six cited atoms in `sse-atoms::light` (editable blocks, standing rule: physics in the chain):
  - `sse.light.effective_temperature`: T⁴ = (1 − A)S/4σ + q/σ. One block for every body: a star's own
    luminosity over its surface gives its effective temperature; a giant adds left-over heat to absorbed light.
  - `sse.light.reflected_area`: A πR² (R*/d)², the effective area for reflected starlight.
  - `sse.light.stellar_wind`: Parker wind (density, speed, field across the flow) with solar values at 1 au.
  - `sse.light.magnetopause_radius`: pressure balance with Chapman-Ferraro compression.
  - `sse.light.auroral_radio_power`: radio-magnetic Bode's law, η = 2×10⁻³ (Zarka); zero inside the body.
  - `sse.light.cyclotron_frequency`: top auroral frequency, the gyrofrequency at the magnetic pole.
- Flow `sse.base-light.body-emission` (hook `body.emission`) in `packs/base-light`, wiring the five scalar atoms,
  with four golden cases (Earth, Jupiter, Neptune, Mars). The pack also adds units `a_m2`, `tesla`, `nt`, `hz`,
  `mhz`, `m2`. A pack can replace the flow (any flow on hook `body.emission`).
- `sse_light::emit`: `Conditions` (radius, own luminosity, Bond albedo, magnetic moment, lit-by sources, optional
  accretion disc, corona X-rays, radiators, transmitters, lamps), `describe`, `publish_all`, the `Model`
  (compiled flow, atoms, light table). No `kind` anywhere.
- Fixture `tests/fixtures/light/solar-system.json`: Sun, eight planets and the Moon, with observed effective
  temperatures and auroral radio powers to check against.
- Kernel op `read_light { fixture?: "solar-system", bodies?: Conditions[] }` → `light_read { source, table,
  bodies }`; each body is `BodyLight { address, name, facts, light }` where `light` is O1's `Published`
  (description, bands, visible_slices, total_w, samples, peak_m). Unknown fixture: `error { code: "no_light" }`;
  bad conditions: `bad_conditions`. Cost class light (per body, one small flow run and about 2,000 Planck
  evaluations).

- Tests: every listed effective temperature within 1.5 K; every auroral radio power within a factor of 3; Venus,
  Mars and the Moon have no auroral radio (only the faint thermal tail); Jupiter loudest; Earth's magnetopause
  9.3 Earth radii (observed about 10). The O2 design check, "Jupiter-like radio emission present only with a
  magnetic field": switching Jupiter's field off removes exactly its auroral power from the radio band and
  changes no other band.
- Infrared: in the far infrared each of Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn and Neptune has a brightness temperature
  within 1 % of its effective temperature; in the visible it is at least 1,000 times brighter by reflected light.
- Built things: a ship with a 600 K radiator is more than twice as bright in the mid infrared as one with a 150 K
  radiator (the cold ship's hull still sits near equilibrium in sunlight: dim, never zero); a radar shows in radio;
  sodium lamps put all their light in the red slice at 589 nm.
- Goldens: new `tests/golden/light-bodies.json` (Solar System facts, bands and a hash of each published light).
  `tests/golden/contract.jsonl` re-blessed on purpose: three new `read_light` lines; `hello` and the unknown-op
  message gain the op; `atoms` lists the six new atoms; responses that carry the base-physics pack's hash or
  kind list change because that pack gains the light kinds. `packs/base-physics/pack.json` regenerated
  (`SSE_BLESS=1 cargo test -p sse-atoms`) and copied to `tests/fixtures/packs/base-physics.json`. TS types
  regenerated (`packages/contracts/src/generated/`).

## Not done, and why
- **Aurorae as light** (UV and the [O I] lines): their power depends on the wind and on ejections, so they come
  with stellar activity (O10). The line ids are already in the table. Lightning and nightglow likewise later.
- **Bodies from the open universe**: `read_light` serves the fixture or given conditions. When B1 publishes the
  conditions as facts on any body (agreed names in `O1.md`, Integration), `read_light { system }` reads them.
- Stars have no absorption lines yet (O12), no limb darkening, no activity (O10).
- Mercury gets a tiny magnetosphere and 1.6e6 W of radio; none is observed (its field is weak and its
  magnetosphere small and dynamic). The law is outside its tested range there.

## Stand-ins (to add to `docs/fudges.md` once that file is on main)
1. Grey albedo and black-body glow at the effective temperature: no absorption bands, no day-night contrast.
   Real physics: spectral albedo and emission from the atmosphere model.
2. Every star gets the Sun's wind at 1 au values (Parker spiral). Real physics: wind from stellar activity, O10.
3. Auroral radio isotropic and flat in frequency from f_max/30 to f_max. Real: beamed cones, rotation modulation.
4. Corona X-rays flat over 0.1 to 10 keV. Real: thermal plasma emission at the coronal temperature, O10.
5. White lamps flat across 400 to 700 nm. Real: lamp spectra per technology (pack data).
6. Accretion disc seen isotropically (from O1).
