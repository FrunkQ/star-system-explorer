# V10c: a living star in True

Alex, 23 Nov 2026: "is that a star at the centre? plans to make that look more interesting and dynamic?" and "flaring that is real".

## What it does

- In True, the star at the centre of the system view is the kernel's star, not a coloured ball. The disc is darkened to the limb (grey atmosphere), it has spots where its activity cycle puts them, and its flares come from the kernel's flare list. It is drawn by `drawStar` (R5a), which the Body screen's star disc already uses, so the star looks the same from the system as close up.
- It turns on its own spin (`rotation_period_s` from `read_activity`), on its own clock of six hours a second from the star's present. The view says so in its note. The universe clock does not move.
- Flares rise in a straight line and fall away exponentially (rise and decay times from the kernel). Each picture, ten a second, shows any flare that peaked since the last one. The note says "flaring now" while one is visible.
- The face is drawn at about its size on screen (16 to 256 px), as a camera-facing sprite, so the limb darkening is the true one from every angle.
- A star the resolved system does not describe (an authored system such as the test drive's Kestrel) is taken from its scene facts (teff_k, mass_sun, radius_sun). A star with neither is drawn plain, and the note says why.
- Granules show only when the disc resolves them (at least 6 px a cell). At system scale they never do: a Sun is about 1,400 granules across.

## Code

- `packages/renderer/src/live-star.ts`: `readLiveStar` (facts and activity, shared with StarDisc), `cycleOf`, `spotsOf`, `flareProfile`, `flaresAt` (with a span), `turnAt`, `drawLiveStar`, `STAR_SECONDS_PER_SECOND`.
- `gpu.ts`: `GpuItem.face`, drawn as a sprite with its own texture, re-uploaded when its version changes.
- `System3D.svelte`: `star` prop, the star's clock (requestAnimationFrame, 10 Hz), `data-star-live` (quiet or flaring) and `data-star-turn`.
- `SystemChart.svelte`: reads the live star once per system on the GM's seat when True is chosen.
- `StarDisc.svelte` reads through `readLiveStar` too. Five typed copies of the day and sigma left the MP22 ledger (424 to 419).
