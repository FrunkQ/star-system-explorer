# L0: life suite sockets

Status: done (18 Nov 2026), kernel track, branch `claude/project-thread-dzkz4p`. Life design section 14, row L0, and handoff 11A stage L0 ("interfaces, habitat graph derived from regions and shells, an empty life suite"). It needs K10 and P4, and the physics branch (F2) was merged in first.

## What was built
- New crate `sse-life`:
  - **Fixed interfaces** (handoff 11A). These do not change as L1 to L5 grow:
    - `LifeRequirementProfile`: per habitat. Medium, phase, solvent, temperature and pressure ranges and swing, energy channels (light with peak, chemical, tidal, radiogenic) and which are open, materials, area, volume, persistence, radiation dose, shielding, other properties, affecting chronicle events, and `missing` (the fields nobody supplied, so a gap is never read as zero).
    - `LifeEnvelope`: P(present), origin time quantiles, energy budget, biomass class, capability probabilities, complexity, fluxes, signatures, uncertainty, the evidence label and the hash of its inputs.
    - `LifeEvidence`: no model, plausible reconstruction, coupled, simulated, authored.
    - `ResolvedBiosphere`: aggregate guilds, never individuals, with their capabilities, habitat occupancy, biomass and since. Relations are ancestry, resource, habitat and capability.
    - `LifeQuery` and `Feasibility`. The answer can be yes, no or "nobody can say", always with a reason.
    - The `LifeSuite` trait (envelope, resolve, query_feasible, explain), alongside K10's `SuiteAdapter`, which carries `advance`.
  - **Habitat graph** (`habitat.rs`, life design section 2): pack rules turn a P4 world stack's shells, regions and features into habitats, and link them.
    - Each rule names the object type and kinds it reads, a place within it (all, top layer, below the top layer, under the bottom, on top, under the top, around a feature), the medium, phase, solvent and open energy channels.
    - A rule can also require a property, require a kind of shell below (a sea floor needs rock under it), open the chemical channel when something below provides heat or vents, or mark the habitat as embedded (caves, vents).
    - Links come in three kinds:
      - vertical: the spans touch and the footprints overlap;
      - lateral: side by side across a latitude border, for regions only;
      - embedded: a cave or sea floor inside what surrounds it.
    - A link through an unmapped region is marked as a guess.
    - Rates are `None` until box B (L1).
    - Areas come from caps and bands on the sphere. Global layers get the exact spherical-shell volume.
    - An object the author marked `provides: habitat` that no rule covers is kept as an "authored" habitat with an unknown medium, so it is never dropped.
  - **`NoLifeModel`**, the empty suite:
    - Its envelope claims nothing (no P(present), evidence "no model").
    - `resolve` refuses with "no life model installed".
    - `query_feasible` answers "nobody can say".
    - It also implements K10's `SuiteAdapter`, so the coordinator can install it.
- **Pack `packs/base-life/pack.json`** (`provides.other.habitat_rules`): the 14 rules for the default habitat kinds of section 2. These are the sunlit ocean, ocean interior, sea floor with vents, ocean under an ice shell, land surface and soil by climate band, deep crust with pore water, cloud decks, hydrocarbon lakes and seas, water lakes, terminator zones, and caves and lava tubes. A rule id may be defined once across a stack.
- **Fixture worlds** `tests/fixtures/worlds/{earth,mars,europa,titan,venus}.json`, each with a `sources` line citing its numbers.

## Checks (`cargo test -p sse-life`)
- **Schema**: one sample of every interface value (`tests/fixtures/life/interfaces.json`) is written field by field and read back unchanged. Any change of shape fails the test.
- **Habitat graphs look right**:
  - **Earth**:
    - The ocean has a sunlit layer, then the interior, then the sea floor, which is embedded in the deep crust and opens the chemical channel from the warm mantle.
    - Four climate bands have land and soil, linked laterally across their borders. Movile Cave is embedded in the northern temperate soil and the crust.
    - The coasts are guessed links, because the ocean basins are unmapped.
    - The ocean's sunlit area is 3.6e14 m² and its volume 1.34e18 m³.
    - The Antarctic ice sheet and the air are not habitats.
  - **Mars**: ground, soil, deep crust and the Arsia Mons cave, at 150 to 300 K and 230 mSv/yr. No water habitats.
  - **Europa**: an ocean under ice of about 2.8e18 m³, with no light but chemical energy. Its sea floor sits on rock with vents.
  - **Titan**: two methane-ethane seas at 92 K and an ocean under the ice. There is no sea floor, because the ocean lies on high-pressure ice, and nothing is linked.
  - **Venus**: the temperate cloud deck at 260 to 340 K, 0.2 to 1.1 bar and 80% sulphuric acid, touching nothing. The plains and soil are listed honestly at 737 K, for box B to cap at zero.
- **Stable under hash**: each graph hashes the same from re-serialised stack JSON, and changing one number changes the hash.
- Invalid stacks and duplicate rule ids are refused. Tarn's biomes and brine ocean are covered by the rules, and an unknown authored habitat is kept.
- The empty suite claims nothing, both directly and through the K10 socket.
- **Golden** `tests/golden/life.json` is new: each world's graph hash and its habitat and link lines. It is checked natively, in Node and in Chromium (the `life` engine scenario).

## Known limits (for L1 and later)
- A habitat's life over time is not modelled yet: an ocean freezing over and a sub-ice habitat starting "by descent" needs the world stack through time (T1/T2). Habitat ids are stable per source object and kind, ready for it.
- Link rates and energy fluxes are what the stack supplies. Most physics fields are `None` and listed in `missing` until the surface and atmosphere flows publish them.
- Land areas use a `cover_fraction` on climate bands, an authored stand-in until surfaces have outlines.
