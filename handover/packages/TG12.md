# TG12: dressing rules

Tags design (`design/SSE4-tags-design.md`) section 5.7 and section 7, package TG12. Built 21 Nov 2026 by the kernel thread. Code: `crates/sse-kernel/src/dressing.rs`, tests in `dressing_tests.rs`, fixture `tests/fixtures/packs/dressing-toy.json`.

## What it delivers
- **`provides.other.dressing`**: `{about?, rules: [{id, when, place, where, count?, chance?, tags?, faction?, applies_to?, explain}]}`.
  - `when` is an ordinary sift predicate over the world's facts and tags (rule and authored tags, TG3 and TG4) and its parent's facts.
  - `place` names a template (J7).
  - `where` is the chart room's label for where it goes (`low-orbit`, `surface`, `L4`, ...).
  - At load, these are refused: a rule id defined twice, a template no pack provides, a count of 0, and odds outside 0 to 1. Tags named in dressing rules are checked by TG1.
- **`dressing {scope, at_s?}`**: every candidate at or under the scope, worked out on demand and stored nowhere. Each candidate carries:
  - an `id` of the form `<world>#<rule>#<n>`;
  - its rule, pack, template and label;
  - `where`, `explain` and `why`, which lists the facts its condition read, plus `chance` when rolled.
  - Held-off and adopted candidates are left out, and placed objects are not themselves dressed.
  - Each copy's chance is drawn from the seed, the world address and `<rule>#<n>`, so it does not depend on rule order.
  - The reply also lists `orphans`: placed objects whose pack or rule has left the stack and that are not retired. Each has `touched`, true when anything but its creation wrote to it. This list is what a pack swap offers to retire.
- **`adopt_dressing {candidate, name?, at_s}`**: makes the candidate an ordinary authored object under the world, through the template `create` (one universe event, kept values in the record). It also carries:
  - the rule's extra tags;
  - `dressing.pack`, `dressing.rule`, `dressing.candidate`, `dressing.where` and `dressing.faction`.

  Removing the pack leaves the object valid. Adopting a candidate that no longer holds, or that was already adopted, is refused (`no_candidate`).
- **`hold_dressing {candidate, held?}`**: holds a candidate off its world, or lets it back with `held: false`. This is a record fact at the world, `observe.dressing.held.<rule>#<n>`. It is an `observe.*` field, so no scene shows it, and a reloaded stack keeps it.

## Verified by
- `a_depot_goes_on_every_giant_with_a_port_within_2_au_and_nowhere_else`:
  - of four worlds, only the giant with a port at 1.5 AU gets the depot;
  - a farther giant, a rock with a port and a giant without one get none;
  - its "Why?" lists `mass_kg` and `orbit.a_au`;
  - the seeded beacons give the same candidates on a second kernel and with the rule list reversed.
- `adopted_objects_stay_when_the_pack_goes_and_hold_offs_survive_a_reload`:
  - adopting changes no fact of any world;
  - the object carries its marks and template facts;
  - adopting twice is refused;
  - a hold-off survives a stack reload, and letting it back works;
  - after an edit and the pack's removal, the object stays and is listed as a touched orphan.
- `a_bad_dressing_rule_is_refused_at_load`.

## Goldens
`tests/golden/contract.jsonl` was re-blessed on purpose for three new contract cases (`dressing`, `adopt_dressing`, `hold_dressing`) and the new op list in `hello`. No other golden changed.

## Not done
- "Automatic on first visit" (the campaign switch) and per-region adoption are the chart room's TG14. They loop `adopt_dressing` over a `dressing` reply.
- The design's example "a port within 2 AU" is read here as the giant itself carrying a port tag within 2 AU of its star. A condition over a neighbouring world's tags needs a cross-subject predicate, which sift does not have yet.
- `faction` is stored as text. Tying it to a record actor is for TG13 or the economy pack.
