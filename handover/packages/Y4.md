# Y4 part 1: other galaxies in the sky

Night sky design (`design/SSE4-night-sky-design.md`) section 6, package Y4, part 1 of 3. Built 22 Nov 2026 by the kernel thread.

## What it does
`sky_from_here` now also gives `galaxies` (default on; `galaxies: false` leaves them out, `stars: false` gives galaxies only) and a `galaxy_walk` summary. The code is `crates/sse-kernel/src/galaxies.rs`.

- **Where we are.** Our galaxy is the campaign's median Milky-Way-like halo. It sits at the centre of region cell 0 (`provides.other.cosmology.sky.home_mpc`), and the web's axes are taken as our galaxy's axes. A web halo within 0.3 Mpc of home is taken to be us and is skipped.
- **Which galaxies.** Every catalogued halo (G2, above 10¹⁰ M☉) in the region cells within `galaxy_reach_mpc` (12 Mpc). That is 30 cells and about 2,400 haloes in the default universe.
- **When.** The comoving distance on the light cone gives the scale factor the light left at (`Cosmology::comoving_mpc` and `scale_at_comoving`, Hogg 1999 eq. 15). The galaxy is read at that epoch: look-back time, redshift, its stellar mass and star formation then, and its shape class then.
- **Its light.**
  - The galaxy's G3 star formation history, up to the emission time, runs through a simple stellar population (`Ssp::of`). That population is built from the fill stars' own IMF and stellar states, each star a blackbody. The galaxy therefore shines with the same stars the sky draws as points.
  - Redshift is O7's 1/(1+z) with the luminosity distance (1+z) D_M.
  - The light is dimmed by our galaxy's dust along the line, out to 30 kpc in four legs, so the thin layer near us is sampled finely.
  - Rest-frame absolute magnitudes come too.
- **Shape.**
  - The half-light radius is 1.678 disc scale lengths, given in kpc and arcmin through the angular diameter distance.
  - Tilt and turn are keyed by the halo (`cosmos.galaxy.sky.v1`) and isotropic.
  - The axis ratio is cos i for a disc (at least 0.15) and 0.6 to 1 for a spheroid.
- **Order.** Galaxies come brightest first, ties by address. Each is a pure function of its halo, so a cone or a different order gives the same entry.
- `GalaxyEnvelope`, `Stars` and the web come together through `cosmos::with_world` (`with_stars` is now a thin wrapper).

## Measured (default universe "g3", from the Sun's place)
- **Brightest.** The brightest other galaxy is a barred disc 2.5 Mpc away: M* 1.7 × 10¹⁰ M☉, V 6.5, M_V −20.7, B−V 0.48, half-light radius 4.6 arcmin.
- **Count.** 69 galaxies are brighter than V 10 within 12 Mpc.
- **Mass-to-light.** M/L_V is about 1. The blackbody population runs a little blue (B−V 0.5 against 0.6 to 0.8 for real spirals).
- **Cost.** About 30 to 50 seconds in a test build. That is mostly G2's cell catalogues, about 0.6 s each; the galaxies themselves take about 2 ms each.

## Verified by
`other_galaxies_shine_as_their_mass_and_distance_say` in `crates/sse-kernel/src/cosmos.rs`:
- the list is brightest first;
- the brightest galaxy's V, less dust, minus its absolute V is the distance modulus of its luminosity distance, within 0.05;
- its M/L_V, against the Sun's own photosphere through `read_photometry`, lies between 0.3 and 5;
- its look-back is its light time (3.26 Myr per Mpc) within 3 percent;
- `galaxy_chart` of the same cell and halo gives the same stellar mass now;
- a 5 degree cone around it returns the identical entry.

The Y2 sky tests now ask for `galaxies: false`. The contract golden has a galaxies-only case to V 8.

## Known limits (fudge F37)
- **Placement.** Home is a median halo set into the web, not a web halo. The neighbourhood is therefore the web's mean, not a Local Group.
- **Missing galaxies.** There are no satellites yet (part 2), and no galaxies below 10¹⁰ M☉ or beyond 12 Mpc (part 3, the halo count tree by mass class).
- **Light.**
  - Populations are solar-metal blackbodies.
  - There is no dust inside a galaxy.
  - Redshift is the Hubble flow only, so even the nearest galaxies recede.
