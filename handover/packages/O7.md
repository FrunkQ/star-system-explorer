# O7 (kernel side): shifts: Doppler, beaming, gravitational and cosmological redshift

Design: `SSE4-nebulae-and-light-design.md` section 5; stage O7 in section 8. Module `sse_light::shift`.

## What was built
- `Shift { factor D, exponent p, cause, why }`, with constructors:
  - `doppler(β, cos θ, continuous_jet)`: D = 1 / (γ (1 − β cos θ)); p = 3 for a discrete source, 2 for a steady jet.
  - `gravitational(M, r)`: D = √(1 − r_s / r), p = 1.
  - `cosmological(z)`: D = 1 / (1 + z), p = 1 (use with the transverse comoving distance; that is d_L in effect).
  - `then` chains shifts (factors and boosts multiply).
- The exact transformation L_λ(λ) = D^(p+2) L'_λ(λD): `shifted_power` (a band's observed power is D^(p+1) times
  the emitted power over the band edges times D, so no resampling), `shifted_spectral_luminosity`,
  `publish_shifted` (bands, slices, samples, peak, with the shift's why and reference added), `line_at`.
- No kernel op: the render pass `shift` needs only D and p per object (the renderer applies them before
  `band-map`); a kernel op with observers comes with O12 (spectrum as it reaches an observer).

## Evidence (`cargo test -p sse-light`, 7 new tests)
- Doppler factor: head-on √((1+β)/(1−β)), receding the inverse, side-on 1/γ (transverse Doppler), to 10⁻¹².
- A moving black body equals a black body at D·T in every band to 10⁻⁶; total rises as D⁴.
- Hα from a source approaching at 0.15 c lands in the green slice with D⁴ of its power.
- At 30 km/s the visible band changes by under 0.1 % ("no visible change at ordinary speeds").
- Twin steady jets at β = 0.9, 60°: total power ratio equals ((1 + β cos θ)/(1 − β cos θ))³ to 10⁻⁹.
- Sirius B (1.018 M☉, 0.00864 R☉): 75 km/s gravitational redshift against Barstow et al. 2005's measured
  80.4 ± 4.8 km/s (inside 2σ).
- z = 1 doubles every wavelength; Hα moves into the near infrared with (1 + z)⁻² of its power.

## Not done

- Diagnostic mode (a blue-to-red map of speed) is a UI recipe; the facts it needs are D and the speed.
- Light bending near black holes: a later render pass, flagged.
