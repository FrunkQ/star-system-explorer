# V9w2: light from haloes in the cosmic web

V9w's open item: "light from haloes and galaxies (G2, G3) instead of one colour for all matter". In True, the web now
shines the way a galaxy survey sees it: only where matter has fallen into haloes deep enough to hold galaxies. The
gas and dark matter of voids and sheets are dark.

## What it does

- **Which haloes.** G2's own catalogue, cell by cell (8 Mpc), from a floor of 10¹¹ M☉ up: pack setting
  `web_light.floor_msun` (`WebLightParams`, cited in its doc comment: Behroozi et al. 2019 puts about 10^8.7 M☉ of
  stars in such a halo; the haloes above it hold almost all the starlight). `Haloes::cell_above` gives exactly the
  haloes `Haloes::cell` gives above the floor, bit for bit (bins are drawn top-down and every draw is keyed by bin and
  halo number); only the merger histories and neighbours are skipped. `cell` itself now reads the same per-bin table,
  with the same numbers in the same order (no golden changed).
- **Where.** A halo sits on the peak of the starting field it collapsed from (G2), so it is moved by the cube's own
  truncated Zel'dovich step and lands on the filaments and knots. As with the parcels, only haloes whose matter
  started inside the cube are drawn.
- **How massive then.** The halo's own growth history (McBride form with Correa's alpha and beta), so Play shows the
  galaxies appear and grow.
- **Its galaxy.** G3 on the median halo of its mass today, once per half dex (as the DM3 ledger does), read at the
  cube's time as shares of the halo's mass then: stars, star formation, quenched. Fudge register: "web light: median
  galaxies".
- **Light.** `galaxyLight` in `renderer/src/web-cube.ts` uses the True galaxy's own laws (`GALAXY_LIGHT`): old stars
  by mass (Bell and de Jong 2001), young by star formation (Starburst99); colour the light-weighted mix of 4,800 K and
  15,000 K. Shown over 3.5 decades below the brightest galaxy in six strength steps and four colours, one GPU draw
  group each (fudge register: "web light: colour by mixing temperatures").
- **Size of cube.** A bigger cube keeps to the brighter galaxies, as a deeper survey does: the foam map asks a floor
  of 10¹¹ × (side / 100 Mpc)³ M☉ (never below the pack's), so the count stays near 10,000 and the time near the same.
- **Kernel.** `web_slice` with `cube: true, haloes: true` (and optional `galaxy_floor_msun`) gives the cube's
  `galaxies` instead of its parcels, so the view shows the parcels first and lights them after. The median galaxy
  table is kept between calls (keyed by everything it is made from).
- **View.** True draws the galaxies as light on black; HUD on adds the dark matter as faint dots and the cube's
  edges. Until the galaxies arrive, True shows the parcels glowing alike, as before, with "Lighting the galaxies…".
  3D (Chart) is unchanged. Phones take the same path: WebGL2 draws the same groups (`GpuDots` with glow and size);
  with no GPU, the canvas draws them with additive blending.

## Numbers (native release, 100 Mpc cube at today)

- About 13,700 galaxies above 10¹¹ M☉, about 2 s to find them; the median galaxy table about 40 ms. The browser is
  slower (wasm, roughly 2×), which is why the galaxies come in their own call after the parcels.
- Galaxies whose nearest parcel is in a void: 28%, holding 26% of the stars (voids fill 77% of space). Real void
  galaxies are fewer and fainter still; the voids here hold too much matter (V9w's fudge row) and the median galaxy
  per mass does not know its environment.
- At z = 3 a 60 Mpc cube has 818 galaxies against 2,721 today.

## Checks

- sse-cosmos `web_light_tests.rs`: the haloes above a floor are the cell's own, bit for bit, at three floors and three
  cells; galaxies light the web not the voids (count, biggest first, Milky Way halo M*/Mh 0.5 to 8%, the biggest
  quenched, fewer galaxies in voids than voids' share of space, void galaxies smaller); the young web has fewer and
  smaller galaxies.
- sse-kernel `a_web_cube_with_haloes_gives_its_galaxies` (galaxies alone, floor honoured, haloes without a cube refused).
- renderer `web-cube.test.ts`: light and colour per galaxy; groups by brightness and colour, the faint dropped, the
  far-flung left out, Real shrinks.
- e2e `foam.e2e.mjs`: True at 100 Mpc lights more than 1,000 galaxies and says so; screens with HUD off and on.
- Goldens: none changed. A contract case for `haloes: true` waits for a golden-lane slot.
- Screens: project files `sse4/handover-screens/V9w-screens/` (v9w2-*).
