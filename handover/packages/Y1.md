# Y1: the fill-star count tree, by brightness class

Night sky design (`design/SSE4-night-sky-design.md`) section 4.2, package Y1. Built 21 Nov 2026 by the kernel thread, after Y7a, as the design requires.

## What it does
`crates/sse-cosmos/src/star_tree.rs` splits star counts down a tree, so a sky draw can find the bright stars far away without drawing every faint one near them.

- **Classes.** `StarParams.class_edges_lsun` (0.1, 1, 10 and 100 L☉, then half decades from 300 to 300,000 L☉) gives twelve luminosity classes. The bright end is fine because a class's reach is set by its brightest member: with decade-wide classes, a sky to magnitude 6.5 would draw tens of thousands of 1,000 L☉ stars to find the few 10,000 L☉ ones it can see. White dwarfs and giants fall in whichever class their present luminosity gives.
- **The tree.** A root block is 2⁶ cells (640 pc) on G5's co-rotating grid (`tree_depth` 6).
  - The root's count of a class is Poisson. Its mean is the sum of its children's expected systems times the block's class share.
  - Each node splits its count among its eight children by sequential binomials, in proportion to their expected systems. That is a multinomial, so every cell keeps a Poisson count about its own mean (the splitting property).
  - Counts are keyed by (node, class, frame) on the stream `cosmos.stars.tree.v<key_version>`.
- **Expected systems.** A 10 pc cell uses G5's own value (`Stars::expected`). A coarser node averages the envelope over four Gauss-Legendre heights at its centre, then multiplies by its volume. A centre sample alone read coarse nodes about 4 percent low, because the disc's vertical profile is steep and curved.
- **Class shares** come from a quadrature per root block: 512 IMF points (finest in the brightest per cent and per mille) by 48 log-age bins of the birth history at the block's radius. The log ages resolve the short lives of the bright classes.
- **The leaf.** A cell's stars are drawn exactly as G5 draws them (mass, companions, birth, [Fe/H], position, motion). Mass and age are found by keyed rejection until the star's present luminosity falls in its class, on `cosmos.stars.c<class>.v<key_version>`. The search keeps to the corner where the class lives: masses above the lowest IMF quantile and ages below the greatest age that the block's quadrature found in the class, each with a margin. That conditions the draw exactly, as long as no member lies outside the corner, and it makes a rare class such as 30,000 L☉ cheap to draw.
- **API.**
  - `TreeWalk::cell_count(cell, class)`.
  - `TreeWalk::cells_within(class, centre, radius)` walks only branches that hold stars of the class and that meet the sphere.
  - `TreeWalk::draw(cell, Some(class))` gives the stars of one class in a cell. `draw(cell, None)` gives all of them, and that is what `Stars::cell` now uses.
  - The walk memoises its node expectations; `reads()` counts them.
- **Frame.** `frame_myr` drops from 1 to 0.1 Myr. Systems move up to 45 km/s, so within a 1 Myr frame they drifted up to about 45 pc from the cell that drew them, and a single cell's systems no longer held each other's true neighbours (found by the voyage finder thread). At 0.1 Myr the drift stays under 5 pc, so a system is never more than one cell from where it was drawn. The fill now redraws every 0.1 Myr, which still never matters within a campaign. It also leaves the design's Q2 ruling (the fill drawn for the observer's frame) sound.
- **Versions.** `key_version` 2 is now the default and selects the tree. Version 1, G5's per-cell draw, still works for anything keyed to it. Promoted stars are stored whole (Y7a), so none moves.
- Test builds optimise `sse-cosmos` (`[profile.dev.package.sse-cosmos]`, as for formation and surface), so the tree's checks take under a minute.
- Fudge F34 holds the approximations: the node means, the normal binomial above variance 25, and one set of class shares per block.

## Verified by
- `cell_counts_are_poisson_about_the_envelope`: 4,000 cells in a slab through the plane at the Sun. Chi-squared is within 5σ of its expectation, and the total count is within 5√N of the envelope's.
- `a_bright_star_found_from_afar_is_the_cell_draws_star`: stars over 100 L☉ found within 1 kpc are the very stars that a whole-cell draw gives there, and of the class asked for.
- `a_sky_walk_costs_what_it_finds`: the reads stay within eight children per level per star found. Eight times the volume costs about eight times the reads, so the cost per star found does not grow with the volume.
- `a_rare_bright_class_is_drawn_in_its_class`: stars over 30,000 L☉ within 1.5 kpc all fall in their class, with none from the fallback.
- `the_tree_is_order_independent`: cells drawn in any order, in one walk or several, give the same stars as `Stars::cell`.
- G5's neighbourhood and pin checks pass under the tree. The pins check now counts cells whose drifting stars leave them short.

## Goldens
- `tests/golden/stars.json` changed on purpose: Y1 changes which fill stars are drawn. Q1 ruled the G5 golden could be re-blessed, and Y7a's stored pins keep every promoted star.
