# U14: Desks (step 2, part 1, of the top-level redesign; UI track)

Status: done 21 Nov 2026. This is the first half of step 2 in `handover/design/SSE4-top-level-ui-and-onboarding.md` section 5. The design's section 2.3 covers the desks, and section 11 covers where Undo and Redo show. Depth (Curious, Science, Engine) is part 2, and copy and paste is part 3.

## What it does

- **Three desks replace Edit and View.** The bar shows Explore, Build and Run as one switch. The ink slides to the chosen desk, so it reads as a switch rather than as links; an alpha tester asked for exactly this on 21 Nov. The motion is transform only and stops under reduced motion.
  - **Explore** is for looking. Nothing can be changed by accident, and there is no Undo or Redo. A fresh browser opens here.
  - **Build** is for changing. Values on cards edit, bodies drag, and Undo and Redo show at the top left of the stage.
  - **Run** is the table. It opens on the Running lens: few facts, player knowledge marked, no editing.
  - The desk is remembered on this device. The keys are V (Explore), E (Build) and R (Run); V and E keep their old meaning.
- **Each desk opens with its own lens.** Explore uses Advanced (Basic on a basic-tier device), Build uses Building, and Run uses Running.
- **"See as"** is the desk's small menu. It is the old lens list, now applied to this desk only: choosing Basic on Explore leaves Build on Building. The desk's own lens is marked "(this desk)", and choosing it again returns the desk to its default. The key L still steps through lenses. Custom, pack and players' lenses are all here, along with "Make your own…".
- **Undo and Redo show only where things can change:** on the Build desk, or in a what-if (section 11). On Explore and Run they are absent.
- **The Workshop is always armed.** Its screens change the tool, not the universe, so Packs, Player data and the rest are editable whatever the desk. A system read back from the universe is always looked at, never edited in place.
- **Under the hood, a desk is the workspace's mode plus a lens.** No new mechanism is needed; this is what design 2.3 describes.
