# U19: Formation replay (UI track)

Status: done 22 Nov 2026. This is for Alex's video on how system generation works, which shows the stages and the realism. The trailer thread films it. Make a system's grown tab gains **Watch it form ›**, which plays the run's nine stages over time in a full-page panel. Before, the run showed them only as the "How it formed" text list.

## What it does

- **Everything comes from the run's own record.** Nothing is simulated in the browser. `packages/ui-contract/src/replay.ts` reads:
  - **Disc:** `history.slices[].disc` (the five condensation lines and the disc mass over time) and `history.disc.bands` (vapour, rock, carbon, water ice, CO₂ ice, CO ice).
  - **Growth:** each embryo in `growth.bodies`, with its birth time, birth orbit, orbit at the end of growth, and ledger of claims. The ledger sets the shape of the growth curve. The body's total sets its size. One conversion to Earth masses, fitted to the surviving worlds, applies to all of them.
  - **Violent phase:** `upheaval.events`:
    - chain-breaks
    - scatter: the body jumps to `to_au` on a stretched orbit
    - ejected: a rogue planet, gone
    - impact: merge, merge with a moon, hit-and-run, or erosion. With erosion the impactor shatters into a debris belt.
    - settled
  - **Leftovers:** `upheaval.worlds` (final orbits), `leftovers.clouds` (debris belts appear at the impact that made them), and `upheaval.moons` with `leftovers.moons`.
- **The nine stages, each told from this run:** inheritance (metals, ultraviolet glare), star, disc and material bands (the water ice line at the start and at the end), growth (number of embryos), giants and migration (or "no giants"), the violent phase (what broke and how many impacts), leftovers, each world's early life (the last magma ocean freezing) and long life (when the star leaves the main sequence, and its age now).
- **Every event in words.** For example: "An embryo gathers at 2.7 AU, in the rock and carbon band: it will become World 7." "Giant impact: Embryo 11 hits World 10; they merge and the debris forms a large moon." "World 9 flings Embryo 15 out to 60 AU on a stretched orbit (e 0.66)."
- **The picture.** It is top down on a log scale with the scale rings from U18.
  - The disc's bands are tinted from hot (inner) to icy (outer), and the water ice line is labelled. It fades as the gas clears.
  - Bodies grow with the cube root of mass. Each orbit is an ellipse with its periapsis and apoapsis placed honestly on the log scale, dashed in accent colour when stretched (e above 0.1).
  - A body in the latest event is inked in accent, and an impact shows a burst (not under reduced motion).
  - The captions give the stage text and the latest event.
- **Playing.** Play steps through the run's beats: every event, every stage start, and even steps in log time between them. A beat with an event lingers for about 1.4 s so it can be read.
  - The scrubber runs in log time from 5 thousand years to a few hundred million. Its marks show the events, with the violent ones in accent.
  - Chips 1 to 9 jump to a stage.
  - Keys: space plays or pauses, the arrows step a beat, and Escape closes.
- **Honest line under it:** "From this run's own record … Orbits between recorded moments are interpolated; the turning round the star is for display. Distances on a log scale."
- Watching writes nothing, and the state hash does not move.

## Checks

- `packages/ui-contract/test/replay.test.ts` grows "first light" and "cold harbour" through the kernel and checks:
  - the same run gives the same replay
  - all nine stages are present, in time order
  - the disc has five lines, and the water ice line creeps inward
  - embryos grow
  - the disc is gone after it clears
  - at the end the survivors are exactly the run's worlds, at the run's orbits, with total mass within 15% of the run's (the remainder is mass lost to hit-and-run and erosion)
  - the impacts are told in words
  - a rogue planet leaves "first light" when it is thrown out
- `tests/ui/replay.e2e.mjs` (added to `npm run ui:e2e`) opens the replay from Make a system and checks:
  - stage chips 3 (disc and lines), 4 (embryos), 6 (an event in words, gas gone) and 9 (the run's worlds)
  - play moves time forward
  - Escape closes it
  - nothing is written
- ui:check and ui:test pass. No goldens change.

Screens: `U19-screens/` (growth, the violent phase, the end, from "first light").

## Not yet

- It plays only a run still in the Make dialog. An adopted system would need its run kept, or re-run from its manifest. The kernel can do this from the seed.
- The hand-off into the Body globe's time-lapse frames at stage 8. The renderer thread's globe API (`setTimeLapse`, `setTime`, `layers`, `frames`, `pick`, PR 123) and proven core's `surface_frames` op come first.
- A companion star is named in the stage text but not drawn.
- Trojans, rings and irregular moons show once F5 sends them with times.
