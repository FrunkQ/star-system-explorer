# F7: long life of generated systems, close binaries by rule table (19 Nov 2026)

Status: done at sketch and standard level as below. Design: formation design section 3 row 9, section 8 row F7.
Builds on T1 and T2 (kernel track, `sse-time`), which already age a system: the star's life line with winds and
the giant core clock, secular orbits, giant tides, engulfment, oceans lost, white dwarf cooling, windows.

## What was built (crate `sse-time`)
- `generated.rs`: `spec_from_run(&SystemRun)` turns a formation pipeline run into the ageing engine's
  `SystemSpec`: orbits from growth, planet masses (giants with their gas), inclinations drawn Rayleigh 1.5 degrees
  (Fabrycky et al. 2014) and angles uniform, keyed by each world's address (stream `time.generated.v1`); oceans
  where early life left surface water and no desiccation; the largest free-floating cloud as the debris belt.
  `long_life(run, settings, target)` ages it and merges one dated story: formation events (history and
  `geology-quiet`), close-binary interactions and ageing events, in date order.
- `binary.rs`: close binaries by rule table, sketch level. Each star on an orbit with what it circles; each side
  aged with T1's ageing star (winds widen the orbit, a M constant); the first side to fill its Roche lobe
  (Eggleton 1983) at periastron, found on the star's own clock and dated by 60-step bisection, is the donor.
  Outcomes: stable transfer below the critical mass ratio (Hurley, Tout and Pols 2002: 3 main sequence, 4 gap,
  eq. 57 giants; conservative, a M_d M_a constant); merger above it on the main sequence; otherwise a common
  envelope with alpha 1, lambda 0.5 (Webbink 1984, de Kool 1990), merging if the companion does not fit its lobe
  at the new separation. Each outcome is a `TimeEvent` with a plain "why" and its numbers.
- `scenario.rs`: `generated_runs`, `sunlike`, `target`, `generated_golden`.

## Fix to T1's epoch stepper (behaviour for the Solar System unchanged; `time.json` golden unchanged)
- A giant's tide could drag a close planet to a = 0 in the step before the engulfment check, and the phase
  window that runs first then planned 2^64 steps (hang). Windows now skip planets already inside the star.
- First-order secular theory can return non-finite or unbound values for crowded, massive generated systems:
  the last good values are kept and e is held below 0.99.
- Close-pair windows open once, and again only when the pair has closed in by a tenth since. Generated systems
  can be born with a tight stable pair; re-checking it at every step cost minutes and showed nothing.

## Checks (`cargo test -p sse-time`)
- Ten G0 systems: deterministic, one heat-and-ageing story in date order, nothing from ageing before now,
  oceans lost only on ocean worlds.
- Twelve Sun-like systems: every world with periastron inside 0.3 au is engulfed by the red giant (18 of 18 in
  systems whose star reaches its end in the run; metal-rich suns outlive a 10 Gyr run) and every ocean world
  inside 1.5 au boils (8 of 8). Nothing beyond 2.5 au is engulfed.
- Binaries: Eggleton's lobe at q = 1 (0.3789) and 0.1; 1 + 0.8 solar masses at 1 au gives a common envelope and
  a post-common-envelope period inside 0.1 to 10 days (observed, Nebot Gomez-Moran et al. 2011); 2.0 + 1.5 at
  0.05 au transfers stably (Algol-like); 1.5 + 0.15 at 0.012 au merges; wide pairs and red dwarf pairs never
  touch. G0 patch: 60 pairs, 2 common envelopes, 1 merger.
- New golden `tests/golden/time-generated.json` (three G0 systems and one Sun-like), in the cross-device harness.

## Not yet
- Giant tables: no horizontal branch or asymptotic giant branch yet; the Sun's largest radius stays at T1's
  0.85 au (detailed models 1.0 to 1.2 au), so Venus survives where Schroeder and Smith 2008 lose it. When added,
  `AgeingStar::star_with` and `core_at` are the places (see T1.md).
- Binary outcomes are reported, not yet fed back into the stars' own life lines or the planets around them;
  planets in binaries age around the primary only.
- Moon tides (recession, spiral-in) and planet spin locking over the long life.
- Rule-table numbers are code defaults (`BinaryRules::default`); move them into `packs/base-time` with sources.
