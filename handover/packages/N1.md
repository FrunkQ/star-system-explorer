# N1: Sound engine

Status: done (19 Nov 2026), branch `claude/project-thread-adgixl`, in the PR into `main`. Design: `SSE4-sound-design.md` (sections 1, 2, 6 and stage N1).

## What was built
- **Contract** (`packages/ui-contract/src/sound.ts`, pure data and arithmetic):
  - `SoundScene` (`sse.sound-scene/0`): the sources audible from a vantage. Each source has its recipe, mixer layer, honesty label (real sound, translated signal, designed), a "Why?" and its driving quantities.
  - `score(scene, window, dial, seed)`: deterministic timed notes, with onsets and levels from keyed draws. It uses only + − × ÷, sqrt and integer hashing, so it is the same on every engine. `scoreHash` fingerprints it.
  - `SoundRecipe` (`sse.sound-recipe/0`): a small graph over a fixed node set (osc, noise, filter, env, gain, delay with internal feedback), wires to `out`, and nodes the full tier only. `checkRecipe` rejects anything outside the schema: unknown types, settings out of range, loops, nothing wired to out, no why.
  - `SoundSettings`: on, tier (silent, basic, full), the four-layer mixer (world, instruments, interface, music), the true-to-settled dial and captions. Stored on the device only (`sse4.sound.gm`, `sse4.sound.players`), never in a session, pack or chronicle.
  - **First scene builder, the orbit bed** (`orbitScene`): one soft tone per orbiting body, its orbital frequency raised by whole octaves into hearing (the SYSTEM Sounds approach the design cites). Giants sit an octave lower and small bodies higher. The dial pulls notes toward a just-intonation scale on the lowest tone.
    - GM voices come from Kepler's third law over the chart's frame (or the derived year when present).
    - Player voices come only from `orbit.period_s` in their own scene, so a period they have not been given is never heard.
- **Engine** (`packages/sound/src/engine.ts`, Web Audio): made only on a user action. It schedules a minute of score ahead, builds each note from its recipe, and routes it through per-layer gains. The basic tier is capped at 6 voices and skips `full_only` nodes; silent makes nothing.
- **Recipe** `orbit-tones` (`packages/sound/src/recipes/orbit-tones.json`): sine plus a quiet detuned triangle, a slow swell (3 s attack, 6 s release), a low-pass, and a soft echo on the full tier.
- **Control** (`packages/sound/src/SoundControl.svelte`) on the GM status bar and the players' page:
  - It contains the on switch, tier, mixer, dial (with "no longer exact" shown when settled) and captions.
  - It lists the sources in view, each with its label and a Why.
  - Captions say what is sounding. A saved "on" starts only at the user's first tap or key after a reload.

## Acceptance (sound design stage N1)
| Criterion | Where it is proved |
|---|---|
| Silent until enabled | `tests/ui/sound.e2e.mjs` counts AudioContexts: none on load, one when the GM ticks Sound on, none after a reload until the first tap |
| A scene's score is identical on two devices | `sound.test.ts` pins the Kestrel fixture's fingerprint (`tests/ui/sound.fixture.json`). The e2e compares the browser's fingerprint for the live derived scene with Node's for the same scene, at dial 0 and dial 1. CI runs it in Chromium and Firefox |
| A recipe outside its schema is rejected | `sound.test.ts`: an unknown node type, an out-of-range setting, a wire loop, no wire to out, an empty why, a bad layer and a bad wave are each refused. `loadRecipes` refuses before any sound and the control shows why |
| State hash unchanged by any sound setting | e2e: turning sound on and moving the mixer, the dial and the tier leave the state hash as it was |

Also covered: players on long-range sensors get a silent bed ("Silence: nothing here has a known orbit to sound"), and through Scanned they hear Tarn (`sound.test.ts`, on the kernel's scene).

## Gaps and choices (for Alex)
- The design says the score is a kernel product. Here it is computed in TypeScript with deterministic arithmetic, since the kernel has no sound op yet. Moving it into the kernel is a K-track item and would not change the contract.
- Web Audio's built-in nodes stand in for the AudioWorklet. Nothing yet needs custom processing.
- Only the music layer has a source (the orbit bed). The other layers and the full mapping (star temperature, composition, atmosphere, activity) are N2 to N5. The SYSTEM Sounds video Alex linked has not been watched from here; the tuning pass (N3) should start with it.
- Hearing the charts as a sweep and GM-pushed ambience are N7.
