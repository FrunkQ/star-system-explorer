# R4 part 1: recipe editing through checked edits (Orac's tool, without Orac yet)

Status: part 1 done (19 Nov 2026), renderer track, PR into `main` after R3.
Authority: `design/SSE4-surfaces-and-rendering.md` 5 and the R4 row ("Orac adjusts a recipe from a written request and the cost stays within the device budget"); surface design section 9 (R4 row: "a recipe change never alters the state hash").

R4 needs the agents track (A2) for the "written request" half, and agents are not started. Part 1 builds everything Orac will call, and a hand editor on the Body screen that uses the same edits. Part 2 (when A2 lands): Orac turns a request into this edit list, shows the cost table, and the person keeps or drops it.

## What was built (three lines)
1. `recipe-edit.ts`: `applyEdits(recipe, edits, {library, pixels, device, budget_ms})`. Edits are a small closed list (`set`, `unset`, `knob_default`, `add`, `remove`, `use_tiers`, `budget`, `name`); each is checked against the pass library, the result is checked whole by `checkRecipe`, and each tier's full-quality frame is costed before and after against the budget (`newly_over` names tiers an edit pushes over). Changes come back in words; an edited recipe has its own id, `derived_from` and a `history`. Shipped recipes are never changed.
2. `editableSettings(recipe, library)`: the menu Orac and the editor choose from (every pass use, its settings, current values, and which knob drives a setting).
3. Body screen, under the quality badge: "Edit this recipe" shows the settings of the passes on the tier being drawn, previews the edit live on the globe, lists the changes and the cost per tier ("about 8.7 ms, now 23.9 ms a frame of 17 ms (over: the governor will reduce it here)"), and "Keep as my recipe" adds it to the Recipe list. Kept recipes live in this browser (a viewer's look, not campaign data) and can be forgotten.

## Results
| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| Edits are checked and refused with reasons | 10 bad edit lists in `recipe-edit.test.ts` (unknown pass or setting, out of range, unknown library pass, duplicate id, removing the last picture, bad budget, unknown op, empty) |
| Cost before and after against the budget | Detail knob to 1 with 64 by 16 air samples: gpu-high 8.7 to 23.9 ms against 16.7 ms, reported in `newly_over` |
| A recipe change never alters the state hash | `render.e2e.mjs` edits True colour (exposure 1 to 3), sees the globe brighten, keeps it, and the state hash check at the end of the run still holds |

## Limits
- No written requests yet (that is Orac, A2). Costs are the reference-device estimates scaled by the probe, not measurements of the edited recipe; the governor still measures real frames once it runs.
- Kept recipes stay on the device; sharing them (they are data only, so safe to share) waits for the packs or campaign file to carry them.
