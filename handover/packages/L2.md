# L2: box H: life's fluxes and the planet's answer at each time boundary

Status: done (19 Nov 2026). Kernel track, PR into `main`. Design: life design section 8, section 14 row L2.

## What was built
**The boundary (`sse-life::history`).** `history_with(world, budget, params, seed, Some(&mut boundary))` runs the L1b history with a physics side attached. `history()` is unchanged (it passes `None`), so every L1 golden is identical.
- At the start of each step the history hands the boundary the previous step's `StepFluxes`: total production, production by metabolism, and whether life holds land.
- The boundary (trait `Boundary`) answers with world facts. These override the epoch's own facts for that step (for example `o2_fraction`), so capabilities that need oxygen wait for the oxygen the planet actually holds.
- `HistoryPoint.world` records the answer (skipped when empty).
- Life only reports fluxes; the physics side decides the consequences. Nothing assumes life stabilises its world.

**The stand-in physics side (`sse-life::feedback::AirBox`).** One air reservoir until the planet layer's atmosphere and interior models exist.
- O2 sources: organic carbon burial (0.1% of oxygenic production) and hydrogen escape.
- O2 sinks: volcanic and metamorphic reducing gases (3e12 mol/yr today, e-folding 2000 Myr back in time as the interior was hotter) and oxidative weathering (6.5e12 mol/yr at the present level, scaling with sqrt(O2 / PAL), solved implicitly).
- Methane at steady state with each step's production: methanogens plus a decay share (0.25% oxic, 50% anoxic), lost to photochemistry (9.6 yr oxic, 1000 yr anoxic) and to escape.
- Escape is diffusion limited (1.3e16 mol O2-equivalent a year per unit CH4 mixing ratio, scaled by area), only through an anoxic upper air, and bounded by the methane that exists. It cancels reducing gases but makes no free O2 beyond them; the rest is booked as oxidising the crust.
- The oxic and anoxic chemistry over a step depends on the O2 the step ends with. More O2 means less methane and less escape, so the end state is a unique fixed point, found by 60 fixed bisection steps. An explicit switch flipped between oxic and anoxic every step.
- Land life multiplies silicate weathering by 4 (`co2_rel` = 0.25).
- Every O2 movement is booked in a `Ledger` (burial, escape, reductants, weathering, change, unmet reductants, escape to crust, carbon buried).
- `coupled(&CoupledManifest)` runs life and the box together. The manifest (`sse.life-coupled/1`) holds the world timeline, budget, history and air settings and the seed; the run carries the manifest's content hash.
- `oxygenated_myr` is the start of the first stretch with O2 at or above 1% for 200 Myr (`sustained_rise`), so brief whiffs do not count.

**Pack:** `provides.other.life_feedback` in `packs/base-life`, with sources (DOIs) for each part.

**Fixtures (`scenario::coupled_manifest`):** "earth" is the Earth timeline with every dated O2 fact set to 0, so only the box sets oxygen. "reduced" is a small world (0.3 Earth area, 5e19 mol of air) with ten times Earth's reducing gases.

## Results
| Fixture, 8 seeds | Oxygenic photosynthesis | Oxygenated (O2 held over 1%) | O2 today |
|---|---|---|---|
| Earth, oxygenic before 1500 Myr (6 seeds) | 450 to 1780 Myr | 2545 Myr in every one | 0.22 to 0.51 |
| Earth, late oxygenic | 2745, 4070 Myr | 2755, 4080 Myr | 0.22 to 0.51 |
| Reduced world | 450 to 4070 Myr | never (O2 under 1e-5) | 0; CH4 about 1e4 ppm |

- Earth's air first holds more than a trace of O2 at about 2300 Myr (2.3 Gyr ago, near the real Great Oxidation at 2.4 Gyr ago) and passes 1% at 2545 Myr. Whatever date life learned oxygenic photosynthesis, the date is set by when the falling reducing gases drop below burial: a balance, not a date.
- Complex cells follow the oxygen on 7 of 8 seeds.
- Seeds where land life arrives reach 0.51 O2 today: land production raises burial, and nothing in the box yet raises oxidative weathering or fires to match.
- The reduced world builds methane and stays anoxic on every seed.
- Conservation: the worst step residual is about 1e4 to 1e5 mol against throughputs of about 1e23 mol (under 1e-12 relative).

## Checks
`feedback_tests.rs` (8 tests): Earth oxygenates through the balance on all seeds, never before oxygenic photosynthesis, at the same point for every early-oxygenic seed, with complex life following; the reduced world never oxygenates; replay from the manifest through JSON is identical (run and hash), and a bad format is refused; the ledger balances and the held O2 equals the booked change; the box answers every step and land draws CO2 down; `sustained_rise` ignores whiffs; the pack equals the defaults; golden.

Golden `tests/golden/life-feedback.json` (new, on purpose): both fixtures, sampled every 500 Myr, with hashes. Harness engine `life-feedback` (Node and browsers identical to native). No existing golden changed.

## Limits
- The air box is a stand-in. There is no climate or temperature feedback, no ozone shield (land waits on O2 at 1%), no nitrogen or phosphorus feedback on the budget, and no oxygen in the ocean separate from the air.
- Reducing gases follow a fixed exponential, not the world's own interior (S-track heat budget and outgassing when they exist).
- Buried carbon and escape to the crust are totals only; nothing deposits them as strata yet.
- Box I's signatures are not yet read from the coupled air; they still use their own sketch of the gases.
- The coupled run is not yet an op in the kernel API or an event in the chronicle. When a consumer needs it: a `life_coupled` op returning the run, and a write-back event citing the manifest hash.
- Earth's present O2 runs high (0.51) on seeds with land life; see Results.
