## What landed

- **Six formula blocks** in `packs/base-physics/formulas/range.json` (atom count 105 to 111):
  - `sse.range.beam_spot`: spot radius w = d √((M² · 1.22 λ / D)² + j²), irradiance 0.838 P / (π w²),
    fluence α I t. Born and Wolf section 8.5.2 for the Airy radius and core share, Siegman 1990 for M².
    M² is the setting's `spread_over_diffraction` (base-tech limit 11): below 1 is past diffraction.
  - `sse.range.burst_fluence`: α f_x Y / (4π d²). Glasstone and Dolan 1977 sections 1.77 (70 to 80 % of
    the energy as primary thermal radiation, mostly soft X-rays) and 1.79 (at high altitude they travel
    far). The share is the weapon's own figure.
  - `sse.range.impact_fluence`: ½ m v² over the projectile's face (computed).
  - `sse.range.burn_through`: ρ t (c (298 K − T₀) + H_m + L_f) to melt, plus ρ t (H_b + L_v) to boil, with
    H_m and H_b the NIST-JANAF integrated heats (the data sheet thread's advice: Cp nearly doubles before
    tungsten or molybdenum melt).
  - `sse.range.hit_odds`: t = d/c + d/v, s = ½ a t², p = min(1, (R/s)²), our own derivation (plan
    section 13 asks for one; not the Atomic Rockets formula).
  - `sse.range.reading`: ratio, the reading (2 in range, 1 marginal, 0 out of range) and the margin
    (model number 2, assumed) as an output so rings use the block's own figure.
- **`sse_sense::reach`** (`effect_at_range(ask, sheet)`): a weapon (`beam`, `burst`, `projectile`), a
  target (skin, radius, dodge acceleration, closing speed) and a distance give what arrives, what the
  skin can take (`takes_from`: `sheet` or `authored`), ratio, reading, odds, delay, wander, spot, rings
  (`in_range_m`, `enough_m`, `marginal_m`, `even_odds_m`, by bisection in the logarithm on the same
  blocks; `None` when energy does not fall with distance), a beam's `Load` for the RK9 ledger
  (electric_w = P / efficiency, its reject_k), missing sheet fields, why-lines and references.
- **The skin from the sheet**: mixture rows are flat, substance rows keep figures under `phases` and
  `thermal`. Field names agreed with the data sheet thread (24 Nov): `density` (or `density_solid`),
  `heat_capacity`, `heat_to_melt`, `latent_fusion`, `heat_to_boil`, `latent_vaporisation` (all J kg-1
  but the heat capacity); a body that sublimes carries `heat_to_sublime` and `latent_sublimation`.
  Until MP27 adds them for the hull metals every material reads as `unknown` and names what is missing;
  a typed `takes_j_m2` then stands in, marked authored.
- **Kernel op `effect_at_range`** (`asks` → `effects_at_range`), Cost light, in the light perf chunk.

## Checks (`crates/sse-sense/src/reach_tests.rs`, kernel `effect_at_range_reads_a_beam_against_a_skin`)

- 1 MW at 1 µm from a 1 m mirror at 100 km: a 0.122 m spot; rings √2 either side of "just enough",
  since fluence falls as the square.
- A 20 MW beam from a 30 % laser: 66.7 MW drawn, 46.7 MW on the heat bus (plan section 13 says 47).
- A 10 m target pulling 10 m/s² one light second away: 2 s of lag, 20 m of wander, one in four.
- 1 kt in space, 70 % X-rays: 2.33 kJ/m² at 10 km; the delivery is left to NAV and said.
- A slug at 3 km/s: 4.5 MJ/kg, about its own mass in TNT; only the odds draw its ring.
