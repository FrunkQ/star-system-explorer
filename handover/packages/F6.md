# F6: each world's early life, and the hand-off to surface, climate and life

Status: done (18 Nov 2026), ahead of F2 to F5 (it reads a plain `Protoplanet`, so growth plugs in later). Physics track.
Design: `SSE4-system-formation-design.md` section 3 (stage 8) and 8.

## What was built (`crates/sse-formation/src/early.rs`)
- Input `Protoplanet`: solid mass, orbit, the date growth ended, solid mass by element group (the provenance ledger's
  sums, `GROUPS` order) and captured gas. F2 to F4 will fill it; F6 needs nothing else.
- Core: metal over metal plus rock, less what water oxidises (up to half the core for a water-rich body; sketch).
- Solid radius: Zeng, Sasselov and Jacobsen 2016, (1.07 - 0.21 CMF) M^(1/3.7), the relation of the
  `sse.interior.rocky_radius` atom (used outside its 1 to 8 Earth-mass port range as a labelled extrapolation), plus
  half the water mass fraction for a water mantle (Zeng et al. 2019: half water is about a quarter larger).
- Primordial hydrogen: none; giant above half the mass in gas (radius 11.2 Earth radii, sketch until F3's giants);
  otherwise energy-limited loss over the first Gyr (Owen and Wu 2017, efficiency 0.1) against the binding energy, with
  XUV at 10^-3.5 of bolometric for 100 Myr then falling as t^-1.5 (Jackson et al. 2012), 200-step fixed log sum.
  Kept envelopes take the Lopez and Fortney 2014 eq. 3 thickness at 5 Gyr.
- Magma ocean (Hamano et al. 2013): a few Myr beyond the runaway limit, about 100 Myr inside it with the water lost
  (runaway at 1.06 Earth insolation, Kopparapu et al. 2013); none for bodies under 0.1 Earth masses.
- Volatiles (Elkins-Tanton 2008): 30 percent of water and CO2 stays in the mantle, the rest outgasses. 90 percent of
  carbon goes to the core (Dasgupta 2013; sketch). CO2 is reported as a surface pressure.
- `HAND_OFF_KINDS`: twelve kinds, each with SI unit and the suite that reads it: body.mass, body.radius,
  orbit.semi_major_axis, orbit.eccentricity, interior.core_mass_fraction, interior.water_mass_fraction,
  atmosphere.h2_envelope_fraction, early.magma_ocean_end, early.surface_water, early.co2_partial_pressure,
  early.desiccated, early.insolation. `WorldStart.facts` carries one value of each.
- `scenario::world` and `scenario::early_golden`: six reference worlds round the Sun.

## Checks (`cargo test -p sse-formation`, 24 tests, 8 new in `early_tests.rs`)
- Earth (1 Earth mass at 1 AU, 0.1 percent water, bulk carbon 730 ppm per McDonough 2003): CMF 0.339 (Earth 0.32),
  radius 0.999, no envelope, early insolation 0.698 (faint young Sun), about 3 oceans at first, CO2 216 bar (test band
  30 to 300, Venus has 92), magma ocean over by 54 Myr.
- Venus at 0.723 AU: insolation 1.34 at formation, past the runaway limit, desiccated, magma ocean to 150 Myr. Mars
  at 1.524 AU: radius 0.546 (real 0.532), keeps its water.
- Radius valley: 5 Earth masses with 3 percent gas is stripped at 0.05 AU (bare core 1.54 Earth radii) and keeps 2.9
  percent at 0.5 AU (2.65 Earth radii); LF14 envelopes of 1 to 5 percent on that core land in Fulton et al. 2017's
  sub-Neptune peak (1.9 to 3.5). A gas-rich body is a giant.
- A half-water 2 Earth-mass world is 1.15 to 1.35 times the dry one, with a smaller core.
- No quantity has two publishers: no base-physics flow publishes any hand-off kind, no flow kind is published twice,
  no hand-off kind is declared twice.
- The hand-off is what the flows read: every base flow whose inputs are all hand-off kinds (planet-bulk) compiles and
  runs on F6's Earth (surface gravity 9.4 to 10.2 m/s²), and every hand-off kind the kernel knows has the dimension of
  the unit F6 writes, in SI.
- Determinism: the same world twice gives identical JSON.

## Known limits
- The host luminosity before the main sequence is the ZAMS value (F7 brings tracks), so pre-main-sequence XUV and
  early insolation are underestimated for very young worlds.
- Envelope loss is a single energy budget over 1 Gyr, not a time integration; core-powered mass loss is not modelled.
- Water oxidising the core, the 30 percent mantle share and the 90 percent carbon-to-core share are first-order
  sketches; tidal heating and radiogenic heat (al26_rel, k40_rel from inheritance) do not yet set magma-ocean length.
- Kinds early.* and interior.water_mass_fraction, atmosphere.h2_envelope_fraction are declared here, not yet in the
  base-physics pack's quantity kinds; the surface and life suites register them when they start reading.
