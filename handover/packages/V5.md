# V5: intents and GM approval

Status: done (19 Nov 2026), branch `claude/project-thread-adgixl`, in the V4 PR into `main`.

## What was built
- **Requests over the session** (`ui-contract/src/p2p.ts`): `request` (player to GM: seat id, request id, intent) and `answer` (GM to that player: approved with the event id, or declined with a reason).
- **Player side** (`PlayerReplica.request`): the player's own session decides first (`decide` in `seat.ts`). What the seat may never send is refused on the spot; what needs the GM goes to the GM. Requests are disabled while the GM is away (H9.2 decided). `requests()` and `onRequests` give the player its own list with status.
- **GM side** (`GmHost`): a request is checked against the seat the GM gave that player (from the session's own seat list, never the player's say-so) and against `checkIntent`, then queued. `approve` applies the intent through the GM's own session, so it is chronicled once and reaches every player as an ordinary event; `decline` answers with a reason. Nothing is applied until the GM acts.
- **Seats travel in the manifest**; a shared session's table is `sharedTableSeat()` (audience everyone, may ask to set a value, never auto-approved).
- **Screens**: the players page gains "Ask the game master" (suggest a name for the body in focus) with the player's requests and their status; the GM's status bar lists waiting requests with Approve and Decline.
