# TG8 part 1: access labels (base hooks pack) and the optional intrigue pack

Track: Traveller pack. Design: `handover/design/SSE4-tags-design.md` (TG8; section 5.3, V3's `frontier/*` and `intrigue/*`). Part 2, the `resource.*` labels and the `resource` group at the surveyed tier, waits on the TG11 inventory blocks (proven core).

## What it adds

- **`packs/base-hooks/pack.json` (`sse.base-hooks`, presentation)**: group `access` (bodies), five derived rule tags at the `scanned` tier, none with a chance:
  - `access.gas-skimming`: a giant (10 to 4000 Earth masses, not a star);
  - `access.aerobraking`: a giant's envelope, or at least 100 Pa at the surface (Mars, about 600 Pa, has been aerobraked by orbiters; Pluto, about 1 Pa, is too thin);
  - `access.gravity-assist`: planet-sized, at least 0.05 Earth masses (Mercury), as real missions used Venus, Earth, Mars and Mercury;
  - `access.ice-mining`: a solid world at or below 150 K;
  - `access.water`: open water at the surface.
- **`packs/intrigue/pack.json` (`sse.intrigue`, universe)**: four hooks (`intrigue.anomalous-signal` 0.03, `derelict-rumour` 0.05, `uncharted-feature` 0.08, `legend` 0.02) as seeded-chance rule tags (TG4) on any body that is not a star. Off by default (not in the opening stack). Its tags carry no tier, so they are GM-only until the GM discloses one.
- Both are offered on the Packs screen ("Add access labels", "Add story hooks (intrigue)").
- The Traveller pack's tags were already declared (TG1, `scripts/traveller-tags.json`).

Generated by `scripts/build-hooks-packs.py` (edit the script, never the JSON). Thresholds are written in both namings (SI facts and the chart room's `mass_earth`, `surface_k`), with the Earth mass read from the master data sheet.

## Checks

`crates/sse-kernel/src/hooks_tests.rs`: Sol (from `base-sol`) has gas skimming on the four giants only and never the Sun; aerobraking on the giants until Mars is given 610 Pa, then Mars too; gravity assists from all eight planets and not the Moon, Pluto or Ceres; Kestrel's Hollow (61 K) has ice mining and Vault (118 K, a giant) does not; the "Why?" of a label lists the facts it read; without the intrigue pack no hook appears; with every chance at 1 all four land on Earth for the GM and none reach a player holding a survey view; the real odds give the same hooks on two runs and fewer than twelve on Sol's eleven worlds; everything stacks together with the Traveller and both universe packs.
