## What was built
- `crates/sse-surface/src/impacts.rs`, settings under `provides.other.surface.impacts` in `packs/base-surface`
  (version `surface-impacts-v1`), with sources for every number.
- **Impact history** (`ImpactHistory`): background flux relative to the Moon's plus the chronicle's bombardment
  spikes. `impact_histories(run)` reads F4's `Bombardment` events per world from a formation run;
  `ImpactHistory::lunar()` is the default for fixtures and imports.
- **Production**: Neukum et al. 2001 chronology N(>1 km) per km² (T measured back from 4.5 Gyr after the world
  formed), gravitational focusing (1 + v_esc²/v_inf²), crater-size scaling as g^-0.216 (Holsapple 1993),
  two-slope size-frequency (-3 below 1 km, -2 above). A spike's delivered mass becomes craters through the
  impactor size distribution (crater/impactor ratio 12 at lunar gravity, largest impactor 300 km), decaying
  with the spike's e-folding time.
- **Saturation**: N_sat(>D) = 0.15 D^-2 per km². Kept craters follow N_sat (1 − e^(−N/N_sat)); each crater's date is
  production-weighted with older craters thinned by later ones (the same formula, so counts and dates agree).
- **Survival**, per crater, in order (the first that applies erases it and is counted in `erased`):
  thick air (no crater under 0.1 km × P^0.75: about 3 km on Venus); global renewal by regime, at a rate that
  scales as (q / q_ref)³ (Europa 60 Myr, Venus 500 Myr, Titan 500 Myr, Io 1 Myr; a cooling Ganymede keeps its
  younger craters); plate crust younger than the crater; lava plains erupted later (craters under 40 km are
  buried, larger ones keep a rim over a flooded floor).
- **Degradation**: relief left = exp(−K / (0.016 D²)), K = 5.5 m²/Myr × age (gardening, Fassett and Thomson 2014)
  + 5e4 m²/Myr × time wet + 100 m²/Myr per bar × age. Under 5% left, the crater is a ghost: on record, no relief.
- **Shape** (Pike 1977, transitions scaled as 1/g): bowls (depth 0.196 D) below 15 km × (1.62/g); complex with a
  central peak (d = 1.044 D^0.301 km lunar, self-similar in D g); peak rings from 150 km; multi-ring from 600 km.
  The radial profile is C1 with bounded curvature, so band-limiting holds.
- **Volcanism** (lid regimes): in 50 Myr steps, lava plains resurface 0.15 of the area per Gyr at 0.06 W/m²,
  scaled by heat flow above 0.02 W/m², capped at 60%. Shields on lid worlds of at least 3000 km radius grow while
  heat flow stays above the threshold, up to 9 km × (9.81/g) (Mars about 24 km), radius 12 × height. Mobile-lid
  volcanoes stay with S2's plumes.
- **Mechanism slots**: `mechanisms.chronology` (`neukum-2001`), `crater_shape` (`pike-1977`) and `volcanism`
  (`heat-flow-plains` or `none`). A pack naming anything else is refused with the known names.
- **Listed and small craters**: craters at least 4% of the radius across are listed globally (the size grows if
  the list would exceed 20,000, so it is never truncated). Smaller ones live in size bands (halving each band) on
  cube-sphere cells at least 1.5 diameters wide; `cell_craters(band, face, i, j)` is a pure function, so any tile
  resolves them alone.
- **Explanations**: every crater, plains unit and shield carries a cause sentence (date, what made it, shape and
  why, relief left, flooding). `Features::explain(lat, lon, radius)` lists what covers a place, newest first,
  and ends with how long the surface has held craters. `Features::model_age_myr` inverts the chronology (a
  crater-count age).
- **Atlas**: `Atlas::with_impacts(features, field)` adds crater and shield relief to every tile down to the fact
  floor. A feature appears at a level only when at least 4 samples cross it (the octaves' Nyquist rule);
  `tolerance_m` gains the relief of features entering at the child level and the curvature of carried ones
  (twice each, for overlaps). Plains set basalt and their own surface age. Tile `tags` list the feature ids;
  `pending` now says "named features (S7)".

## Checks (`cargo test -p sse-surface impacts`)
- `what_survives_follows_how_the_surface_is_renewed`: the Moon, Mercury, Mars and Vesta keep the saturated count
  within Poisson error (about 1180 listed each); Earth, Venus and Titan keep under 20; Europa and Io none;
  Ganymede between; Jupiter has no surface.
- `crater_density_matches_the_chronicle_on_a_renewed_surface`: Venus with craters listed from 24 km: 153 kept
  against 157 expected from production times survival. The crater-count model age is 354 Myr (observed Venus:
  about 300 to 750 Myr). Hemispheres hold their share.
- `shapes_follow_size_and_gravity`: simple-to-complex at 15 km (Moon), 6.5 (Mars), 2.5 (Earth), as observed.
- `every_feature_answers_why_it_is_here`, `chronicle_spikes_add_dated_craters` (a spike adds craters dated after
  it, attributed to its event), `lava_plains_bury_and_flood_older_craters`,
  `small_craters_resolve_per_cell_and_respect_the_air`, `mechanism_slots_refuse_unknown_atoms`,
  `the_same_world_gives_the_same_features`.
- `craters_refine_to_the_fact_floor_within_tolerance`: Moon, Mars, Mercury and Vesta, a chain of tiles from level
  0 to the fact floor over a listed crater: worst child-to-parent error 27 to 46% of the stated tolerance.
- `a_crater_shows_in_the_tiles_heights`, `golden_impacts` (`tests/golden/surface-impacts.json`, new; no existing
  golden changed). Harness: `surface-impacts` in the engine list; Node passes all engines and the worker contract.

## Known limits
- A 256-sample cratered tile takes about 0.2 s native (twice an uncratered one), mostly drawing small craters.
- Craters do not yet feed back into S3 (basins do not shape later drainage) or into S2's plate heights.
- One chronology shape for every system (lunar, shifted to the world's formation); spikes come only from F4.
- Shield and plains rates are rule-of-thumb values for lid worlds, labelled so in the pack sources.
- Ice-world crater relaxation and secondary craters are not modelled.
