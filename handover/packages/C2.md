# C2: base physical atlas, habitability profiles, gas mixes

Status: done (18 Nov 2026), kernel track, branch `claude/project-thread-dzkz4p`. Classification design section 12, row C2.

## What was built
- **Pack `packs/base-atlas/pack.json`** (`sse.base-atlas`, kind base-physics), generated by `scripts/build-base-atlas.py` (edit the script, not the JSON). It has 69 classes on 34 axes in four region-scored schemes. Every class has names, a one-line learn text and at least one passing example.
  - **physical** ("What kind of object is this?"), section 10 restructured by bulk:
    - Base families: small body (carbonaceous, icy, metallic, stony), iron-rich, rocky, water-rich, sub-Neptune, ice giant, gas giant and brown dwarf.
    - The gas giant splits by equilibrium temperature into ammonia clouds, water clouds, cloudless, alkali clouds, silicate clouds and ultra-hot. These are Sudarsky's classes I to V plus ultra-hot.
    - 17 stacking modifiers: tidally locked, eccentric, ringed (flourish), volcanic, tide-heated, unshielded, young, ancient, rogue, captured, living, inhabited, terraformed (fictional), rubble pile, contact binary (flourish), puffed and massive.
  - **surface** ("What is its surface like?"): airless, molten, hot greenhouse, hot desert, temperate desert, temperate ocean, ocean-covered, cold desert, ice with an open equator, frozen, ice shell over an ocean, hydrocarbon lakes, and hycean (speculative). Every class is gated to solid bodies (envelope fraction ≤ 0.005), except hycean, which needs a thin hydrogen envelope.
  - **air** ("What is its air made of, and how much is there?"):
    - Gas mixes as base regions: none, nitrogen-oxygen, nitrogen, carbon dioxide, hydrogen-helium, sulphurous, steam and methane.
    - Pressure bands as modifiers with hard edges: trace, very thin, thin, standard, dense, very dense and crushing.
    - A `tainted` modifier.
  - **habitability** ("What do people need to live here?"): requirement profiles nested from loosest to strictest, so the deepest one that fits wins. They run sealed habitat, then pressure suit, then breathing apparatus, then filter mask, then shirt-sleeve, on gravity, temperature, pressure, O2 and CO2 partial pressure and surface radiation dose.
- **Gates now count in region scoring** (`Atlas::fit`): a gate bound limits the fit like a region bound. Gates still add nothing to a class's volume. K8 atlases had no gates, so no K8 result moved.
- **`Atlas::overlaps(scheme)`** lists the pairs of same-depth base classes whose cores could both hold one subject, which would leave the label to a tie-break.
  - Two classes count as apart when some axis that both constrain (region or gate, ancestors included) has cores meeting in at most a point, categories with nothing in common, or opposite flags.
  - An axis that only one of the two constrains does not separate them.
- **Fixtures**:
  - `tests/fixtures/classify/solar-system.json`: the 18 bodies gained the V4 facts the new axes read, plus an `expect` block per scheme. The SSE 3 classic labels are unchanged.
  - `tests/fixtures/classify/exoplanets.json` (new): 14 reference exoplanets and brown dwarfs. Each has its published description and the classes that description allows.

## Checks (`cargo test -p sse-sift base_atlas`, plus the golden)
- **Every class has passing examples**: `check_examples()` is empty. The examples are the Solar System and the exoplanets, real small bodies (Bennu, Eros, 16 Psyche, 67P, Arrokoth, PSO J318.5-22) and named "illustrative:" variants of Earth, Mars and Titan, with "out" examples where a class must not apply.
- **No unintended overlaps**: `overlaps()` is empty for all four schemes. A test adds a copy of `rocky` to check that the overlap check does find an overlap.
- **Solar System**: every base and every modifier matches the fixture. Examples:
  - Earth: rocky, living, inhabited; temperate ocean; nitrogen-oxygen, standard; shirt-sleeve.
  - Venus: hot greenhouse, carbon dioxide, crushing; sealed habitat.
  - Mars: cold desert, very thin; pressure suit.
  - Europa: ice shell over an ocean.
  - Titan: hydrocarbon lakes, nitrogen, standard.
  - Triton: captured.
- **Reference exoplanets classify as their published descriptions suggest**:
  - HD 209458 b: silicate clouds, puffed.
  - WASP-12 b and KELT-9 b: ultra-hot.
  - WASP-107 b: cloudless, puffed.
  - Gliese 229 B: brown dwarf.
  - GJ 436 b: ice giant.
  - GJ 1214 b: sub-Neptune.
  - K2-18 b: sub-Neptune with a hycean surface.
  - Kepler-10 b and 55 Cancri e: rocky, molten.
  - TRAPPIST-1 b: rocky, airless, tidally locked.
  - Kepler-138 d: water-rich.
  - HD 189733 b: alkali or silicate clouds.
- **Tainted air if and only if filter mask**: a sweep over the O2 and CO2 partial pressures shows the air modifier and the habitability profile draw the same line.
  - Bounds are closed, and at exactly 0.005 bar of CO2 the deeper shirt-sleeve profile wins. Tainted therefore starts at the next double above 0.005.
- **Golden `tests/golden/classify.json` changed on purpose**:
  - New keys `base_atlas` (content hash), `base_solar_system` (every body's label in all four schemes) and `base_surface_relabels`. The warming Earth now goes temperate ocean, then hot desert at 2 Gyr, then molten at 3 Gyr.
  - The existing classic relabel event ids and `chronicle_head` moved for one reason: the warming Earth scenario authors more facts (the new V4 fixture facts, plus `surface.liquid_fraction` and `surface.liquid` at 2 Gyr). The classic labels and their times did not change.
  - The golden is checked natively, in Node and in Chromium (engine scenarios identical; kernel API 110 of 110 through a Web Worker).

## Choices made (for review)
- Pressure and radiation dose are linear axes, because a log axis needs strictly positive bounds and "no air" is 0.
- Thresholds are textbook and approximate:
  - The iron-rich and rocky line is 45% metal.
  - The sub-Neptune radius is 1.5 to 3.5 Earth radii.
  - The brown-dwarf line is 4130 Earth masses (13 Jupiter masses).
  - The Sudarsky cloud bands use equilibrium temperature.
  - The shirt-sleeve profile is 263 to 313 K, 0.3 to 1.5 g, CO2 ≤ 0.005 bar, O2 0.16 to 0.5 bar and ≤ 20 mSv/yr.
- Most of the new axes are `provisional`: no V4 flow publishes those facts yet, so they are read as authored or imported. The surface and atmosphere flows should publish them under these field names, or the axes should be re-pointed.
