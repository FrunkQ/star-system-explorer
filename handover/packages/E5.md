# E5 (functional traits): traits under selection

Evolution design (`design/SSE4-evolution-design.md`) section 6 and section 14, package E5, cut to the functional traits E6 needs, as the design's V4 first stage says: no lineage art, no body plans, no branching yet. Built 21 Nov 2026 by the kernel thread. Code: `crates/sse-life/src/traits.rs`, tests in `traits_tests.rs`, fudge F32.

## What it delivers
- **Seven traits** from section 6.1, each with an optimum that the habitat's conditions set and limits that physics sets:
  - temperature optimum: the mean temperature, within life's limits of 253 to 395 K (the upper limit from Takai et al. 2008);
  - temperature breadth: the swing;
  - water strategy, from save to spend: the water balance;
  - tissue economics, from slow and tough to fast and cheap (Wright et al. 2004): nutrient supply;
  - stature: up to the hydraulic limit, 130 m at Earth's gravity (Koch et al. 2004), scaling as 1/g, times the water there is;
  - dormancy: variability;
  - disturbance strategy, from resist to reseed: disturbance.

  Each trait is a `TraitDef` (id, optimum, tolerance, tau0), so a pack can change or add traits, up to the design's eight.
- **Conditions** (`Conditions`) are what E6 hands in per region: temperature and swing, water balance, gravity, nutrient supply (E4's `nutrient_rel`), disturbance and opportunity.
- **Relaxation.** `v' = opt + (v - opt) exp(-dt / tau)`, with `tau = tau0 / opportunity`. A rich, warm habitat tracks change in about 0.1 Myr, and a sparse one with opportunity 0.001 takes 100 Myr, as section 6.2 asks. Values are clamped to the limits in force.
- **Strain** is the largest gap in tolerances. The share of production kept is `exp(-s^2 / 2)`.
  - Past 3 the guild is lost, a `lost` event naming the trait that strained most.
  - If strain passes 2 and comes back under 1, a `rescued` event is recorded.
- **Seed everything.** `seed_guilds` draws 16 candidates per habitat inside the limits (`life.guild.v1`, keyed by habitat, candidate and trait id). A candidate the habitat cannot hold never takes hold.
- **Envelope.** `TraitParams::envelope` gives every trait at its optimum: the cheap answer for a world nobody opened. `mean_traits` is the share-weighted mean of the living guilds, which is what E6 reads.

## Not yet, and why
- **Branching and the lineage tree** (section 6.3): the full E5, not needed for E6's cover.
- **Wiring into the history.** Strain does not yet lower production in `history.rs`. E6 builds the per-region conditions from E4's soil and the air box, steps the guilds with the history, and feeds the share back. Until then the module stands alone, like E4, and changes no golden.

## Verified by
- `limits_never_broken_across_10000_worlds`: 10,000 random habitats (temperature 200 to 450 K, gravity 0.5 to 30 m/s², opportunity 0.001 to 100), each with four abrupt changes. No living guild's trait ever leaves its limits, and every share stays between 0 and 1.
- `extinctions_scale_with_the_speed_of_change`: the same 40 K warming over 0.01, 0.1, 1, 10, 100 and 1000 Myr, across seven habitats of different opportunity. Guilds lost: 32, 21, 10, 0, 0, 0. The count never rises as the change slows.
- `order_independent`: reversing the guild list gives the same guilds and the same events.
- `envelope_agrees_with_stepped_history`: for three habitats held still, the survivors' mean traits are within 1 % of a tolerance of the envelope. At Mars's gravity the hydraulic limit allows trees over 300 m.
- `a_survivable_shift_is_recorded_as_rescue`: a 25 K shift strains the guilds past 2, they adapt, and rescue is recorded with no loss.
