# TG13: two universe packs written for SSE4, and Traveller ports and bases as dressing rules

Track: Traveller pack. Design: `handover/design/SSE4-tags-design.md` (TG12, TG13, section 5.7). Depends on TG12 (dressing rules, kernel thread).

## What it adds

- **`packs/near-future/pack.json` (`sse.near-future`)**: a generic near-future hard science fiction setting. No faster-than-light travel. Powers: Terran Accord (inner worlds), Jovian Consortium (fuel and ice at the giants), Free Haulers' Union (claims on cold rocks). Seven templates (orbital yard, skimmer depot, patrol station, ice-moon station, claim outpost, patrol cutter, ice hauler), four labels (home-type world, volatile giant, ice moon, frontier rock), five dressing rules, a political lens.
- **`packs/space-opera/pack.json` (`sse.space-opera`)**: "the Meridian Reach", an original space opera setting. Powers: Meridian Crown, League of Free Worlds, Ashen Syndicate. Seven templates (free port, Crown citadel, moon-forge yard, smugglers' den, watch beacon, household lancer, lane trader), four labels (throne world, forge giant, rimward world, shadow moon), five dressing rules, a political lens. Its starlane drives carry `so.fictional_drive` and the pack says `fictional: true`.
- **Traveller ports and bases on dressing rules** (`scripts/build-traveller-pack.py`, `DRESSING`): `society.starport` A and B give a highport candidate, A to C a downport, D a class D spaceport, E a beacon; each `traveller.bases.<code>` (N, S, W, D, M, C) gives its base template. These are candidates the GM adopts or holds off (TG12); nothing is placed until adopted.
- **Packs screen**: "Add the Near future setting" and "Add the Meridian Reach setting" beside the Traveller pack (`packs.gm.json`, `scripts/build-shell.sh`).

## Choices

- Both packs are generated by `scripts/build-universe-packs.py`. Edit the script, never the JSON.
- **Conditions read both fact namings.** Catalogue and formation facts are SI (`body.mass` kg, `orbit.semi_major_axis` m); the chart room's Kestrel scene uses `mass_earth`, `a_au`, `surface_k`. Each threshold is written once in Earth masses or au and emitted as an `any` of both; the Earth mass and the au are read from the master data sheet (`base-reference`), not typed. Where there is no surface temperature, "temperate" falls back to 0.8 to 1.3 au and "cold" to 5 to 60 au (a stand-in, said here; a world with `surface_k` is read by it).
- **Stars are no worlds**: every world condition first asks "no `star.effective_temperature`, no `mass_sun`".
- **Giants are 10 to 4000 Earth masses** (Uranus is 14.5; 4000 is about 13 Jupiter masses, where brown dwarfs begin). A moon is "of a giant" when its parent's mass is in that range (`parent.` facts).
- **No kept values in universe templates.** Crew aboard, readiness and discipline are a game system's record kinds (the Traveller pack declares them); a universe pack carries only facts (`construct.crew_max` and so on), so it stacks under any game system or none.
- Shared tags (`construct`, `ship`, `station`, `site`) are declared identically to the Traveller pack's, which TG1 accepts as a shared vocabulary. Everything else is namespaced `nf.*` or `so.*`.
- Seeded chances (ice-moon stations 0.5, claims 0.4, forge yards 0.6, dens 0.35) are for what physics cannot decide; each rule's words say "drawn from the seed".
- Every name in both packs was written for SSE4. Neither copies a published setting.
