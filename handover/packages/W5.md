# W5: weather hooks: kernel op, descent column, sound cues, GM pins and orders (19 Nov 2026)

Weather design sections 5 and 7, stage W5. `crates/sse-weather/src/hooks.rs` and a single kernel op `weather`
(`crates/sse-kernel/src/weather.rs`, one `Request` and one `Response` variant).

## What was built
- `hooks::column`: the column over a place, top down, three bands (low, middle, high, from the scale height and tropopause),
  each with cloud, wind, turbulence, icing (supercooled water cloud), lightning, visibility inside, a hazard 0 to 3 and a
  telemetry line ("4 to 9 km: cumulonimbus of water, wind 90 km/h, severe turbulence, lightning"); corrosive acid decks and
  dust or ash at the bottom are hazards too; plus the ground report.
- `hooks::sound`: wind, gustiness, what falls and how hard, thunder heard per hour (strikes within 15 km), pressure and the
  main gas; `silent` on airless worlds. For the sound engine's world sound (N5).
- GM controls as pins with `evidence: "authored"`: `order_clear` (clear skies over a place for a scene), `order_calm`, and
  `trigger_storm` (a cyclone or tropical cyclone where the story needs it, drifting with the local steering wind). Orders win
  over everything drawn. Saves keep them as pins, like any recorded storm.
- Kernel op `weather { want, world? | conditions?, day?, tile? [face, level, x, y], n?, lat?, lon?, pins? }`, `want` one of
  `climate` (averages and circulation with its "why" lines), `systems`, `field`, `report`, `column`, `sound`. Response
  `weather_read { want, world, provenance, data }`. Light cost class. Worlds are the weather fixtures by label (Earth, Mars,
  Venus, Titan, Locked temperate world, Fast-spinning Earth, Moon, Jupiter) or given conditions.

## Checks
- `cargo test -p sse-weather` (20 tests): one storm seen from orbit (the field tile over it is thick cloud), met on descent
  (hazard at least 2), heard on the ground (thunder, heavy rain), and still there from a freshly made block; GM clear and
  calm orders; airless silence; Venus descent meets corrosive cloud.
- `cargo test -p sse-kernel`: contract golden re-blessed on purpose (`tests/golden/contract.jsonl`: `hello` and the
  unknown-op message gain `weather`, plus seven new lines); TS types regenerated (`Request.ts`, `Response.ts`). CI's wasm job
  replays the contract in Node, Chromium, Firefox and WebKit, so a weather tile is checked byte-identical on every engine.

## Fudges
Band heights (0.24 and 0.83 scale heights), icing range 248 to 273 K, hazard thresholds (wind 30 and 50 m/s, turbulence 0.3
and 0.7), thunder audible to 15 km. Stand-ins until a real column model (radiative-convective) and aviation hazard rules.

## Known limits
- The op serves fixtures or given conditions. Chart worlds need conditions from the physics track (spin, tilt, gas mix);
  `sse_weather::conditions::from_surface` builds them from a surface envelope with stated assumptions; wiring a `body`
  address waits on the one-body object.
- No terrain in the op yet (the Rust API takes `Some(&atlas)`); the renderer can ask the field per tile at any level.
- Pins are passed by the caller; recording them in the chronicle (GM pin, order, trigger as authored events) is for the UI
  and kernel tracks: the event payload is the `Pin` JSON.

## Integration
- Renderer ("Build photoreal renderer"): `weather { want: "field", world, day, tile, n }` per tile for the cloud passes
  (layers' cover, type, condensate, optical depth) and aerosols for the atmosphere pass; `systems` for named storms.
- Physics (S5 hazards, descent scene): `want: "column"`.
- Sound: `want: "sound"`.
- UI: `want: "report"` for the table; `climate` for a "Why?" on the belts.
