sse.magnetic.dynamo_field	its steps ρ^⅓ and q^⅔ are not whole units on their own (only their product is): waits for unit-free working lines
sse.nebula.ionising_photon_rate	iterates: a 400-term series for the photons above 13.6 eV
sse.orbit.kepler_position	iterates: Kepler's equation solved by a fixed number of Newton steps
