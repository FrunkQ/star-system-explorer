# P1: atom library, first wave

Status: done (18 Nov 2026). Branch `claude/project-thread-h9y77o` (physics track).

## What was built
- Crate `sse-atoms`: 27 cited atoms (31 after P4), each with an `AtomDecl` (ports with validity ranges, bounded params, cost
  class, references, curious and science lenses, formula) and golden cases in `src/tests.rs`.
  - Stars (`star.rs`): main-sequence luminosity (Eker et al. 2018 six-piece MLR), radius (Demircan and Kahraman
    1991), effective temperature (Stefan-Boltzmann), main-sequence lifetime (M/L scaling), luminosity at age
    (Gough 1981 by fraction of main-sequence life).
  - Orbits (`orbit.rs`): total mass, period and its inverse (Kepler III), apsides, Kepler's equation with a fixed
    30 Newton steps (true anomaly, r/a), Hill radius at periapsis, Roche limit (fluid or rigid coefficient).
  - Planets and interiors (`planet.rs`): Chen and Kipping 2017 mass-radius forecast, bulk density, surface
    gravity, escape velocity, rocky radius from core mass fraction and its inverse (Zeng et al. 2016), core radius
    (Zeng and Jacobsen 2017).
  - Climate and atmosphere (`climate.rs`): insolation, equilibrium temperature with a redistribution factor, grey
    Eddington surface temperature, scale height, surface pressure from column mass, Jeans parameter.
  - Tides (`tides.rs`): eccentricity tidal heating (Peale et al. 1979, Segatz et al. 1988), despinning time
    (Gladman et al. 1996).
- `sse_atoms::kinds()`: every port kind with a description and dimension (tests forbid an undescribed or
  two-dimension kind).
- Pack `packs/base-physics/pack.json` (`sse.base-physics` 0.1.0), generated from the kinds and
  `packs/base-physics/flows/*.json` by `SSE_BLESS=1 cargo test -p sse-atoms base_physics_pack_is_current`. Five
  flows: star main sequence, planet bulk, planet climate, orbit basics, moon tides.
- `docs/atom-spec-template.md` for P5 issues.

## Checks (all in `cargo test -p sse-atoms`)
- Every atom is cited, has both lenses and a formula, bounded params with defaults inside them.
- Formula reproduction against the source papers to 1e-12 (Eker, Demircan and Kahraman, Chen and Kipping
  Neptunian constant from continuity, Gladman).
- Kepler's equation residual below 1e-12 for e 0 to 0.99 and mean anomaly -10 to 10 rad.
- Solar System fixture (NASA fact sheets, IAU 2015 B3, Lainey et al. 2009), 11 golden cases in the pack flows,
  all passing: Sun, Sirius A, Earth, Mars, Moon, Venus, Jupiter, Io. Tolerances are stated in each case.
- The pack resolves with `sse-pack` and every flow compiles against the real atom registry.

## Known limits, recorded rather than hidden
- No metallicity dependence and no evolved stars. The empirical relations are for solar-neighbourhood main-sequence
  stars; adding a metallicity input that changed nothing would be fake. Needs a stellar track table (MIST or
  PARSEC, tabulated as pack data). The network policy blocked fetching one here. Raise as the first P5 issue.
- Radius is for a typical-age star: young Sirius A comes out 16 percent large (case tolerance says so).
- Grey surface temperature has no convection, so it overstates hot thick atmospheres; Venus needs an absurd τ.
- Chen and Kipping is a forecast: Jupiter comes out at 1.23 RJ.

## Integration notes (parallel tracks)
- `sse-atoms` touches no other crate. The kernel still registers the toy atoms (`sse-kernel/src/lib.rs` line ~88).
  To serve real physics, the kernel thread (K7 to K10) or the integration step adds `sse_atoms::registry()`
  alongside the toy registry and loads `packs/base-physics/pack.json`. Deliberately not done here to avoid
  editing the kernel crate another track owns.
- Constants not in `sse_core` (Boltzmann, atomic mass unit) live in `sse_atoms::common::k`; move them into
  `sse_core::quantity::consts` at integration if others need them.
