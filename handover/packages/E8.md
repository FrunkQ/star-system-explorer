# E8: how far the air is out of equilibrium, feeding box I

Design: `handover/design/SSE4-evolution-design.md` section 10 (signatures).

## What it does
`crates/sse-life/src/disequilibrium.rs` computes the **available Gibbs energy** of a world's air, following Krissansen-Totton, Bergsman and Catling 2016 (doi:10.1089/ast.2015.1327). It is the Gibbs energy of the observed air less that of the same elements at equilibrium, in J per mol of air.

- **The air.** The air is an ideal gas. Each species has μ = μ°(T) + RT ln(xP/P°), with μ° from E0's formation energies (constant-enthalpy van 't Hoff, as in E0). Liquid water is a pure phase at activity 1, when the world has it.
- **Finding equilibrium.**
  - The search directions are E0's gas reactions (knallgas, methane oxidation, methanogenesis) plus CO oxidation and the water-gas shift, which are balanced on load.
  - Line searches by bisection cycle along those directions. G is convex in the extents, so this converges to the one minimum. The extra directions keep a step from stalling on an exhausted species.
  - Each drop is credited to its reaction, which names the pair that carries the signal.
- **Box I.** `refine(signatures, diseq, ...)` replaces the crude gas-pair signals (`o2_ch4`, `o2`, `ch4`) with one `disequilibrium` signal. Its likelihood ratio is 20 when the air is held out of balance, and 0.5 when gases a single-gas scan would flag turn out to be at equilibrium. The confidence is then worked out again.

## Verified by
- **Earth today: 1.66 J/mol**, 98 percent of it in methane oxidation. The published atmosphere-only value is 1.51 J/mol, and theirs also counts water vapour and N2O.
- **Archean air: 21.5 J/mol, carried by H2 with CO2 (methanogenesis)** and the water-gas shift. "A different pair before oxygen" (design 14, E8).
- **The water-loss world** round a red dwarf (O2 0.5, CO2 0.3, N2 0.2, no reductant) is at equilibrium. With the air measured, box I's red-dwarf false positive stops being a candidate, and Earth stays one at over 0.9 confidence.
- **Order independence.** Reversing the basis changes the answer by under 1e-5.

## Not yet, and why
- **The atmosphere-ocean figure.** Krissansen-Totton and colleagues get Earth's 2326 J/mol mostly from N2 + O2 + water to nitrate, and the Archean's from CH4 + N2 + CO2 + water. That needs dissolved species, ions and pH, which come with E7's ocean chemistry. The ions are already in E0's table.
- **Through-time series.** A history run that samples its air at each epoch and plots the measure. The air box (L2/E3) carries O2 and CH4 but not yet H2 and CO.
- **The gradient catalogue.** The design says the measure reads the catalogue's open gradients. The catalogue holds activities per medium from the pack, not the air's measured composition, so E8 reads the composition directly. The two agree on which reactions count, because they share E0's table.
- **Wiring into `signatures()`.** That needs the observables to carry an air composition (P4 world stacks). Until then, `refine` is called by whoever has the air.
