# E3: competing oxygen sinks (fixes L2's high oxygen)

Status: done (19 Nov 2026). Kernel track. Design: the evolution design (`/mnt/project-files/sse4/SSE4-evolution-design.md`), section 7 and row E3 of its stage table. That design has not yet been synced into `handover/design/`, because its section 15 questions are still with Alex. E3 does not depend on any of them.

Asked for by Alex on 19 Nov at 14:50: competing sinks for oxygen, with fire the easiest and microbes one of the options.

## What was built
L2's air box (`sse-life::feedback::AirBox`) now has three brakes on organic burial, its one big oxygen source. Each brake falls as O2 rises, so oxygen regulates itself near today's level.

| Brake | Acts on | Form | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fire | land burial | ignition `clamp(48 r - 9.08, 0, 5)` from the O2 mixing ratio r; factor `k / (k - 1 + ignition)` with k = 3 (1 at today's air, 1.5 with no fires, 0.43 at the cap) | COPSE reloaded (Lenton, Daines and Mills 2018, eq. 4; checked at source by the citations thread); the threshold behaviour matches Belcher et al. 2010 |
| Photorespiration | land burial | `1 + a (1 - r / r_pal)` with a = 0.5 (COPSE's 1.5 - 0.5 O2/O2_0), floored at 0 | COPSE (Bergman, Lenton and Watson 2004, eq. 4; checked) |
| Aerobic decomposers | land and marine burial | above today's air, `(1 + h) / (1 + h r / r_pal)` with h = 1; at or below today's air, 1 | Betts and Holland 1991, as summarised by Lenton and Watson 2000. The form and strength are a stand-in. |

**How burial is split.**
- `StepFluxes` gains `land_by_metabolism`, the production made in `land-surface` habitats, so burial is split into land and marine.
- The brakes read the step's end-of-step O2, so the bisection for the fixed point now also settles burial. More O2 means less burial means less O2, so the fixed point stays unique.

**Ledger.** It gains `burial_land` and `burial_suppressed`. Suppressed burial went back to the air as CO2, so no O2 moved and it is not part of the balance; the balance itself is unchanged.

**Series.** `AirPoint` gains `fire_factor`.

**What-if switches.** `fire_k`, `photorespiration` and `decomposer_h` set to 0 switch each brake off.

**Pack.** `packs/base-life` `life_feedback` gains the six settings and three sources.

## Fudges (for `docs/fudges.md` once it is on main)
- **The aerobic decomposer form** is a stand-in that only acts above today's air. Real physics would give burial efficiency from bottom-water O2 and sediment rates (Betts and Holland 1991), with a separate ocean O2 reservoir.
- The COPSE constants were checked at source (19 Nov): the fire constants are COPSE reloaded's (Lenton et al. 2018 eq. 4), not Bergman et al. 2004's (eq. 7 there is uncapped, 586.2 O - 122.102, with k_fire 100); the photorespiration term is Bergman et al. 2004 eq. 4. Betts and Holland's DOI is 10.1016/0921-8181(91)90123-E.
- **Replacement for the decomposer stand-in:** COPSE reloaded's f(O2) = 2.1276 exp(-0.755 O2/O2_0) on organic burial (eq. 35) is the published form; switching to it is a follow-up with a golden change.
- The ignition curve is for Earth's pressure and an N2 filler. On other air it should read O2 partial pressure and heat capacity, as design 7.3 says, and be labelled extrapolated. That is not built yet.

## Not built here (design 7.2, later E packages)
- Weathering suppression through phosphorus, which Lenton and Watson find the strongest regulator. It needs a phosphorus loop and E4 soil.
- A second, ocean O2 reservoir with the phosphorus feedback of Van Cappellen and Ingall 1996.
- Oxygen-using chemotrophs as generated sinks, which come with E1 and E2's gradient catalogue.
- Fire as a regional flux (ignition supply from weather and volcanism, moisture, fuel) and as a hazard: E6 and the weather track.
