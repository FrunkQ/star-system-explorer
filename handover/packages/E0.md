# E0: chemistries as data, the thermodynamic table, free-energy blocks

Evolution design (`design/SSE4-evolution-design.md`) sections 3 and 4, package
E0 of section 14. Built 21 Nov 2026 by the kernel thread.

## What it delivers
- **Two physics blocks** in `sse-atoms` (`chem.rs`), usable in any flow:
  - `sse.chem.standard_gibbs_at_temperature`: ΔG°(T) = ΔH° − T (ΔH° − ΔG°₂₉₈)/298.15 K. This is Gibbs-Helmholtz with a constant ΔH°.
  - `sse.chem.reaction_gibbs`: ΔG = ΔG° + RT ln Q.
  - The plain functions behind them (`standard_gibbs_at`, `reaction_gibbs`, `ln_quotient`) are what `sse-life` calls, so the blocks and the life suite share one formula.
  - The atom count is 64 (see P1.md). `packs/base-physics/pack.json` was re-blessed for the new quantity kinds.
- **The chemistry schema** (`sse-life/src/chemistry.rs`, `Chemistry`). Each chemistry holds:
  - solvent and the pressure below which it cannot be liquid;
  - scaffold element and temperature window;
  - activity property and its limit, plus an optional dose limit;
  - element recipe and build cost;
  - light bands and redox species;
  - one of Evolution Lab's six evidence tags (checked when the pack loads), an origin dial, and sources.
- `life_budget.chemistry` in `packs/base-life` now holds `carbon-water`, tagged *observed*. It carries the same window and limits as before, the Redfield recipe and the same build cost.
- **Box B's closure tests** now ask the chemistry that the habitat's solvent names (`chemistry::chemistry_for`). No code names a solvent any more; a test greps four source files to keep it that way.
  - Old packs and manifests still read. If `chemistry` is empty, the old `chemistries`, `window_k`, `water_triple_pa` and `min_water_activity` fields build a legacy chemistry.
  - The biosphere's fallback solvent comes from the pack.
- **The thermodynamic table** (`life_chemistry` in `packs/base-life`, `ThermoTable`):
  - 25 species, each with formula, charge, ΔG°f and ΔH°f.
  - 15 reference reactions, each with its electrons, a published ΔG and a source.
  - Every reaction is checked to balance in elements and charge when the pack loads.

## Verified by
- `every_reference_reaction_balances_and_matches_its_published_value`: all 15 reactions are within 5 percent of their published values. Most are within 0.3 percent, because the ΔG°f values come from the same table (Thauer et al. 1977). The pH 7 cases put the H+ activity at 10^-7, which gives −39.95 kJ against Thauer's −39.87.
- `the_redox_tower_comes_out_in_order`: per electron from H2 at pH 7, the order is O2 < NO3− < SO4²− < HCO3− (to CH4).
- `local_conditions_move_the_energy`:
  - methanogenesis gives up less energy when warm;
  - at 1e-5 bar H2 it falls below the 20 kJ quantum that E1 uses;
  - a zero activity is an error.
- `carbon_water_closures_are_unchanged`:
  - all five fixture worlds and Archean Earth give identical budgets under the new chemistry and the legacy fields;
  - each of the five closure reasons gives the same words as before.
- `a_chemistry_needs_an_honest_tag`, `no_code_names_a_solvent`.

## To verify (honesty)
- ΔH°f values are recalled from the NBS tables (Wagman et al. 1982), not re-read. They only matter away from 298.15 K.
- These published values are recalled and should be checked against the papers: Bock and Wagner 2006 (−275, ammonium to nitrite), Kelly 1999 (−798, sulfide to sulfate), and Thauer's −1120.5 for denitrification with H2. The citations thread may want to check them.
- The 20 kJ per mole minimum quantum (Schink 1997) belongs to E1, not here.
