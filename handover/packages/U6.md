# U6 part 1: the galaxy chart, under a new Explore tab (19 Nov 2026)

Build plan row U6: "Galaxy and patch charts, history inking, find tool". Galaxy design sections 2, 4, 7 and 9
(grown, never prebaked; rings; the green band; what died here). Second interface track ("Build galaxy charts and
travel screens"). Part 1 is the chart from the kernel's own galaxy model; inking (G6 events), the patch survey and
"find a home" are part 2.

## What was built
- **Kernel ops** in `crates/sse-kernel/src/cosmos.rs` (read only, Medium cost, on the open universe's seed):
  - `galaxy_chart {halo | mass_msun, at_gyr?, place_kpc?, place_phi?}` grows the galaxy with sse-cosmos
    (`Haloes::median`, `galaxy::evolve`, `rings::evolve`, `GalaxyEnvelope`) and returns the history, rings with
    abundances by element group and their dead (core collapse, type Ia, neutron-star mergers, AGB gas), arm crests
    (log spirals from the pattern speed and pitch, rotated to `at_gyr`), green bands every half Gyr, the cosmic
    epochs, the conditions and green verdict at a place, and the model's own limits. Named haloes: dwarf (1e11),
    milky-way-like (1e12), group (1e13); anything else is an error. Test: matches the G3 golden and repeats.
  - `web_slice {z | t_gyr, centre?, extent_mpc, n}` (for V9): a slice of the cosmic web (`Web::slice`), octaves
    capped at 4, n from 4 to 256. Test: contrast grows with time.
  - Contract cases added; `tests/golden/contract.jsonl` re-blessed on purpose (new ops only).
- **`ViewSession.galaxyChart` / `webSlice`** (GM only for now: they read the universe seed).
- **`ui-contract/src/galaxy.ts`**: types and the pure chart parts (disc frame, arm paths, ring shades by layer,
  green band at a time, log history curves over three decades, epoch ticks, foam cells for V9). No physics.
- **View `sse.view.galaxy-chart`**: galaxy picker, layers (metals, uranium/thorium/gold, stars, supernovae), the
  face-on disc (rings shaded, arms in ink, bar, green band dashed, the Sun's distance dot), a time slider (the
  arms turn and the lede reads the history at that time), the ring ledger (abundances against the Sun, what died
  here, conditions, the green verdict with each criterion) and history curves (stars, gas, star formation) with
  epochs and active-nucleus episodes.
- **View `sse.view.explore`** and the **Explore** tab: this track's one tab in the bar (agreed with the chart room
  thread), with an in-screen switch between Route and Galaxy (remembered per browser). Route moved under it.

## Honest limits
- The galaxy is the median halo for its mass, grown with the base cosmology pack; it is not yet this campaign's
  own galaxy placed by G7 (the Milky Way pack) nor the patch the system sits in.
- Arms are drawn as crests of a fixed pattern (density-wave picture); real arms are transient and ragged after
  mergers. G6 merger events are not yet inked.
- One-dimensional rings: no azimuthal structure in abundances.
