# U16: The Oort cloud as a sphere (night-sky Y0, drawing), belts on grown systems, and the desk switch fix (UI track)

Status: done 21 Nov 2026. This package is the chart room's half of stage Y0 in `/mnt/project-files/sse4/SSE4-night-sky-design.md` (sections 10 and 11; accepted by Alex on 21 Nov). The kernel thread owns the other half, the `shape` field. The package also answers three things raised the same afternoon:

- Alex noted that the Solar System's Oort cloud was drawn as a belt, when it "really should be a sphere".
- The kernel's PR 64 now sends a grown system's clouds in `resolved_system`.
- The trailer capture showed the desk switch reading "uild", with the B of Build hidden.

## What it does

- **A belt has a shape.** `SceneSubject.shape` is either `"disc"` or `"sphere"`.
  - It comes from the kernel's F5 tilt model: Rayleigh tilts make a disc, and isotropic tilts make a sphere. The kernel thread is adding `shape` and `i_sigma_deg` to each cloud, after its PR 68.
  - The kernel thread sends `"disc"` or `"sphere"`, while the design names them `ring`, `thick-disc` and `shell`. `cloudShape` reads both vocabularies.
  - Until then, `cloudShape` treats kind `"oort"` as a sphere and every other kind as a disc. The kernel will send the same answer.
  - V3 has no shape, so for a V3 belt `v3BeltShape` decides. A belt is a sphere when its name says "Oort", or when its inner edge lies beyond 1,000 AU. That far out, the Galaxy's tides have stirred every orbit's tilt.
- **The Oort cloud is drawn as a sphere round the system.**
  - **2D chart:** a soft disc out past the frame, faint inside the inner edge (the shell's near and far sides lie over the whole system) and stronger beyond it. It has a dotted inner edge and the "sphere" mark: "Oort cloud · sphere". The chart scale counts only the inner edge, so 100,000 AU does not crush the inner planets.
  - **3D view:** a sparse, round haze of points.
    - 420 points, spread evenly over the sphere by the golden angle and through the shell's depth by a second even sequence, so it always draws the same.
    - It begins just outside the outermost orbit.
    - The renderer gained `dots` (three.js `Points`, one pixel each) beside its orbit lines. The canvas tier draws the same points.
- **A V3 Oort belt imports as a sphere, and the import report says so.** For example: "Oort Cloud is drawn as a sphere round the system, not a ring: V3 gives a belt no shape…"
- **Belts in 3D.** Flat belts show as two dotted circles in the plane: the inner and outer edge.
- **Grown systems keep their belts after adoption.** `sceneFromResolved` now reads `clouds` from `resolved_system`. `cloudSubjects` makes the belts, the discs and the one Oort cloud.
  - F5 can fill a system's Oort cloud from several stirred belts. Those reservoirs are drawn as one shell spanning all of them.
  - Rings, Trojans and irregular moons belong to their worlds and are not drawn on the system chart.
  - The scattered disc is still left off the system chart, because it spans 30 to about 1,000 AU at a wide tilt. The design draws it as a thick disc; that is still to do.
- **The desk switch can no longer hide a letter.**
  - At rest, the chosen option paints its own ink. The sliding bar shows only while it travels, for one motion length.
  - The bar is measured from the buttons' boxes, and measured again whenever a button resizes or a web font arrives.
  - Cause: the bar was measured once. When the font swapped in (`font-display: swap`) or the display was scaled, the bar could stay wider than "Explore" and cover the B of "Build". This could not be reproduced headless here, so the fix removes the cause rather than tuning the measure.
- **The bar never overlaps itself.** A long place name now ends in "…", and the kicker gives way first. A grown system called Seed "cold harbour" used to run its title under the kicker.

## Checks

- **`make.test.ts`:**
  - A V3 save given an "Asteroid Belt" (2.1 to 3.3 AU) and an "Oort Cloud" (2,000 to 100,000 AU) imports them as a disc and a sphere.
  - The Oort cloud is a sphere, and the kernel's shape wins.
  - The Oort reservoirs merge into one shell.
  - Rings and the scattered disc are left off.
  - The V3 shape rule is covered.
- **`tactile.e2e.mjs`:** at 1920, 1536, 1440, 1280, 1100 and 390 px, and after each desk change, it checks every segmented switch:
  - No sliding ink shows at rest.
  - The ink is not over any other option's letters.
  - Every option's text stays inside its button, and the chosen option is inked.
  - Nothing in the bar overlaps anything else.
  - Forcing the old, too-wide ink makes the check fail on "Build" and "Science".
- **`adopt.e2e.mjs`:** growing "cold harbour" draws an Oort cloud as a sphere, by name.
- `npm run ui:e2e`, `npm run ui:check` and `scripts/check.sh` all pass. No goldens change.
