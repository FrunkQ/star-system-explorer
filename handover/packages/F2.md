## What was built (`crates/sse-formation/src/accrete.rs`)
- System Lab's random streams exactly: FNV-1a 32 over a length-framed path (`len:part|...`) seeding mulberry32, and
  Accrete's two-draw mean (`legacy_rand`). Streams `formation.accrete.nucleus-NNN.axis` and `.eccentricity`.
- The kernel line for line: dust cloud to 50 AU, density A exp(-5 r^(1/3)) with gas K / (1 + sqrt(Mc/M)(K - 1)) for
  giants, critical mass B rp^(-3/4), sweep and coalescence, up to 100 nuclei; System Lab's run length (90 percent of
  10 Gyr M^-2.5, at most 100 Myr) and events (nucleus injected, body accreted, bodies coalesced, formation complete).
  Controls are refused outside System Lab's slider ranges.
- What SSE4 adds: a provenance ledger per body (seed nucleus, every sweep with its radius, iteration, mass and the
  gas part of it, and whole merged bodies with their own ledgers), and `protoplanets()`, which hands each body to F6
  with its solids split by element group from the SSE4 disc band at each sweep's radius.

## Checks (`cargo test -p sse-formation`, 30 tests, 6 new in `accrete_tests.rs`)
- Fixture `tests/fixtures/formation/system-lab-accrete.json`: 206 runs made by System Lab's own `AccreteAdapter` under
  Node 22 (15 seeds, including System Lab's three snapshot seeds, by four star presets by four control sets). Its
  default solar run reproduces System Lab's committed vitest snapshot.
- Reproduction: all 206 runs give the same iteration count, dust exhaustion, body count, body ids, merge history,
  gas-giant flags, band count and band flags, and event count. 82 of 206 are bit-identical in every number; the rest
  differ by at most 6.7e-13 relative (worst over 610 bodies and every band edge). Explained: JavaScript's `Math.pow`
  and `Math.exp` (V8) and SSE4's pinned `libm` can differ in the last bit; the model repeats them thousands of times.
  SSE4 is itself bit-identical across devices (the harness below).
- The ledger sums exactly (bit for bit) to each body's mass, and every merged sub-ledger to the mass it merged with,
  over 60 runs; rocky bodies that never merged carry no gas.
- Hand-over: F6 runs on every body of the default solar run; solids plus gas equal the body's mass (to 1e-12).
- Random streams: FNV-1a vectors and mulberry32(1)'s first value as JavaScript gives it.

## Differences from Dole and Fogg, kept on purpose (`KNOWN_DIFFERENCES`)
1. One-sided partial sweeps (a nucleus reaching into a band from inside it takes nothing from it); System Lab records
   this as inherited and frozen until a versioned correction is approved.
2. Reach is 1 - q and 1 + q AU with q = (m/(1+m))^(1/4), not r q, so each nucleus reaches about 1 AU either side.
   This is why System Lab's default solar request makes three gas giants and no rocky planets.
3. Coalescence uses sqrt(sqrt(1 - e^2)) for the second body.
4. Nuclei are placed by the mean of two draws times the outer bound (a triangle peaking at 25 AU), not uniformly,
   so the inner system is rarely seeded (found while building the corrected rule).

## Corrected rule `accrete-dole1970-v1` (Alex, 18 Nov: "Correct accrete")
`Request { rule: Rule::Dole1970, .. }`; the default stays `Rule::SystemLab`, so System Lab parity, its fixture and
`formation-accrete.json` are unchanged (the field is not even written for System Lab requests).
- Undoes all four differences: reach rp (1 - q)/(1 + W) to ra (1 + q)/(1 - W); dust collected within the reach
  as Burrows's accrete and Fogg 1985 do (volume 4 pi a^2 q (1 - e (t1 - t2)/w) times the overlap width, iterated
  to a fixed point, then the lanes cleared once, gas too for giants), which also makes partial sweeps two-sided;
  coalescence with sqrt(1 - e^2); nuclei uniform between the bounds.
- Fogg's scalings with the star: nuclei 0.3 to 50 M^(1/3) AU, cloud to 200 M^(1/3) AU, critical mass
  B (rp sqrt(L))^-3/4, dust density A sqrt(M). Runs until the planet zone's dust is gone (cap 5,000 nuclei).
- **Driven by the galaxy**: `Request::from_history(&FormationHistory, seed)` takes the star from F0 and the metals
  from the inheritance (G0 birth facts or a patch envelope): A = 0.0015 sqrt(M) 10^[Fe/H], K = 50 / 10^[Fe/H]
  (more dust, same gas). The hand-off `protoplanets(run, system, Some(&history.disc))` splits each body's solids
  by the F1 bands, which were built from the same inheritance ([Fe/H], [alpha/Fe], C/O, cluster FUV).
- Checks: 40 Sun runs give 66 rocky worlds inside 2 AU and 113 giants (System Lab's rule, same seeds: 3 rocky);
  ledgers still sum bit for bit; +0.3 dex gives over twice the planet mass of -0.5 dex; every G0 solar-patch
  request carries its own [Fe/H] and star. New golden `formation-accrete-dole.json` (8 G0 solar-patch systems),
  in the harness as `formation-accrete-dole`; Node and Chromium identical to native.
- Not yet read by any planet block: Al-26, K-40 and r-process abundances and Mg/Si are inherited but no stage
  uses them yet (radiogenic heating belongs with F6 and the surface suite); and `FormationHistory` does not yet
  run stages 4 to 8 itself, callers chain growth (F2 or F3), F4, F5 and F6 on the history's disc.

## Known limits
- Single star only, as System Lab; companions and the SSE4 disc's own mass budget do not yet constrain it (the dust
  cloud is Dole's, not F1's). F3 brings the SSE4-native growth block that reads F1's bands.
- Body composition from the ledger uses F1's band mix at each sweep radius; with no disc, the rock-band mix.
