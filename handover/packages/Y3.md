# Y3: the glow map (part 1: the sphere grid; part 2: the glow)

Night sky design (`design/SSE4-night-sky-design.md`) section 5, stage Y3. Light track.

## What was built

- New crate `sse-sky`, module `healpix`: the HEALPix equal-area grid in the RING scheme, written fresh from
  Górski et al. 2005 (ApJ 622, 759, section 4, doi:10.1086/427976), not from the GPL reference library. The
  reference was checked on 22 Nov (the design had it marked "from memory").
  - `Grid::new(nside)` (power of two, 1 to 8192), `npix`, `pixel_area`.
  - `centre_z_phi`, `centre`, `centre_vec`: pixel centre as (cos θ, φ), (θ, φ) or a unit vector.
  - `pixel_z_phi`, `pixel`, `pixel_vec`: the pixel holding a direction.
  - Integer arithmetic plus `sqrt`, `floor` and the pinned `acos`/`atan2`, so every device gives the same
    pixel.
- Nside 32 (12,288 pixels) is the design's fact resolution.

## Checks (`crates/sse-sky/src/tests.rs`)

- Known nside 1 centres: pixel 0 at z = 2/3, φ = π/4; pixel 4 on the equator at φ = 0; pixel 11 at z = −2/3,
  φ = 7π/4.
- Every pixel's centre maps back to that pixel, by angle and by vector, at nside 1, 2, 4, 8, 32 and 64.
- Ring counts: 4 nside − 1 rings, 4i pixels on cap ring i, 4 nside in the belt.
- Equal area: 768,000 evenly spread directions fill the 768 pixels of nside 8 to within 3 % each.
- Edge directions (both poles, φ = 2π) land on valid pixels.

## Part 2: the glow (op `sky_glow`)

- `sse-cosmos::glow_mix`: `Stars::light_points(r_kpc, t_myr)`, the light of a place's stars in expectation.
  Same quadrature as Y1's class shares (512 IMF points, finest in the bright tail, by 48 log-age bins of the
  birth history at that radius); each point carries its share of the systems (summing to 1), its class,
  luminosity and temperature.
- `sse-sky::glow`: `glow(stars, sky_params, settings, observer_pc, t_myr, limit_mag, limit_band, bands,
  pc)` gives a `GlowMap` (per band per RING pixel: I_λ at the band's effective wavelength, W m⁻² m⁻¹ sr⁻¹;
  per pixel: total A_V; the class walk radii).
  - I_λ = (1/4π) ∫ n(s) Σ w L_λ e^(−τ_λ) ds along each pixel's centre line, 96 log-spaced steps from 1 pc to
    30 kpc (`GlowSettings`).
  - No double counting, keyed on the draw's own rule. `class_radii` repeats the draw's walk radius exactly
    (the tests compare them). A class's light goes in the glow beyond its radius; inside it, only the members
    fainter than the limit at that distance through the dust in front (each class's points are sorted by
    absolute magnitude with suffix sums, so the cut is one binary search).
  - Dust is the draw's column (gas times 10^[Fe/H] over `gas_per_av_msun_pc2`), spread over wavelength by
    `sse.light.dust_extinction` (CCM89, R_V 3.1); light at a step is dimmed by the dust in front of its middle.
  - `mag_per_arcsec2_ab` turns I_λ into AB magnitudes per square arcsecond.
- Kernel op `sky_glow {position_pc?, t_myr?, limit_mag? (6.5), band? (V), bands? (B, V, Ks), nside? (32,
  power of two up to 64)}` returns `sky_glowed {glow: {nside, order "ring", frame, limit_mag, limit_band,
  observer_pc, t_myr, radii_pc, bands: [{band, intensity, mag_arcsec2 (Vega), whole_sky_ab,
  whole_sky_mag}], a_v, units}}`. Pixel axes are galactic: x toward the galaxy's centre (l = 0), z toward
  its north (b = 90). Use `sse_sky::glow::pixel_of_lb` (or the same HEALPix maths in TS) to look up a
  direction. Cost Medium: about 13 s for nside 32 in a test build, so ask nside 8 or 16 for a quick look.
- Fudge F36 (`docs/fudges.md`).

### Checks (`crates/sse-kernel/src/glow.rs`)

- The north galactic pole reads V 24.1 mag/arcsec² (Leinert et al. 1998 give about 23 to 24 for the
  integrated starlight there); the two poles agree within 0.5.
- The plane beats the poles by more than a magnitude; the centre beats the anticentre.
- Dust: along the midplane Ks gains far more toward the centre over the anticentre than V does (V sees only
  the near disc), and the midplane lane is deeper in V than in Ks. A_V straight through the centre is over 5.
- No double counting: drawn stars (`sky_from_here`) plus the glow give the same whole-sky V light for a
  draw to V 2 and to V 4, within 5 %; the glow's radii equal the draw's.
- Bad asks (negative time, limit over 30, unknown band, nside 6 or 128) are refused.
- Contract cases: an nside 4 map and a refused nside; the golden changed on purpose for the new op.

Sample (nside 8, limit V 6, seed g3), Vega mag/arcsec²: pole V 24.1, Ks 22.1; l 0 b −8 V 21.8, Ks 18.2;
l 180 b 0 V 22.8, Ks 19.9. A_V through the midplane to 30 kpc toward the centre is 134.
