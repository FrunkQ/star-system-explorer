# U2: system chart editor (edit in place, cards pinned to bodies, why on every number)

Status: done (18 Nov 2026), branch `claude/project-thread-adgixl`, on top of P1 to P4 and the kernel's base-physics wiring (00db0e7).

## What was built
- Real physics on the card: `ui-contract/src/derivation.ts` assembles the scene. It applies the session's authored values, then asks the kernel to derive what `packages/shell/src/documents/derivations.base.json` names: star main sequence (mass and the ruler's age), planet bulk, orbit basics, planet climate (fed by the star's derived luminosity now). Which flow serves which object is data, so physics changes are edits to that document or the pack, never to views. Only unit scaling happens in the UI.
- Session gains `derive`, `explain` and the pack stack at open (`stack.json` lists `packs/base-physics.json`, copied from `packs/base-physics/pack.json` at build).
- Editing: type any settable value in place (mass, radius, orbit, eccentricity, albedo, greenhouse τ, name); drag a body along its radius to move its orbit (ghost ring and value while dragging, one intent on release); Page Up or Page Down on a focused body moves it 5 % out or in. Derived values re-derive after every edit and when the ruler moves (the star's luminosity depends on age).
- Undo and redo (`ui-contract/src/edit.ts`): Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Shift+Z or Ctrl+Y, and tool-rail buttons. Each is a further set_value intent, so the chronicle only grows, like a correction inked on a chart.
- "Why?" on every number: derived values open the kernel's explanation (formula, value, Curious, Science and Engine lenses, inputs with their sources, parameters, references, out-of-range and fictional stamps), and a derived input's "why?" follows the chain with a trail back. Set and fixture values say where they came from.
- Fields carry "set", "derived" and "out of range" marks.

## Fixture changes, and why
Real physics disagreed with the invented mockup numbers, so the inputs moved rather than the physics: Kestrel 0.78 to 0.82 M☉ (the main-sequence relation makes it a K4-ish dwarf at 4612 K, so the subtitle now says "K dwarf", not "K2 V"); Tarn 0.63 to 0.52 AU with greenhouse τ 1.1, so it is temperate (291 K). The future event "Kestrel leaves the main sequence" was already replaced in U1, since a K dwarf lives tens of billions of years.
