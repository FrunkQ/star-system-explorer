# O9 (kernel side, first slice): sensing by band

Design: `SSE4-nebulae-and-light-design.md` section 5A; stage O9 in section 8. Crate `sse-sense`; kernel op `detect`.
Construct sensor data (H7A part 7), contacts on the disclosure ladder and seat bands (O8) are for later packages.

## What was built
- `detect(seen, sensor, table) -> Detection`. The target's light (any `LightDescription`: ships from `emit`
  conditions with radiators and transmitters, stars, planets, nebulae) is integrated over the sensor's band, thinned
  by 4πd² and dimmed by dust (A_V scaled as (λ/0.55 µm)^−1.75, nothing in radio). Neighbours inside the sensor's
  resolution (default the diffraction limit 1.22λ/D) add their light to the noise (glare).
  - Light bands: photon counting, S/N = S t / √((S + B + D) t) with diffuse background radiance and dark counts.
  - Radio: radiometer equation, S/N = (P / k T_sys B) √(B t), with glare added to T_sys.
  - Outputs: flux, dust dimming, S/N, detectable, the stare needed for the threshold, the range at which this stare
    reaches the threshold (solved exactly), lost in glare, Why.
- `radar_ranges(radar)`: how far the echo reveals a target (∝ R⁻⁴) against how far anyone in the beam hears the
  pulse with the same receiver (∝ R⁻²).
- Kernel op `detect { targets: [{ address, conditions | light, distance_m, extinction_av?, neighbours? }],
  sensors: [Sensor], radar? }` → `detected { detections (target by sensor), radar? }`; error `bad_sensing`.

## Evidence (`cargo test -p sse-sense`, 6 tests)
- A ship with 2000 m² of 600 K radiators is detected by a 1 m mid-infrared telescope at 2 au in a minute; at the
  computed range S/N is the threshold to 10⁻⁶; a cold ship's range is under a tenth of the hot one's, and the stated
  stare reaches the threshold exactly.
- In front of its star (inside the resolution) the hot ship is lost in the glare in visible light.
- Behind A_V = 30 the ship vanishes in visible light (over 25 magnitudes) but loses under 0.3 magnitudes at 8–14 µm.
- A 10 kW S-band transmitter is heard by a 10 m dish at 1 au in a minute (S/N about 16); a silent ship is not.
- A radar is heard over a hundred times further than it sees; 16× power doubles its seeing range and quadruples
  its heard range.
- The Sun at 10 pc is easily seen by a 0.5 m visible camera.

## Stand-ins (to add to docs/fudges.md)
- Dust law is one power law from 0.3 µm to 1 mm, held flat below 0.3 µm, absent in radio and X-rays.
- Glare is all-or-nothing inside the resolution element (no diffraction halo or coronagraph).
- Photon energy taken at the band's geometric centre; detector dark counts and background are inputs, not modelled.
- Radar listener assumed in the main beam with the same antenna; sidelobes not modelled.
