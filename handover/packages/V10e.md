# V10e: Mag through 3D and True, every world's magnetosphere at system scale

Alex, 23 Nov 2026: "x-ray - IR, magnetosphere - seeing as REAL and diagrammatic", and "One set of controls and similar levels of visualisation at EVERY scale".

## What it does

- The system chart's Band now offers **Mag** (it used to send you to a world). In 3D and True each world with field facts gets its magnetosphere drawn round it:
  - its traced field lines, orange where the field points away from the world and blue where it points in;
  - the wind's bubble (the magnetopause) as three faint outlines (through the poles, along the equator and round its waist), its nose toward the star.
- The facts and the tracer are the Body screen's Mag layer's: `read_field` (Solar System fixture until MF2 publishes a field for every world) and `traceField`. The body frame puts x toward the star and z out of the orbital plane, as `fieldFromRead` does.
- Mag replaces the picture: worlds are dark grey (a little lighter when they have a field), and the star is a dim grey (its own field is not drawn yet). The living star's face is not drawn in Mag.
- **Real** draws every field at its true size against true distances (Jupiter's bubble shows as a ring a few hundredths of an AU across).
- **Readable** enlarges the worlds, so each field is drawn to its world's enlarged size and stopped short of the nearest other body drawn (fudge register: "Mag reach in Readable").
- The note (`data-true-band="mag"`, `data-mag-worlds`) says how many worlds have fields, what the inks mean and which scale rule applies. A system with no field facts says so, and the canvas tier says the lines need the GPU tier.
- The flat Chart style is unchanged in every band, as it was for Infrared and X-ray.

## Code

- New `renderer/src/mag-true.ts`:
  - `magShape(read)` traces a world once (12 seeds a hemisphere, 4 meridians, a few ms) and splits lines where the field turns round.
  - `placeMag(shape, centre, radius, sunward, reach)` turns it into the view's frame and cuts it at the reach.
- `system-3d/System3D.svelte`: `fields` prop, `magShapes` (traced once per change of facts), `magRings`, dark emit in Mag, the note. `starK` reads `constant("T_sun")`.
- `system-chart/SystemChart.svelte`: reads the Solar System fixture once per page in 3D and True with Mag on, matched by name. `controls.ts`: `mag: true`.

## Checks

- `renderer/test/mag-true.test.ts` (3 tests):
  - both inks and open lines, everything inside the reach, the nose at the stand-off;
  - no field gives no shape, and an ionosphere obstacle gives no bubble;
  - placed: the nose faces the star, sizes scale with the drawn world, and the reach cuts lines.
- `mag.e2e`: on the desk, the system chart in 3D with Mag draws at least 5 worlds' fields (6 in the Solar System); True with Real says "true size". `system3d.e2e` passes.
