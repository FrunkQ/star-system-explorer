# O3: nebulae from causes (first slice)

Design: `SSE4-nebulae-and-light-design.md` section 3 (kinds from causes; fact above, decoration below; on the
time ruler); stage O3 in section 8.

## What was built
- Five cited atoms in `sse-atoms::nebula` (editable blocks):
  - `sse.nebula.ionising_photon_rate`: photons above 13.6 eV from a photosphere (exact series of the Planck
    photon integral, 400 fixed terms).
  - `sse.nebula.stromgren_radius`: R = (3Q / 4π n² α_B)^⅓, α_B = 2.59e-13 cm³/s at 10⁴ K.
  - `sse.nebula.balmer_alpha_power`: case B, 0.45 Hα photons per recombination (L(Hα) = 1.37e-19 W per s⁻¹).
  - `sse.nebula.shell_radius`: R = v t (interacting winds, constant-speed shell).
  - `sse.nebula.remnant_radius`: free expansion → Sedov-Taylor (ξ = 1.1517) → Cioffi, McKee and Bertschinger's
    pressure-driven snowplough (t_PDS = 1.33e4 E₅₁^(3/14) n₀^(-4/7) yr, R_PDS = 14.0 E₅₁^(2/7) n₀^(-3/7) pc).
- Crate `sse-nebula`: a nebula is a dated object with a `Cause` (event, source body, date), `born`/`fades` dates,
  a phase, shells (radius, speed, thickness, mass), optional lobes (axis, length ratio), elongation, the
  surroundings' density, the lighting bodies, a decoration seed, its light (`sse_light::LightDescription`), why,
  stand-ins and references. Three kinds so far:
  - **Planetary nebula** from a star's death (`Death` → `PnInput::from_death`: envelope = initial − remnant mass,
    central star at 10⁵ K with Nauenberg's white dwarf radius). Shell speed drawn 15–35 km/s; fades when the
    shell is only 10 times denser than its surroundings; lit fully or partly by the central star's ionising
    photons against the shell's recombinations; Hα and Hβ from recombination. Shed layers: earlier pulses of the
    slow giant wind as halo rings, spaced 500–1500 years. A companion or fast spin gives two lobes along the
    companion's orbit normal (or a drawn axis).
  - **Supernova remnant** (core collapse or thermonuclear): radius and speed by phase, swept mass, merges when
    slowed to 10 km/s (bisection, 200 fixed steps), the collapsed core at its centre.
  - **Star-forming glow** round hot stars in a cloud: Strömgren sphere, Hα and Hβ, dark when the last hot star dies.
  - `expected_count` and `expected_remnants`: steady-state numbers in a volume = cause rate × lifetime, for the
    sse-cosmos patch rates (`cc_sn_gyr_pc3`, `ia_sn_gyr_pc3`).
- Kernel op `read_nebulae { at_s, seed?, deaths?, planetary?, remnants?, glows? }` →
  `nebulae_read { at_s, nebulae, absent }` (descriptors; addresses not existing at that date). Cost light.

## Evidence (`cargo test -p sse-nebula`, 9 tests; `cargo test -p sse-atoms`, 3 new)
- Photon series equals a 200,000-step numerical integration to 10⁻⁴ at 20, 40 and 100 kK.
- Strömgren: Q = 10⁴⁹ s⁻¹ in 100 cm⁻³ gives 3.15 pc (Osterbrock and Ferland); Hα = 1.37e-19 W per photon s⁻¹.
- Remnant phases: R ∝ t^0.4 in the Sedov phase to 10⁻⁹, equal to 1.1517 (E t²/ρ)^⅕; the snowplough starts at
  R_PDS = 14.0 pc for E = 10⁴⁴ J, n = 1 cm⁻³ (and the Sedov radius meets it there); merges after 0.1 to 5 Myr
  (2.0 Myr for that case); denser surroundings stop it sooner and smaller.
- A ring nebula's shell radius equals age × speed exactly, doubles when the age doubles, and the nebula lasts
  10 to 40 thousand years (28.6 kyr in the golden case); none before the death, none after it fades.
- A companion gives lobes on its axis; halo rings always lie outside the bright shell.
- A late O dwarf (37,000 K, 8 R☉) in 100 cm⁻³ gas ionises a sphere of a few pc; dark after the star dies.
- Counts scale exactly with the rates and equal rate × volume × lifetime.
- The Sun's death gives a nebula addressed `<star>/nebula:0`, tied to the star and dated on the event.
- Nauenberg: 0.6 M☉ white dwarf ≈ 0.0125 R☉.
- Golden `tests/golden/nebulae.json` (new: three descriptors byte for byte). `tests/golden/contract.jsonl`
  re-blessed on purpose for two new `read_nebulae` lines, the op list, the five new atoms and the base-physics
  pack gaining their kinds.

## Not done, and why
- **Colours beyond hydrogen** (oxygen teal, nitrogen and sulphur red, helium): O4's line-strength grid.
  Remnants publish no light yet (shocked-gas lines, X-ray interior, radio shell): O4.
- **Patch envelopes**: `expected_remnants` gives the counts; wiring them into `sse-cosmos` patch envelopes, and a
  white dwarf birth rate for planetary nebula counts, is for the kernel track (asked).
- **Ties to the time track**: `Death` carries what a `planetary_nebula` event knows (star, date, initial and
  remnant mass); reading the events straight from an ageing run, and supernova events from B3, comes when the
  screen asks.
- Dark clouds, reflection nebulae, wind bubbles, superbubbles, nova shells and other binary remains: later O3
  slices (nova shells after B4 binaries).
- The central star does not cool; uniform surroundings; no blister, pillars or dust (listed in each descriptor's
  `stand_ins`).
