# W2: formations on rocky and ocean worlds, drawn storm systems, weather report (19 Nov 2026)

Weather design section 2 step 2 and section 5. Crate `sse-weather`: new `formations.rs`; `rules.rs` wires it in; pack key
`provides.other.weather_formations` (settings with sources).

## What was built
- Drawn systems (`formations::systems`), keyed by world address, stream and day of birth, so a storm has the same id and
  whole track whichever date it is asked from:
  - mid-latitude cyclones on every storm track, wavelength 3.9 N H / f (Eady 1949), travelling east with the westerlies,
    stronger in winter;
  - tropical cyclones only over sea warmer than 26 °C, at least 5° from the equator, on worlds whose spin organises them
    (Emanuel 2003), with seasons from the warm area and tracks west then recurving;
  - dust storms on dry thin-air worlds, planet-encircling in some years in the dust season (Zurek and Martin 1993);
  - star-locked worlds: a permanent storm under the star and twin standing gyres (Yang, Cowan and Abbot 2013; Showman and
    Polvani 2011); a winter polar vortex where there are seasons.
- Formations placed by rule in each cell (`formations::imprint`): the day-side cloud shield and clear night on locked worlds;
  a marine stratocumulus layer over sea under sinking subtropical air; cloud banked on windward slopes with a dry lee and cap
  clouds on peaks when terrain is given (`Terrain`, implemented for `sse_surface::atlas::Atlas`; Roe 2005); cyclone comma
  clouds and winds; tropical cyclone cores; dust; lightning from cloud-top height (Price and Rind 1992); snow where freezing.
- `RuleBlock::field_with` (field reading terrain), `systems_at` (pins honoured: a pinned system replaces the drawn one of the
  same id and lives for its recorded life), `cell_at`, `report` (plain lines: wind, sky, what falls, aerosols, visibility,
  thunder, an approaching storm front or cyclone in the next day, systems nearby).

Real Earth: about 80 to 90 tropical cyclones a year, storm tracks 40 to 60°.
Report at 45° N on day 200: "Wind 87 km/h from the north-west. Sky mostly cloudy. Rain 6 mm a day. Visibility 16 km".

## Checks (`cargo test -p sse-weather`, 16 tests)
Earth storms in the right latitudes and numbers; no tropical cyclones on a dry Earth, a cold Earth, Mars, Venus or Titan;
a storm identical from two dates; a ridge banks rain windward (more than twice the lee); locked day side clouded, night
clear; pins replace drawn systems and outlive them; reports. Golden `tests/golden/weather-storms.json` (new). Golden
`weather.json` field digests changed on purpose (fields now carry formations); every climate digest is unchanged, so the
averages slow physics reads did not move.

## Candidates to move into flows
Genesis conditions (sea temperature, latitude, regime) are logic-block material; the Eady wavelength and Price and Rind
flash rate are atoms. Kept in Rust because they run per cell and per day.

## Known limits
- No geography of currents, so marine decks sit over any subtropical sea, not just the east sides of basins. Monsoon comes
  only from the rain belt following the sun more over land; no land-sea pressure pattern.
- Systems do not see terrain: tropical cyclones are placed over the sea only statistically; the field weakens them over land.
- Seasonal snow line and frost follow the latitude temperature profile, not a ground model.

## Integration

- Hazards and descent (S5): `report` and the field's layers (turbulence, lightning, visibility).
