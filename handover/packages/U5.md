# U5: packs screen, data views screen, screens composer

Status: done (18 Nov 2026), branch `claude/project-thread-adgixl`. Packs and the composer landed first on fixture scenes; the data views screen and live scenes followed once the kernel track added `scene`, `scan_toggle`, `stack.adopt` (commit 862dfa7) and the numbers package (d773847), both merged here.

## What was built
- **Stack model in the session** (`ui-contract/src/session.ts`): the stack is opening packs, then packs added in this campaign, then the campaign's own physics pack (`campaign.physics`, U3). `stack()` returns `{base, added, campaign, hash}`. `trial(candidate, probe)` loads a candidate stack inside the serial queue, lets the probe derive under it, and restores the stack in force. U3's physics preview now uses the same call.
- **`stack_packs` intent** (`intent.ts`, `seat.ts`): replaces the added packs and keeps the campaign pack on top. Non-GM seats get `ask_gm`, as for `adopt_physics`.
- **Packs screen** (`packages/views/pack-stack`, PackSetup board): the live stack, top to bottom, with role, kinds, flows, quantity kinds and declared `replaces`. "Check stack" asks the kernel. Adding a pack (a file, or the sample `sample.warm-worlds` fictional pack) or removing one shows a preview: every derived value that changes, before and after, labelled fictional where a fictional pack touches it. Accepting sends one `stack_packs` intent. Removing a pack that the campaign pack's `replaces` names is refused with the kernel's reason. Helpers live in `ui-contract/src/packs-edit.ts` (`packSummary`, `previewStack`, `stackPacks`, `checkPackDoc`).
- **Fictional labels through to the card**: derived facts carry `fictional` when their provider pack is fictional; the subject gets the Fictional stamp and the card field reads "· fictional".
- **Screens composer** (`packages/views/screen-composer`, Compose board): the finite view stack (five live, the rest marked "later"); the player screen document as a list of items (view, area, params as JSON) checked by the same `checkScreen` the player host uses; a live preview of the screen drawn with the real player views on K9's scenes, with the Long range / Scanned toggle; Show document and Download; a sample Orac screen request ("Bridge approach") validated against the stack before Accept or Decline; and the packs the screen draws on.

## Second half: live scenes and the data views screen
- **Session** (`ui-contract/src/session.ts`):
  - `scene(scope, {audience?, data_view?, at_yr?})` returns the kernel's scene. A GM seat may ask for any audience and look through any data view; any other seat gets only its own audience.
  - New intent `scan_toggle {address, audience, at_yr}` is the GM's Scanned button. It applies for the GM; other seats get `ask_gm`.
  - Stack changes are now chronicled by the kernel (`stack.adopt`): `adopt_physics` and `stack_packs` return the event id, and the state hash moves.
  - **Trials moved to a scratch kernel.** Loading a candidate stack into the open universe would now be chronicled, so `trial()` loads it on a second kernel with no universe open (`Session.open(..., scratch)`; `host-browser.ts` starts it on first use). Previews chronicle nothing; the node tests assert this.
- **The campaign's player data** (`shell/src/documents/`): `kestrel.player-data.pack.json` (the disclosure map and four data views, from the kernel's Tarn fixture) is in the opening stack. `kestrel.player-facts.json` (`sse.authored-facts/0`) is the GM's recorded player-facing facts, entered as authored edits at year 0 when the campaign opens (`enterFacts`). The ruler inks them at year 0.
- **Data views screen** (`packages/views/data-views`, key "Player data", PlayerData board):
  - The views list with each one's role (default, before and after Scanned).
  - A view editor: label, description, tiers shown (the GM-only tier can never be ticked), tags none, listed or all, and how unknowns read.
  - The Scanned button's pair, and the disclosure map (field or tag to tier, add a field).
  - Edits are a draft, checked by `checkSetup`. Apply stacks them as the campaign's own `campaign.player-data` pack, as one chronicled `stack_packs`. Put back re-applies the previous setup.
  - Preview: the live scene for an audience through their own grants or any view, drawn with the real player Card and Table, plus the GM's Scanned button for the body in focus.
  - Helpers are in `ui-contract/src/data-views.ts`.
- **Player page live:** `players.html` hosts its own kernel with the campaign stack and recorded facts and draws the kernel's `everyone` scene. Its Scanned control is a stand-in for the GM's button and sends `scan_toggle` for the body in focus. The fixture scenes are gone from the build.
- **Composer live:** the preview draws the kernel's scenes through the campaign's before and after views (read only, no grants changed).
