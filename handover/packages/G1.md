# G1: cosmology, the cosmic web as a function, epochs

Status: done (19 Nov 2026). Kernel track (cosmos suite), branch `claude/project-thread-dzkz4p`, PR into `main`.
Design: galaxy design sections 2, 3 and 12 (stage G1).

## What was built
In `sse-cosmos` (new modules; G0's code untouched):
- `cosmology.rs`: flat Lambda-CDM with radiation (photons plus N_eff neutrinos). E(a); cosmic time t(a) by fixed
  16-point Gauss-Legendre quadrature in sqrt(a); a(t) by fixed 80-step bisection; the closed form for matter plus
  Lambda and its sinh inverse (the atoms); growth D(a) by Carroll, Press and Turner 1992 and the exact Heath 1977
  integral as its check; the Eisenstein and Hu 1998 no-wiggle transfer function; P(k) normalised to sigma8;
  sigma(R) with a top-hat window; sigma(M) tabulated once per parameter set (1e3 to 1e17 solar masses, 0.05 dex);
  collapse redshift of a nu-sigma peak. erfc by Abramowitz and Stegun 7.1.26 (`sse_core::math` has none).
- Atoms `sse.cosmo.time_of_scale`, `sse.cosmo.scale_of_time`, `sse.cosmo.growth`, `sse.cosmo.hubble_rate`
  (`sse_cosmos::cosmology::atoms()`), cited, with explain text.
- `epochs.rs`: recombination (Hu and Sugiyama 1996 z*), dark ages, first stars (3-sigma 1e6 solar-mass minihaloes),
  reionisation half way and complete (Q = zeta times the Press-Schechter collapsed fraction above the 1e4 K cooling
  mass), cosmic noon (peak of the Madau and Dickinson 2014 fit), now, and the speculative far future (last stars,
  degenerate era, black hole era with the proton-decay switch, dark era from Hawking evaporation of the largest black
  hole). Epochs on the chronicle's clock are recorded as derived `cosmo.epoch` events with their "why".
- `web.rs`: delta0(x) as band-limited octaves of keyed normal draws (stream `cosmos.web.o<j>.v1`, one per octave,
  counter from the lattice index) with tricubic Catmull-Rom interpolation (64 values per octave), amplitudes set so
  octave j adds sigma^2(R_j) - sigma^2(R_j-1); lognormal density; potential per octave -delta_j / k_j^2; one
  Zel'dovich step; the tidal tensor by central differences and T-web classification (fixed Jacobi eigenvalues);
  `slice()` returns a foam-view slice (density, environment, displaced positions) as data for V9. A lattice cache
  lives only for one batch; nothing is stored.
- Pack `packs/base-cosmology` (`provides.other.cosmology`: params, web, epochs, sources) and
  `scenario::CosmologyPack::from_packs`.

- Age 13.786 Gyr (Planck 2018: 13.787); Omega_r 9.14e-5; z* 1091.8 (Planck 1089.8; the fit is good to about 2);
  age at z* 371 kyr. Closed form within 0.5 percent of the radiation-aware time below z = 10 (0.04 percent today);
  inverses round-trip; t proportional to a^1.5 in the matter era.
- Growth: CPT92 within 0.2 percent of the exact integral at a = 0.1 to 1.
- Spectrum: sigma8 reproduced; T(k) to 1 on large scales and ln k / k^2 on small; sigma(1e12) = 2.21; slope at
  1e14 solar masses -0.21; sigma(M) strictly falls.
- Epochs in order: first stars z = 17.3; reionisation half way z = 7.67 (Planck 7.7 +- 0.7), complete z = 6.16;
  cosmic noon z = 1.86; seven epochs fit the chronicle's clock, the rest are speculative and beyond it.
- Web (2000 points in a 640 Mpc box): variance of the generated field against sigma^2 at three scales (32, 8 and
  2 Mpc spacings): 0.297 vs 0.305, 1.849 vs 1.883, 6.67 vs 6.68 (within 3 percent); correlation 0.99, 0.71, 0.06
  at 2, 10, 40 Mpc. (With 4000 points over 2 Gpc, within 1.2 percent; cut for test time.)
- T-web fractions: void 0.070, sheet 0.408, filament 0.424, knot 0.097 (Doroshkevich 1970 for a Gaussian field:
  0.08, 0.42, 0.42, 0.08; with 4000 points 0.079, 0.420, 0.419, 0.082).
- Foam grows: density spread 0.28 at a = 0.1 and 12.6 today on the same slice; reading again and in another order
  gives identical values.
- Atoms: each cited; time of scale 13.79 Gyr; round trip; growth matches; H0 returned exactly; bad input refused.

## Known limits
- The octave noise is band-limited lattice noise, not a Gaussian random field with the exact spectrum: variance
  per band is matched, the shape inside a band is the kernel's. Good for maps, halo bias and T-web; a heavy block
  can replace it.
- The potential per octave uses one wavenumber (the band's centre), so displacements and tidal tensors are
  approximate within a band.
- The cosmology pack's Omega_L is not a parameter: the universe is flat with radiation (Omega_L = 1 - Omega_m -
  Omega_r). The atoms take Omega_L and allow curvature.
- Far-future epochs (beyond about 292 Gyr) are not on the chronicle's clock (universe time is i64 seconds); they
  are returned with log10 years only.
