# P4: regions, layers and features on, in and above worlds

Status: done (18 Nov 2026). Build-plan check "Tarn fixture reproduces the Strata mockup's stack" passes.

## What was built
- Four atoms in `sse-atoms` (`src/layers.rs`), cited, with explain text. The library is now **31 atoms**.
  - `sse.ocean.brine_freezing_point`: freezing-point depression (Blagden, van 't Hoff factor, osmotic coefficient).
  - `sse.heat.ice_shell_heat_flow` and `sse.heat.ice_shell_thickness`: conduction through ice Ih with k = 651/T.
  - `sse.interior.hydrostatic_pressure`: ΔP = ρ g h.
  - The base-physics pack was re-blessed for the new atoms (`SSE_BLESS=1`); no flow golden changed.
- Crate `sse-world`: the world stack (`sse.world-stack/1` JSON).
  - **Shells** by altitude (metres from datum, up positive), global or `within` a region.
  - **Regions** `on` a shell, with an extent (global, polar cap, latitude band, unmapped) and optional altitude span.
  - **Features** `in` a region, shell or feature, at an altitude, with optional latitude and longitude.
  - Every object has `properties` (numbers by name) and `provides` (heat, habitat, dynamo...).
  - `address_of(key)`: `<body>/shell:@key`, `region:@key`, `feature:@key` (handoff H5.1 segments).
  - `validate()`: every problem, not the first: duplicate keys, unknown containers, overlapping global shells,
    features outside their region's span, contained features above their container, latitudes off the globe.
  - `ladder()`: the Strata "above, on and within" strip, top to bottom, with depth text (U+2212 minus, thousands
    commas), what, and where.
  - `physics_notes()`: checks the stack with the atoms. A brine ocean colder than its freezing point, or an ice shell
    with no warmer base, is reported as a problem, never hidden.
- Fixture `tests/fixtures/worlds/tarn.json`: Tarn from the Strata mockup (`SSE4 mockups`, LookStrata). City, biome and
  plate names are invented; the mockup only counts them.

## Checks (`cargo test -p sse-world`, `cargo test -p sse-atoms`)
- Tarn's ladder equals the mockup's nine lines exactly. One documented difference: the mockup writes the ocean as
  "−3 to −11"; the ladder adds "km".
- The brine ocean (12 percent salt, 266 K) freezes at 265.1 K by the atom, so it stays liquid, as the mockup says.
  Cooled to 260 K the stack reports it frozen.
- The 3 km polar lid over it carries about 0.031 W/m² at a 230 K top.
- Validation errors, addresses, depth formatting, JSON round trip.
- Atom goldens: sea water freezes near 271.2 K; Europa-like shell thickness; Challenger Deep pressure within 1 percent.

## Known limits
- Ice conduction only: thick shells convect and carry more heat. Brine model good to about 10 percent below
  20 percent salt.
- Hydrostatic pressure assumes constant gravity, fine for oceans and crusts, not deep interiors.
- Extents are latitude-only for now. Drawn outlines arrive with the surface design's height map (surface stages).
- No proc-gen: stacks are authored. Generating them from a planet's history is the surface design's job.

## Integration points
- A V3 import (P3) keeps `hydrosphere`, `atmosphere`, `makeup` raw as `v3.*` facts. Mapping them into a world stack
  is a later step once both sit on one branch.
- The chart room (U-track) can render `ladder()` directly as the Strata strip.
- L0 (life suite) needs K10 and P4: habitats come from `provides: ["habitat"]`.
