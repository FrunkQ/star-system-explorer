# F4: the violent phase and the upheaval system

Status: done (18 Nov 2026). Physics track. Design: `SSE4-system-formation-design.md` section 3A and section 8 (F4).
Standard level: published stability rules and scaling laws with keyed draws (stream `formation.upheaval.v1`),
no orbit integration. F8's integrator can replace the cascade later and write the same events.

## What was built (`crates/sse-formation/src/violent.rs`, `upheave(seed, system, disc, growth)`)
1. **Priming** at the gas's end: smallest neighbour spacing in mutual Hill radii, resonant chain or not, giants,
   the belt outside the outermost giant. Chains break with probability 0.9 (Izidoro et al. 2017, 2021) at a
   log-uniform 1 to 100 Myr after the gas; two or more giants beside an outer belt cross a resonance (Nice
   trigger) with probability 1 - exp(-M_belt / 10 Earth masses), 10 to 500 Myr after (Nesvorny 2018).
2. **Break dates**: each unprotected neighbour pair gets a keyed instability time: Obertas et al. 2017
   (log t/P = 0.951 Delta - 1.202, 0.45 dex scatter) where it sits next to another pair under 12 Hill radii,
   otherwise Gladman's two-planet rule (crossing only under 2 sqrt 3). The draw is keyed by the pair and each
   world's change counter, so an untouched pair keeps its date.
3. **Cascade** of typed, dated events, each with its cause: chain break (each chained pair stirred to
   e of order v_esc/v_kep while spreading so its energy and angular momentum are both kept exactly);
   encounters resolved by the Safronov number (collision chance 1/(1+Theta^2), Ford and Rasio 2008);
   impacts by angle (sin 2 theta) and speed, merging by Genda et al. 2012, hit-and-run above the grazing
   limit (Asphaug 2010), erosion or shattering by Leinhardt and Stewart 2012 with mantle stripped first
   (Marcus 2010), oblique 5 to 50 percent impacts leave a large moon (Canup 2004); strong scatterings throw the
   lighter body in or out (energy and angular momentum kept) or eject it as a **rogue planet** at the system's
   `rogue:N` address; pericentres inside twice the stellar radius are engulfed; a giant whose orbit sweeps a
   belt stirs it: 99 percent removed, hits on every world by focused cross-section (Earth takes 1e-6, Gomes
   et al. 2005) as **bombardment spikes**, 4 percent to the Oort cloud, 1 percent scattered disc, 10 percent
   to the star, Trojans (7e-7, Nesvorny 2013) and irregular moons (1e-7) captured, the rest ejected.
4. **Settling**: pericentres under 0.05 AU circularise at constant angular momentum (Ford and Rasio 2006);
   spins and tilts drawn (giant impacts: 0.3 to 1 of breakup, isotropic tilt, Kokubo and Genda 2010; giants
   0.1 to 0.35 of breakup, Bryan et al. 2018); a settled date 10 to 100 Myr after the last event.

Outputs for F5: belts (F3 rings, stirred or not, plus impact debris), reservoirs (oort, scattered-disc, trojans,
irregular-moons, each with its giant), impact moons; for surfaces and life: `Bombardment` events (date, mass,
water, e-fold 50 Myr); for F6: `violent::protoplanets()` (formation time is the last giant impact).

## Books
- Mass, integer units, exactly: start (F3 bodies + rings) = worlds + belts + rogues + moons + reservoirs +
  ejected small bodies + star, per element group; gas = worlds + rogues + blown off + star.
- Orbital energy and angular momentum (Earth masses, AU, G M = 1) to rounding (1e-9): start = bound + heat +
  rogues + star + belts + debris. Heat and rogue energy are never negative. The closure checks the bookkeeping;
  the physics is in which sink each change goes to.
- Each world's ledger: its F3 record, worlds merged in whole, deliveries, losses (each loss and delivery
  names the water's source bands), so water by band sums to the world's water exactly; belts, reservoirs and
  moons carry the same lists.

## Known limits
- Giant eccentricities sit a little low (median 0.14 against about 0.2): single giants with no partner stay calm.
- Hit-and-run is common (about 40 percent of impacts), as in Asphaug's studies; the runners usually merge later.
- Moons formed here are records for F5; moon systems disturbed by later events, and the passing-star and
  late-trigger uses of the cascade, come with F5 and F8.
- No dynamical friction from leftover debris; tidal evolution beyond circularisation is F7.
