# J4: triggers, effects, the gate and approval policies

Status: done (19 Nov 2026). Kernel track. Design: `handover/design/SSE4-record-logic-programs-design.md` section 5, and section 10 row J4.

## What was built
**New crate `sse-trigger`.** Packs declare rules under `provides.other.triggers` as `{rules: [...], pass_limit?}`. The key is `triggers`, not `rules`, because `provides.other.rules` is already K8's classification rules.

A rule is `{id, phase?, when, scope?, effects, approval?, explain}`.

**When a rule fires:**

| Trigger | Fires when |
|---|---|
| `on_event {kind, tag?}` | An event of that kind becomes active in the window. A trailing `*` matches a prefix. `tag` narrows to notes that carry that tag. Universe and record events both count, except `rules.*`. |
| `on_condition {condition, hold_s}` | A K8 `Predicate` (the one condition language) holds and has held for `hold_s`. It fires once per stretch. Hysteresis: while the rule is armed, the predicate is read as "staying". |
| `on_time {every_s, from_s}` | Once per tick in (last boundary, now]. Before the first boundary it counts from `from_s`. |
| `on_intent {kind}` | `intent.<kind>` arrives in the record. This is J5's hook: J5's registry will validate kinds and seats, and the same event kind carries intents. |

**Scope** (`tag`, `addresses`, `under`, `pairs`) chooses what a rule watches.
- `pairs {observer_tag, position?}` watches each subject with each observer that carries the tag.
- The condition reads the subject's facts directly and the observer's facts as `observer.<field>`.
- `pair.distance_m` is computed from both bodies' position fields, `pos.x_m`, `pos.y_m` and `pos.z_m` by default.
- The sensor ladder is written this way, with no Traveller special (Care 4).

**Effects** are the closed list of design 5.2:

| Effect | What it does |
|---|---|
| `set_kept {kind, value \| add, reason?}` | Changes a kept value. The writer is `effect.<rule id>` and the kind must list it. `add` is clamped to the kind's range. |
| `add_note {text, audience, tags, tier?}` | Writes a note. `{subject}` and `{observer}` are filled in. |
| `grant {audience, view \| ladder}` | Grants a view. A ladder moves one rung up from the audience's present view. |
| `tag`, `untag` | Universe `tag.<name>`, so tag grants and scenes see it. |
| `send_intent {kind, payload}` | Sends an intent. |
| `set_pin {field, value}` | Sets an authored universe fact. This is the bridge to physics. |
| `create`, `retire` | Refused at load until templates exist (J7). |

**The gate:** `Rules::step(gate, at_s)`.
- **Order (design 5.4):** due firings run in phase order, then rule id, then subject, observer, tick and cause.
- **Passes:**
  - Pass 1 sees what became active since the last boundary.
  - Each later pass sees what the previous pass wrote, and conditions are read again.
  - At the pass limit (default 8, or set by the pack) the gate writes `rules.limit` holding the chain of rule ids per pass, and returns it in the report. Nothing is cut silently.
- **Boundaries run forwards.** A step back in time is refused.

**Approval** is `auto`, `ask` or `ask_if {condition}`.
- Defaults follow the design: `ask` if any effect is `create`, `retire` or `set_pin`, and `auto` otherwise.
- `set_policy` lets the GM override a rule's policy without editing its pack (a `rules.policy` event; the latest one wins).
- An `ask` writes `rules.proposed` with the effects already filled in and the rule's explanation. Nothing else changes.
- `decide(proposal, yes, at_s?)`:
  - checks every effect first, then applies all of them, or none;
  - a no is recorded as well;
  - each proposal can be answered once.

**Provenance, and what a seat sees.** All runtime state is record events, which stay on the GM machine and are never broadcast (J1, 8A). The events are `rules.boundary`, `.armed`, `.disarmed`, `.fired`, `.proposed`, `.decided`, `.applied`, `.policy` and `.limit`.
- Record effects cite the firing or the decision as their cause.
- Universe effects (grants, tags, pins) cite nothing, because cross-chronicle causes are impossible and would leak hidden ids. Their publisher names the pack, rule and version. The GM-side `rules.applied` event links the firing to each event it wrote.
- So a seat receives results (a view granted, a tag), never the trail, the ids or the inputs.

**Replay.** The engine is a fold over the two chronicles plus the list of boundaries, so a run replays bit-identically.

**Kernel ops (GM machine only):**
- `rules_step {at_s}` returns the report, `state_hash` and `record_hash`.
- `rules_pending`
- `rules_decide {proposal, approve, at_s?}`
- `rules_policy {rule, approval, at_s}`
- `rules` returns the rules with their effective policies, plus the trail.

**Loading.** `load_stack` reads the rules and checks them against the stack: every kept kind exists and lists `effect.<rule>` as a writer, and every granted view exists. A failure rejects the stack, with the problem named.

New fixture pack `tests/fixtures/packs/rpg-rules.json` holds two rules over `rpg-record`:
- `sensor-ladder`: pairs with ships, in range for an hour, moves one rung up the ladder long-range, scanned, survey;
- `consequence`: a note tagged `insult` costs one point of `politics.standing` and adds a GM note. Approval is `ask`.

## Checks
`sse-trigger` has 5 tests.
- **Sensor ladder:**
  - the ship comes into range at 1000 s, and the body moves up to scanned at exactly 4600 s (not at 4599 s);
  - it fires once per stretch;
  - leaving range keeps what was learnt;
  - back in range at 8000 s, the body moves up to survey at 11600 s;
  - each move is a universe grant published by the rule, with no causes;
  - the trail shows 2 armings, 1 disarming and 2 applications;
  - a step back in time is refused.
- **An `ask` changes nothing until approved:**
  - the universe hash, kept values and notes are unchanged;
  - stepping on neither applies it nor fires again;
  - yes applies both effects at the answer's time;
  - a second answer is refused, and a no changes nothing;
  - a GM override to `auto` applies at once;
  - `ask_if` asks only when standing is already -2 or below;
  - `add` clamps to the range (-10 after 12 more insults).
- **Defaults and refusals:**
  - `set_pin` is proposed and `tag` is applied;
  - bad ids, `every_s` 0, `create` (J7), empty effects and a duplicate id across packs are refused;
  - `check` names a missing writer, a missing kind and a missing view.
- **Pass limit:** ping-pong intents at limit 5 report the 6-pass chain `start, ping, pong, ...` and write `rules.limit`. A chain that ends by itself runs to its end, and `{subject}` is filled into the note.
- **Replay:** the whole script (ladder plus consequence, six boundaries) gives identical universe and record heads and reports.

Kernel test `rules_through_the_api`:
- the crew's scene shows scanned at 4600 s;
- the insult is proposed, the universe hash is untouched, and the kept values are unchanged until `rules_decide`, after which standing is -1;
- the crew's disclosures contain no trail id, no rule name, no GM note and no secret kind;
- two runs of the session give the same bytes.

## Limits and next
- **Positions for pairs are facts on both bodies.** Moving bodies need their positions written, by NAV, programs (J6) or the GM. Orbits do not yet publish positions. That comes with the `orbits_at` op the chart room asked for, and a later pair source can read it.
- **Pairs are subjects x observers,** fine for tens of ships, not thousands. J6 should narrow with `under` and tags.
- **`on_intent` reads `intent.<kind>` record events.** J5 turns this into the kernel's intent registry, with pack-declared kinds and seat scopes.
- **Effects at one boundary apply at that boundary's time.** There are no delayed effects yet; a program (J6) can schedule its own.
- **A refused auto effect is reported as `refused: <why>` and applies nothing.** It is not retried.
- **No UI yet:** the approval queue and rule list are J9. The chart room can call `rules_pending` and `rules_decide` now.
