# NM3a: base-names, the Catalogue scheme and the avoid list

Track NM, design `handover/design/SSE4-naming-design.md` 4.8 A and 4.9. Built 22 Nov 2026 by the master data
sheet thread on NM1's engine (PR 207). Sources and licences are in NM0 and `docs/citations/naming.md`.

## What was built

- **`packs/base-names`** (`sse.base-names`), generated by `scripts/build-base-names.py`. `scripts/check.sh`
  runs its `--check`. The schemes sit under `provides.other.names`, as NM1 reads them.
- **`catalogue`**, the default form (design decision 6). Stars are `SX {serial:6}`, a fictional survey prefix
  so that no real catalogue is named, or `SX J0535-0523` (`star-sky`) from the sky position. Components are
  `{parent} A`, `B`. Planets are `{parent} I`, `II` by orbit, moons `{parent} b`. Small bodies are
  `{parent} 1`, and galaxies, clusters and nebulae are `SXG`, `SXC` and `SXN` with a number. Streams are
  `{parent} Stream 1`.
- **`catalogue-letters`**, the real-practice form. Planets take a letter from b, and their moons a Roman
  numeral (as Kepler-1625 b I). The IAU letters planets by discovery order. Where a survey record exists, that
  is NM2's job: it passes the discovery index. Otherwise the letters go by orbit.
- **Surface features** take the IAU's Latin descriptor terms with the world's name and a number, for every
  kind the surface crate names. For example, peak and shield become Mons, plains Planitia, scarp Rupes, sea
  Mare, ice cap Planum (as Planum Boreum) and range Montes. Kinds the IAU has no term for (settlement, port,
  mine, base) take a plain word. Themed lists for each world's features (the IAU's practice for crater names)
  belong to the Common layer, in NM3b and NM3c.
- Every designation **follows its parent** (`follow` lists each kind whose form has `{parent}`). Numbers come
  from the caller, never from a draw, so designations are unique by construction.
- **The avoid list** has 77 folded words. Common profanity in English, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian, French and
  German comes first, a few slurs next, then well-known brands. It applies to generated names only and is best
  effort, as the pack's `about` says. Packs and the GM add their own.

## Choices (open to tweak)

1. The survey prefix is `SX`. A universe pack replaces the scheme to use its own prefix.
2. A moon after a numeral starts at **a** ("SX 000102 V a"), using NM2's `{letter:a}` token. Until 22 Nov
   evening it started at b. Planet letters still start at b.
3. Circumbinary planets ("(AB) b", Hessman et al. 2010) need the parent to be the pair. That is NM2's address
   work, so no form here.

## Checks

- `base_names_designates_sol_as_real_practice_does` (sse-names). On Sol's shape it checks:
  - the star's two designations;
  - eight planets as III and VIII, and as b and f;
  - moons as "V b" and "f I";
  - a component B, and a Mons;
  - a rename of the star carried down to the moon.
- `base_names_gives_a_200_body_system_no_duplicate` (sse-names). It covers 200 siblings in each form, finds
  every surface kind with a form, and checks the avoid list is loaded.
- `base_names_loads_with_the_stack` (kernel) checks that `name_sample` answers from the shipped pack.

Goldens: none change. Nothing in the shell's stack loads `base-names` yet. NM4 puts it there.

## Follow-up (22 Nov, after NM2)

- Catalogue moons use `{letter:a}`.
- `base-names` sets `defaults: {"designation": "catalogue"}`. Every object has a designation by default, and
  common names wait for the GM to choose a scheme.
