# J7: templates, create and retire

Status: done (19 Nov 2026). Kernel track. Design: `handover/design/SSE4-record-logic-programs-design.md`, section 8, and section 10 row J7.

## What was built
**`sse_trigger::templates`.** Packs declare templates under `provides.other.templates`:
- Shape: `{templates: [{id, kind, label, facts?, kept?, tags?, programs?, explain?}]}`.
- `kind` is the address segment of the objects made from the template (`ship`, `station`, `wreck`, `faction`, ...).
- `facts` are authored universe facts, for example `sensor.range_m`.
- `kept` are starting kept values. Each kind must exist, fit the value, and list `template.<id>` as a writer.
- `tags` become `tag.<name>`.
- `programs` lists what objects made from the template may run (J6).
- **Refused at load:**
  - a duplicate id, or an empty label;
  - a `kind` that can't be an address segment;
  - a fact that `create` sets itself (`template.*`, `retired`, `tag.*`);
  - a kept value the record would refuse;
  - an unknown key;
  - a rule whose `create` names a template no pack declares.

**`create {template, under, key?, name?, at_s}`** makes `<under>/<kind>:<key>`. Without a key it takes the next free number. An address already in use is refused.
- **Universe side:** one authored `template.create` event, published by the pack, the template and its version. It sets the facts and tags, plus `template.id`, `template.pack`, `template.version`, `template.programs` and `name` (the label by default).
- **Record side:** one `record.set` per kept value, written by `template.<id>`.

**`retire {address, reason?, at_s}`** writes `template.retire`, setting `retired = true`. Nothing is deleted, and the history is untouched. Only objects made from a template retire; anything else is an ordinary GM edit.

**Effects.** J4's `create {template, key?, name?}` (made under the rule's subject, with `{subject}` filled into the name) and `retire {reason?}` (retires the subject) now run through the gate.
- By default they ask the GM, as the design says.
- They are prechecked with the rest: the template exists, the address is free, the subject is retirable. So a proposal applies all its effects or none.
- The kept values cite the firing; the universe event cites nothing secret.

**`may_run(universe, address, program, at)`** is J6's check. It reads only the object's own facts:
- its template listed the program;
- it exists at that time;
- it is not retired.

**Pack removed, object still valid.** Everything is copied onto the object at creation. With the pack unloaded, its facts, tags, programs and name read as before, and its kept values read as orphans (J1).

**Kernel ops** (GM machine only): `templates`, `create`, `retire`.

**Fixture** `tests/fixtures/packs/rpg-templates.json` holds:
- the templates `raider`, `freighter` and `wreck`;
- the kept kinds `ship.hull` and `ship.cargo_t`;
- the rule `wreck`: a ship with no hull left breaks up into a wreck and is retired.

## Checks
- **Unit, `templates::tests`:**
  - a drone template's kept value is refused without its writer;
  - `create` with a key, then with the next number;
  - facts, tags and kept values are on the object;
  - `may_run` is refused before creation, for an unlisted program, and after retiring, but the past is unchanged;
  - the load refusals.
- **`sse-trigger` J4 test:** `create` now loads, and `check_templates` names a missing template.
- **Kernel, `templates_through_the_api`:**
  - a template with a bad writer rejects the stack;
  - the raider is created as `ship:1` and the freighter as `ship:2`, with 2 kept values;
  - a duplicate key is refused;
  - the history shows `template.create`;
  - hull 0 makes the wreck rule propose; a yes writes the wreck and the retirement;
  - retiring twice, or retiring a system, is refused;
  - with the pack unloaded, the ship reads as retired, keeps its name, programs and tags, and the wreck has its name; the freighter's kept values read as kept by a pack that is not loaded.
- **Contract:** 7 new lines (181 to 187). Lines 1 and 41 list the new ops. The TypeScript types are regenerated.

Golden changes (on purpose): `contract.jsonl` lines 1 and 41, plus the new lines 181 to 187. No engine golden changed.

## Limits and next
- There are no `create` or `retire` intents for player seats yet. A pack can declare its own intent and a rule that creates. If the chart room wants a direct one, it can be a built-in later.
- Retired objects still appear in rule scopes and scenes, marked `retired`. J6 must skip them, and the chart room may grey them out.
- Templates are copied at creation, so editing a pack's template does not change objects already made. That is on purpose: the record stays true to what was made.
