# A4: Tars, support that cites its source

Status: done (19 Nov 2026, agents track).

## What was built
- **`packages/agents/src/tars.ts`**, on the contract of Ovid's tech support (`play/support-ask.ts`): rules first, model last.
  - **The shelf** (`tars.shelf.json`): 23 short passages on the chart room (chart, why?, physics, lenses, player data,
    sharing, time, what if, look ahead, make a system, body, sizes, life, packs, screens, light, sound, campaign, agents,
    Orac, Minerva, device tiers, honesty marks), each with an id, its place in the app and the package logs it was written
    from. Add a passage when a screen ships.
  - **Explanation bundles**: `whyPassage` turns the kernel's explanation of a derived value (Curious and Science lenses,
    validity, references with their DOI or address) into a passage Tars can cite. The shell builds these for the
    selected body's derived values (up to eight) when a question is asked.
  - `findPassages`: the six passages nearest the question (shared words, title counted double).
  - `askTars`: with no model, the best passage's first sentences, cited ("Tars's own pages"). With a model, the passages
    go with the question; the answer is `{"words", "sources"}` and `checkAnswer` requires at least one citation of a
    passage he was given (or exactly "I don't know that one yet.") and every number in it to be in what he cites or in
    the question (the same number check as Minerva's). A failing answer goes back (two fixes), then Tars falls back on
    the rules answer. So no uncited answer is ever shown.
  - **Humour setting**: plain (no jokes), light, or dryly funny, in his standing instructions; kept with the agent settings.
- **Panel** (`TarsTab.svelte`): ask; the answer with "From: <passage> (<where in the app>)" and, for a why? passage, its
  references with links. Tars now works with no key at all (from the shelf), and says so.
- A1's first Tars prompt (`tarsSystem` in `agents.ts`) is replaced.

## Acceptance (build plan A4: answers cite the bundle or doc they came from)
| Criterion | Where it is proved |
|---|---|
| Answers cite their source | `packages/agents/test/tars.test.ts`: an uncited model answer goes back and the cited one passes with its source; a citation of a passage not given, or a number not in what is cited, is refused; a model that never cites falls back on the passage, still cited; with no model the answer cites the best passage. A why? passage built from the real kernel's explanation of Tarn's surface temperature carries its references and is what Tars cites when asked why. Browser (`tests/ui/agents.e2e.mjs`): with no key, the answer says "From: Changing the physics (Physics (key P)) · Tars's own pages" and nothing is sent; with a model, "From: Changing the physics", and the request carried the selected body and the passages |
| Humour setting | Same test: the system turn says "Be dryly funny, briefly" at 0.9 and "Be plain and direct; no jokes" at 0 |

## Honest limits
- Tars reads his shelf and the why? of the selected body. He does not yet read run manifests, validation reports or
  harness output (Ovid's "reads the shape of a failed call"), and he does not file bug reports: both are a later step.
- The shelf was written from the package logs; a passage may lag a screen that changed since. It names the logs it
  came from so it can be checked.
- Passage finding is word overlap, not meaning: an oddly worded question may miss the right passage (then he says so,
  or answers from a near one, still citing it).
- Tested with scripted mock providers; no model measured for the Support job yet.
