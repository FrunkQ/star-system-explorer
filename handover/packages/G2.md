# G2: haloes per cell, growth, spin, mergers, neighbours

Status: done (19 Nov 2026). Kernel track (cosmos suite), PR into `main`. Design: galaxy design sections 4 and 12.

## What was built
`sse-cosmos::haloes`:
- A cell (default 8 Mpc comoving, address `<universe>/cell:<ix>.<iy>.<iz>`) takes its linear overdensity from the
  web (G1) at the octave whose spacing matches the cell, and its matter from the lognormal density.
- Counts: Sheth and Tormen 1999 mass function with the peak-background split (their bias, eq. 12), as expected
  counts per bin (4 per decade, 1e10 to 1e16 solar masses by default); keyed Poisson draws per bin (stream
  `cosmos.halo.count.v1`), top-down from the largest bin, capped so the cell's halo mass never exceeds its matter.
- Positions: the highest of 6 keyed candidate points on the finest octave, so haloes sit on the web's peaks.
- Growth: M(z) = M0 (1+z)^alpha exp(beta z) (McBride et al. 2009 form), alpha and beta from the power spectrum by
  Correa et al. 2015 paper I (eqs. 14, 15 and 20 to 23); per-halo scatter and an environment shift (denser forms earlier), both rough.
- Spin: lognormal, median 0.035, sigma of ln 0.5 (Bullock et al. 2001; the design's "0.5 dex" read as Bullock's
  natural-log width).
- Major mergers (ratio above 1:4): keyed Poisson process on 60 fixed redshift slices from z = 6 with the Fakhouri,
  Ma and Boylan-Kolchin 2010 rate at the halo's mass then; ratio from the rate's slope; orbit sketch (circularity
  0.3 to 1, pericentre 0.05 to 0.5 virial radii; Benson 2005 ranges, rough). `record` writes each as a derived
  `halo.merger` event with its "why".
- Neighbours: nearest more massive halo within 3 Mpc; bound if the Hubble-flow speed is below the escape speed;
  time to first approach by radial free fall (sketch: the hook for an Andromeda-style collision).
- Pack: `provides.other.cosmology.haloes` in `packs/base-cosmology`.

## Checks (`cargo test -p sse-cosmos g2`, 5 tests)
- Sheth-Tormen: the multiplicity integrates to 1.0000 and the mass-weighted bias to 1.0000.
- 64 cells (a 32 Mpc cube), haloes above 1e11: drawn against the unconditional Sheth-Tormen expectation per decade:
  488 against 427 (1e11 to 1e12; the sample's mean density is above the universe's), 52 against 55, 5 against 6.4.
  Halo mass never exceeds the cell's matter. The 16 densest cells hold 24 times the halo mass of the 16 emptiest.
- Spin median 0.0338 over 545 haloes.
- Major mergers since z = 6 for 57 haloes above 1e12: 184 drawn against 178 expected.
- Half-mass redshift: 1e11 at 1.40, 1e12 at 1.20, 1e15 at 0.59 (Milky-Way haloes near 1 to 1.5, clusters near 0.5:
  Wechsler et al. 2002; Correa et al. 2015); small haloes first; denser surroundings earlier.
- Order independence: a cell read before and after another is identical; neighbours are always larger and within
  the radius; merger events recorded one per merger.
- Cross-device: golden `tests/golden/haloes.json`, harness engine `haloes`.

## Known limits
- The peak-background split is linear (1 + b delta), floored at 0: in very dense cells it overpredicts big haloes
  slightly; the budget cap stops it exceeding the cell's matter.
- Halo positions do not move with time (no Zel'dovich step for haloes yet); minor mergers are smooth growth; sub-halo
  catalogues and streams are not drawn yet (G6 and section 8).
- Formation scatter and the environment shift are rough pack values.
- Merger events are derived; editing them (add, delete, move with recompute) arrives with G6's history editing.
- Debug-build test time for the cosmos suite is about 35 s (the web is evaluated at thousands of points).

## Integration points
