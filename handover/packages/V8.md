## What was built
- **`ui-contract/src/route.ts`** (pure parts, no physics):
  - `navFromScene(scene)`: the system NAV reads, from any chart scene. The primary star's mass, and its luminosity
    and radius as the base-physics flows derive them; each planet orbiting the star directly, with `a`, `e` and the
    scene's phase as the mean longitude at the scene's "now". It says what it leaves out (moons, bodies without a
    mass, inclinations) instead of hiding it. Belts come from the scene's belt bands.
  - `shortlist` (one course of each kind first, so three torch throttles don't crowd out a coasting arc),
    `safestFirst` ("Keep us safe": difficulty, then dose), `fuelLeft` against the ship's reserve, words for days,
    Δv, dose, light delay and "in N days" on the campaign clock.
  - Chart geometry in the star's frame (rendering rules 1 to 3): `routeFrame` sizes a linear frame to the course
    and both ends' orbits in f64; `trackPath` and `trackPoint` turn the kernel's 64-point track into pixel numbers;
    `orbitEllipse` draws an orbit from its elements (geometry only); `trackIndexAt` places burns and hazards, which
    sse-nav times from departure.
- **`ViewSession.planRoute`** (kernel `plan_route`). Any seat may plan against an inline system (what it knows);
  planning against an adopted `system` reads the full record, so it is GM only.
- **View `sse.view.route`** (`packages/views/route`), mounted on a new GM screen **Route** (since U6, a section of the Explore tab):
  - left, "Tell the computer": ship (base-nav stock ships), from, to, how long the crew can wait, what matters most
    (fastest, save fuel, balance, keep us safe), what the ship has (Δv aboard, usable after the reserve, top g),
    and the honest notes from `navFromScene`;
  - the chosen course drawn in the star's frame (orbits, belts, the track, burns, hazards, "Tarn at departure",
    "Vault at arrival"), the strip (depart and arrive from now, fuel after arrival against the reserve, Δv, peak g,
    dose, light delay home, closest to the star), burns, why it is hard, how the computer worked it out, what is
    beyond this ship, and the model's own limits;
  - up to three course cards beside it (mini chart, days, fuel left, peak g, difficulty pips, dose, hazards).
  - Defaults: from the first world in the temperate zone, to the next world out. A planet picked on the chart
    becomes the destination. Departure is the campaign's "now" when the workspace carries `campaign_now_yr`
    (U11), else the chart's.
  - Theme tokens only; no theme code. Params for Orac: ship, prefer, window_days, shown.
- **Registration hook** agreed with the chart room thread: `packages/shell/src/screens.more.ts` (`MORE_VIEWS`,
  `MORE_SCREENS`) and `screens.more.css` (layouts keyed on `data-screen`). The shell's only edits are three spread
  lines in `registry.ts` and `App.svelte`. Later screens from this track register there.

## Reproducing the boards (PlayerPlan, Route)
Kept: tell the computer, what matters most, side-by-side courses with time, fuel, g, difficulty and hazards, the
course on the map, the strip with fuel against the reserve. Done differently: the GM screen is the chart-room look
(the boards are a player theme); the course is chosen from cards rather than dragged, because players command the
computer and every number comes from the kernel. Not yet: "Stay unseen" and "Low g, we have wounded" (need track O
sensing and a g limit per request), crew votes and "Send to GM" (part 2), dragging a waypoint for an assist (NAV
has no gravity-assist courses yet), moons as destinations (NAV flies between planets).

## Fudges (for docs/fudges.md once PR 22 lands)
- `navFromScene` takes orbits as coplanar (the chart scene carries no inclinations or perihelion longitudes), and a
  missing luminosity as one Sun (said on screen). Real replacement: read the system's full elements from the
  kernel (`plan_route` with `system`, once chart systems are adopted, or the scene carrying elements).
