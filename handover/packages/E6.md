# E6 part 1: a region's cover and biome, with fire

Design: `handover/design/SSE4-evolution-design.md` section 9 (biomes that form by themselves) and section 7.3 (fire).

## What it does
`crates/sse-life/src/cover.rs` turns one region's climate into cover, and labels the cover with a biome.

- **Inputs.** A `CoverInput` holds the warm, cold and mean temperature, potential and actual evaporation, the ice balance, PAR at the ground, gravity, and the O2 ignition index relative to today's. `CoverInput::from_water` takes the shared climate's `WaterSupply` at a place, so E6 computes no climate of its own.
- **Cover** is the record:
  - α (actual over potential evaporation, BIOME's moisture index) and the growing season (share of a sinusoidal year above 0 °C);
  - woody potential (water between α 0.28 and 0.65, warmth about the 6.4 °C tree line of Körner and Paulsen 2004);
  - ground cover, closure after fire, and canopy height (35 m × g0/g × water × season);
  - NPP from CASA's light-use efficiency (0.389 g C/MJ, Potter et al. 1993);
  - biomass and fires per year.
- **Fire.** The grass layer is the fuel. It burns with the water deficit, the fire weather (saturation vapour pressure in the warm season) and the oxygen ignition index. Trees that cannot escape between fires stay open. The fixed point is solved from an open start, with shade cutting the fuel, so fire keeps savannah open where trees cannot outgrow it.
- **Biome** is a label read off the cover by an editable atlas (`CoverParams.atlas`, first matching row). Each row gives three names:
  - a structural name for any world ("closed tall canopy", "open canopy over ground cover", "low mat", "bare", "ice");
  - an Earth name, using Köppen's 18 °C and 10 °C lines and BIOME's −15 °C boreal limit;
  - the design's broad type.
- Fudge F33 holds the curve shapes and fire constants.

## Verified by
- `earth_sites_get_their_broad_types`: eight stations, all right:
  - Manaus: tropical rainforest;
  - Lubumbashi: miombo open woodland;
  - In Salah: desert;
  - Astana: steppe grassland;
  - Frankfurt: temperate forest;
  - Yakutsk: boreal forest;
  - Utqiagvik: tundra;
  - Vostok: ice.

  The climate normals are approximate and listed in the test, and α comes from the shared climate's own Fu curve.
- `without_fire_the_savannah_closes_into_forest`: Lubumbashi goes from 0.48 closure to closed forest without fire (Bond, Woodward and Midgley 2005). An anoxic world gives the same result as fire switched off.
- `production_and_mass_are_earthlike`: rainforest NPP comes out at 1128 g C m-2 yr-1, with biomass in 8 to 30 kg C m-2 (Saugier, Roy and Mooney 2001). Tundra NPP is 50 to 200.
- Twice the gravity halves the canopy height and leaves closure unchanged. A cold dark world is bare, and an ice world is ice.

## Not yet (part 2), and why
- **The design's own check:** at least 70 percent of land area right against a named land-cover map. It needs land regions (P4) and the dataset, so for now the check runs against stations.
- **Zonal and regional runs** from `sse_climate::orbit` with `water_at` per place, PAR from the climate's own surface sunlight, and the `biome` tile layer hand-off to the surface track.
- **The locked red-dwarf terminator ring.** It needs the shared climate's substellar geometry for a locked world.
- **E5's guild traits** setting the cover strategy (mean stature, light-response colour), and the cover feeding back into albedo and evaporation for the weather track.
- **Fire on soil carbon** (E4), and fire season as a hazard read by the game layer.
- **Wiring E4, E5 and E6 into the history**, so that coupled runs use them. That changes the life goldens on purpose, in one step.
