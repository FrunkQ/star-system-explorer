# V9w3: the web reads as a web

Alex, 23 Nov 2026: the view must read as "the 3D spiderweb" of filaments, nodes and voids, not a "wrinkled rug". The
3D cube after V9w drew every parcel of matter alike. Voids hold 44% of the parcels, so they filled the cube with an
even grey fog and the filaments were lost in it. Each dot was also scattered over its whole starting cell (2 Mpc),
which blurred filaments that the step had squeezed much thinner than that.

## What it does (all presentation of the kernel's own numbers)

- **Inked by density today.** Every parcel has the same mass, so its density today is one over the room the
  Zel'dovich step leaves it (`room`, already in the cube). Parcels fall into four levels: under the mean, up to 3×,
  up to 10×, and over 10× (`WEB_DENSITY_LEVELS`). In 3D each level is drawn a share of the way from the ground to its
  environment's colour (`WEB_LEVEL_INK`: 12%, 35%, 70%, 100%), so the thin voids fade back and the dense web stands
  out. The same levels shade True's stand-in glow and the dark matter that HUD shows.
- **Kept inside its squeezed room.** A parcel stands for the matter that started in its grid cell. That matter is now
  squeezed or stretched to its room, so the dot is scattered inside a cube of side `cell × room^⅓` instead of the whole
  cell. Matter in a filament stays in the filament, and matter in a void spreads through the void. This is an
  isotropic stand-in for the real stretch, which is a different factor along each axis (the tidal eigenvectors); that
  is the next step if it is wanted.
- **Big galaxies marked in 3D.** The cube's galaxies (V9w2) are now read in 3D too. Haloes over 10¹² M☉ are drawn as
  ink dots, groups (10¹³) larger, and clusters (10¹⁴) larger still in the accent colour (`WEB_MARKS`). The knots now
  read as where clusters sit.
- **Play** no longer waits for each step's galaxies, which take a few seconds each. It runs on the parcels, and the
  galaxies come when it stops.
- `Web3D` builds one list of layers that both the GPU path and the canvas path draw. On a phone that is WebGL2
  `GpuDots`, or the canvas with additive light in True.

## Next

- An anisotropic scatter along each parcel's own stretch (the tidal eigenvectors the kernel already computes for the
  T-web) would draw filaments as threads and sheets as walls.
- Real filaments are a few Mpc thick; at the truncated smoothing (16 Mpc) one Zel'dovich step makes them broader.
  2LPT or the adhesion model would sharpen them (kernel, golden lane).
