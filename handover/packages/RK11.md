# RK11: one instrument shape, channels beyond light, no typed ranges

Light track, 23 Nov. Design: `handover/design/SSE4-atomic-rockets-plan.md` section 12. Alex answered yes to questions 13 and 15 on 23 Nov.

## What changed (`crates/sse-sense`, op `detect`)

- **Methods.** `Sensor.method` is "image" (the default), "spectrum", "astrometry", "active", "magnetometer" or "gradiometer". Any other method is refused in words.
  - **spectrum**: `resolving_power` R splits the light over R ln(to/from) elements, and each element must reach the threshold on its own. It reaches less far than an image from the same telescope.
  - **astrometry**: `precision_rad` is the resolution over the S/N (King 1983).
  - **active** (radar or lidar): needs `transmit_power_w` on the sensor and `radar_cross_section_m2` on the target. It uses the radar equation with the gain of its own aperture (4πA/λ²). The detection range is the echo range, and `heard_to_m` is how far a listener with the same receiver hears the pulse. `transmit_power_w` is reported so the power ledger (RK9) can take it as a load.
  - **magnetometer**: reads the target's `magnetic_moment_a_m2` as the equator-plane field μ0 M / (4π r³).
  - **gradiometer**: reads the target's `mass_kg` as the gravity gradient 2GM / r³.
  - Field noise is `noise_density` (per √Hz) averaged for `stare_s`, as n / √(2t). `field_clutter` (the channel's own wander) adds in quadrature and does not average away.
- **Channels.** A target may give light, a magnetic moment and a mass. A target with only a moment or a mass needs no light: it is dark.
- **Constants.** G and μ0 come from the data sheet.
- **No typed ranges.** `authored_range_m` keeps a range typed in an older file (SSE3's `sensors.json`), marked `range_source: "authored"`. It is used only when the instrument has no aperture (light) or no noise figure (fields). Otherwise the computed range is used, and the authored one is shown beside it.
- **Range bands are a measure.** `detect` takes `range_bands` ({id, label, range_m}, from the setting) and adds `distance_band` and `range_band` to each detection. The sheet home for bands sits with the setting's measures (matter design 7A, "bands"), and the data sheet thread owns that table. The Traveller pack's `sensors.range_bands` already has this shape.
- **Detection gains** `method`, `channel`, `range_source`, `authored_range_m`, `precision_rad`, `transmit_power_w` and `heard_to_m`.
