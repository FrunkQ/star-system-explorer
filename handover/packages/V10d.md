# V10d: bands through True, and the Window's sky on the one Band control

Alex, 23 Nov 2026: "this is going to look amazing through all the different lenses too... x-ray - IR, magnetosphere - seeing as REAL and diagrammatic", and "One set of controls".

## What it does

- **X-ray** (True): the star is drawn as `drawStar` draws its X-ray image: the optically thin corona's emission measure on a log scale, brighter over spots and much brighter over flares, in mapped colour. It turns and flares on the living star's clock (V10c). The corona reaches past the disc, so the disc takes 0.6 of the sprite. Worlds give off almost no X-rays, so they are dark; HUD On names them.
- **Infrared** (True): thermal infrared at 10 µm, where a world's own heat outshines the starlight it reflects. Each body is drawn in mapped grey by its blackbody radiance at its own temperature (a star's effective temperature, a world's surface temperature, else its equilibrium temperature), on a log scale 6 decades deep from the star. Each disc is uniform (no day and night map yet).
- Ultraviolet and Radio are drawn as Visible in True for now, and the note says so. Mag in True waits for magnetospheres (next package).
- Every band's note says what is mapped and how.
- **Window**: the stars beyond follow the view controls' Band. `WINDOW_CONTROLS.bands` = visible (V) and infrared (Ks). SkyBeyond's own band buttons are gone, and with them the Blue (B) filter, which is a filter within Visible rather than a band. It can come back as a Light recipe.

## Code

- `renderer/src/true-look.ts`: `thermalGrey`, `THERMAL_IR_M`, `THERMAL_DECADES`.
- `gpu.ts`: `GpuItem.emit`, a body's own light as unlit grey.
- `system-3d/bodies.ts`: `Body3D.t_k`. `System3D.svelte`: `band` prop, X-ray face, infrared emit, `data-true-band` note.
- `SystemChart.svelte` passes `shownView.band`.
- `window/src/controls.ts` declares the bands; `SkyBeyond.svelte` reads them.
