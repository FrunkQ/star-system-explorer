# F0: inheritance, stars, life lines, FormationHistory and time slices

Status: done (18 Nov 2026). Physics track, branch `claude/project-thread-h9y77o` (with the kernel track's K7 to K10
merged in). Design: `SSE4-system-formation-design.md` sections 3 (stages 1 and 2), 4, 5 and 8.

## What was built
Crate `sse-formation`:
- `hurley/`: the main-sequence fits of Hurley, Pols and Tout 2000 (HPT00) with Tout et al. 1996 for the zero-age
  main sequence: L, R, t_BGB, t_MS, terminal values, the hook, L(t) and R(t), base-of-giant-branch L and giant radius.
  **Ported from the SSE Fortran as shipped in COSMIC 4.3.4 (MIT licence, reproduced in NOTICE)**, with Fortran array
  indices kept so each line checks against the source and the paper. Coefficients (`coeffs.rs`) were transcribed
  mechanically from `zdata.h` (numbers only, 76 + 31 + 72 + 119 + 112 + 99, counts checked). Not REBOUND, not GPL.
- `lifeline.rs`: a star's life line, drawn once, read at any age: pre-main sequence (Kelvin-Helmholtz time), main
  sequence (HPT00, standard level), subgiant and giant phases (sketch rules on the same fits), and the remnant:
  white dwarf mass from Cummings et al. 2018, Mestel cooling and the HPT00 radius law; neutron star above 8 solar
  masses and black hole above 25 (sketch).
- `inherit.rs`: stage 1. `PatchEnvelope` (`sse.formation-envelope/1`) is the suite's inlet: [Fe/H], age, C/O,
  Mg/Si, Al-26, birth-cluster size, far-ultraviolet field, each a `sse-resolve` distribution. Draws are keyed by
  the system address, so a system forms without its neighbours. Any value can be pinned.
  Z = 0.02 x 10^[Fe/H], the fits' own solar scale.
- `stars.rs`: stage 2. Primary mass from Kroupa 2001 (exact inverse), multiplicity by primary mass (Duchene and
  Kraus 2013), companions with flat mass ratios, log-normal periods and circularisation below 12 days (Raghavan
  2010), triples kept hierarchical (Mardling and Aarseth 2001, order of magnitude). Roche lobes (Eggleton 1983).
- `history.rs`: `FormationHistory` (`sse.formation-history/1`): manifest (seed, system, envelope, pins, suite version)
  and its hash, inheritance, budgets, stars, dated events (born, ZAMS, leaves main sequence, giant, end, and
  `binary.mass_transfer` when a star first fills its Roche lobe at periastron, found by fixed 60-step bisection), time
  slices (0 to 3 Myr, every 10 Myr to 300 Myr, every 250 Myr to the horizon, plus every phase boundary and "now"),
  per-value evidence (calculated, drawn with its distribution, authored) and the level of each stage.
  `state_at(t)` reads slices (log-linear inside a phase) and never recomputes. `chronicle_events()` dates events in
  universe time.
- `scenario.rs`: the golden scenario (24 systems from the fixture envelope), also run by `sse-wasm`'s `engines_run`.
- Fixture `tests/fixtures/formation/solar-neighbourhood.json`: a cited stand-in envelope, kept for GM-made patches
  and tests.
- **G0 integration (kernel track, merged at 5b2235d).** `Manifest::from_birth(seed, SystemBirth, now_gyr)` forms a
  system the cosmos suite resolved: its birth facts become the inheritance ([Fe/H], [alpha/Fe], C/O, Mg/Si, Al-26,
  r-process, K-40, cluster mass and FUV), and the cloud core's mass is shared exactly among its stars
  (primary = M / (1 + sum of mass ratios); companions that would fall below 0.08 are dropped). The manifest's
  `source` is either `envelope` or `birth`. `scenario::solar_patch()` forms all 301 systems of G0's solar patch.
- Brown dwarfs (0.0124 to 0.08 solar masses) have their own life line: Burrows et al. 2001 cooling, radius 0.1
  R_sun (sketch). Stars run 0.08 to 100 (HPT00 fit 0.1 to 100; 0.08 to 0.1 is a labelled extrapolation).
- Metal content outside the fits' Z = 1e-4 to 0.03 uses the nearest edge for the stars, and the evidence entry
  `stars.metallicity_for_fits` says so (metal-rich thin disc, the most metal-poor halo).

## Checks (`cargo test -p sse-formation`, 12 tests)
- Tout 1996: a 1 solar-mass Z = 0.02 star starts at L 0.698, R 0.888 (within 0.2 percent). Metal-poor: more than
  twice as bright, 6400 to 7000 K.
- HPT00: t_MS 11.0 Gyr at 1 solar mass (3 percent), 24.3 Myr at 10, 8.7 Myr at 20 (5 percent), 5 to 7.5 Gyr at
  Z = 0.001.
- The pinned Sun at 4.57 Gyr: L within 6 percent of 1, R within 3 percent, Teff within 3 percent of 5772 K (the
  fits' Sun gives L 0.956, R 0.985). It ends as a 0.569 solar-mass white dwarf after 11 to 13.5 Gyr.
- IFMR: a 5 solar-mass progenitor gives 1.006 (Sirius B is about 1.02). 12 gives a neutron star, 40 a black hole,
  0.05 is a brown dwarf near 4e-5 L_sun and 1200 to 1900 K at 1 Gyr, still cooling (Burrows 2001); 0.005 is
  refused as planetary mass. White dwarfs fade more than tenfold over 5 Gyr; radius about 0.0125 R_sun.
- Determinism: 12 systems formed forwards and backwards give identical hashes; another seed differs; a slice and
  the whole history reproduce from the manifest's JSON.
- The ruler: on a slice the stored state is returned exactly; between slices on the main sequence within 1 percent
  of the life line; at least 35 slices in the first 300 Myr; every phase boundary is a slice.
- Budgets: stellar mass equals the sum of the stars; every star has its events; events are in date order; chronicle
  events accept the history.
- Populations: 76.1 percent of Kroupa draws on 0.08 to 100 fall below 0.5 solar masses (1 percent); Sun-like primaries over 6000
  drawn systems are multiple at the Duchene and Kraus rate within 7 points.
- Close pairs: Eggleton's lobe at q = 1 is 0.379; pairs wider than 50 AU never transfer mass; more than 20 of 400
  pinned pairs do, always before the donor's end.
- Metals: [Fe/H] = -3 forms with the fits at Z = 1e-4 and says so in the evidence.
- G0 solar patch: all 301 systems form (none refused); each system's stars share its core mass to 1e-12; 13 hold a
  white dwarf now (4.3 percent; Hollands et al. 2018 find about 6 to 8 percent within 20 pc; the test band is 2 to
  20 percent while giant phases are sketches); 51 are multiple (17 percent: mostly M-dwarf cores, and companions
  below 0.08 are dropped); forming the patch twice gives identical histories.
- Cross-device: `tests/golden/formation.json` (24 systems). Identical in Node (wasm) and Chromium through the
  harness here; CI runs Firefox and WebKit too.

## Known limits
- After the main sequence the star is a sketch: giants last 0.1 of the main-sequence life, the giant-tip luminosity
  is 2500 L_sun (low mass) or 10 times the base, no wind mass loss, no helium flash detail. F7 brings the tables.
- Metallicity range is the fits' 1e-4 to 0.03 (edges used outside, labelled); Population III needs other tracks.
- Multiplicity for G0 cores is drawn against the core's total mass, an approximation of the primary-mass relation.
  Brown-dwarf companions are not kept yet.
- Binary outcomes (common envelope, merger, stable transfer) are not decided: only the start of mass transfer is
  dated. Rule tables arrive with F7.
- The history hash uses JSON text of the record (deterministic: shortest round-trip floats). Full JSON round trip of
  a stored history needs serde_json's `float_roundtrip` feature; not enabled to avoid changing other goldens.
- Red dwarf futures beyond about 250 Gyr stay in the history but not in the chronicle (universe time ends at 2^63 s).
