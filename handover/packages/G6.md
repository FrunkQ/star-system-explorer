# G6: galaxy events, cross-patch effects, encounters, history editing

Status: done (19 Nov 2026). Kernel track, PR into `main`. Design: galaxy design, sections 9 and 11, and section 12 row G6.

## What was built
**History edits** (`sse-cosmos::galaxy`). `Edit` is one authored change: `merger {t_gyr, ratio}` (ink a merger), `remove_merger {index}`, `starburst {t_gyr, gas_fraction}`, or `quiet_nucleus {from_gyr, to_gyr}` (no black hole growth and no active episodes in the window). Other pieces:
- `check_edits` refuses bad ratios, fractions, windows and indices.
- `evolve_with(c, p, halo, edits)` evolves the galaxy with the edits. `evolve` is now `evolve_with(.., &[])`. With no edits it does the same arithmetic in the same order, so every golden is unchanged.
- `mergers_with` gives the merger list after the edits.

Each edit acts from its own time onward. The galaxy loop is causal, so history before the earliest edit is bit-identical.

**Rings keep the unedited clock** (`rings::evolve_clocked`, `inflow_clock`). The inside-out infall profile was normalised by the galaxy's total lifetime inflow. That total looks into the future: any edit changed it and reshaped the rings back to the start. An edited history now keeps the unedited galaxy's clock total, which makes the rings before the edit bit-identical too. `evolve` is unchanged (it uses the galaxy's own total), so all goldens stay as they were.

**Events** (`sse-cosmos::events`). Events are drawn and never stored. Each draw is keyed on its own cell, so an event belongs to the cell and not to whoever asks.

| Event | Cell and bin | Rate |
|---|---|---|
| Core-collapse and type Ia supernovae | 100 pc cells, 10 Myr bins | the envelope's rates per pc^3, at the cell centre and bin middle (`expected_cell`, `cell_events`) |
| Neutron-star mergers | as above | `nsm_per_ia` (0.005) x the Ia rate |
| Lethal long gamma-ray bursts | 1 kpc columns, 100 Myr bins, near the mid-plane | the envelope's within-`grb_kpc` rate per area (`expected_column`, `column_events`) |
| Mergers (halo or inked), authored starbursts, active-nucleus episodes | galaxy-wide (`galaxy_events`) | from the galaxy |

Event ids name where each one came from (`merger/3`, `edit/0`, `<cell>/<kind>/<bin>/<j>`), so inking an event never renames another.

**Cross-patch effects** (`exposure(site, from, to)`). A site is either fixed in the co-rotating grid or on a star's G5 `Orbit`. For each bin, the site's path is swept and every cell it comes within reach of is read. Each event's distance is taken at the event's own time. Travel time is respected:
- Light and radiation arrive at d / c.
- A remnant's shock arrives at d / 200 km/s and lasts 1 Myr inside 50 pc.

What a site gets from each event:

| Event | What the site gets |
|---|---|
| Supernova or kilonova | harmful inside the sterilising distance (10 pc); a remnant hit inside 50 pc (fresh metals) |
| Gamma-ray burst | harmful inside 2 kpc |
| Active nucleus | G4's dose |
| Merger or starburst | galaxy-wide notes |

**Encounters** are keyed on the site, per 10 Myr bin, at the envelope's rate inside `encounter_pc`. Each passage draws:
- a passage distance, uniform over the target area;
- a Maxwellian relative speed about the envelope's mean;
- the passing star's mass from G5's Kroupa IMF.

Each passage also gets an impulse measure, M / (b v). A passage inside 0.05 pc (about 10,000 au; Hills 1981) is a comet shower, the impact hazard handed on to the physics and life suites.

**Arm crossings:** entries into and exits from spiral arms along the site's path, sampled every 1 Myr. A site counts as in an arm while the density wave's cos(phase) is above 0.5.

**Before and after.** `compare(before, after)` gives the first changed history point, the morphology both ways, and six curves (stars, bulge fraction, gas, star formation rate, gas metals, black hole), each with before and after values and its largest change. That is the "ghost" the chart draws behind the redrawn curves.

**Chronicle.** `events::record` writes:
- each GM edit as an authored `galaxy.history_edit` event on the galaxy;
- each hit as a derived event on the site. Hit kinds are `galaxy.supernova.core_collapse`, `.type_ia`, `galaxy.kilonova`, `galaxy.gamma_ray_burst`, `galaxy.active_nucleus.exposure`, `galaxy.encounter`, `galaxy.arm.entry`/`.exit`, `galaxy.merger` and `galaxy.starburst`. `occurred_at` is when the event happened and `activated_at` is when it reached the site.

**Scripted scenario.** `Manifest` (`sse.galaxy-scenario/1`) holds:
- the universe id (which is also every seed's text);
- the halo's mass and key;
- the edits;
- the site (a fraction of the Sun's radius, an azimuth and a height);
- the window.

`run_manifest` runs the galaxy without and with the edits, then the rings, the envelope, the site's exposure both ways, the comparison and the chronicle. It returns a report with locality flags. The fixture is `tests/fixtures/cosmos/ink-a-merger.json`: a 0.5 merger at 9 Gyr, with a site at the Sun's radius watched from 8.5 to 10.5 Gyr. Its golden is `tests/golden/galaxy-events.json`, and the harness engine is `galaxy-events`.

**Pack:** `cosmology.events` in `packs/base-cosmology`, with every default and `sources.events`.

## Checks (`cargo test -p sse-cosmos g6`, 6 tests)
- **Edit locality.** Four edits were tested: a merger at 9 Gyr, a starburst at 6 Gyr, a quiet nucleus from 4 to 8 Gyr, and removing the first halo merger. For each one:
  - galaxy history before the edit is identical;
  - per-step inflow before the edit is identical;
  - every ring state ending at or before the edit is identical;
  - something does change.

  In the scenario, every site hit drawn from a bin that ends before the edit (less one ring step, because the envelope interpolates to the end of a ring step) is the same hit.

  The merger at 9 Gyr (ratio 0.5) takes the bulge fraction from 0.089 to 0.441 and the black hole from 2.45e7 to 3.49e7 solar masses. A quiet nucleus for the whole life leaves the seed black hole and no episodes. Bad edits are refused.
- **Rates against section 6:**

  | Check | Events | Reference |
  |---|---|---|
  | Whole disc now (24 rings x 32 azimuths x 2 kpc columns of expected counts), core collapse | 3.93 per century | rings 3.91 |
  | Whole disc now, type Ia | 0.74 per century | rings 0.80 |
  | Drawn near the Sun (16 cells over 1 Gyr), core collapse | 2322 | expected 2297 |
  | Same block, type Ia | 173 | 174 |
  | Same block, kilonovae | 1 | 0.87 |
  | Lethal bursts at the Sun now, from columns | 0.444 per Gyr | envelope 0.437 |

  Bands: the disc totals are within 25%, the drawn counts within 4 sigma, and the burst rate within 35%.
- **Cross-patch.** Two sites 60 pc apart, either side of a cell face, over 500 Myr:
  - they record 61 and 71 supernova-scale hits and share 17 of them, with the same times and the triangle inequality holding;
  - the nearer site is reached first;
  - site a has 61 hits within 50 pc, against about 51 expected.

  Windows compose: [t0, tm) plus [tm, t1) holds exactly the hits of [t0, t1). A star on an epicycle records 34 hits, and each one is a real cell event seen by a fixed probe placed where the star was.
- **Encounters.** Over 2 Gyr at the Sun's radius:
  - 271 passages within 0.1 pc, against about 310 expected;
  - 62 showers, a share of 0.23 against the area share of 0.25;
  - median speed 44 km/s;
  - median passing mass 0.23 solar masses.

  **Arm crossings:** 12 between 4 and 13.8 Gyr, alternating between entry and exit.
- **"Ink a merger" reproduces from its manifest.** The manifest round-trips through JSON, and two runs give identical reports. Both locality flags are true, the bulge fraction rises, there is one extra merger hit, and the golden matches. A bad manifest is refused.
- **Pack:** the pack's values equal `EventParams::default()`.

Golden added: `tests/golden/galaxy-events.json`. No existing golden or contract line changed.

## Discrepancies and known limits
- **A merger's burst does not reach the rings as a spike.** The galaxy turns gas into bulge stars at the merger, but the rings run their own gas from the galaxy's inflow. So the site sees fewer supernovae after the merger (203 against 230 in the window), from the changed inflow, instead of a burst of them. Routing the burst into the inner rings is the fix, as a pack choice. Arguably it belongs in G7 with the Milky Way pack.
- **Edited rings keep the unedited inside-out clock.** This is intended: the clock is the halo's, so an edit never rewrites the past. But an edit that changes a galaxy's lifetime inflow a lot leaves its profile a little off what a fresh run of that history would give.
- **The edits are four kinds.** The design also lists a passing satellite, an earlier or later reionisation and a different spin. Those change the halo, not the galaxy. They need halo-level edits and touch everything from z = 20, so they are not local by nature.
- **Rates are taken at cell centres and bin middles.** Inside a 100 pc cell, arm and texture variation is smoothed; inside a 10 Myr bin, the rate is held flat.
- **Encounter speeds are Maxwellian about the mean, not weighted by speed** (faster stars should be met more often), and the sample count is 13% low against the rate (271 against 310). This is inside the test band, and partly the texture at the site.
- **Remnants are a fixed bubble** (50 pc, 200 km/s, 1 Myr), not a Sedov-Taylor expansion by the gas density. Kilonovae use the supernova harm radius.
- **GRB beaming is folded into the envelope's lethal-burst yield** (`grb_per_msun`); no beam direction is drawn.
- **Nucleus exposure** uses G4's dose measure, with light travel time from the centre. No jet axis.
- **Cloud passages are not drawn as events.** The envelope's texture is the gas the site moves through. A "cloud passage" event would be a threshold on it, much like arm crossings.
- **References are from memory and need verifying:** Abbott et al. 2023 (Phys. Rev. X 13, 011048), Li et al. 2011 (MNRAS 412, 1473) and Hills 1981 (AJ 86, 1730).

## Integration points
