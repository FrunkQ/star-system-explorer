# V10: seats and audiences (part 1)

Status: part 1 done (19 Nov 2026), branch `claude/project-thread-adgixl`, in the V4 PR into `main`. Part 2 is sealed seats (Alex said yes on 18 Nov, as a later per-seat step).

## What was built
- **Seat plans** (`ui-contract/src/seats.ts`, schema `sse.seats/0`): audiences (`id`, `label`, `tag`, `starts_with`, `discovered_view`) and seats. `defaultPlan()` is one table for everyone; `isDefaultPlan` tells the shell to show no seat list. `addSeat` and `removeSeat` keep ids unique and drop an audience no seat uses. `checkSeats` gives readable problems: unknown audience, data view or screen, an auto-approve the seat may not send, GM authority on a player seat, and sealed seats (not built yet).
- **Seat contract** (`seat.ts`): seats gain `screens` (several screens per seat, each its own window or device) and `lens`. `auto_approve` entries gain `fields` (e.g. names only).
- **Disclosure is the GM's**: `decide` refuses any `set_value` of `observe.*` from a player seat, even inside an auto-approve scope. The GM declines a forged one the same way.
- **Knowledge per audience, fog of war**: an audience's starting view and its discoveries are K9 grants per audience. `startIntents` chronicles `observe.view.<audience>.default` on the universe root once when the seat is added. `discoverIntent` sets or takes back `observe.view.<audience>` on a body; its moons follow (K9 inheritance). `fogViews` picks the campaign view that shows nothing (the Kestrel player-data pack gains "Uncharted" for this) and the campaign's before view for a discovery.
- **Auto-approve over the session** (`p2p.ts`): the GM applies what a seat's auto-approve covers at once, through the GM's own session, so it is chronicled once and replayed by every player. Anything else is queued as in V5. A player never applies anything locally, auto-approved or not. Seats travel in the manifest and in `state` messages when the GM changes them (`GmHost.setState({ seats })`, `PlayerReplica.seats()`).
- **Join links per seat**: `joinLink(base, room, seat, screen)` gives `players.html?join=<room>&seat=<id>&screen=<file>`; the table's link is the plain room link. The players page opens with the seat from the manifest. It builds scenes for the seat's audience, through its lens when it has one, and opens the seat's first screen unless `?screen=` picks another. It hides "Ask the game master" when the seat may not ask. A link to a seat that no longer exists says so.
- **GM screen** (`shell/src/SeatsPanel.svelte`): nothing before sharing. Once shared, the status bar gains a quiet "More seats" link (it reads "N seats" once there are more). The panel shows "Everyone sees: Long-range sensors" for the default plan; with more seats it lists them with their join links per screen. A form adds a seat (fog of war, may suggest names, names apply without asking, screens). A Discoveries table ticks what each fog-of-war seat has found, read back from the kernel's scene for that audience.

## Kernel gaps (for the kernel track; no change made to /crates)
- No op grants a named view to an audience. Discoveries and audience defaults are `authored.set` events on `observe.view.<audience>` (the fields K9 reads), not `observe.grant` events. History shows them as authored sets. An `observe.grant` op with a view id would make them proper grants.
- Tags do not drive grants. The design's "show what carries my audience's discovery tag" needs a data-view rule on tags, or a pack rule that grants on arrival. Today the GM's tick writes the grant. `Audience.tag` (`discovered:<id>`) is recorded for when that rule exists.

## Not done, and why
- **Sealed seats** (part 2): the GM's machine builds and sends finished scenes, and the player gets no manifest seed, packs or log.
- **Seat plans are not saved** with the campaign: they live for the shared session. They come with campaign saves.
- **Role intents** beyond the four intent kinds (scan requests, course proposals) wait for those intents (T3/V8). Seats carry them by kind already.
- **The Scanned button's audience chooser** ("for: everyone ▾" when more than one audience exists) belongs on the GM's Scanned control, which comes with the GM-side Scanned UI. The Discoveries table covers fog of war for now.
