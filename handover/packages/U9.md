# U9: Lenses

Status: done (19 Nov 2026), branch `claude/project-thread-adgixl`, in the PR into `main`.

## What was built
- **Lens documents** (`packages/ui-contract/src/lens.ts`, schema `sse.lens/0`), following H8.6:
  `{ id, label, description?, data?, view_as?, cards: [{ subject_kind, fields?, density }], layers, tools, cost }`.
  - Layers: orbits, zones and belts, names, distance rings, survey grid, unsurveyed hatching, moon ticks, evidence stamps, key, and "what the table knows".
  - Tools: undo and redo, drag orbits, open the flow from a why. Typing on a card is allowed in every lens (H8.6: "editing is allowed in any lens").
  - Card rules per kind of body (or `*`), with a field list in order and a density (one line, compact, full with notes). A field a lens hides is untouched, never cleared.
  - `cost` declares the heaviest layer's cost class, so the V11 device edge rule applies (`lensFits`).
- **Shipped defaults**: Basic (three headline facts; a basic-tier device opens in it), Advanced (everything; desktops open in it), Building (all editing tools, hatching and evidence stamps), Running (four facts on one line, no editing tools, and each body marked with what the table knows of it).
- **Every player data view is a lens** ("Through Scanned", "Through Uncharted", ...), plus "As the table sees it" (everyone's real grants, so each body's scanned state) and one "As X sees it" per fog-of-war audience from the seat plan. Built from the stack and the plan, so a new view or seat brings its lens.
  - The chart fades bodies that audience does not know and says "N of M known".
  - The card becomes the players' card (the player stack's Card, in the plain vocabulary), with the GM's honesty stamps over it.
  - The scene comes from the kernel's own scene op (`session.scene` with `audience` and `data_view`), fetched once by the shell and handed to the views as `workspace.lensScene`.
- **Switcher**: a "Lens" select on the bar, grouped (for the job, your own, from packs, as players see it), and the L key. It sets `workspace.lens`; nothing is sent to the kernel.
- **Make your own** (`packages/shell/src/LensPanel.svelte`, from the switcher's last option): start from the lens in use; switch layers, tools, card fields and density per kind of body; save under a name.
  - Saved lenses live in this browser's profile (`sse4.lenses`), and the chosen lens in `sse4.lens`.
  - "Export my lenses as a pack" downloads a presentation pack (`provides.other.lenses`). "Load lenses from a pack" reads one back. A pack stacked on the Packs screen also puts its lenses on the switcher.
- **Honesty**: `gmStamps` keeps authored, impossible, fictional and changed-physics on the GM card whatever the lens. On player views, honesty marks are the data view's `show_honesty` switch (off by default), as before.

## Choices made (for Alex to overrule)
- Advanced is "everything" except the "what the table knows" layer. That layer writes the table's view under each name, which is clutter while building. It is on in Running and in the player lenses.
- "Through <view>" lenses use the kernel's `data_view` override: every body the players have facts for is shown through that view, whatever was granted. "As the table sees it" uses the real grants.
- Bodies with no recorded player-facing facts (Ember, Sable, Hollow in Kestrel) do not exist for players at all, so every player lens fades them.
- The lens editor edits one card rule at a time ("For: Any body, Star, Planet, Moon").
- colour_by from H8.6 is not built. The symbology (V12) already says what colour encodes. A lens-level override would be a symbology choice per lens, so it is left for when the Symbols screen grows per-lens bindings.
- Lenses on the GM's 3D chart are not applied yet; it shows every body.
- "Orac can build one from a sentence" waits for A2.
