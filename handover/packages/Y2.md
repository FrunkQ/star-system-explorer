# Y2: the sky from here (kernel side)

Night sky design (`design/SSE4-night-sky-design.md`) section 4.3, package Y2, the draw. Built 22 Nov 2026 by the kernel thread. The light side (passbands, magnitudes, dust per band, `read_photometry`) is `packages/Y2-light.md`.

## What it does
New op `sky_from_here {observer?, position_pc?, t_myr?, limit_mag?, band?, bands?, cone?, max_stars?}` (Cost Medium, read only). It gives the stars seen from a place at a time down to a limiting magnitude.

- **Observer.** A star system's address, a galactic position in pc, or (neither) the Sun's radius in the campaign's galaxy. Defaults: the campaign's now, V to 6.5, at most 5,000 stars.
- **Which stars** (`crates/sse-cosmos/src/sky.rs`). Each luminosity class of Y1's count tree gets a visibility radius: the dust-free distance at which a blackbody of the class's top luminosity, at whichever temperature is brightest in the limit band, reaches the limit plus 0.5 mag. The radius is capped at 4 kpc. One `TreeWalk` serves every class, so envelope reads, class shares and splits are made once (`Stars::sky_walk(radii, observer, t)`).
- **Their light** (`crates/sse-kernel/src/sky.rs`). Light time from where the star is now, then its place when the light left. Each shining component (primary and companions) is a blackbody from its temperature and luminosity, summed into one f_λ and passed through `sse_light::photometry::photometry_of`. A star too faint without dust is dropped before its dust is worked out. Dust A_V is the gas column along the line (envelope `gas_pc3`, 24 steps) times 10^[Fe/H], over 21 M☉ pc⁻² per magnitude (`provides.other.cosmology.sky`, Güver and Özel 2009 with helium).
- **Pins.** A promoted star drawn from a reached cell replaces its own slot, so it appears once; other pins are added.
- **Out.** `stars` brightest first (ties by address): address, pinned, class, distance, light time, l and b (l from the galaxy's centre direction), dir, A_V, T_eff, L, phase, double, `components [{teff_k, radius_m}]`, magnitudes (AB and Vega, with extinction) and colours (with excess). Also cumulative `counts` per whole magnitude, the walk's radii, found and reads, `truncated`, and a conventions line.

## Changed on the way (on purpose)
- **Giants** (`StarParams.giant_l_factor` 2, `giant_l_floor_lsun` 50, `giant_teff_k` 4,500; fudge F-S11 updated). G5's stand-in made a giant 30 L_MS + 50 at 20 R_MS. A 30 M☉ giant came out above 10⁶ L☉ at 17,000 K, and the first sky had 2,785 stars brighter than V 4 against about 513 real. Now a giant is 2 L_MS + 50 at 4,500 K: the red clump at the low end (Girardi 2016) and a supergiant near its terminal main-sequence luminosity (Hurley, Pols and Tout 2000). This changes the fill draw, so `tests/golden/stars.json` (counts and digests) and the contract golden's `star_celled` line were re-blessed. The cell's pinned star is unchanged. Promoted stars are stored whole (Y7a), so none moves.
- **Speed.** These changes leave every number the same except where a test says otherwise.
  - `GalaxyEnvelope::systems_pc3` and `gas_pc3` do only the part of `local` a count or a dust column needs, with the same arithmetic. `local` also scanned the metal history for `rocky_for_gyr` on every call, which made a read about 340 times dearer.
  - A star's abundances come from `surface` directly, with the same arithmetic.
  - The tree remembers its splits, and keys class shares by the block's column (they depend only on its radius).
  - Test builds also optimise `sse-light`, `sse-core`, `sse-atoms` and `sha2`. These are the same IEEE operations, so no result changes.
  - Measured in a test build: a sky to V 6.5 went from about 20 minutes to 26 seconds, and V 4 from about 9 minutes to 5 seconds.

## Verified by
In `crates/sse-kernel/src/cosmos.rs`:
- `the_sky_from_the_suns_place_counts_like_the_real_one` checks the counts from the Sun's place to V 5.
  - Stars brighter than V 3, 4 and 5 come within a factor of 1.5 of the real 171, 513 and 1,602. Measured: 196, 594 and 1,902; to 6.5, 10,742 against about 9,000.
  - The list is brightest first and every star reaches the limit.
  - Reddened stars have a positive B−V excess.
  - A single star's V equals `read_photometry` of its own photosphere through the same sight line, within 1e-9.
  - The star is the one its cell draws (`star_cell`).
- `the_sky_is_the_same_twice_and_a_promoted_star_appears_once`:
  - The same ask gives the same JSON.
  - Every star lies inside the cone.
  - After `promote_star` the star appears once, pinned, equally bright, and the list keeps its length.
- `a_sky_ask_out_of_range_is_refused`: a negative time, a limit over 30, an unknown band and an unknown observer are each refused.
- The contract golden has two new cases: a V 2 sky in B, V and Ks, and an unknown band.

## Known limits (fudge F35)
- **Bright end.** The bright end is high: 19 stars brighter than V 0 against about 4. A fill sky is statistical, and this galaxy is only Milky-Way-like.
- **Radius.**
  - The 4 kpc cap hides the few real naked-eye stars beyond it.
  - The radius uses no dust, so classes 8 to 11 walk the full 4 kpc and find about ten times the stars they keep.
  - A dust radius per class (the least dust along any line) would cut that.
- **Light.** Blackbodies have no line blanketing. Dust is smooth, with R_V 3.1 everywhere.
