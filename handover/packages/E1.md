# E1: the gradient catalogue

Evolution design (`design/SSE4-evolution-design.md`) sections 3 and 14,
package E1. Built 21 Nov 2026 by the kernel thread, on top of E0.

## What it delivers
- **`sse-life/src/gradients.rs`, model `gradients/1`.** For each habitat it lists the free energy on offer:
  - **light**: the photon cap of each band a pack lists, with an optional electron donor (water, Fe²⁺, H₂, H₂S), so anoxygenic photosynthesis competes with oxygenic for the same light;
  - **redox**: every reaction in the thermodynamic table (E0) whose reactants the habitat supplies, at its own temperature, pH and activities (ΔG = ΔG° + RT ln Q, through the `sse.chem` blocks);
  - **organic**: organic carbon exported from producer habitats into linked ones (`export` map, for example land surface to soil 0.9, sunlit ocean to the interior 0.2), burnt with whatever oxidant is there.
- **The three tests** (section 3.2), each with its reason in `why` when it fails:
  - energy per turnover: |ΔG| at least the 20 kJ per mol quantum (Schink 1997);
  - power: at least maintenance (Tijhuis et al. 1993, 1.25 W per C-mol at 25 °C, Ea 69.4 kJ per mol) times a minimum population;
  - materials: phosphorus supply (the existing box B nutrient cap).
- **Abiotic competition**: a reaction can be marked spent when the chemistry runs by itself faster than life could use it (`fast_above_ph`, `fast_above_k`). Iron oxidation by O₂ above pH 6 is the first case.
- **Shared supplies counted once**: the supply of one shell (an ocean, an ice shell) is split across the habitats that report the same value, so Europa's ocean is not counted twice.
- **Allocation**: gradients draw on shared reactants greedily, best carbon yield per mol of limiting reactant first; H₂O and H⁺ never limit, and species above `abundant_above` (1e-3) are treated as unlimited.
- **Yield**: production = turns × |ΔG| × growth efficiency / build cost. Heterotroph production goes in the new `secondary_gc_yr`, never in primary production.
- **`world_catalogue`** runs two passes (producers, then importers) and returns the catalogues plus a `WorldBudget` in the listed model's shape. `world_budget_by_model` switches on the stack's new `life_budget.life_model` (default `listed/1`).
- **`budget.rs`**: the tail of a habitat budget became `finish`, shared by both models, so feeding levels, biomass and high-power flags are computed one way.
- **Pack data** (`packs/base-life`): species `ch2o`; nine aqueous reactions (no published values, so they are checked for balance only); the `life_gradients` block with supplies, activities by medium, pH, exports, light bands and donors, abiotic limits, a 32-gradient cap and sources.
- `scenario::archean_with_hydrogen` for tests.

## Verified by
- `earth_today_meets_the_fidelity_contract`: the most powerful regenerated gradient is oxygenic photosynthesis; production 80 Gt C per year and biomass inside the same published ranges L1a uses (Field et al. 1998; Bar-On et al. 2018); 3 to 6 feeding levels; soils and the ocean interior now have secondary production instead of unknown; total within a factor 2 of the listed model.
- `early_earth_regenerates_the_anaerobic_metabolisms`: Archean Earth regenerates anoxygenic iron photosynthesis; with 5e12 mol per year of H₂ (Kharecha et al. 2005) hydrogen photosynthesis and methanogens appear.
- `europa_counts_its_supply_once`: the aerobic chemolithotroph comes first; 1.07e9 g C per year, inside Chyba and Hand 2001's range and half the listed model's (which counted the ocean twice).
- `closed_and_spent_gradients_say_why`: Mars surface (triple point), Venus clouds (water activity), Titan lakes (no chemistry), iron oxidation spent at pH 8.1.
- `random_habitats_never_break_a_cap`: 10,000 random habitats; nothing negative or infinite; production never above the energy or nutrient cap; no reactant used beyond its supply; biomass built never beats energy released; no open gradient with a reason.
- `the_model_switch_picks_the_budget`.

## Goldens
None changed. The listed model is the default and no stack sets `life_model`, so every existing output is identical.

## Known limits
- Nothing in the coupled run or the kernel calls the catalogue yet; wiring and capability gating (which gradients a lineage can use) are E2.
- Soils are treated as anoxic because the habitat profile carries no O₂ supply for them; aerobic respiration in soil waits on a soil air supply.
- Sea floor, deep crust and caves stay unknown: no supplies are published for them yet.
- Growth efficiency 0.2 and the activity tables are assumptions (fudge register, Climate, weather and life, F28 and F29).
- Constant ΔH° (E0); no heat-capacity terms above about 150 °C.
