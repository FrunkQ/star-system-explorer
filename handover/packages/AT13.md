# AT13: controls that switch on and off

Thread "Build Run desk playbook and players' side", 24 Nov 2026. Atlas and reports design (`handover/design/SSE4-atlas-and-reports-design.md`) section 11A.4, accepted by Alex 24 Nov 20:14 ("Yes to all").

## What
- ui-contract `part-controls.ts`: a `PartControl` is `{ id, words, kind, default }`, with kinds button, switch, choice, step, dial, tab, link (declared as a kind, never as a drawing). `checkItemControls`, `resolveControls`, `controlOn`, `isPicture`, `controlLines`.
- `ViewModule.switchable`: the part's own presses. The five view controls stay on `ViewModule.controls` (a different thing: View style, Seen from, Scale, HUD, Band).
- `ScreenItem.controls`: `"none"`, `"all"`, or `{ "<id>": true | false }`; unstated take the part's default. `checkScreen` refuses a control the part has not declared (naming the ones it has). Checked mounts carry `controls` and `picture`.
- `PlayerContext.controls`: what a view reads (`controlOn(ctx.controls, "sort")`); absent means every declared control is on, so older hosts keep working.
- The screens runtime passes them on; a part with every control off (or `"none"`) is a picture: its area is `inert`, so nothing in it is pressed or reached by keyboard. `still` makes every part a picture, and the clean output (`?clean=1`, PB8) uses it.
- Declared and honoured: chart `select`; card `open` (a moon's link); table `sort` (the older `sortable` setting still works too) and `select`; system in 3D `turn` (the whole stage: turn, zoom, pick). Text and readout declare none.
- The parts catalogue: Reference, This setting, Parts (every player part with its controls, kind and default; settings count and tiers at Science and Engine). Orac's view entries gain a controls column, and the screen form names `controls`.
