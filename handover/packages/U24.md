# U24: The place line at every scale (UI track)

Status: done 22 Nov 2026. Alex found it very hard to get back to a system once he was out at the galaxy or the cosmic web. He asked for a breadcrumb for going up and, on its right, a drop-down of what is nested below for going down. This is step 4 of the top-level design's build order ("Scale ladder upward", section 2.1 and section 5).

## What it does

- **One line, every scale.** The title is now the line of places: **Cosmic web › Galaxies nearby › Galaxy › Kestrel › Tarn**.
  - The steps above the place in view are quiet links. The step just above is in accent: it is the one Esc goes to, shown as "‹" on a phone.
  - The place in view is the big serif name.
- **Down: the "Below" list**, beside the name.
  - At a system or a world it is the bodies list as before ("8 bodies"), plus Recent.
  - At the galaxy, the galaxies nearby or the web it lists:
    - the next scale down, where it is a scale of its own;
    - "Systems in this campaign": the base system and every adopted one, each one press away;
    - Recent.
- **The way back.** Going up keeps the deeper steps on the line, in quiet italics. For example: "Galaxy ▾ › *Kestrel* › *Tarn*".
  - The rail's top door says "‹ Back to Tarn".
  - One press returns to exactly where the GM was.
  - The trail lasts until the GM goes somewhere deeper or to another system.
- **Recent places.** The last six places are kept on this device (pref `places`) and listed under Recent.
- **Deep links.** Each place is the address bar's hash: `#chart/<scale>/<system>/<world>~<screen>`.
  - The screen is written only when it is not the scale's first instrument.
  - A new place is a step in the browser's history; another instrument at the same place replaces the step.
  - Back and Forward move through places.
  - A pasted link opens straight on its place, with no start sheet. `#chart` alone is still the base system, so every existing test and the trailer capture work as before.
- **Esc climbs.** From a world it goes to the system, as before. Above a system it goes up one scale at a time.
- **The scales are screens.** The galaxy chart, the cluster map and the foam map are each a screen document with a rail entry at their own scale: `scale-galaxy.gm.json`, `scale-cluster.gm.json`, `scale-web.gm.json`, gathered in `documents/scales.ts`.
  - `RAIL_SCALES` grows to web, cluster, galaxy, system, world, workshop. A later view joins a scale by declaring a rail entry there (one mechanism).
  - "Out to the galaxy" left the system rail. It is now a door ("Out to the galaxy ›") at the foot of the system rail.
  - The Explore screen document stays as it was, for Orac and screen documents, with no rail entry.
  - The cluster map's "Chart this galaxy" still works: its bubbling `sse:explore` event becomes a scale change outside Explore.
- **Phone:** "‹ name ▾". The far steps and the trail hide, and the ▾ opens the same list. Nothing runs off the side. Night chart uses theme tokens only.
- **Presentation only.** Nothing here reaches the kernel or the state hash.

## Code

- `packages/ui-contract/src/place.ts`:
  - `PLACE_SCALES` and `PLACE_WORDS`;
  - `placeSteps(here, trail, names)`: steps marked current or below;
  - `nextTrail`, `rememberPlace`, `samePlace`;
  - `placeHash` and `parsePlaceHash`;
  - `readRecent`.
- `packages/shell/src/App.svelte`:
  - `place` (derived), `trail`, `recent`, `goPlace`, `toScale`, `upAStep` and `goBelow`;
  - the address-bar effect, and the linked place applied once the session opens;
  - the `.place` markup and its CSS.
- `tests/ui/nav.mjs`: `go(page, "galaxy" | "cluster" | "web")` climbs the place line. `galaxy`, `cluster` and `foam` e2e now use it instead of the Explore tabs.
