# E4: soil as state, made by life

Evolution design (`design/SSE4-evolution-design.md`) section 8 and section 14, package E4. Built 21 Nov 2026 by the kernel thread. Code: `crates/sse-life/src/soil.rs`; the coupling is in `feedback.rs` (`AirParams.soil`, `AirBox`) and `history.rs` (`StepFluxes.land`). Tests are in `soil_tests.rs`, and the fudge is F31.

## What it delivers
- **Regions.** Each land-surface habitat of the world's habitat graph is one soil region, with its own area, mid temperature and production. On the Earth fixture these are the climate bands. When the surface track's regions (P4) arrive, they become the habitats, and nothing here changes.
- **The five soil quantities** of section 8.2, per region:
  - **Loose depth.** Soil production falls with depth, `P = P0 exp(-h/h0)` (Heimsath et al. 1997, doi:10.1038/41056: 77 m/Myr, 0.5 m), set against geological erosion (17 m/Myr, Montgomery 2007, doi:10.1073/pnas.0611508104). A soil answers in thousands of years, and the step is 5 Myr, so each step holds the steady depth `h0 ln(P/E)`. It is bounded by construction, and zero (bare rock) where production cannot beat erosion.
    - Production scales with weatherer cover, and with temperature through an Arrhenius term (63 kJ/mol, Brady 1991, doi:10.1029/91JB01898).
    - With no life it is 15 % of the biotic rate.
  - **Fine fraction.** It relaxes towards a share set by weathering intensity.
  - **Organic carbon.** It is at steady state between litter (the region's production) and decomposition.
    - Turnover is 23 years at 288 K with today's oxygen (Carvalhais et al. 2014, doi:10.1038/nature13731), and Q10 is 2 (Davidson and Janssens 2006, doi:10.1038/nature04514).
    - Below today's oxygen, only anaerobic decomposers work, at a tenth of the rate, so carbon piles up. This is the coal-swamp lever.
  - **Nutrient stock**, relative to today's Earth: weathering release, multiplied by how much decomposers reuse before burial (they reuse more with oxygen).
  - **Water-holding capacity**, from depth, fines and organic carbon.
- **The two roles, by function.** Weatherers are the land life measured by its production (cover `npp / (npp + 100 g C m-2 yr-1)`). Decomposers are measured by the oxygen they have.
- **Derived weathering factor.** It replaces L2's fixed 4: `1 + 3 x (cover x root contact)`, scaled to Berner's 4 at today's Earth and capped at 10. It is area-weighted over the regions. The air box's `co2_rel` is its inverse.
- **Soil carbon in the ledger.** Carbon put into soil leaves its O2 in the air, and carbon lost from soil takes the O2 back, only as far as there is O2. The ledger has a new `soil_store` term, and `residual()` includes it. `AirBox.soil` keeps each region's soil, and `soil_series` keeps the land-wide mean every step, with the temperature change (`delta_t_k`) that the CO2 drawdown gives at 3 K per doubling (IPCC AR6 WG1 chapter 7 best estimate).
- **Opt-in.** `AirParams.soil` defaults to none, and is skipped when serialising, so the L2 runs, their manifests and their goldens are exactly as they were. The same pattern was used for E2.

Across seeds a to h, land life arrives between 2780 and 3250 Myr after the world formed on five seeds, and cools the world 5.5 K when it does. On three seeds land life never arrives, and their soil stays bare regolith (0.11 m) with no cooling.

## Not yet, and why
- **The cooling is reported, not fed back into the habitats' temperatures.** The world's temperature belongs to the shared climate (W7a). When that lands, `delta_t_k` becomes its input, and a world can freeze itself by growing soil.
- **Nutrient reuse does not yet feed production.** L1a still takes reuse before burial as a constant per habitat kind. The soil's `nutrient_rel` is the number that will replace it, with E5's guild traits.
- **Not yet in `packs/base-life`, so kernel coupled runs do not use it.** Adding it there changes the life goldens on purpose, which is one step for the package that turns E2 and E4 on together in the kernel (with the gradient model wiring).
- **Fire on soil carbon.** E3's fire acts on burial. Soil carbon burning in a high-oxygen world comes with E6's fire season.

## Verified by
- `barren_to_soil_is_monotonic_and_bounded`: as land production rises over 80 steps, depth, fines, carbon and the weathering factor never fall, depth never passes `h0 ln(P0/E)`, and bare rock with no life has no soil and a factor of 1.
- `carbon_piles_up_without_oxygen_and_burns_off_in_warmth`: with the same litter, an anoxic soil holds 10 times the carbon, and 10 K warmer halves it.
- `earth_soil_carbon_within_a_factor_of_three`: 500 to 4500 Gt C, a factor between 2 and 6, the ledger balanced to under 1e6 mol over the run (O2 is 3.8e19 mol), and soil carbon stored.
- `a_seed_exists_where_early_land_life_cools_the_world`: one seed is cooled by more than 1 K before 3000 Myr, and one never holds land and never cools.
- `without_soil_settings_nothing_changes`.
