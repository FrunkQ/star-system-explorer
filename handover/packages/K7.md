# K7: envelopes, partitioning, resolve, feasibility queries

Status: done (18 Nov 2026). Track "kernel resolve and rules", branch `claude/project-thread-dzkz4p`, built in parallel with P1/P3/P4 and U1 from the M1 state.

## What was built
New crate `sse-resolve` (H15 layout): envelopes (clouds) as data, eight distributions sampled through their quantile functions, exact integer partitioning of budgets (pins claim first; flat or tree splits), `resolve` / `resolve_all` / `resolve_recorded` with child envelopes so conservation holds all the way down, life lines drawn once with `phase_at(t)`, and the two read-only QUERY shapes (location-first outlook, outcome-first scoring and child search) with `commit_pick`.

## Design sections implemented
H6.1 (all six hard rules), H6.4, H5.5 (resolved store; recorded facts win). Choices the design left open:
- **Budgets** are `u64` minor units with a declared SI quantum and dimension (CBOR integers stop at 2^64). Budgets share a `group`: budgets in one group split by the same share vector (metals follow gas), another group (angular momentum) splits independently.
- **Partition** is drawn on the *parent's* address: `resolve.count.v1` (child count), `resolve.split.<group>.v1` (flat weights) or `resolve.tree.<group>.v1` (tree halving). Flat splits use exact largest-remainder apportionment over 2^40-scaled integer weights (ties to the lower index); tree splits cost log2(n) draws per child for very large clouds. Either way sum of children + pins + residue = budget exactly.
- **Pins** have non-numeric keys (authored `@n` or a catalogue key) so they never collide with generated indices. They count toward the drawn count by default (catalogue fill generates only what the catalogue misses). Pins over budget are honoured, never trimmed: the deficit is reported and each pin carries `resolve.over_budget`. Unmeasured fields of a pin are drawn on its own address and listed in `resolve.fill`.
- **Fields** draw on the child's own address (`resolve.field.<name>.v<version>`), so a child resolves without its siblings and a new field cannot reshuffle old ones. `inherit` copies a parent fact.
- **Resolve events are timeless** (occurred and activated at the origin): they fix the whole life line (`life.born_s`, `life.ends_s`), and any time is a read of it (rule 6). They go to the resolved store with evidence `resolved`; a pin's own evidence is the fact `resolve.evidence`, so resolving never changes the authored hash. A child's event cites its parent's resolve as its cause.
- **Recorded facts win**: `resolve_recorded` returns the recorded facts (and rebuilds the child cloud from them) if the child was resolved before, even if the envelope has since changed.
- **Queries**: location-first is closed-form from the envelope (quantiles, category probabilities, expected count, budget per child) and draws nothing. Outcome-first scores each cloud by P(one child matches every criterion) x expected count; clouds are taken in address order whatever order the caller passes, each costs 1 + criteria work units, and an empty result is `not-found-within-budget` with the nearest clouds and the reasons they fail. `search_children` walks real children (same facts a later resolve records) and says where it stopped. `commit_pick` writes a `query.pick` event (`query.selected_for`, wish hash), evidence authored.
- Normal quantile: Acklam's approximation plus one Halley step against pinned `erfc`; `erfc` comes from the same pinned `libm` (sse-core's math module has no `erf`; I did not edit sse-core to keep the parallel tracks conflict-free; move it there when convenient).

## Results
- Order independence (H12 exp. 2): 24 random permutations x 2 zoom paths (top-down, or straight to the bodies via an unrecorded parent resolve) over a patch of 14 systems and their ~100 bodies: identical state hash and event count every time. `resolve(i)` equals `resolve_all()[i]` for every child.
- Budget closure (H12 exp. 3): exact at both levels of the fixture; property test over 256 random clouds (0 to 300 children, 0 to 4 pins, flat or tree, any jitter): closes exactly, and over-budget pins are kept with the deficit reported.
- Temporal coherence (identity half of H12 exp. 4): a resolved system read at 20 times in scrambled order has bit-identical facts; phase (unborn, alive, ended) matches its life line.
- Persistence: changing a field's algorithm after recording leaves the recorded facts and child cloud unchanged and appends nothing.
- Not found within budget: a 9-unit budget searches 3 of 30 clouds and reports not-found (never impossible) with nearest clouds; a wish on an undescribed field reports "this cloud says nothing about dragons"; a 3-unit child walk stops with a resume index.
- Queries mutate nothing until `commit_pick` (state hash and length checked).
- Distribution unit tests: normal quantile inverts the cdf to 1e-13 at 999 points; quantile and cdf agree to 1e-9 for uniform, truncated normal, log-normal, two power laws.

## Cost classes and edges
`sse_resolve::ops()`: resolve light, resolve_all medium, location query trivial, outcome query and child search medium and bounded by the caller's work budget (weak devices pass a smaller budget and the result says where it stopped). Work units here are keyed draws.

## Known limits
- Not wired into the kernel message API yet (`resolve`, `query_feasible`); done at the end of this track so the other tracks are not disturbed mid-package.
- Location-first expected count with pins is `max(0, mean - pins)`, an approximation of E[max(0, N - pins)].
- Fields are drawn independently; correlated fields (mass and metallicity, say) are a suite's job via child templates or code-built envelopes.
- The evolutionary envelope at time T (galaxy design) and frames/promotion (G8.3) belong to G0 to G5; this crate supplies the resolve those build on.
