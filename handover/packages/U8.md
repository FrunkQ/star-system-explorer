# U8 part 1: the story finder (19 Nov 2026)

Build plan row U8: "Story finder", check "hits listed with evidence; send to Minerva". Design handoff board
"story finder"; SIFT engine H6.6 (sse-sift sifters with evidence and significance). Second interface track.

## What was built
- **Kernel op `sift`** (one op with the agents' sift, `packages/sift-op.md`) (Light, GM only in the UI): runs the stack's sifters (`provides.other.sifters`, now kept
  by the kernel at load) plus any given inline over every address at or under `scope` (default the universe)
  at `at_s` (default now). Tags are the rule tags plus the GM's `tag.*` facts (with the fact as evidence), as
  scenes see them. Hits come best first with evidence (fact, value, the event that set it) and significance.
  Test `sift_finds_story_hooks_with_their_evidence`; contract cases added and re-blessed on purpose.
- **`ui-contract/src/story.ts`**: types, words for fields and values, and `storyBrief` (a hit as a brief a
  storyteller can start from). `ViewSession.sift` (GM only).
- **View `sse.view.story-finder`**, the **Stories** section of Explore:
  - hits best first: what, where, the score and its four parts (lasts, reaches, can't be undone, rare), the
    evidence with the events behind it;
  - pick the subject on the chart; copy the brief (or show it);
  - the sifters as data (`packages/views/story-finder/src/starters.json`: danger where people live, ruins,
    a GM secret, a rumour, a garden world, something worth taking, something ending within a century), each
    switchable (remembered in this browser), plus the GM's own sifter pasted as JSON, plus any from packs.
- Check: `tests/ui/story.e2e.mjs` (in `ui:e2e`). Screenshots in `U8-screens/`.
