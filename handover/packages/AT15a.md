## What is built
- **Data and rules** `ui-contract/src/delivery.ts`: five moments (`power_on`, `arrive`, `turn`, `leave`, `power_off`); 14 deliveries with words, defaults and the scales each can act at (`DELIVERIES`); `resolveDelivery(look, moment, {first, reducedMotion, back})` gives the plan to play: full form for Power on and a send's first Arrive, the short form after, Turn always short and at most 400 ms, reduced motion a 150 ms fade, wrong-way choices fall back; `checkLookDelivery` says in words what is wrong (for the loader and Orac); `STARTER_DELIVERIES` for Hauler, Corporate, Deck, paper and the Guide.
- **Player** `chart-grammar/src/deliver.ts`: `playDelivery(node, plan)` (Web Animations; a tap or key anywhere finishes it; `data-delivering="<kind>"` while it plays), the Svelte action `use:deliver={plan}` (plays on mount and on each new plan) and the transition `out:deliverOut|global={plan}` (a leaving picture is held where it stood, above its replacement). Three scales: `screen`; `parts` (`[data-part]` children, else direct children, one after another); `inside` (`[data-deliver-item]`, else blocks of text, rows and figures; Type on uses `[data-deliver-text]`).
- **Looks** carry `delivery` (ThemeDoc in `skins/chart-room/src/themes.ts`): Phosphor = Hauler (Warm up then Build up inside, Build up, Type on, none, Collapse to a dot); Clean glass = Corporate; Deck panels = Deck; Paper = paper (Page turn); Field guide = the Guide (stepped wipe). Chart has none and shows the picture as before.
- **Players' screen** `shell/src/Players.svelte` (agreed with the Run desk thread): Power on when the picture first shows or a hold is released, Arrive for each new send, Power off as the GM holds the screen.

- **The paper curl** `chart-grammar/src/page-turn.ts` (25 Nov, part 1b): `curlTurn(page, ±1, swap, {ms})` turns a paper page. A copy of the old page lies over it and peels from the bottom outer corner along a slanted fold that straightens as it crosses, with a small wobble; the lifted part is drawn (SVG) as the pale back of the paper with a highlight along the curl, and casts one blurred shadow on the new page, which is already there to read beneath. Back is the same turn reversed: the previous page comes down over this one. About 0.7 s (`CURL_MS`); a tap or key finishes it; reduced motion just swaps. Pure geometry in `curlAt(u, w, h, eased)` (keeps the paper's area; tested). Page turn alone may run to `PAGE_TURN_MAX_MS` (800 ms) instead of the 400 ms Turn limit. The copies and the flap are laid in the page's parent, made positioned before anything is measured, and placed by their on-screen rects, so a page centred in a static sheet inside an offset box lines up; if the swap replaces the page element (a keyed block) the turn finds the one that took its place; going back, the page coming down is copied again while it is still filling in (fix of 25 Nov after the Atlas's first frames showed the new page 390 px off). A page that arrives without `curlTurn` (plain `playDelivery` with a Page turn plan) gets a quick 300 ms swing.
- **Turning by hand**: `use:pageTurn={{ onstep, sheet }}` on a page. With `sheet` (paper looks) a horizontal drag carries the curl with the finger; it calls `onstep(dir)` as soon as the drag becomes a turn, so the next page is readable beneath; past a third, or flicked onwards, it completes; short (or flicked back) it settles and calls `onstep(-dir)` to put the page back. Without `sheet` a swipe just steps when let go. Links, buttons and fields keep their presses; vertical drags still scroll; reduced motion steps without the curl. Pure maths in `turnDrag` and `turnCommits`.

The page is `article[data-atlas-page]`, blocks `data-deliver-item`, prose `data-deliver-text`. For paper editions add `use:pageTurn={{ sheet: true, onstep }}` on the same article so a swipe turns the page too (its `onstep` may be undone by the opposite step).
