# G3: galaxy reservoirs, rings, element groups, "what died here"

Status: done (19 Nov 2026). Kernel track (cosmos suite), PR into `main`. Design: galaxy design sections 5, 6 and 12.

## What was built
`sse-cosmos::galaxy` (Gx, a regulator or "bathtub" model, stepped every 10 Myr from z = 20 to now):
- Gas supply = baryon fraction x halo growth (G2's `mass_at`) x hot-halo suppression 1 / (1 + (M_h / 5e11)^2)
  x preventive feedback 1 / (1 + (v_c / 60 km/s)^-3) (Dave, Finlator and Oppenheimer 2012) x radio-mode feedback
  1 / (1 + (M_h / 1e12)^2 M_bh / 3e7) (Croton et al. 2006, smooth, no threshold); zero below the filtering mass
  after reionisation (Gnedin 2000).
- Star formation = gas / t_dep, t_dep = 1.5 Gyr (1+z)^-1, longer in small atomic-gas-dominated galaxies
  ((v_c / 150)^-1.5 below 150 km/s; Leroy et al. 2008). Implicit gas step, stable at any step length.
- Winds: eta = (v_c / 200 km/s)^-2 (1+z), capped at 60 (energy-driven slope; Muratov et al. 2015 for the z term).
  Wind material waits in an ejected reservoir and falls back on t = 60 Gyr (1e10 / M_h) through the hot halo
  (Henriques et al. 2013), so small galaxies form their stars late.
- Return fraction 0.4 (instantaneous here; the rings carry the delays), metal yield 0.02.
- Ex-situ growth: major mergers bring ratio^2 x the host's stars into the bulge; in big haloes a smooth term
  (0.3 x 0.02 x halo growth x (1 - hot-halo factor)) counts sinking satellites (Rodriguez-Gomez et al. 2016).
- Black hole: seed 1e5, steady 5e-4 of stars formed plus 1% of each merger burst; each burst is an active nucleus.
- Shape: disc scale length 0.6 x spin / sqrt 2 x R_200 (Mo, Mao and White 1998, simplified); bars by the
  Efstathiou, Lake and Negroponte 1982 criterion; morphology a rule over B/T, sSFR and mass.
- History curves every 50 Myr (`Galaxy::history`, for the time ruler and the galaxy chart).
- `galaxy::record` writes `galaxy.active_nucleus` events (derived, with a "why").

`sse-cosmos::rings` (R, multi-zone chemical evolution, 24 log-spaced rings from 0.1 to 8 disc lengths, 50 Myr steps):
- Infall: the galaxy's inflow shared out over an exponential disc, inside-out with Chiappini et al. 2001's
  tau(R) measured in the galaxy's own clock (share of all inflow so far), so ring timing follows the halo's growth.
  Fallback gas carries half the disc's mean abundances; fresh gas is primordial.
- Star formation: Kennicutt 1998 (Sigma^1.4) with a threshold at 4 solar masses per pc^2.
- Winds: the galaxy's loading times exp(0.6 (R/R_d - 3.15)) (radially rising, Belfiore et al. 2019).
- Inward gas drift 1 km/s (Spitoni and Matteucci 2011).
- Element groups in solar units (Asplund et al. 2009 mass fractions): alpha (O tracer) and prompt iron from core
  collapse; type Ia iron with a 1/t delay from 40 Myr, 2e-3 events per solar mass formed (Maoz and Graur 2017);
  C+N and the s-process from AGB stars, 1/t from 100 Myr; r-process from neutron-star mergers (1e-5 per solar
  mass formed, 1/t from 30 Myr) as a keyed Poisson draw per ring and step (stream `cosmos.rings.nsm.v1`), so it
  is lumpy; K-40 from core collapse, decaying.
- The "what died here" ledger per ring: stars formed per step with the [Fe/H] and [alpha/Fe] they were born from,
  cumulative counts of core-collapse and Ia supernovae and neutron-star mergers, AGB gas returned, present rates.
- Pack: `provides.other.cosmology.galaxy` and `.rings` in `packs/base-cosmology` (every setting listed, sources cited).

## Checks (`cargo test -p sse-cosmos g3`, 11 tests)
Tolerances are factors because the observed relations differ by those factors between calibrations.
- Stellar-to-halo mass (Behroozi et al. 2019, z = 0) within 2.5x at 1e10.5 to 1e14: 0.0011, 0.0084, 0.035, 0.031,
  0.012, 0.007, 0.006 against 0.002, 0.005, 0.015, 0.03, 0.025, 0.016, 0.004. Peak between 3e11 and 1e12. Worst:
  2.35x high at 3e11, 2.2x low at 1e13 (median haloes, no scatter; the peak is too broad on the low side).
- Main sequence (Renzini and Peng 2015) within 3x for star-forming galaxies of 5e9 to 1e11 stars; our z = 0 star
  formation rates run about 1.8x high (Speagle et al. 2014's z = 0 line is lower still).
- Quenching rises with mass and stays: 1e13 haloes quenched, 3e11 star-forming.
- Mass-metallicity (Andrews and Martini 2013) within 3x; low-mass galaxies are the low side (about 2.3x).
- Cosmic star formation history (Madau and Dickinson 2014) within 2.6x from z = 0 to 6, peak between z 1.5 and 4.
  Ratios at z 0, 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2, 3, 4, 6: 1.82, 0.80, 0.49, 0.43, 0.46, 0.75, 1.17, 1.48. Known shape error: the
  peak is too flat and early. Median haloes miss the mean boost from growth scatter.
- Milky-Way-like disc (1e12 halo, R_d 3.1 kpc; radii scaled to the Milky Way's 2.6 kpc): present gas [Fe/H]
  gradient -0.043 dex per kpc over 4 to 14 kpc (band -0.03 to -0.08; Genovali et al. 2014 give -0.06, so ours is
  a little shallow); [Fe/H] of the Sun's ring 4.57 Gyr ago +0.01 (band 0.2 dex); 0.9% of its stars below [Fe/H] = -1
  (no G-dwarf problem); [alpha/Fe] plateau 0.3 to 0.4 falling to about 0.05 at solar; core collapse 3.4 and type Ia
  0.7 per century (Milky Way 1.6 and 0.54: this galaxy forms stars about 1.7x as fast).
- r-process spread between neighbouring dwarf rings at least twice iron's.
- Determinism: galaxies and rings identical on rerun; pack settings equal the defaults.
- Cross-device: golden `tests/golden/galaxy.json` (new, on purpose: dwarf, Milky-Way-like and group galaxies with
  their rings, digests of full histories, chronicle head), harness engine `galaxy`.

## Known limits
- Galaxies run on one halo history each (median or drawn); no satellites as separate galaxies yet, no scatter in
  growth beyond G2's per-halo scatter. Environment acts only through the halo.
- Inner rings (inside about 2 kpc) are bulge-like and very metal-rich (+0.7); no bar-driven inflow or bulge
  chemistry yet.
- Yields are IMF-integrated net yields in solar units, tuned with the wind settings so the solar ring is solar
  at the Sun's birth; the pack says so. Yield tables by stellar mass (Nomoto et al. 2013, Karakas 2010) and
  lifetime functions are a later refinement behind the same ring interface.
- Al-26 needs a nearby supernova within a megayear of a birth cluster: it belongs to G4 and G5.
- The galaxy chart UI with history curves is the UI track's (data is in `Galaxy::history` and `Disc`).

## Integration points
- G4 reads ring abundances, death rates and gas at a patch's radius (`Disc::ring_at`, `feh_history`).
- Formation (F track) can take [Fe/H], [alpha/Fe], C/O and r-process abundances from a ring instead of G0's
  envelope.
- Shared files touched: `crates/sse-wasm` (engine `galaxy`), the harness lists, `packs/base-cosmology`.

## Later change (19 Nov 2026): holes arrive with ex-situ stars
`exsitu_black_hole` (1e-3) adds the merging galaxies' holes with every ex-situ star, so the biggest galaxies have holes of 0.1% of their bulges. See `packages/G-nuclei.md`, section "G3 change", for the numbers and the goldens that moved.
