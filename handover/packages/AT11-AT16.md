# AT11 and AT16: Orac and Minerva draft for the Atlas and for player interfaces (track A's half)

Design: `handover/design/SSE4-atlas-and-reports-design.md` sections 6.4, 11A.7, 12 and 12.8. The atlas thread
("Build star atlas and editions") owns the documents and their loaders; this track calls those loaders as its checks.

Status: done (24 Nov 2026, agents track). Limits are listed under "Not yet".

## Orac's three targets, `packages/agents/src/orac-atlas.ts`
- `kit` (AT16): `checkKit` over a stack of the shipped and campaign kits, parts, looks, editions, vocabs and palettes.
  The check is "Hauler but amber": `{extends: "sse.kit.hauler", palettes: ["phosphor-amber"]}` passes every check.
  An invented palette name comes back in the loader's words, and Orac fixes it.
- `edition` (AT11): first `checkEdition`, then `testRender`. The test render draws the edition for the GM's and the
  table's scenes beside the Survey atlas. The summary says how many audiences were drawn and that every figure matched.
- `note_set` (AT11): `checkNoteSet`. A figure must be a slot that its `when` tests, so a typed figure is refused.
- Ids: a shipped id (`sse.*`) is never overwritten, and an id that does not start with `mine.` is refused with an
  example. These run before the loader's checks.
- Accept keeps the document in the campaign pack `campaign.atlas`, under `provides.other.atlas: {editions, note_sets,
  kits}`. The pack's kind is `presentation`, and it is stacked through the kernel like any other pack. Each keep bumps
  the version, and an edit replaces the document with the same id. This is the seam agreed with the atlas thread, whose
  `atlasDocsOf(packs)` reads the pack back.
- Parts are the real ones: the shell passes `partsOf([...PLAYER_STACK, the Atlas with ATLAS_CONTROLS])` (AT13's
  declared controls), so a kit can switch only a control a part declares. Kits may carry `banners` from a note set.
- Catalogue (`atlasEntries`): every edition (`edition:<id>`), note set (`notes:<id>`) and kit (`kit:<id>`), plus
  `palettes`, `parts` (with their controls, and GM-only parts marked) and `looks`.
- Orac's handbook has a new `interfaces` passage, and the `tools` passage names these targets. The Tars shelf and the
  Orac tab's DONE messages name them too.

## Minerva's Guide's note (AT11, Alex 24 Nov: "never live on a player's screen")
- On the Minerva tab, "A Guide's margin note" sets `length: "note"`. A note is one line of at most 200 characters
  (`NOTE_MAX`, atlas design 6.2), and a longer one goes back to her with its length. Her numbers come only from the
  audience's facts, as for every Minerva text.
- "Keep as a Guide's note" stores it as a GM-only narrative record note tagged `guide-note`. The GM decides whether
  it ever reaches a player.
