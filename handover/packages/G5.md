# G5: stars resolved from a patch, moving through time

Status: done (19 Nov 2026). Kernel track, PR into `main`. Design: galaxy design, section 14 row G5.

## What was built
`sse-cosmos::stars`. `Stars{env, green, params, seed, galaxy}` turns a G4 patch envelope into individual star systems, drawn only when looked at.
- **Cells.** Space is cut into 10 pc cells and time into 1 Myr frames; the fill is drawn afresh per (cell, frame). The grid co-rotates with the disc (`cell_rotation_kms_kpc` 26.83, about 220 km/s at 8.2 kpc), so stars stay in their cell over Gyr instead of streaming through an inertial grid. `cell_at(xyz, t)`, `from_grid`, `frame_of`, `cell_address`.
- **Counts and draws.** `expected(cell)` from the envelope's stellar density; `draw_all` uses keyed streams `cosmos.stars.count.v1` (Poisson count per cell) and `cosmos.stars.v1` (each star), so a cell always gives the same stars whatever else was drawn.
- **Each system:** birth time from the ring's star formation history, mass from a Kroupa IMF quantile (`imf_quantile`, from 0.08 solar masses), abundances from the gas at birth plus scatter, multiplicity by primary mass (`multiplicity_at`), companions, and an `Orbit` (epicyclic, with a vertical amplitude that grows with age) so `Orbit::at(t)` and `xyz` give position at any time.
- **State at any age:** `StarParams::state` gives phase (main sequence, giant, white dwarf, ...), luminosity, radius and Teff analytically from mass, age and `lifetime_gyr`.
- **Pins.** `cell(cell, frame, pins)` places pinned (authored or catalogue) stars and keeps the drawn fill consistent around them. `promote` makes a drawn star a named system; `at(t)` gives `SystemAt`.
- **Formation input.** `birth(..)` returns the `SystemBirth` the F track's formation pipeline takes (mass, metallicity, environment), so a picked green-region star can be formed into a system.
- Pack: `cosmology.stars` in `packs/base-cosmology` with every default and `sources.stars`. Golden `tests/golden/stars.json`, harness engine `stars`.

## Checks (`cargo test -p sse-cosmos g5`, 7 tests)
- **Sun's neighbourhood** (10 pc): 404 systems against RECONS's 317 (Henry et al. 2018; test band x/÷1.6, from G4's 0.099 systems per pc^3), M dwarfs 0.863 of the main sequence, white dwarfs 0.042 of systems (about 0.06 observed), multiple systems 0.245 (about 0.3), mean age 5.38 Gyr.
- **Disc heating:** vertical amplitude young 179 pc, old 416 pc (older stars wander further).
- **Order independence:** cells drawn in any order give identical stars.
- **Identity through time:** the same star keeps its id and properties at 20 times over 10 Gyr.
- **Pins:** 240 cells, 264 pin visits, every pin exact and in its cell.
- **Birth feeds formation:** `birth` output is accepted by the formation input.
- **Pack and golden:** the pack's values equal `StarParams::default()`; golden matches.

## Discrepancies and known limits
- **M-dwarf share 0.86 against about 0.75 observed.** The Kroupa IMF from 0.08 solar masses with no correction for unresolved binaries gives more M dwarfs than RECONS. The test band is 0.65 to 0.9; a pack lower mass cut or binary-merged counting would close it.
- **No sibling groups:** each system draws a birth cluster mass (M^-2, 50 to 1e5 solar masses; it sets the formation FUV field), but stars are independent draws per cell, so open clusters and moving groups of siblings sharing age and metals are not modelled.
- **404 against 317:** the count follows G4's local density; 27% high, inside the band.
- **No radial migration:** orbits are epicyclic about a fixed guiding radius.
- **Analytic stellar evolution,** not tracks: phases and luminosities are fits, good for display and fates, not for detailed spectra.
- **Fixed rotating grid:** one pattern speed; spiral arms and bar resonances do not move stars between cells.

## Integration points
- UI galaxy and patch views call `cell`/`draw_all` for what is in view; `promote` when the GM picks a star.
- F track: `birth` into `run_formation`.
- T track: `state` at a target age for look-ahead of a star's fate.
- Shared files touched: `crates/sse-wasm` (engine `stars`), the harness lists, `packs/base-cosmology`.
