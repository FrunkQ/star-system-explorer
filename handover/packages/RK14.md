# RK14 fog of war from sensors, light side (part 1)

Track RK (Atomic Rockets plan section 17, accepted 23 Nov 2026), light thread. Block side (grants on
events, the chart's marks) is the kernel and chart room threads'.

## What landed (24 Nov)

- Four Equations in `packs/base-physics/formulas/fog.json` (family `sse.fog`):
  - `sse.fog.radiator_area`: A = P / (ε σ T⁴), Rybicki and Lightman §1.5.
  - `sse.fog.plume_temperature`: T = (γ − 1) m v² / (2 γ k), Sutton and Biblarz chapter 3.
  - `sse.fog.plume_light`: L = f P_jet, with f = 0.01 a stand-in (F56).
  - `sse.fog.last_seen_ring`: r = v t + ½ a t².
- `sse_sense::fog`:
  - `signature(craft, model)`: the craft's light from its power flow (the ledger's `heat_by_k` as
    radiators, its plumes, transmitters, sunlit hull). Nothing is set up by hand.
  - `base_fits()` and `default_fit(fits, tech, hull_length_m)`: the free passive set from base-light's
    `sensing.default_fits` (tech `today`: visible camera, cooled infrared telescope, soft X-ray
    monitor, radio receiver; apertures a share of the hull, between a floor and a cap; zodiacal glow
    at 1 au as background).
  - `mark(contact, sensors, model)`: `ink` (detected and resolved), `dotted` (detected only) or
    `unseen`; which instruments did it; how far it would be noticed; the light delay; the last-seen
    ring when it was lost; why-lines.
  - `noticed_to(craft, sensors, model)`: the crew's own "a craft like ours would be noticed out to
    here" ring (section 17.1), which swells when the drive is lit.
- Kernel op `fog_of_war {seats: [{seat, sensors | fit, own?, contacts}]}` (Cost::Light, light perf chunk);
  errors are `bad_seat`.
- Tests: `fog_tests.rs` (4) and kernel `fog_of_war_marks_contacts_for_a_seat`. Fudge F56.
- Golden: the base-physics and base-light pack hashes, the op list and the two new contract cases.
