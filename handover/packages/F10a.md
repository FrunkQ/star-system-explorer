# F10a: the Formation screen's chart (the reading half, part 1)

Design: `design/SSE4-system-formation-design.md` section 7. Thread "Build the Formation screen", 26 Nov 2026.

## What it is
"How it formed" on the system rail (group Understand; Explore and Build desks, not Run, as the three desks note's section 5 row "How it formed" says). It opens for the system in view.

- **Chart** (`views/formation/src/FormationChart.svelte`, model `ui-contract/src/formation-chart.ts`): time since the star lit down the page and distance across, both log, in whole decades. Each body of the run is a track sampled at every recorded moment and every tenth of a decade, its width the cube root of its mass at the time (`massWidth`). A merging body's track joins its target with a short dashed rule at the impact; a thrown-out one runs to the right edge with an arrow; a shattered one ends in a cross. The gas disc is hatched until the gas leaves; the five condensation lines lean in while it lasts (the water line in the accent). Belts are hatched strips from when they formed; the Oort cloud a tint at the right edge. Events that involve a body are diamonds (violent ones in the accent) with a few words where they do not collide; the full sentence is the title. The upheaval (break to settling) is a tinted span.
- **Time slice**: the rule across. It is the time ruler's date: drag it (or the arrow keys on its handle, Shift for half a decade, Home and End) and on release the ruler and every other view move; move the ruler and the slice follows. A stage button puts the slice at that stage's start. Moving it reads nothing again (the record is read once per system).
- **Stage strip** and a one-sentence account of the stage in force, from the run (`replay.ts` stages).
- **Inherited** panel: star and companions, metals (× Sun from [Fe/H]), carbon to oxygen, disc mass and size, how long the gas lasts, the birth cluster's glare, aluminium-26, the seed; and "Worked out at" (growth Standard or Classic Accrete, violent phase drawn, orbit windows not here). Each row's title says it in full.
- A system that was imported or written fact by fact says it has no formation history, and that a system grown with Make a system keeps one. A grown system with no worlds says why (`noWorldsWhy`). If the formation rules changed since the system was adopted, the screen says the re-grown history may differ in detail.

Everything is read through the replay's reader (`replayModel`, `replayAt`), so the chart, "Watch it form" and the system chart agree.

## Engine: one read-only op
`formation_record { system }` (`crates/sse-kernel/src/formation_record.rs`): a grown system keeps its formation request on its `adopt.formation` event, but no op handed it back. The op re-runs that request through the same pure pipeline as `run_formation` and returns `{system, source: "formation", request, adopted_manifest, record}` (`record` is the `formation_ran` answer); an imported (`v3`) or authored (`facts`) system gets `why` and no record; an address with nothing under it is `unknown_system`. Nothing stored is read differently or changed. Contract area `formation` (`tests/contract/areas/formation.json`) covers the authored and error answers; it does not play a whole run, so formation pipeline changes do not move it.

## Checks
- `crates/sse-kernel/src/formation_record_tests.rs`: the re-run equals the run grown fresh, reading writes nothing, v3 and unknown answers.
- `packages/ui-contract/test/formation-chart.test.ts` (in `ui:test`): the lasting tracks are the run's worlds at their orbits, tracks never lose mass, merged and ejected ends, the water line moves in, the nine stages, the slice's stage, the inherited rows in words, the log scales.
- `tests/ui/formation.e2e.mjs` (new CI line): Kestrel says it has no history; grow and add "first light", open How it formed: tracks, lines, gas, upheaval, events, inherited rows; a stage moves the slice and the chart stays up; the slice handle takes keys; Enter on a track selects the world; no sideways scroll on a phone; the state hash does not move.
