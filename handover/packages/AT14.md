# AT14: interface kits as data (`sse.kit/1`), palettes, and the four starters as documents

Track AT (atlas design sections 11A.2 to 11A.5, 11A.7 and 12.7). Built 24 Nov 2026.

## What it is
- `ui-contract/src/atlas/kit.ts`: the interface kit document `sse.kit/1` and its pure loader checks.
  - A kit names a `look`, its `palettes` (shipped ids or inline colour sets), a `view_style` (the shared
    `VIEW_STYLES` of `view-controls.ts`), an Atlas `edition` (and an optional look for it to wear), a `delivery` per
    moment (`power_on`, `arrive`, `turn`, `leave`, `power_off`, from the toolkit in `DELIVERIES`), `needs.tier` with a
    `falls_back_to` kit, and a `layouts` entry per situation (`sector`, `system`, `world`, `ship`, `landing`,
    `holding`). A layout picks an arrangement (`ARRANGEMENTS`, each with its areas), puts parts in areas and switches
    each part's controls on or off (`"all"`, `"none"`, or a map; unstated controls take the part's default).
  - `extends` takes a starter (or any kit) and changes only what it states; a layout may extend another situation.
    `resolveKit(id, docs)` follows the chain and refuses a loop.
  - `checkKit(doc, stack)` returns sentences that say what to change: unknown extends, look, palette, edition,
    vocab or part; a figure in the kit's words (a kit never states a fact); a Turn over 0.4 s; a delivery not in the
    toolkit; a GPU kit with no fallback; a part in an area its arrangement lacks; a control a part does not declare;
    a GM-only part on a player's screen; an unknown situation. Empty means it loads.
  - `checkPalette(p)`: WCAG contrast of `ink`, `ink-muted` and `accent-ink` on `ground` (and `ground-raised` when
    given) must be 4.5 to 1. `contrastOf(a, b)` is exported for swatches.
  - `controlsOn(item, part, defaults)` gives the controls a part shows in a layout.
- `ui-contract/src/atlas/kits/starters.json`: ten shipped palettes (phosphor green, amber, white; glass blue,
  graphite; deck amber-violet, teal-blue, red alert; guide night, day) and the four starters: **Hauler**
  (phosphor, wireframe, Company edition, text tier), **Corporate** (glass, True 3D, gpu-basic, falls back to Deck),
  **Deck** (deck, Chart, canvas, falls back to Hauler) and **The Guide** (guide gadget, Guide edition, stepped wipe,
  text tier).
- The same checks serve a starter, a pack's kit and an Orac draft (11A.7): Orac's thread can call `checkKit` and
  `checkPalette` from `ui-contract/src/atlas/index.ts`.

- `ui-contract/src/atlas/docs.ts`: `atlasDocsOf(packs) -> {editions, note_sets, kits, refused}` gathers the Atlas's
  documents from every pack in the stack under `provides.other.atlas` (three lists of whole documents). A later pack's
  document replaces an earlier one with the same id. Orac's accepted drafts go in the campaign pack `campaign.atlas`
  with ids starting `mine.` (agreed with the Orac thread).

- Reconciled with AT13 (PR 485, 25 Nov): arrangements are the layout editor's own (`ARRANGEMENTS` in
  `ui-contract/src/layouts.ts`: one, side, four, report, blank, with their areas); a part's controls come from its
  `switchable` declaration through `partsOf(views)`; switching on or off is `resolveControls` and `controlOn` in
  `part-controls.ts` (this package's own `controlsOn` is gone). The starters name only real controls (chart `select`,
  table `sort` and `select`, card `open`, 3D `turn`). The Atlas view declares `ATLAS_CONTROLS` (contents, step,
  print, links; no edition, 14A note 6) and draws only those on. `tests/ui/kit-parts.test.ts` checks the starters
  against the real declarations.
- `banners: {at, sets}` names the one-liners at the top and bottom of the role's screens (AT7, 25 Nov).

## Not in this package
- "Interface" on the role card, choosing a kit in one press, and the swatches: the Run desk thread (AT13's role
  card). This package gives it `resolveKit`, `checkKit`, `SHIPPED_PALETTES` and `contrastOf`.
- Wearing a kit on screen (layouts arranged, deliveries played): AT15, with AT15a's `delivery.ts`.
- The looks `deck`, `phosphor` and `glass` are named by the starters; a look the stack does not hold is refused by
  `checkKit` with the stack the caller passes, so the shell decides which looks are present.

## Checks
- `node --test packages/ui-contract/test/kit.test.ts`: the four starters load against a parts stub; every shipped
  palette passes contrast; extends (a salvage console from Hauler, `world` extends `system`, a loop refused); every
  refusal sentence; `controlsOn` for none, all and a map.
- `npm run ui:check`, `npm run ui:test`.
