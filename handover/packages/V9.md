# V9 part 1: the cosmic web (foam map), under Explore (19 Nov 2026)

Build plan row V9: "Map views: cluster, galaxy, foam". Galaxy design sections 2 and 11 (scale up to filaments,
simple-maths 3D foam). Second interface track. Part 1 is the foam; the cluster map and a galaxy-scale map of
neighbours come with U6 part 2 (patches).

## What was built
- **View `sse.view.foam-map`** (`packages/views/foam-map`), the **Cosmic web** section of the Explore tab:
  - a square slice of the web from the kernel's `web_slice` (added in U6 part 1): 50, 100, 200 or 400 comoving
    Mpc a side, 96 samples a side (param `samples`, 16 to 160);
  - drawn on a canvas in the theme's own tokens (ink, ground, accent; redrawn when the theme changes): matter as
    dots where the Zel'dovich approximation has moved it, over the density (log) or the environment (void,
    sheet, filament, knot in accent);
  - a time slider (0.6 Gyr to today, 12 steps) and Play, which grows each frame before showing it, so the foam
    sharpens in front of you; frames are kept per slice size (deterministic, so caching is safe);
  - the ledger: share of each environment, density spread, growth factor, scale factor, how to read the map and
    the model's limits.

## Checks
- `tests/ui/foam.e2e.mjs` (added to `ui:e2e`): drawn, all four environments present, contrast lower at 1 Gyr than
  today, Play reaches today, environment shading, 100 Mpc slice, night chart darkens the ground, phone has no
  sideways scroll. `ui:check` clean.
- Screenshots: `V9-screens/`.

## Honest limits
- Linear growth plus Zel'dovich displacement: right in pattern, wrong in detail inside knots (no shell crossing,
  no haloes). The kernel's own model line says so on the map.
- Octaves are capped at 4 in the kernel op for speed; finer structure below a few Mpc is missing.
- Comoving distances only; the slice does not show the universe getting bigger.
- The slice is centred on the origin of the universe's web; centring on the campaign's galaxy waits for G7.

# V9 part 2: the cluster map ("Galaxies nearby")

## What it does
- New kernel op `halo_cell {cell?, min_mass_msun?, limit?}` (`sse-kernel/src/cosmos.rs`, cost Medium): one G2 halo
  cell (8 Mpc a side at pack defaults) with its haloes biggest first (address, key, mass, comoving position,
  half-mass time, spin, merger and major-merger counts, last major merger, nearest neighbour) and the mass-function
  bins (expected and drawn). Defaults: cell 0.0.0, floor 1e10 (the catalogue's), at most 300. No merger trees are
  sent.
- `galaxy_chart` takes `cell` with `halo: "halo:N"` (or the full address): it charts that catalogued halo with its
  own growth history and mergers instead of the median halo of a mass. Unknown haloes give `unknown_halo`.
- View `packages/views/cluster-map` (`sse.view.cluster-map`, params `min_mass`, `web`), the Explore section
  "Galaxies nearby": haloes seen from above, sized by the cube root of mass, the biggest in accent, dashed lines
  between bound pairs, over a 48 x 48 `web_slice` through the cube's middle. Step to neighbouring cells on x, y, z;
  raise the floor. The ledger shows the picked halo's history and a mass-function chart (bars drawn, ticks
  expected).
- "Chart this galaxy" selects the halo's address in the workspace and asks Explore to show Galaxy (a bubbling
  `sse:explore` event with `{section}`; any section can use it). The Galaxy chart charts any catalogued halo
  address that is selected (`cellHalo()` in `ui-contract/src/galaxy.ts`), and its picker gains that halo.

## Checks
- Kernel test `a_cell_lists_its_haloes_and_one_can_be_charted`; contract cases for `halo_cell` (a floor of 3e11
  with limit 5, and a refused limit 0). `contract.jsonl` re-blessed on purpose for these; no engine golden changed.
- `tests/ui/cluster.e2e.mjs` (in `ui:e2e`): haloes drawn, biggest marked and picked, mass function, another halo
  picked, neighbour cell and back, a higher floor shows fewer, Chart this galaxy opens that halo, back keeps it
  picked, night chart, phone without sideways scroll.
- Screenshots: `V9-screens/v9-cluster*.png`.

## Honest limits
- The map flattens the cube's height: every halo shows whatever its z; the web behind is one slice through the
  middle, so a halo near the top or bottom may sit on a pale patch. At 8 Mpc the 4-octave web is smooth, so the
  shading is faint.
- Haloes are dark matter; galaxies are only grown when charted (one galaxy_chart each), so the map shows no
  morphology yet.
- The cell grid starts at the web's origin; which cell holds the campaign's own galaxy waits for G7.

## Next

## The place line's side (22 Nov, after U24)
The galaxy chart, the cluster map and the foam map are scales on the place line (U24). Each now tells the shell
where it is and opens on a linked place. `packages/ui-contract/src/place-event.ts` holds the contract:
- **"sse:place"**, a bubbling event from the view's root, `detail: {scale, id, name, children?}`, sent whenever the
  place in view changes (`announcePlace`).
- **`place`**, a string param on each view, read at mount; it wins over the GM's selection and the screen's own
  params.
- The ids:
  - **web:** `web:<side in Mpc>` (the foam map has no centre control yet, so the side is the whole place). It
    carries no children: the galaxies nearby are the scale below.
  - **cluster:** `cell:x.y.z` (`cellId`, `parseCellId`). Its children are the cell's 24 biggest haloes, biggest
    first, by the kernel's halo address (`…/cell:x.y.z/halo:n`, named "Halo n"). A `place` of a halo address opens
    its cell with that halo picked.
  - **galaxy:** a reference halo's key (`milky-way-like`, `dwarf`, `group`) or a catalogued halo's address, named as
    the chart's heading. No children until systems are placed in galaxies (GH).
- Checks: `place-event.test.ts` (ids round-trip), and the galaxy, foam and cluster e2e listen for the event (first
  place, a changed halo, extent or cell, and the cluster's children). The `place` param is not yet passed by the
  shell, so no browser test opens a view on one; the chart room thread wires the hash to it.

## Shared view controls: Scale on the foam map (23 Nov)

- Alex's ruling (23 Nov) is one set of view controls at every scale. The foam map now declares `FOAM_CONTROLS`
  (`ui-contract/src/map-controls.ts`): Chart from above, with Readable and Real. It is not `flat`, because a flat
  chart is always Readable.
- Real draws the slice at its physical size at the slider's time. The comoving slice is shrunk by `scale_factor`
  about its centre, so at 1 Gyr (a ≈ 0.15) the web is a small square and it swells as Play runs. The scale bar keeps
  its length and now reads "across then"; Readable reads "comoving". "Reading the map" says which one is shown.
- HUD is listed but greyed, with its reason (the foam has nothing to hide). The cluster map stays `flatChart`: it
  shows today only, where Real and Readable are the same.
- e2e: `tests/ui/foam.e2e.mjs` switches to Real at 1 Gyr and back again.
