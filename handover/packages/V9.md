# V9 part 1: the cosmic web (foam map), under Explore (19 Nov 2026)

Build plan row V9: "Map views: cluster, galaxy, foam". Galaxy design sections 2 and 11 (scale up to filaments,
simple-maths 3D foam). Second interface track. Part 1 is the foam; the cluster map and a galaxy-scale map of
neighbours come with U6 part 2 (patches).

## What was built
- **View `sse.view.foam-map`** (`packages/views/foam-map`), the **Cosmic web** section of the Explore tab:
  - a square slice of the web from the kernel's `web_slice` (added in U6 part 1): 50, 100, 200 or 400 comoving
    Mpc a side, 96 samples a side (param `samples`, 16 to 160);
  - drawn on a canvas in the theme's own tokens (ink, ground, accent; redrawn when the theme changes): matter as
    dots where the Zel'dovich approximation has moved it, over the density (log) or the environment (void,
    sheet, filament, knot in accent);
  - a time slider (0.6 Gyr to today, 12 steps) and Play, which grows each frame before showing it, so the foam
    sharpens in front of you; frames are kept per slice size (deterministic, so caching is safe);
  - the ledger: share of each environment, density spread, growth factor, scale factor, how to read the map and
    the model's limits.

## Checks
- `tests/ui/foam.e2e.mjs` (added to `ui:e2e`): drawn, all four environments present, contrast lower at 1 Gyr than
  today, Play reaches today, environment shading, 100 Mpc slice, night chart darkens the ground, phone has no
  sideways scroll. `ui:check` clean.
- Screenshots: `V9-screens/`.

## Honest limits
- Linear growth plus Zel'dovich displacement: right in pattern, wrong in detail inside knots (no shell crossing,
  no haloes). The kernel's own model line says so on the map.
- Octaves are capped at 4 in the kernel op for speed; finer structure below a few Mpc is missing.
- Comoving distances only; the slice does not show the universe getting bigger.
- The slice is centred on the origin of the universe's web; centring on the campaign's galaxy waits for G7.
