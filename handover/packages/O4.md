# O4 part 1: forbidden lines in glowing gas (light track, 25 Nov 2026)

Asked for by MATCH1 (renderer thread, famous pictures against SSE4 postcards): the false-colour nebula pictures
(Pillars of Creation, Helix, Butterfly) are made from H-alpha, [O III] and [S II]. The band mix itself already
existed as data (the `sho` and `hoo` recipes in `packages/shell/src/documents/light.recipes.json`, mode
`composite` over line channels). What was missing was the metal lines: nebulae gave only H-alpha and H-beta.

## What was built

- Four formula blocks in `packs/base-physics/formulas/nebular.json` (family `sse.nebular`); the atom count goes to 143:
  - `sse.nebular.collisional_excitation`: q₁₂ and q₂₁ for an electron knock to a line's upper level, from the
    effective collision strength and the level weights; β is worked from the sheet's h, k and mₑ;
  - `sse.nebular.ion_zone_share`: photon counting, ion by ion, against the gas that takes those photons;
  - `sse.nebular.photoionisation_cross_section`: the Verner et al. 1996 fit;
  - `sse.nebular.ion_balance`: an element's split between two ions where starlight strips and electrons rejoin.
  The loss to knocks before a line shines reuses `sse.glow.line_survival` (one mechanism with the aurorae).
- base-light lines gain `nebular` (`makes_ev`, `strips_ev`, `upsilon` (effective collision strength), `lower_weight`, `upper_weight`,
  `rate_s`, `upper_cm`, `share`, optional `balance {fit, recombination_m3_s}`, `source`) on `oiii`, `oiii-4959`,
  `sii`, `sii-6731`; and a `photon_takers` list (hydrogen from 13.6 eV, helium from 24.6 eV).
- `sse_light::LineEmission` gains `zone: Option<Zone {inner, outer}>`: where in the glowing gas the line comes from,
  as shares of the way through it (0 facing the stars, 1 at the outer edge; of the radius for a filled sphere, of the
  thickness for a shell). Lines without a zone come from all of it.
- `crates/sse-nebula/src/forbidden.rs` `add_forbidden_lines`: every line with a `nebular` row, for any glowing gas,
  with abundances from the sheet's protosolar row. Planetary nebulae (`Layout::Shell`) and star-forming glows
  (`Layout::Sphere`, cut into 32 cells for the balanced ions) both call it; each adds a Why sentence and the
  references. `read_nebulae` carries the new lines with no change to the op.

## Checks (`crates/sse-nebula/src/forbidden_tests.rs`)

- The sulphur pair is a density gauge: 6716/6731 is 1.49 at 10 cm⁻³, 1.25 at 10³, 0.48 at 10⁵ (textbook 1.45 to 0.44).
- An O star's glow (40,000 K, 10 R☉, 100 cm⁻³): [O III] 5007/Hβ 2.0 (Orion about 3), [S II]/Hα 0.15, [O III] in
  the middle (0.09 to 0.46 of the radius), [S II] at the rim (from 0.62 out). 5007/4959 in power is 3 × 4959/5007.
- A 25,000 K B star: [O III]/Hβ 0.05.
- A planetary nebula round a 100,000 K core: [O III]/Hβ 9.3 (real ones about 5 to 20), with its triply ionised core
  inside the [O III] layer.
- Abundances read from the sheet; an unknown element errors.

## Goldens

`tests/golden/nebulae.json` and the kernel goldens that hold nebula descriptors change on purpose: every glowing
nebula now carries [O III] and [S II] lines with zones, their Why sentence and references.

## For the renderer (MATCH1)

Under a composite recipe each channel takes its line's `power_w`; each line's `zone` weights the march along the
radius, so the colours separate by depth. Visible stays eye colour from the same lines. Webb's infrared Ring and
the Crab's synchrotron light are later packages.
