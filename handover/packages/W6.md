# W6: the swap, proven: a zonal energy-balance block and the save-side swap (19 Nov 2026)

Weather design sections 1, 6 ("Why saves do not break") and 8, stage W6. Crate `sse-weather`: new `zonal.rs` (the second
block), `swap.rs` (manifest, registry, swap report) and `swap_tests.rs`; `lib.rs` gains only the two `pub mod` lines. Pack
key `provides.other.weather_zonal` appended to `packs/base-weather/pack.json` (no other key touched). New golden
`tests/golden/weather-swap.json`. The contract (`contract.rs`) is unchanged.

## What was built
- `ZonalBlock`, `sse.weather.zonal-ebm` v1, level `simulated`, evidence `calculated`. A deliberately different block behind
  the same `WeatherBlock` trait: a one-dimensional diffusive energy-balance model in latitude (North 1975),
  C dT/dt = Q(φ)(1 − α(T)) − (A + B T) + D (1/cos φ) ∂/∂φ (cos φ ∂T/∂φ).
  - Q(φ) is the daily-mean sunlight at the season's declination. Each of 12 seasons is solved to its steady state by 80
    relaxed (0.5) tridiagonal solves (Thomas algorithm), in f64 with `sse_core::math` only, so the answer is the same on
    every device. The annual climate is the mean of the 12 seasonal states; a field on a date uses that date's own season.
  - OLR is linear in T (Budyko 1969). B is the world's blackbody slope 4 OLR / T times a dimensionless 0.47 taken from
    Budyko's Earth fit. A is set each iteration so that the global mean temperature is the world's own `t_surface_k`. The
    ice-albedo step on water worlds is renormalised so the sunlight-weighted albedo stays the world's Bond albedo. So the
    model moves heat and ice between latitudes, but leaves how warm or bright a world is unchanged.
  - D = 0.45 W m⁻² K⁻¹ on Earth (equator-to-pole contrast 43 K), scaled by air column × (Ω_Earth/Ω)², capped at 10⁴ B.
    Venus, Titan and the locked world come out near-isothermal; Mars and the 6 h Earth come out steep.
  - Rain and cloud come from the block's own temperatures, not from the rule block's wetness bumps. Evaporation is bulk
    ρ C_E|U| q_sat(T)(1 − RH), capped by the energy reaching wet ground. Moisture (L q / c_p) diffuses down its own
    gradient, and rain is evaporation plus convergence (clipped at zero, with water conserved). Cloud is
    0.8 (1 − e^(−L P / 25 W m⁻²)) plus a baroclinic term from |dT/dφ|. With no surface liquid, frost cloud forms where a gas
    in the air saturates at the band temperature (Mars: water at the cold poles).
  - Shared with the rule block, and read from the same `weather` pack key: the substances (Clausius-Clapeyron), the chemical
    deck and haze rules (Venus acid deck, Titan haze, which are chemistry rather than weather), the regime classification
    (published as `regime`), the sunlight-at-ground form and the wet-area factor.
  - Systems use one simple rule: a cyclone every 3 days in each hemisphere at the steepest temperature gradient poleward of
    20°, on worlds with a solar day under 48 h that are not star-locked. Lon is a keyed draw
    (`weather.zonal_cyclones.v1`), so a storm has the same id and track from any date. Pins are honoured exactly as the rule
    block honours them (a pin replaces the drawn system of the same id; `authored` stays authored). GM `order-clear` and
    `order-calm` act on the field; pinned and triggered storms put thick cloud and rain over their cores.
- `swap.rs`:
  - `WeatherManifest { block: BlockId, pins: Vec<WorldPin>, authored: Vec<WorldPin> }` is what a save stores. A `WorldPin`
    is `{ world: Address, pin: Pin }`, because a campaign spans many worlds and `Pin` carries no address. A save holds no
    bulk weather.
  - `registry()` lists the two blocks. `block_for(stack, &BlockId)` builds them by id and version. An unknown id or
    version is an error, never a stand-in, so an old save keeps its old block.
  - `compare(from, to, conditions)` → `WorldDiff`: mean cloud (absolute) and mean precipitation (relative) against
    `contract::TOLERANCE`, plus condensate, `rain_cells` and the largest single-band cloud change, all as plain lines.
  - `swap(stack, manifest, worlds, to)` → `SwapReport`. It gives the shown difference per world, carries every pin over and
    checks it: on its birth day and mid-life, the new block's `systems()` must contain the same id, kind, track, born and
    ends. It also gives `within_tolerance`, `pins_kept` and the new manifest. The swap itself never fails on a difference: it
    shows it and the GM decides.
  - `weather_digest(stack, manifest, world, days, tile, n)`: a hash of climate, field and systems, used for the "restored
    exactly" check.

## Results (golden `weather-swap.json`; cloud is mean cover, rain is mean m/yr)
| World | Rule cloud | Zonal cloud | Rule rain | Zonal rain | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Earth | 0.63 | 0.59 | 1.012 | 0.878 (−13 %) | within |
| Mars | 0.10 | 0.04 | 0 | 0 | within (cloud is water frost at the poles in both) |
| Venus | 1.00 | 1.00 | 0 | 0 | within (same chemical deck rule) |
| Titan | 0.00 | 0.00 | 0.004 | 0.004 | within (both energy-limited) |
| Locked temperate world | 0.58 | 0.25 | 0.705 | 0.137 (−81 %) | **outside tolerance, reported** |
| Fast-spinning Earth (6 h) | 0.70 | 0.63 | 1.012 | 0.871 | within |
| Moon | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | within |
| Jupiter | 0.10 | 0.00 | 0 | 0 | within by 0.006 only; the EBM is not a giant model (see limits) |

Shown differences besides the means: Earth's cloud moves between latitudes by up to 0.32 in a band, because the EBM has no
Hadley cell, so its wettest belt is broad and equatorial and its subtropics are not dry. On Mars and Jupiter `rain_cells`
changes (2 → 1, 16 → 1). Earth keeps 3.

Which worlds, stated plainly: Earth, Mars, Venus and Titan agree within tolerance. The locked temperate world does not, and
cannot. Its rain falls under the substellar storm on the hot day side, which a zonal-mean model at 260 K cannot represent:
bulk evaporation at the zonal mean temperature is about a fifth of the rule block's. The report says "outside tolerance" for
it, and the test asserts that the report says so, not that the two blocks agree.

## Checks (`cargo test -p sse-weather`, 30 tests, 9 new)
- The zonal block is its own physics: mean temperature equals the world's (within 0.5 K); Earth contrast is 30 to 60 K;
  Venus and Titan are isothermal within 2 K; its rain profile differs from the rule block's by more than 0.1 m/yr in some
  band; it is deterministic.
- Earth, Mars, Venus and Titan are within tolerance. The locked world is reported outside, by name, in the final line.
- Swap on a saved campaign carries every pin over and honours it in the new block: a named Earth cyclone ("Storm Ada"),
  the locked world's permanent substellar storm ("The Eye"), a GM clear-sky order and a GM-triggered storm. The manifest
  keeps pins and authored events unchanged; the pinned storm keeps its track under the new block.
- Switching back restores the original exactly: the manifest is equal to the original, it round-trips through JSON, and the
  climate, field tile and systems digests on 5 worlds × 3 days are identical. The zonal digests differ from the rule ones
  (a real swap).
- An unknown block version is refused by `block_for` and by `swap`.
- Zonal storms sit at the hemisphere's steepest gradient on their day of birth, replay identically from a later date, and
  are absent on Venus, Titan, the locked world and the Moon.
- The zonal field honours GM clear and calm orders.
- Golden `tests/golden/weather-swap.json` (new). No existing golden changed: `weather.json` and `weather-storms.json` pass
  unchanged.
- `cargo clippy -p sse-weather --all-targets -- -D warnings` is clean, and `cargo fmt --all` was run. fmt also wanted to
  reformat W5's `crates/sse-kernel/src/weather.rs`, which is not this package's file. That change was reverted and is left
  for its owner.

## Candidates to move into flows
- `compare` (a tolerance test on two averages) and the pin-honoured check are small logic blocks a GM could inspect.
- The insolation formula, the Thomas solve and the bulk evaporation are atoms.
- The seasonal solve stays in Rust: about 36 bands × 80 iterations × 12 seasons per call.

## Known limits
- The zonal block has no longitude. Day-night contrast on locked worlds, continents, monsoons and orography are all absent
  (hence the locked world is outside tolerance), and the field is the same along a latitude except under storms and orders.
- No Hadley cell: the EBM's tropics are wet and its subtropics are not dry. The rain profile differs band by band even where
  the means agree, and `rain_cells` (erosion's form) can change. The report shows this but tolerance does not test it,
  because the contract's `TOLERANCE` covers only mean cloud and rain.
- Titan's rain and Venus's deck agree partly by construction: both blocks are energy-limited on Titan, and both read the
  same chemistry rule on Venus.
- Giants: the EBM has no deep envelope or internal-heat dynamics. Jupiter happens to fall within tolerance (0.10 → 0.00
  cloud) because its rule cloud is thin, not because the zonal block models belts. A giant block (the shallow-water model
  in design section 8) is still the natural next one.
- Quasi-steady seasons: there is no heat capacity lag (C is not used), so seasons peak at the solstice rather than a month
  later.
- Pins without a world in the given conditions are carried over but reported "not checked".

## Integration
- Save manifest (handoff section 5): store `weather: WeatherManifest`, that is `{ block: { id, version, level }, pins:
  [{ world, pin }], authored: [{ world, pin }] }`. Name the block by its `BlockId`, for example
  `{ "id": "sse.weather.rules", "version": "1", "level": "rule" }` or
  `{ "id": "sse.weather.zonal-ebm", "version": "1", "level": "simulated" }`. A new campaign defaults to the rule block.
  Loading builds the block with `swap::block_for(stack, &manifest.block)`. GM pins, orders and triggers (W5) go in
  `authored`; seen and named systems go in `pins`.
- Kernel `weather` op (W5, `crates/sse-kernel/src/weather.rs`, not edited here): add an optional `block: BlockId` (default
  the rule block) and replace `scenario::block()` with `swap::block_for`. `report`, `column` and `sound` are `RuleBlock`
  methods today, so for the zonal block they either need trait-level versions or should be answered with "not provided by
  this block". A second op, or `want: "swap"`, can return `swap::swap(...)`'s `SwapReport` (lines, per-world diffs, pin
  checks, new manifest) for the GM's "switch weather block" dialog. The GM confirms, and the chronicle records the manifest
  change as an event.
