# T2: branch look-ahead and commit; "age the Solar System" end to end

Status: done (18 Nov 2026). Kernel track, branch `claude/project-thread-dzkz4p`. Design: handoff section 6.3; build
plan row T2 (after T1).

## What was built
`sse-time::branch`:
- `look_ahead(chronicle, request, go)`: runs the ageing (T1) and opens a chronicle branch holding an authored
  `time.request` (the whole request: system, settings, target age, format `sse.time-request/1`), then one
  `evolve.transition` per epoch step (facts `star.phase`, `star.luminosity` L_sun, `star.radius` R_sun,
  `star.teff` K, `star.mass` M_sun, `star.colour`; per planet `orbit.a` au, `orbit.e`, `orbit.i` degrees,
  `body.engulfed`) and one `time.<kind>` per named transition (data: `why`, `level`, `plausible_future`,
  `age_myr`, `numbers`). All derived, all caused by the request, dated in universe time (birth year plus age).
  The main chronicle is untouched; `go` sees deterministic work units and can cancel, leaving no branch.
- `commit(chronicle, look, move_now)`: `Chronicle::commit` (re-stamped events plus `commit.branch`); with
  `move_now`, an authored `system.now_year` fact on the system moves its "now" to that age.
- `replay(chronicle)`: rebuilds a chronicle from its authored events alone, re-running every `time.request`.
- `scenario::age_the_solar_system()`: the Sun from today to 10 Gyr past the planetary nebula, committed, now moved
  to 1 Gyr after the nebula. `scenario::golden()` feeds `tests/golden/time.json` and the harness engine `time`.

## The scenario's story (each step's "why" is in its event)
1. Oceans lost at 6.12 Gyr: the Sun at 1.10 times today's luminosity; above 1.1 times Earth's insolation the
   water vapour greenhouse runs away (Kopparapu et al. 2014).
2. Leaves the main sequence at 11.05 Gyr; red giant from 11.63 Gyr.
3. Mercury engulfed at 12.73 Gyr: its orbit widened by mass loss and pulled in by tides met the star's surface.
4. Planetary nebula at 12.73 Gyr: a 0.569 solar-mass white dwarf; surviving orbits widened 1.76 times (Jupiter
   to 9.15 au); the giant reached 0.85 au.
5. White dwarf colours: blue-white, white, yellow-white, yellow, orange, red (dated by bisection on Teff,
   Mestel cooling).
6. Pollution 100 Myr after the nebula: belt asteroids scattered inward by Jupiter (sketch).
Everything after 4.63 Gyr is labelled "one plausible future".

## Checks (`cargo test -p sse-time`, the T2 part: 5 tests)
- A look-ahead leaves the main head unchanged; on the branch the Sun is brighter in 5 Gyr; discard leaves nothing.
- Cancellation: progress arrives as work units 1, 2, ... 10; cancelling at 10 returns nothing and the chronicle
  stays empty.
- Commit: the request first, every named kind present, every named event has a why and a horizon label; at the
  committed "now" the Sun is a white dwarf, Mercury engulfed, Jupiter at 9.0 to 9.3 au; at today's date the Sun is
  on the main sequence.
- Replay from the authored chronicle gives the same head and authored hash; a second full run gives the same
  manifest and head; a tampered derived event changes the head.
- Golden `tests/golden/time.json` (156 chronicle events, 212 234 work units, three windows). Identical in Node
  (wasm) and Chromium through the harness.
