# G0: default patch envelope (Milky-Way-like)

Status: done (18 Nov 2026). Kernel track, branch `claude/project-thread-dzkz4p`, on top of PR #1 (K7 to K10). Authority: galaxy design sections 6 to 8 and 12; system formation design section 3 (stage 1, inheritance) and section 8 (F0 needs G0).

## What was built
- New crate `sse-cosmos` (the cosmos suite; G1 to G8 grow in it). Module `patch`: closed-form conditions at any galactocentric radius, height and universe time, and the envelope built from them. Module `birth`: `SystemBirth`, a typed read of what a newborn system inherits. This is where F0 starts.
- New pack `packs/base-cosmos/pack.json`: every number lives under `provides.other.patch_model` with its source, as pack defaults, not code constants (galaxy design preamble). `PatchModel::from_pack` validates it with readable errors.
- Three tiers, all resolved by the generic K7 resolver:
  - `patch` facts: conditions and hazards. Its `birth_mass` budget is split exactly between cohorts by the model's weights.
  - `cohort:k`: one population born in one time span. The defaults are halo (0.8 to 1.8 Gyr after the origin), thick disc (1.8 to 5.8) and five thin-disc age bins (5.8 to 13.8). A cohort's [Fe/H] and [alpha/Fe] means are the ISM curves at its birth time, which carries the age-metallicity relation and the two disc sequences without correlated draws (galaxy design 8.1: "a cohort is birth patch and birth time step").
  - `sys:i`: the cloud core handed to formation (formation design 3, stage 1). Its fields:
    - birth time
    - [Fe/H] and [alpha/Fe]
    - C/O and Mg/Si
    - r-process, K-40 and Al-26, each relative to the Sun's at birth
    - birth cluster mass and far-UV field
    - population
    - mass: its own draw from the Kroupa IMF
- Conditions per patch (galaxy design 7, the G0 subset):
  - number density by population
  - ISM [Fe/H] and [alpha/Fe]
  - star formation
  - core-collapse and Ia supernova rates
  - sterilising supernovae within 8 pc per Gyr
  - stellar passages within 1 pc per Myr
  - time since the ISM passed the rocky-planet floor
  - what died here so far (supernovae of each kind, as the patch's `legacies`)

## Additions to K7 (`sse-resolve`; additive, no existing behaviour or golden changed)
- `Distribution::BrokenPowerLaw { edges, alphas }`: continuous multi-piece power law (the Kroupa IMF), with quantile, cdf and mean.
- `Weights::Given { weights }`: model-computed split weights by index (needs a fixed count of the same length).
- `CountSpec::Fill { budget, dist, unit }`: draw children from `dist` until the next one would not fit in the budget. Each child's share is its own draw, so the drawn distribution comes through unscaled. Closure stays exact: what is left over is residue, under one draw. Other budgets follow these shares. Stream `resolve.fill.<budget>.v1` on the parent's address. The outlook's expected count is the budget over the mean draw.
  - **Why it was needed:** the first version split each cohort's mass by IMF draws (flat partition with drawn weights). The tests caught it. Because the IMF's mean is set by rare massive stars, a sample missing one rescales every other star upward: 10.4% of systems came out above 1 solar mass against the IMF's 9.7% (3 sigma). With Fill it is 9.9% (1 sigma). Any suite that partitions a mass budget by a heavy-tailed distribution should use Fill.

## Determinism note for every track
serde_json's default float parser is exact only for short mantissas (Clinger's fast path). A code-built envelope written to JSON and read back differed by one ulp in one field, and that changed a resolved system. `sse_cosmos::patch::tidy` rounds every derived number to 11 significant digits before it enters an envelope or a fact, so the fixture resolves bit-identically. The alternative (serde_json's `float_roundtrip` feature) would change parsing for every crate in any build that includes it, so it needs a workspace decision, not one crate's. Anyone shipping envelopes or packs as JSON with computed floats should do the same or raise it.

## Results (evidence)
- **Acceptance: system generation from an envelope fixture.** `tests/fixtures/cosmos/solar-patch.json` holds the Sun's neighbourhood now: a 16 pc leaf at 8.2 kpc, 20 pc above the plane. It contains the conditions, the patch envelope and its seven cohort envelopes. Resolving it with the generic resolver alone gives the same 338 systems as the cosmos code. Budgets close exactly from patch to cohorts to systems, residue included. Resolving systems straight from their cohorts, in a scrambled order with the cohorts recorded last, gives the same state hash and event count. A test fails if the code and the fixture drift apart.
- Statistics over a 60 pc box (15,726 systems) against the cited values:
  - Mean [Fe/H] is -0.14, inside the Geneva-Copenhagen range: -0.14 (Nordstrom 2004; Holmberg 2009) to -0.06 (Casagrande 2011).
  - Spread is 0.26 dex, against about 0.2 there. The wider spread is honest: a volume sample counts old thick-disc and halo stars in full, which a magnitude-limited dwarf survey underweights.
  - Under 2% of systems are below -0.8, so there is no G-dwarf excess.
  - Thick to thin is 0.124 (Juric 2008: 0.12).
  - Mean [alpha/Fe] is +0.17 in the thick disc and +0.05 in the thin disc, so the two sequences appear. Every thick-disc system is 8 Gyr old or more, every thin-disc one 8 Gyr or less.
  - 9.9% of systems are above 1 solar mass (IMF: 9.7%), and total mass is within 1% of the budget.
  - C/O is higher in metal-rich systems.
  - 78% of systems have less Al-26 than the Sun had.
  - Halo systems have mean [Fe/H] below -1.
- Rates at the solar site:
  - 1 core-collapse supernova per 92 solar masses formed. Galaxy-wide that is 1.79 per century from 1.65 solar masses a year (Rozwadowska 2021: 1.63 +- 0.46).
  - Passages within 1 pc: 12.3 per Myr (Bailer-Jones 2018: 19.7). It runs low because this counts stellar systems without brown dwarfs; the test allows a factor of 2.
  - Sterilising supernovae within 8 pc: 0.17 per Gyr.
  - Metals have been enough for rocky planets here for 12.4 Gyr.
- Place and time:
  - The ISM gradient is exactly -0.06 dex per kpc.
  - At 1.5 kpc above the plane, the thick disc outnumbers the thin.
  - The inner disc reached the rocky floor earlier than the outer disc, and at 40 kpc it has not reached it yet.
  - 4 Gyr after the origin there is no thin disc yet, the ISM is poorer, there are under a fifth of today's systems, and every thick-disc system is born by then.
- Cross-device: native, Node and Chromium give identical golden digests (the harness now also runs `cosmos`). CI covers macOS, Windows, Firefox and WebKit.

## Cost classes and edges
`sse_cosmos::ops()`:
- conditions and patch_envelope: trivial (closed form)
- form_patch: medium, linear in the patch's systems (about 330 in a 16 pc leaf). With Fill, a single child's resolve costs one pass over its cohort's draws, so a view that shows one star still pays for its cohort. That is tens to a few hundred draws at the leaf scale.

## Known limits (each belongs to a later stage)
- No spiral arms, bar, bulge, lognormal gas texture, local events or authored edits in the conditions (G4 and G6).
- No frames or promotion (G5). A patch's systems are its present population, including remnants (stars that have since died are still systems, and F0 decides their state).
- Thin-disc star formation is constant in time, so type Ia rates run low against the Milky Way's (local Ia/CC is 0.08; Li 2011 galaxy-wide is about 0.23). A declining history comes with G3's rings.
- Within a cohort, fields are drawn independently (the cohort carries the correlations). Al-26, far-UV and cluster mass are independent draws, though physically the far-UV field rises with cluster mass. F0 may derive one from the other and record it.
- Radial migration only as scatter in [Fe/H] (G5).

## Integration points
- **F0 (physics track):** start from `SystemBirth::from_facts(address, facts)` on any resolved system, or from the fixture file. The fields are listed above. Mass is the system's total stellar mass; F0 splits it into stars and multiplicity.
- `form_patch(&mut chronicle, &seed, &model, address, &site)` resolves and records a whole patch. `Site::solar(&model)` is the default neighbourhood.
- New K7 features (above) are available to every suite.
- Not in the kernel message API (same policy as K7 to K10).
