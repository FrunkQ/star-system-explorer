# S14a: lava tubes, part 1 (envelopes)

Design: `handover/design/SSE4-rock3-comparison.md` section 5.5 (accepted 21 Nov; S14a is first among the new
features, and Alex's favourite). Built under the processes-not-classes rule: every number is a process
strength or a cited scaling.

## What it does
`sse-surface/src/tubes.rs` builds an envelope for every lava source in the world. Passages are not placed
yet. The lava sources are:
- S4 shield volcanoes, on their flanks;
- S4 lava plains, on the gentle ramps at their margins;
- on plate worlds, hot-spot volcanoes. These are the S2 plume cells that erupted in the last 10 Myr and stand
  above the sea now. Older plume tracks have sunk as seamounts, whose lava erupted under water as pillows,
  not tubes. Arc volcanoes are left out because their viscous lava makes no tubes.

Each `TubeField` holds:
- **forming**, 0 to 1. This is a log-normal curve in slope, best near 2°, falling to e^(−1/2) at a factor of
  2.5 either side (Greeley 1971; Calvari and Pinkerton 1998). Shield flanks use S4's shield aspect, which
  gives about 4.8°.
- **width_m**, between Earth's typical and largest widths (10 and 30 m; Sauro et al. 2020), scaled by 1/g,
  because a roof spans wider as gravity falls (Blair et al. 2017). The Moon allows about 6 times Earth's
  width and Mars about 2.6 times. The roof is a quarter of the width.
- **length_km** of passage, which is 0.05 km per km² × forming × the field's area × the share not buried by
  later resurfacing (`CraterField::renewal_kept`).
- **roof_kept**, 0 to 1, from two losses:
  - Impacts breach the roof wherever a crater is at least 5 roof thicknesses wide. This is the saturation
    law, the same one that thins old craters.
  - Rain wears the roof down at 20 m per Myr per metre of rain a year from the one climate, over the
    world's wet span.
- **stage**, read off roof_kept: intact roof (0.9 and above), skylights, pit chains (0.1 to 0.5), and a
  sinuous rille once the roof is gone.
- **skylights**, a count from the length of roof lost.
- **speculative**, set for cryolava on an ice world.
- **cause**: the why-answer, with the numbers and citations.

`Survey.tubes` carries the envelopes at both the Orbit and Survey levels; they are cheap. The kernel's
`survey_surface` returns them as `tubes`.

## Results (base fixtures)
| World | Fields | Widths | State |
|---|---|---|---|
| Moon | 39 | 75 to 170 m | oldest maria open as rilles; some roofs still standing |
| Mars | 130 | 40 to 55 m | many shield flank tubes with skylights; roofs mostly kept |
| Mercury | 14 | 30 to 70 m | mostly rilles |
| Earth | 1 (the hot spot) | about 17 m | intact, young |
| Venus, Io | 0 | | every field buried by resurfacing (see limits) |
| Europa | 0 | | no lava |

## Checks
- `tubes_tests::tubes_form_on_gentle_slopes`.
- `tubes_tests::tube_roofs_scale_with_gravity_and_wear_with_time`: the Moon-to-Earth width ratio is g_E/g_M;
  every stage matches its roof_kept; Earth's young tubes are roofed; the Moon has both rilles and roofs kept;
  Europa has none; the output is reproducible.
- The contract golden is re-blessed on purpose, for the new `tubes` in survey replies.

## Not in this part (S14a part 2)
- The passage graph on approach: a keyed draw from the envelope that follows the downhill path from the
  vent over the atlas heights. It gives nodes, segments, widths, heights, depth, and entrances where
  skylights and rilles meet the surface. These are kernel facts, identical on every device and nameable.
- The cave survey map (plan and profile) in the shell, and a void band in the Cutaway.
- Io and Venus get no tubes because the S4 volcanism stage stops making new plains once its cover cap is
  reached, while renewal buries the old ones. Young flows on resurfaced worlds need S4 to keep erupting
  (or the renewal itself to be the lava that holds tubes).
- Lunar maria come out about 4.5 Gyr old, which is S4's volcanism timing; real maria are 3 to 3.9 Gyr old.
  Younger maria would keep more roofs.
- The `tubes` in survey replies are not yet read by the shell. The Body view's why-panel and the
  sifter's "tubes wider than 300 m" query need a shell package.
