# O1 (kernel side): light descriptions, band set, line table, band integrals

Design: `SSE4-nebulae-and-light-design.md` sections 1, 2 and 5B (spectrum chart). The UI side of O1 (the Light
switch, recipes, legend chip) landed earlier: `O1-ui.md`. Track O thread: "Build light and nebulae".

## What was built
- Crate `sse-light` (new; depends on `sse-core`, `sse-pack`, `sse-flow` for `Reference`):
  - `LightDescription { address, parts, lines, why, stand_ins, references }`. Parts: `thermal` (T, area,
    emissivity), `reflected` (source, T, effective area with albedo and dilution folded in), `power-law`
    (F_ν ∝ ν^-α, total power over a range: coronae, synchrotron, cyclotron radio), `disc` (thin accretion disc,
    Shakura and Sunyaev 1973, both faces, rings). Lines: `{ line, power_w, cause }`.
  - Units: spectral luminosity L_λ in W per metre of wavelength for the whole object, isotropic-equivalent.
  - `publish(desc, table)` → `Published { description, bands, visible_slices, total_w, samples, peak_m }`:
    power in each of the nine bands (gamma to radio) and three visible slices, the continuum sampled on the
    pack grid (145 points, 10⁻¹⁴ to 10⁴ m) for the spectrum chart (O12), and the λL_λ peak.
  - `power_between`, `brightness_temperature` (fixed 200-step bisection), `planck`, `disc_temperature`.
  - Fixed-step Simpson in ln λ (pack `steps_per_decade`, 64), never adaptive, so every device gives the same bits.
    All transcendental maths through `sse_core::math`.
- Pack `packs/base-light` (`provides.other.light`): the band set (the UI's `BANDS` edges, except radio runs to 10 km
  (30 kHz) instead of 10 m, because planetary auroral radio is below 40 MHz), visible
  slices, 21 named lines from the 511 keV annihilation line to H I 21 cm (nebular, auroral and lamp lines), the
  grid, ring count. `LightTable::from_packs` takes the last pack in a stack, so packs can add lines.

## Evidence (`cargo test -p sse-light`, 10 tests)
- Bands close on Stefan-Boltzmann: summed bands equal σT⁴ to 10⁻⁶ for 3 K, 30 K, 300 K, 5772 K, 10⁵ K, 10⁷ K.
- Sun as a 5772 K blackbody of 695,700 km gives 3.828e26 W (IAU nominal) to 0.2 %; peak within one grid step of
  λT = 3670 µm K; 43.775 % of its light in 380 to 750 nm, matching an independent 200,000-step Python
  integration to 10⁻⁴ (that is a check against a separate computation, not a published figure).
- Visible slices sum to the visible band to 10⁻⁶. Lines land in their band and slice exactly.
- Power laws carry their stated power for α = -1, 0, 0.7, 1, 2.
- A 10 solar-mass black hole fed at 10⁻⁸ M☉/yr: disc total within 5 % of GMṀ/(2 r_in) (finite outer edge
  allowed for); brightest band X-rays.
- Brightness temperature inverts Planck to 10⁻⁶ in mid IR, far IR and visible.
- Golden `tests/golden/light.json` (new): four published descriptions (Sun, cold dust, black hole disc, radio
  power law with an auroral line), band powers and a hash of the samples.

## Not done, and why
- No kernel op yet: O2 adds `read_light` with the first real emitters (bodies' conditions). Kept out of this
  PR so the contract golden changes once.
- No shifts (Doppler, beaming, redshift): O7. No dust extinction or absorption lines (stars' Fraunhofer lines,
  needed for O12's "listed lines" and wobble): O12 adds an absorption part. Line widths come with O7.
- The disc is seen isotropically (no inclination, limb darkening or relativistic corrections). To go in
  `docs/fudges.md` when that file lands on main (PR 22); O2 adds the entries.

## References
Every description carries Rybicki and Lightman 1979 (doi 10.1002/9783527618170) and CODATA 2018 (doi
10.1103/RevModPhys.93.025010); a disc adds Shakura and Sunyaev 1973 (A&A 24, 337,
https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/1973A&A....24..337S). Line wavelengths: NIST ASD (https://physics.nist.gov/asd)
and Splatalogue for CO and 21 cm. Wavelengths were written from memory against those sources' published values
and are to be spot-checked by the citations thread.

## Integration
- New crate and pack only. No existing file changed except `START-HERE.md`.
- Physics thread (B1, agreed 19 Nov): B1 publishes conditions on any body (`body.radius`,
  `star.effective_temperature`, `star.luminosity`, `body.bond_albedo`, `body.geometric_albedo`,
  `body.rotation_period`, `body.magnetic_moment`, `body.magnetic_tilt`, `body.accretion_rate`,
  `body.accretion_inner_radius`, `body.coronal_xray_luminosity`); `sse-light` turns them into light. No kind.
- UI: `Published` maps onto `ui-contract/src/light.ts`: `thermal` and `reflected` parts are the same idea, with
  area in place of weight; lines become `[id, power]`. The band ids are identical.
