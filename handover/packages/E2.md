# E2: metabolisms grown from open gradients

Evolution design (`design/SSE4-evolution-design.md`) sections 5.1 to 5.3 and 14, package E2. Built 21 Nov 2026 by the kernel thread, on top of E1.

## What it delivers
- **`history::history_model`** (sse-life) runs boxes C, D and E on the gradient catalogue when it is given a `GradientModel` (the stack's `life_gradients` and thermodynamic table). `history` and `history_with` are unchanged: they run the listed model.
- **Metabolisms are generated, not listed.** Every light and reaction gradient in `life_gradients` is a capability `metabolism:<gradient id>`:
  - gradients of reach at most `origin_reach` (0) come with life at origin: hydrogen methanogenesis and organic methanogenesis;
  - the pack's listed metabolic capabilities (anoxygenic, oxygenic) give way to the generated ones;
  - the rest of the capability graph (complex cells, multicellularity, land, ...) is unchanged.
- **Rate = base × dial × reach_factor^reach × prize**, per Earth-Myr of opportunity (`life_history.metabolic`). Opportunity, the waiting-time draw and the monotonic dials are as in L1b.
  - **prize** = (the power the gradient would add, by running the catalogue with it tapped / (the power the biosphere runs on + floor))^0.5, capped at 1000.
  - **reach** is pack data per gradient: 0 for the oldest metabolisms, 1 for one new pathway, 3 for oxygenic photosynthesis, with sources in `life_gradients.sources.reach`.
  - Oxygenic photosynthesis keeps its hard-step dial (`dial: "oxygenic"`). With the default of 1, prize and prerequisites decide: the design's gated default.
- **Box B is the catalogue with only the evolved gradients tapped** (`gradients::world_catalogue_using`). Gradients life has not evolved stay in the catalogue with their tests, but draw and produce nothing. Life reaches only the media it holds (land waits for the land capability).
- **Oxygen.** Gradients that consume free oxygen open only once a high-power (oxygen-making) metabolism exists and the world's oxidant fact is above zero. They then carry large prizes, so aerobic respiration follows quickly. No script is involved.
- **Power gate on complex cells** (`metabolic.power_gate`, Lane and Martin 2010): `complex_cells` needs an oxygen-using metabolism tapped.
- Each metabolism event's "Why?" says what the gradient would add, against how much power, the prize, the reach and the opportunity so far.
- Draws are keyed by body and by a hash of the gradient id and attempt (`life.metabolism.v1`), so adding a gradient never moves another's draw.

## Verified by
- `earth_outline_at_least_as_often_as_the_listed_model`, 200 seeds:
  - the outline is life before 1 Gyr, microbial for 2.5 Gyr and large bodies after 3.5 Gyr;
  - listed model: 118 of 200;
  - gradient model: 120 of 200;
  - oxygenic photosynthesis appears in 195 seeds, median 1170 Myr. That is about 3.4 Gyr ago: early against the 2.7 to 3.0 Gyr consensus, but inside the contested range. A `reach_factor` of 0.07 gives 1830 Myr but only 86 outlines, so the tuning favours the outline.
- `aerobic_respiration_follows_oxygen_on_every_seed_that_oxygenates`:
  - on 100 seeds, aerobic respiration is always after oxygenic photosynthesis, and complex cells are always after aerobic respiration;
  - without an oxygen maker, neither appears;
  - every event explains its prize.
- `dials_stay_monotonic_on_the_gradient_model`:
  - oxygenic, complex cells and origin dials at 0.01, 1 and 100 give 3, 58 and 60, then 0, 54 and 58, then 3, 60 and 60 of 60 seeds (run here at 30 seeds);
  - per seed, a bigger oxygenic dial never makes oxygenic later.
- `the_same_seed_gives_the_same_history_and_the_listed_model_is_untouched`.

## Cost
Measured natively, release build, one Earth history over 4.57 Gyr:
- listed model: 28 ms;
- gradient model: 79 ms, about three times the listed cost. That is the design's starting estimate (section 13).

Each new tapped set costs one catalogue per untapped gradient, cached by epoch, tapped set and oxygen state. Not yet timed in a browser. That belongs with the first wiring into the kernel.

## Goldens
`tests/golden/life-feedback.json` was re-blessed on purpose: only its two `manifest_hash` values and the two run `hash` values that include them changed. The coupled manifest carries `life_history`, which now holds the `metabolic` block. Every series, date and first appearance in it is identical. No other golden changed. `life_history.metabolic` and the gradients' `reach` and `dial` are new pack data that the listed model never reads.

## Not done
- Nothing calls `history_model` yet: the kernel and the coupled run (L2 manifest) still run the listed history. Wiring them to `life_budget.life_model` needs the coupled manifest to carry the gradient parameters. It goes with E4, which also needs the coupled run.
- `reach_factor` (0.1 per step) and the prize exponent are assumptions tuned on one data point (fudge register F30).
