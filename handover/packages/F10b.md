# F10b: the Formation screen's story card, "How this was worked out" and "Watch it form" (the reading half, part 2)

Design: `design/SSE4-system-formation-design.md` section 7. Thread "Build the Formation screen", 26 Nov 2026. Part 1: `F10a.md`.

## What it adds
- **The world's story** (right of the chart, `views/formation/src/StoryCard.svelte`, model `ui-contract/src/formation-story.ts`): the track pressed on the chart, else the world selected anywhere in the app, else the heaviest world (and it says so). A body lost on the way has a story too; pressing a lasting world also selects it for every other view.
  - **Material by band**: a bar summed from the body's provenance ledger, by the band each claim came from (pebbles drift in, so an inner world can hold outer-band material), then each body that merged into it as one share named "from World 4 (born at 1.4 AU)", then gas pulled from the disc. Shares sum to one. Below it, how much of it is water and how much of that came from beyond the water ice line (the water group of the ledger's claims in the water ice, CO₂ ice and CO ice bands).
  - **Its story**: where it was born, its drift through the gas, resonances, the violent phase's events it takes part in (in the accent), its own later events from the run (geology going quiet and the like), and where it is now. Events after the system's present follow "now".
  - **How sure**: orbit and mass calculated; impact dates and angles drawn; scattering drawn; material from the ledger. Each row's title says what that means.
- **How this was worked out**, under the chart a press away (a fold: rarely needed, so not always open): stars tabulated; bands, growth, giants and migration calculated and stepped in time (or Classic Accrete); impacts, ejections and scatterings drawn from calibrated odds; each world's early life calculated; passing stars and comet showers "not yet" (they wait on star slots). "Nothing is worked out again when you move the slice."
- **Watch it form** is this screen's Play: the existing replay (`views/system-chart/src/FormationReplay.svelte`) of the same record, over the page; Escape closes it.

## Not changed
- Make a system keeps its own "Watch it form" for a grown preview that is not in the campaign yet (there is no system on the chart to open this screen for). Once added, How it formed is one press away on the rail.
- No engine change, no golden change.
