# S0: surface envelope, regime over time, regime sketch (19 Nov 2026)

Surface design sections 1, 2.1 and 6; build plan track S. Taken on by the physics track overnight at the
coordinator's request (no other track owns surfaces). Needs P1 and P4 (done).

## What was built
- Crate `sse-surface` (new; depends on sse-core, sse-pack, sse-formation).
- Pack `packs/base-surface` (`provides.other.surface`): 14 ordered regime rules and 14 regime records (label, kind,
  sketch style, relief, albedo, dial, speculative flag, linked source). The first rule whose conditions all hold
  names the regime; the last rule is the unconditional fallback (checked at load, as are unknown quantities and
  regimes). Quantities a rule may test: `QUANTITIES` in `lib.rs`.
- `envelope(settings, &Body) -> SurfaceEnvelope`: kind of outer layer, reference radius, gravity, regime now,
  timeline of spans (with heat flow at each end), and a `RegimeEvent` per change. Dates are sampled every
  0.25 Gyr (plus the magma ocean's end) and each change is bisected (24 steps) to its crossing. Each event's
  cause is the new rule's reason plus the quantity that crossed, e.g. "heat flow 1.000 of the critical value".
  - Heat flow at each date is the base-physics flow `sse.base.planet-heat` (via `pipeline::world_heat`) plus
    tidal heating (Peale and Cassen form) from the orbit, or a given value for moons.
  - Spin-orbit lock from the Gladman et al. 1996 despinning time (Q 100, k2 0.3, 10 h initial spin).
  - Surface temperature: fixed for fixtures; for generated worlds the equilibrium temperature under the
    ageing host star (`LifeLine::state_at`) plus 30 K greenhouse when the world has volatiles.
- `bodies_of(run)` / `envelopes_of(run)`: every world of a formation pipeline run. Giants and worlds with a kept
  hydrogen envelope above 0.1% of their mass are cloud decks.
- `sketch(settings, envelope, seed, SurveyState) -> Sketch`: the cheap globe for unexamined and scanned worlds.
  Latitude-longitude grid (8 × 16 unexamined with heights withheld, 32 × 64 scanned, 64 × 128 beyond), keyed
  smooth bumps on the sphere in three octaves (stream `surface.sketch.v1`), one grammar per style: plates
  (continents at the pack land fraction, two-level heights), lid (hemispheric split, three giant shields), plains,
  ice, smooth (magma, a locked lava world's molten day side), ocean, dunes (equatorial dunes, polar lakes),
  lumpy (small bodies), bands (giants; band count from rotation). Relief scales with 9.81/g (clamped 0.5 to 3).
  Heights are whole metres, albedo in thousandths.

## Checks (`cargo test -p sse-surface`)
- Fixture worlds (`tests/fixtures/surface/bodies.json`: Earth, Venus, Mars, Mercury, Moon, Io, Europa, Ganymede,
  Titan, Vesta, Jupiter, 55 Cancri e, and synthetic ocean, wet super-Earth and carbon worlds) all get their
  expected regime.
- Earth: magma ocean, then mobile lid from 0.1 Gyr; aged to 14 Gyr its plates stop between 8 and 13 Gyr (radiogenic
  heat below 0.019 W/m²), with the reason in the event. Every event's cause contains its rule's reason; spans
  tile the life.
- Sketches: Earth-like bimodal (Sarle coefficient above 5/9) with 15 to 50% land; Venus-like unimodal and
  narrower than Mars; Mars-like hemispheric split above 1.5 km; Jupiter flat with bands. Same call, same globe.
- Disclosure: unexamined withholds heights; scanned is finer.
- Twelve G0 systems through the pipeline: every world gets an envelope; small dry worlds are never mobile lids.
- Tides: Earth at 0.05 au locks within 1 Gyr, at 1 au not within 100 Gyr.
- A pack rule reading an unknown quantity is refused.
- Golden `tests/golden/surface.json` (new; fixtures and four G0 systems), in the cross-device harness as
  `surface`: identical in Node and Chromium. No existing golden changed.

## Known limits
- No atmosphere stage yet: generated worlds have pressure 0, so the hydrocarbon regime only appears for
  authored or imported worlds (Titan fixture). Outgassed CO2 (`WorldStart.co2_bar`) is not treated as an
  atmosphere because it freezes out on cold worlds and is drawn down on wet ones.
- Generated surface temperature is equilibrium plus a flat greenhouse; the weather design (W track) replaces it.
- Heat flow is radiogenic plus eccentricity tides; no secular cooling term, so young worlds' heat is low against
  the q_crit threshold (same as the P1 heat atoms).
- Super-Earth and episodic-lid dials are recorded (`dial`) but no GM edit sets them yet (S7's reshape tools).
- Moons are not yet produced by formation, so moon regimes come from fixtures and imports only.

## Integration
- Kernel: no op yet; the first consumer (U2's body view or the chart's globe) calls `envelopes_of` and `sketch`.
- `sse-wasm` gained `sse-surface` for the harness scenario.
