# V10a: True on the system chart

Alex (23 Nov): "I just wanna start seeing what the renderer looks like". True is now one press from the chart room:
the View style switch on the system chart offers Chart, 3D and **True** (Wireframe still to come).

## What was built

- `SYSTEM_CHART_CONTROLS` draws True. Choosing it shows the 3D frame (`System3D`, `trueLook`) against black space:
  - the star in its own colour, from its surface temperature (`starCss`, the sky's thermal colour table);
  - each world wrapped in its own surface, read from the same level-0 tiles the Body globe draws
    (`trueSurface`: `prepare` then `equirect`, 128 by 64), so a world looks the same from the system as close up;
  - worlds lit by the star (a point light at the star in its frame; in a planet's frame, a light from where the star
    lies, opposite the planet's place on its orbit), with night sides and phases;
  - only the selected world's orbit; no belts or shells; names on hover or focus (the selected world keeps its name),
    per the view styles ruling of 22 Nov.
- Surfaces are read on the GM's seat (the kernel's `resolved_system` tiles, about 3 s a world), one at a time, the
  selected world first, kept for the page. The note under the chart says how many are read; a world with no surface
  (a giant, or the test drive's sketched worlds) is drawn plain and the note gives the kernel's reason.
- The canvas tier keeps the plain look and says True needs a GPU. Players' screens are unchanged.

## Scale and HUD (Alex, 23 Nov 2026)

Two more switches in the one `ViewControls` (chart-grammar), beside View style and Seen from, for every view and scale:

- **Scale: Readable / Real** (`ViewChoice.scale`, `ViewControlsSpec.scale`, `pickScale`, `SCALE_WORDS`). Readable is
  what the views draw now (sizes and spacing exaggerated; V3's Toytown) and the default. Real: distances in proportion
  (outermost orbit at 10 display units) and every body at its true radius on that scale (`layoutReal`,
  `Body3D.radius_m` from the radius facts); a body smaller than a pixel or so shows as a point of light. The flat chart
  is always Readable; choosing Real on it turns it into 3D, as Seen from does. Changing scale puts the camera back.
- **HUD: Off / On** (`ViewChoice.hud`, `ViewControlsSpec.hud` per style, `HUD_WORDS`): orbit lines, belts, names and
  Real's locator rings. Off looks like a photograph (names on hover only), on like a heads-up overview. Unchosen it is
  off in True and on in the diagrams; once chosen it holds in every style. The system chart offers it in 3D and True;
  the flat chart lists it quiet ("Chart always shows its labels") until the chart side is wired.
- A view that does not declare Real or HUD lists them quiet with the reason, so the control never rearranges.
- Kept choices from before read back as Readable with HUD unchosen.
- The 3D chart's "Moons of" bar moved low in the stage, clear of the wider controls.



- `packages/renderer/test/true-look.test.ts` (3 tests): the picture wraps the globe as the sphere is turned (north row
  +z, u = ½ on the equator +x, u = ¾ +y), a face with no tile is grey, a star's colour follows its temperature.
- `tests/ui/system3d.e2e.mjs`: True in one press from the 3D chart, `data-look` true on the GPU tier and plain on the
  canvas tier, with the note. `tests/ui/view-controls.e2e.mjs`: True is no longer disabled on the system chart.
- `view-controls.test.ts` (6 tests): Scale and HUD defaults, Real only where declared, the flat chart Readable and
  turned to 3D by Real, HUD by style, kept choices read back. `system3d.e2e.mjs` also presses Real and HUD On.
- Pictures: `V10a-screens/real-hud.png` (Real scale, HUD on: true orbits and names) and `real-photo.png` (Real, HUD
  off: the system as it is, points of light), `V10a-screens/true-system.png` (seed "first light", tilted) and `true-close.png` (zoomed in, edge-on:
  cratered worlds with their terminators).
