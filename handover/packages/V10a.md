# V10a: True on the system chart

Alex (23 Nov): "I just wanna start seeing what the renderer looks like". True is now one press from the chart room:
the View style switch on the system chart offers Chart, 3D and **True** (Wireframe still to come).

## What was built

- `SYSTEM_CHART_CONTROLS` draws True. Choosing it shows the 3D frame (`System3D`, `trueLook`) against black space:
  - the star in its own colour, from its surface temperature (`starCss`, the sky's thermal colour table);
  - each world wrapped in its own surface, read from the same level-0 tiles the Body globe draws
    (`trueSurface`: `prepare` then `equirect`, 128 by 64), so a world looks the same from the system as close up;
  - worlds lit by the star (a point light at the star in its frame; in a planet's frame, a light from where the star
    lies, opposite the planet's place on its orbit), with night sides and phases;
  - only the selected world's orbit; no belts or shells; names on hover or focus (the selected world keeps its name),
    per the view styles ruling of 22 Nov.
- Surfaces are read on the GM's seat (the kernel's `resolved_system` tiles, about 3 s a world), one at a time, the
  selected world first, kept for the page. The note under the chart says how many are read; a world with no surface
  (a giant, or the test drive's sketched worlds) is drawn plain and the note gives the kernel's reason.
- The canvas tier keeps the plain look and says True needs a GPU. Players' screens are unchanged.

  canvas tier, with the note. `tests/ui/view-controls.e2e.mjs`: True is no longer disabled on the system chart.
- Pictures: `V10a-screens/true-system.png` (seed "first light", tilted) and `true-close.png` (zoomed in, edge-on:
  cratered worlds with their terminators).

## Fudge register

Row "True system view": no fall-off of light across the frame (distances are on a log scale), a trace of ambient so
night sides do not vanish, sizes not to scale, worlds without a surface drawn plain.
