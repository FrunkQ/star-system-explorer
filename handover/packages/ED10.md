# ED10: "Could be…" (editing design section 11C)

## Part 1 (25 Nov): the line and its recipes

- Records are data: `could_be` in `packages/ui-contract/src/handles.json`. Each has an id, a label, a test
  (`{result, is}` over a yes-or-no result of the kernel's surface entry) and `by`, the world handles that may make it
  true, in order. The base records are "have a field of its own" (`core.dynamo_now`, by mass then orbit), "be locked
  to its star" and "turn freely" (`locked`, by orbit then mass).
- `packages/ui-contract/src/could-be.ts`:
  - `couldBeNow(results)` returns the records whose result the kernel gives and whose test the world does not pass.
  - `recipeFor(session, record, world)` runs Reach it (`reachIt` with `want`) for each handle, from an eighth to eight
    times its value now, on scratch branches. It returns the passing values, smallest move first (as a ratio).
  - Nothing is written. Test: `test/could-be.test.ts`, where Mars's way to a field is a heavier Mars and the state hash
    does not change.
- `packages/views/editing/src/CouldBe.svelte` sits under the handles row (GM, Build, main line): "Could be  have a
  field of its own · be locked to its star". Pressing one works out the recipe; each way has "Do this" (the handle's
  own commit, so one change and one Undo, and What followed). It stays closed until pressed.
- e2e (`editing.e2e.mjs`, desk): Mars, field, the Mass step, Do this, What followed, Undo. Screen: project files
  `sse4/handover-screens/ED10-screens/could-be-desk.png`.
