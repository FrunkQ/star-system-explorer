# U17: Climate "Why?" on the Body screen (UI track)

Status: done 21 Nov 2026. A small package asked for by the weather thread (the coordinator's call, not one of Alex's rulings). Alex asked whether "Why?" works for weather and climate. The kernel's data had it (W7a: `survey_surface` returns a summary of the one shared climate), but nothing on screen showed it. The Workshop's full climate blocks come later in W12.

## What it does

- **The Body screen's words gain a "Climate" line with "Why?".** Pressing it reads the world's climate and opens a plain readout under the facts, growing from the button.
  - The regime, in words ("Earth-like", "Airless"). It also stays on the Climate line once read.
  - A short table: the year-round mean temperature and rain for six 30° belts, north to south. Each belt is the area-weighted mean (by the cosine of latitude) of the kernel's 5° bands.
  - Temperatures read in °C at every depth; Science and Engine add kelvin, and a line with the circulation cells, the Hadley edge, the lapse rate, the surface pressure and the surface liquid.
  - The kernel's own "why" lines. A line saying the world is outside the climate's tested range carries the "out of range" stamp.
  - What the kernel assumed (year from Kepler, spin and tilt and where they came from, sunlight, air), listed once.
  - Engine adds the climate key (`address|climate-v1|level|epoch|hash`).
- **Asked for, never fetched on a click alone.** The readout is a quick survey (`survey_surface` with `quick`, the Orbit level, the same world the globe shows; `limit: 1`). It takes about half a second the first time, and the kernel keeps its last surveys. The page keeps each world's climate too. It never changes the state hash.
- **Under the hood:** `ViewSession.surveyClimate(system, body)` (GM only) and `packages/ui-contract/src/climate.ts` with `ClimateSummary`, `climateBelts`, `regimeWords` and `isExtrapolation`. The view is `packages/views/body/src/ClimateWhy.svelte`.

## Checks

- `packages/ui-contract/test/climate.test.ts`: the belts and their order and weighting; the regime and extrapolation words; through the kernel, sample Earth is Earth-like with three cells, warmer tropics than poles, why and assumed lines; the Moon is airless and dry; the station and a player seat are refused.
- `tests/ui/globe.e2e.mjs`: "Why?" opens the readout for sample Earth with six belts, why and assumed lines, closes again, and the state hash does not move.
- `npm run ui:check`, `npm run ui:test`, `npm run ui:e2e` and `scripts/check.sh` pass. No goldens change.
