# U15: Depth (step 2, part 2, of the top-level redesign; UI track)

Status: done 21 Nov 2026. This is the second part of step 2 in `handover/design/SSE4-top-level-ui-and-onboarding.md` section 5, from the design's section 2.4. U14 built the desks (part 1). Copy and paste on the Build desk is part 3.

## What it does

- **One dial, three stops: Curious, Science, Engine.** These are the same three depths "why?" already used, so the app has one idea of depth. Depth never hides an instrument, a scale or a desk; it changes how much each one says.
  - **A new visitor starts at Curious.** The choice is remembered on this device. The key D steps through the three.
  - **On a wide screen the dial sits in the bar** beside the desk, with the same sliding ink. Below 1280 px, and on phones, it is the first line of More.
- **Cards by depth** (body card):
  - **Curious** leads with one sentence and four headline facts, in words: "about 18 °C", "about 31 hours", "about 150 days", "about 0.52 AU". The sentence is the body's own prose, or one made from what it is and where it goes round ("An ice giant. It goes round the Sun at about 19 AU."). The headline facts are where it goes round, how warm it is, its day and year, and its mass, in that order where the card has them. "All 17 facts ›" opens the rest, still in words.
  - **Science** is the card as it was: every value, set or derived, with evidence stamps and honest precision.
  - **Engine** adds the body's address and, for each derived value, the pack, flow and version that published it.
  - **On the Build desk** the card keeps every field it can edit, in the units you type, at any depth. Curious still gives the sentence.
- **Why? opens at the chosen depth.** Its own Curious, Science and Engine tabs still move freely.
- **The status line by depth.** Curious shows the seat, "Kept", and the players' link. Science adds the device tier. Engine adds the kernel version and the state and physics hashes. The hashes stay on the page for tests and tools as attributes of the app (`data-state-hash`, `data-stack-hash`, `data-physics`), whatever the depth.
- **Under the hood:** `Workspace.depth` (presentation only, Science when absent), and `packages/ui-contract/src/depth.ts` with `DEPTHS`, `headline`, `curiousDisplay`, `curiousSentence` and `roughly`.
