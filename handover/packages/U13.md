# U13: Shell frame, step 1 of the top-level redesign (UI track)

Status: done 21 Nov 2026. This is step 1 of the build order in `handover/design/SSE4-top-level-ui-and-onboarding.md` section 5, which Alex accepted on 21 Nov ("UI front end - ready to roll"). Step 1 adds no new features: it moves what is already built to where people can see it. On the way it fixes a tester's bug: imported and grown systems had no belts.

## What it does

- **The place line is the title** (design 2.1). At a system it reads "Kestrel". At a world it reads "Kestrel › Tarn", and "Kestrel" is a link back up. Esc clears the selection, and a second Esc goes up a step. Beside the title, a quiet "10 bodies" list goes to any body, with moons set in under their planets. When the campaign holds more than one system, the system list ("System on the chart") comes first in the line.
- **The instrument rail** (2.2): every way of looking at the place in view, in words, on the left.
  - At a system:
    - Look: Chart, Sizes, Sky from here, Out to the galaxy.
    - Find: Stories.
    - Travel: Route.
    - Understand: The physics.
  - At a world:
    - Look: Globe, Cutaway, Sizes, Sky from here.
    - This world: Life.
    - Travel: Route to here.
    - Understand: The physics.
  - At a system, the rail opens with one solid door, "Open Tarn ›" (or "Open a world ›" with nothing selected). It ends with "Workshop ›".
  - On a phone the rail is one row of chips that scrolls sideways.
- **The rail is built from the screens, not written into the shell.** A screen document can carry `rail`: one entry per scale, each giving a group, a label, an order and a one-line hint (`RailEntry` in `packages/ui-contract/src/screen.ts`). `checkScreen` checks it. `railOf(screens, scale)` builds the rail, with groups in a fixed order (Look, Find, This world, Travel, Understand, Workshop). `scalesOf(doc)` says where a screen sits. So a new screen adds its own rail entry, and packs, Orac and player screens can read the same list.
- **Route, Sky from here and Stories are instruments of their own.** Each is a screen that mounts its view directly (`route.gm.json`, `window.gm.json`, `stories.gm.json`), rather than a section hidden inside the Explore tab. The galaxy, the galaxies nearby and the cosmic web stay together as "Out to the galaxy", until step 4 moves them onto the place line. The Explore view gained a `sections` parameter for this. **Cutaway** is its own world instrument (`cutaway.gm.json`); the Globe still shows it as well.
- **A screen with no card is a page.** Its one view fills and scrolls in the stage (`data-page` on the stage), so the new screens need no layout of their own.
- **Workshop** (2.6): the six setup screens, out of the main bar. They are Physics flows, Packs, Player screens, Player data, Symbols and Styles, with "‹ Back to Kestrel". The physics is still one click away as "The physics" on every rail, and from every why?.
- **Time tools sit on the ruler** (2.7). What if… and Look ahead… sit at the right-hand end of the ruler, with a what-if's Compare, Keep it and Throw away. The Light and Sound switches sit beside them, as modifiers of any instrument.
- **The bar** now holds the place line, the lens, Edit/View, **Ask** (the agents, promoted from the status line) and **More**. More holds Workshop, Campaign… (save, open, start fresh), Make a system…, Night chart (key N) and Keyboard shortcuts. The chart-style switch and the eleven screen tabs are gone.
- **A slim status line.** It shows the seat, "Kept 13:30", the players' link with seats and requests, and, quietly at the end, the engine's numbers: kernel, tier, state and physics hashes. In step 2 these go to Engine depth.

## The tester's list (21 Nov), checked against the build

- **Delete save data.** This is More › Campaign… › Start fresh, and the menu line now says "save, open, start fresh". Before, it was "Campaign…" among 11 px links.
- **Change styles.** This is Workshop › Styles, now listed in words on the rail.
- **Compare two times.** What if… and Look ahead… now sit on the ruler. A side-by-side of two times is not built yet. Look ahead shows one future on a scrubber, and Compare shows a what-if against the main line.
- **The "View" control reads like a way into a detail view.** Step 2 replaces Edit/View with the three desks (Explore, Build, Run), so it is left as it is here.
- **Tag view, and switching K, C or F.** No unit switch is built yet: temperatures show in kelvin. Depth (step 2) brings "about 15 °C" at Curious and units at Engine. "Tag view" could not be placed, so the tester needs to say more.
- **Bug: imported systems draw no asteroid belts.** This was real.
  - The V3 preview (`sceneFromImport`) and the read-back (`sceneFromResolved`) carried a belt's kind but no band. The formation preview (`sceneFromFormation`) dropped the leftovers altogether.
  - All three now draw belts round the star as bands. `beltBand` takes a V3 belt's inner and outer radius when they are at orbital scale, or ±10 % about its orbit otherwise.
  - For a grown preview, belts come from F5's clouds. Rings, Trojans, the scattered disc and the Oort cloud are left off the system chart.
  - Not yet: a *grown* system read back from the universe still has no belts, because `resolved_system` does not carry F5's clouds. That needs the kernel thread.
  - Not yet: no real V3 save with a belt was available to test the V3 path, only the synthetic Sol (which has none).
- **Bug: the planet view loads forever in a new tab.** Not reproduced. A sample Sol was added, a second tab of the same browser opened, and Earth's Globe drew in both. This needs the tester's browser and exact steps.
