# ED12: The Orbit and Spin boxes in full (and ED7 part 1, "Try changing it")

Status: ED12 part 1 and ED7 part 1 done 24 Nov 2026 (thread "Build planet and star editing"). Design: `handover/design/SSE4-editing-design.md` sections 3B, 11F and 13.

## ED12 part 1

- **The box is data.** `packages/ui-contract/src/handles.json` gives the world's Orbit and Spin handles (and the star's Spin) a `box`: `parts` (a strip or a ring on a kernel field, shown from a `depth` up, `range` fixing a strip's ends in SI, `marks`, `walls`, ring `catches`), `chips` (`mirror`, `flip`, `lock`, `resonance`) and `results`. A pack can declare a box for a handle of its own and the same components draw it.
- **Orbit box:** the size strip (the handle's own, which now also takes a year typed in: "365.25 d" is the distance with that year round the same host, scaled from the kernel's own year so the masses are kept); Shape (`orbit.eccentricity`, 0 to 0.99, said as 0.0935, a wall at 1 "leaves the system" that dragging cannot reach and typing can); Where it is now (a ring of the true anomaly at the campaign's now, from orbits_at's position; it writes `orbit.mean_anomaly_at_epoch` so the world is there now, and catches at the closest and farthest points); Lean (`orbit.inclination`, 0 to 180°, "past 90 is backwards"); the turn of the ellipse and where it crosses the plane as two rings at Science depth; chips Retrograde (mirrors the lean through 90°) and In step with (for the nearest neighbour inside and out, the nearest of 2:1, 3:2, 4:3, 5:3, 5:2, 3:1 within a quarter, setting the size; "on" when already there). Results: the year (kernel `orbits_at` period), closest and farthest, how much more sunlight at the closest point, and the kernel's `orbit_stability` verdict for this world (its worst check, in its own words).
- **Spin box:** the day strip (the handle's own), Tilt (`body.obliquity`, 0 to 180°), chips Retrograde (flips the day's sign; the change reads "… backwards") and Locked ("Locked to its star", or "to its planet" for a moon: the day set to the year or month). Results: the sun's day (noon to noon, or "none: the same face always looks at the star"), days in a year, and whether tides would have locked it by now (the surface's `locked`, which is lock time against age).
- `packages/ui-contract/src/box.ts`: `boxAt`, `partHandle`, `mirrorAngle`, `trueFromMean`, `meanFromTrueRad`, `trueAnomalyFrom`, `epochMeanFor`, `orbitShape`, `solarDay`, `resonances`, `stabilityFor`. Presentation arithmetic only (the orbit's geometry and Kepler's equation for the place); every physical result is the kernel's.
- `Session.orbitStability` (kernel `orbit_stability`, ST1) joins the view session.
- `packages/views/editing/src/`: `Box.svelte` (the box), `Ring.svelte` (drag the dot round, catches with Alt off, arrows a degree and Shift ten, typing any angle and a bare number in degrees), `Strip.svelte` (marks and walls; a bare number is in the unit the figure shows; `fromTime`; `onclose` optional inside a box).
- Each move is one set_value through `commitEdit`, so one Undo. A box part can name its change itself ("Where it is now 180° from its closest point").

Waiting, and why: season phase ("when midsummer falls") and oblateness on the card are kernel K12; gravity at the equator and the poles and the Day strip's "noticeably lighter" and "lifts off" tipping points want the sweep (K1) and K12; the ring's catches at "lined up with", "opposite" and "square to" each neighbour, and the year and verdict on a held (not yet let go) value, want `resolved_system {branch}` (BC1). Moons in full and the phone dock are ED7's later parts.

## ED7 part 1: "Try changing it" (question 7, Alex yes)

- On Explore, a GM's read-only strip says where the world sits and offers **Try changing it**. It opens a what-if named "EXAMPLE · change anything" (onboarding section 12): the strip and box move on the branch, the main line does not, and the time ruler's Keep it and Throw away decide. The handles show the branch's own values over the kernel's.
- Any what-if now lets the handles move on Build too (they used to lock while one was open). On a branch the box shows only the orbit's geometry, with "The year, the verdict and the tides follow once it is kept", and the card says the change is on the example only, until BC1 lets the kernel resolve a branch.

Checks: `packages/ui-contract/test/box.test.ts` (9 tests: the sheet's boxes by depth; Retrograde; anomaly round trips; the place from a position at any lean; the epoch's mean anomaly; Earth's sunlight swing; the sun's day for Earth, Venus and a locked world; Pluto's 3:2 with Neptune; the verdict). `tests/ui/editing.e2e.mjs`: on Explore, Try changing it, type 1 AU, the main hash stays, Throw away; on Build at Science, the Orbit box's parts, year and verdict, a typed year, Retrograde, the place-now ring catching at the farthest point, Locked and the sun's day, a backwards spin, each with one Undo.
