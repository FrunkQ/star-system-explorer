# F1: disc and material bands with moving ice lines; budgets

Status: done (18 Nov 2026). Physics track. Design: `SSE4-system-formation-design.md` section 3 (stage 3) and 8.

## What was built (`crates/sse-formation/src/disc.rs`)
- A viscous disc on the Lynden-Bell and Pringle similarity solution, gamma = 1 (Hartmann et al. 1998): mass falls as
  T^-1/2, the characteristic radius grows as T, accretion onto the star is M0 / (2 t_visc) T^-3/2, with
  T = 1 + t / t_visc and t_visc = rc0^2 / (3 nu), nu = alpha c_s^2 / Omega (alpha 0.01).
- Clearing: when accretion falls to the photoevaporation rate (X-ray, about 1e-9 per solar mass, Owen et al. 2012,
  plus external FUV, about 1e-9 at 1000 G0 for a 30 AU disc, sketch after Haworth et al. 2018), the gas goes.
- Midplane temperature: passive flared-disc interior (Chiang and Goldreich 1997 eq. 12a) plus accretion heating
  under an optically thick column, T^4 = T_irr^4 + (3/4)(kappa Sigma / 2) T_eff,acc^4 (Nakamoto and Nakagawa 1994),
  kappa 3 cm^2/g scaled with dust.
- Condensation lines found by fixed 80-step bisection in log radius: rock 1400 K and metal (Lodders 2003), soot
  500 K (Li et al. 2021), water 135 K, CO2 47 K, CO 20 K (Oberg et al. 2011).
- Six material bands between them: vapour, rock, carbon, water-ice, CO2-ice, CO-ice. Each carries gas and six
  element groups (metal, rock, carbon, water, CO2, CO) as solid or vapour.
- Mix by group from protosolar abundances (Lodders 2003, carriers as Oberg 2011), scaled by [Fe/H], [alpha/Fe] for the
  oxygen carriers, and C/O (more carbon, less water).
- Draws per system (stream `formation.disc.v1`): initial mass 0.03 of the star, 0.4 dex, capped at 0.15; rc0 30 AU,
  0.3 dex (Andrews et al. 2010, 2013).
- Companions (Artymowicz and Lubow 1994): pairs closer than 2 AU share one circumbinary disc with a cavity of 2.5
  separations; wider pairs truncate the circumprimary disc at a third of periastron (rc0 at most a third of that).
- Budgets in integer units of 1e-12 solar masses: bands claim their share of the t = 0 profile (closed form,
  1 - exp(-r / rc0)), floors, the outermost band takes the rest. Ledger: gas initial = accreted + dispersed; each group
  initial = sum of solid and vapour over bands.
- History: `FormationHistory.disc` (params, viscous and clearing times, bands, ledger); `disc.forms` and `disc.clears`
  events; every slice before clearing carries a `DiscSlice` (mass, accretion, radius, the five line radii); disc
  slice times 0.1, 0.2, 0.5, 2 and 5 Myr and the clearing time join the slice grid. Evidence entries for structure,
  temperature, lines, mix, lifetime, and the drawn mass and radius.

## Checks (`cargo test -p sse-formation`, 16 tests, 4 new)
- Three ages of a young-Sun disc (0.03 solar masses, 30 AU, L 0.7): the water line at birth is 6.1 AU (test band 4 to
  8); at 0.5 Myr accretion is 1.15e-8 a year and the line is at 2.8 AU (band 1.5 to 4, as Garaud and Lin 2007 and Oka,
  Nakamoto and Ida 2011 find near 1e-8); near clearing it is 1.39 AU, just outside the passive CG97 value of 1.0 AU.
  Every line moves inward, and the lines stay in temperature order. Accretion at 1 Myr is 5.3e-9 (Hartmann 1998: about
  1e-8, within half a dex).
- Budgets close exactly: gas over bands, every group over bands as solid plus vapour, accreted plus dispersed; and the
  same over 60 drawn systems. Water ice is solid only from the water-ice band outward; metal vapour only in the hot band.
- Lifetimes over 400 Sun-like systems at 300 G0: median 1.5 to 5 Myr, under a quarter last past 6 Myr (Haisch et al.
  2001, Mamajek 2009). At 1e5 G0 the same disc lives less than half as long as at 10 G0.
- Metals: +0.3 dex gives 1.995 times the rock and metal. Pairs: circumbinary cavities at 2.5 separations, truncated
  circumprimary discs with rc0 at most a third of the truncation radius.
- The history has both disc events, the clearing date equals the disc's, disc slices exist exactly before clearing.

## Known limits
- The host luminosity is the zero-age main-sequence value (pre-main-sequence stars are brighter; tracks arrive with F7),
  so early ice lines for young stars sit somewhat inward of where they would be.
- Constant opacity in the accretion term; no dead zone, pressure bumps or dust drift (those belong to growth, F3).
- Bands claim mass on the t = 0 profile; growth stages (F2, F3) claim from bands as they are when solids form.
- External photoevaporation is a scaled rule, not the FRIED grid. Disc lifetimes for M dwarfs and massive stars follow
  only from these rules.
- Circumbinary discs inherit the pair's summed ZAMS luminosity; circumsecondary discs are not formed.
